### We find out why before we fix anything

A furnace that stops has a reason, and our first job is finding it. We diagnose the furnace on site, tell you what failed and what it needs, and explain it in words that make sense to you. If a part is needed, we tell you which one and go get it. Then you decide how to go forward.

### Furnace problems we handle

#### No heat at all
We diagnose the furnace at your home and tell you the cause and the fix. You get the explanation first and the decision after.

#### The furnace runs nonstop
In extreme cold, a furnace may be doing all it can. Turning the thermostat down a couple of degrees saves money and reduces wear on the unit. If it runs nonstop in ordinary weather, call us and we'll find out why.

#### Some rooms stay cold
Closed or blocked vents and registers cause cold rooms and can damage your HVAC system. If every vent is open and clear and a room is still cold, we'll look at the system.

#### Your furnace is old
Heating typically makes up about 42% of a home's utility bill, and a heating system more than ten years old is only 40 to 60 percent efficient. Gas furnaces on the market today reach as high as 97 percent. We'll go through those numbers with your furnace in front of us.

### Repair or replace: a rule you can check

Age alone doesn't settle it. Just because a furnace is old doesn't mean it doesn't work well. We recommend a repair when one part has failed and the furnace otherwise runs well. We price a new furnace next to the repair when the furnace is old and the repair is a major one, or when it has failed more than once. Either way, we lay out both and you choose. A new furnace alone won't cut your bill much either. The biggest savings come from the whole home: insulation, sealed windows and your thermostat habits.

### Winter questions

#### How often should I change my furnace filter?
Check it every month and replace it at least every 90 days. If you run the unit more than normal, or the unit is older, change it more often. A good test is dust: if the filter is covered in it, replace it. A clean filter helps the furnace heat more efficiently and cuts the dust in your home.

#### Which filter should I buy?
Most homes do well with a medium-efficiency pleated filter, rated MERV 5 to 13. Flat-panel filters keep the furnace running efficiently but aren't built for allergies. A filter rated MERV 14 or higher can put added stress on your system, and HEPA filters aren't recommended unless the system has had significant updates.

#### Where does my furnace vent, and what should I check?
Know where your gas furnace vents out of your home. After snow and wind, check that the vent is clear of snow and debris. Check indoor registers too, and keep furniture and rugs off them.
