### A New Roof Starts With a Look at the Old One

Before we talk about a new roof, we look at the one you have. Jose comes out, inspects the roof and explains what it needs. You don't have to know the vocabulary of roofing to make the decision. We explain what we find, and then you decide.

### Repair or Replace

Some roofs only need a repair, and we'll tell you when that's the case. A repair is usually the right call when the trouble is in one spot and the rest of the roof is sound, such as a leak at a single vent or a few damaged shingles.

We would price a replacement when:

- the roof leaks in more than one place;
- the same spot has been repaired before and leaks again;
- the roof is old and worn across its whole surface;
- the wood under the shingles has rotted.

That last one matters because new shingles need sound wood to sit on. When we replace a roof, we replace the shingles, the underlayment and any rotted wood.
