### We Check Whether You Need a New Roof First

A new roof is a big job, so the first question is whether you need one. We come out, look over the whole roof, and tell you what we see: where the damage is, how far it goes, and whether a repair will hold. Sometimes a repair is the right answer, and we say so. ### Repair or Replace? A Rule You Can Check

The question is how widespread the trouble is. Look from the ground, and from the attic for stains and daylight. Please don't climb onto the roof.

A repair is usually the right call when:
- a few shingles are missing, cracked or damaged in one area
- wind has lifted a vent or a few shingles
- you have one leak with one source

A new roof is worth pricing when:
- shingle edges are curled, or shingles are cracked, missing, damaged or buckling across the roof, not in one spot
- bare spots show on the roof
- you can see sunlight through the roof boards from the attic
- the roof is sagging
- moss has taken hold
- you see water stains on ceilings or walls, or visible leaks, in more than one place
- the roof is old and showing several of these at once

The longer you wait on a roof in poor condition, the bigger the problem can get.
