### The demo, page by page

**1. Home**

- **Current site problem:** The browser title is "Roettger's Heating & Cooling | Schedule Your Comfort Today", with no city named. The page carries three H1 headings ("Keeping Lincoln Comfortable Year-Round", "Our Services" and "Contact Us"), so no single headline says what the page is. There are no reviews, hours or street address, and that costs you calls from people who can't tell whether anyone will pick up.
- **What the demo fixes:** One H1, "HVAC Company in Lincoln, NE — Roettger's Heating and Cooling", with call and quote buttons on the first screen. The page opens on the thing your customers get: you speak directly with Matt.
- **A full build would also add:** pages for furnace repair, furnace installation and heating maintenance, and a Google Business Profile that matches the site.

**2. Air Conditioner Repair**

- **Current site problem:** AC repair is one sentence under a shared "Cooling" heading: "AC Repair – From warm air to unusual noises, we'll troubleshoot and fix your cooling issues quickly." Someone searching "AC repair Lincoln NE" on the hottest day of the year has no page to land on, and that search is your most urgent call.
- **What the demo fixes:** A full page with its own H1, "Air Conditioner Repair in Lincoln, NE — Roettger's Heating and Cooling", and the phone number first. It explains what happens when you call, how you tell a repair from a replacement, and what the customer gets.
- **A full build would also add:** mini-split and heat pump repair pages if you service them.

**3. HVAC System Replacement**

- **Current site problem:** Replacing a furnace or air conditioner gets one line each. The repair-or-replace answer sits in the FAQs, financing sits on its own page, and the equipment brand appears only in one FAQ answer. A customer weighing a big purchase has to piece it together from three places.
- **What the demo fixes:** One page with the repair-or-replace rule, the Comfortmaker equipment and the financing together, under the H1 "HVAC System Replacement in Lincoln, NE — Roettger's Heating and Cooling".
- **A full build would also add:** separate furnace installation and air conditioner installation pages, each with its own keyword.

**4. Contact**

- **Current site problem:** The page has 125 words. It shows the phone number and a Gmail address, but no hours or street address. Every field is labeled "(required)" in text only, and the phone field has no label, so a visitor on a phone can't tell what to type there.
- **What the demo fixes:** Phone number first, Matt's name, the area you serve, what to tell us when you call, and a way to reach you by email.
- **A full build would also add:** your hours and address once you confirm them, a map, a branded email address in place of the Gmail one, and a quote form that asks which service the visitor needs.
