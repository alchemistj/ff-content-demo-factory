### Why we sell one window

Not every window holds up to Indiana weather. Infinity by Marvin windows are built with Ultrex, Marvin's patented 100% fiberglass material, which delivers eight times the strength of vinyl. Fiberglass is stronger and more energy-efficient than vinyl or wood. It holds its shape through temperature swings, which keeps the seal tight and keeps drafts and moisture out. Vinyl expands and contracts with Indiana's weather, and that movement can break the seal over time.

> Glad we did our research in comparing them to vinyl products that don’t last very long.
>
> — Grace K.

That is why Infinity by Marvin is our exclusive replacement window for Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana homeowners.

### The beauty of wood, none of the work

Real wood inside a window means real maintenance. EverWood is Infinity's interior finish, and it gives you the look and feel of wood without the sanding, staining or worry about swelling and rot. You can stain it or paint it, and it won't warp, swell or rot.

> The EverWood interior stained wood grain finish matched perfectly with the rest of the house trim and are we are very pleased with the final results.
>

### Insert or full-frame

We offer both, and we settle which one fits your house in your consultation. An insert fits a new window into your existing frame, which keeps your interior trim and shortens the install. A full-frame replacement removes everything down to the rough opening and installs all-new framing, trim and window.

### Installation day

Our own employees arrive, protect your home and remove the old windows with care. Each new window goes in with spray foam insulation around the frame, the right screws and professional caulking, for an airtight seal. Most window installations finish in a single day, and your consultant gives you an estimate of the time before work begins. We install all year, winter included, and we manage the work to protect your home whatever the season.

### Sweating or fogged: two different problems

Moisture on the inside of the glass in winter is humidity from indoors meeting cold glass, and it is common in Indiana winters. Fog between the two panes is different: that points to a failed seal, and it may mean it's time to consider replacement. Call us and we'll look at which one you have.
