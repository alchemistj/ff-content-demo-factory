### What we do

Residential and commercial, from a single outlet to a new panel.

- **Troubleshooting and repairs.** Finding what has stopped working, then fixing it: outlets, switches, light fixtures and the wiring behind them.
- **Panel upgrades.** The panel is the metal box where power enters your home and splits into circuits. Each circuit has a breaker, a switch that shuts it off when it's overloaded. We upgrade and replace panels.
- **Panel labeling.** A clear label on every breaker, so anyone in the house knows what each one controls.
- **Whole-house surge protection.** A device at the panel that limits power spikes before they reach your appliances.
- **Meter base replacement.** The meter base is the box on the outside wall where the utility's meter plugs in.
- **Outlet installation.** New outlets where you need them.
- **Ceiling fan installation.**
- **Outdoor lighting installation.** Lampposts, motion lights and custom lighting packages.
- **New wiring and safety upgrades.** For additions, finished basements and converted rooms.
- **Electrical permit and inspection.** For the jobs that need one.
