### Damage You Can't See From the Yard

Hail can do damage without a missing shingle in sight. It knocks the gritty protective granules off asphalt shingles, and a handful of them in the gutter is a warning. Water spots on an attic ceiling are often the first sign inside the house. Our free inspection finds hidden damage, and we tell you which repairs are needed.
