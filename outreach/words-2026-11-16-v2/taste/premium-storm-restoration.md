### From the First Inspection to the Final Cleanup

Storm restoration means repairing and rebuilding what the storm damaged: the roof, the siding, the gutters and a check of the structure underneath. Quick action stops leaks, mold and weakened wood from following the storm. Here is how a storm job goes with us.

1. **Damage assessment.** We inspect your home and document the damage.
2. **Emergency repairs.** We make repairs right away to stop further damage.
3. **Insurance coordination.** We work with your insurance company to move the claim along.
4. **Full restoration.** We complete the roof, siding and structural repairs.
5. **Final inspection and cleanup.** We check the work and clean up the property.

### Damage You Can't See From the Yard

Hail can do damage without a missing shingle in sight. It knocks the gritty protective granules off asphalt shingles, and a handful of them in the gutter is a warning. Water spots on an attic ceiling are often the first sign inside the house. We inspect for hidden damage and recommend the repairs that are needed.
