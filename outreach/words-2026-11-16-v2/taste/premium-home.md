### Roofs, Siding and Gutters

Our residential work starts with taking the old roof fully off before the new one goes on. We install new roofs and re-roofs, cedar shake and wood shingle roofs, and flat roofs, from asphalt shingles to metal and tile. Roof repair runs from small patch jobs to major repairs: leaks, missing or broken shingles, and storm damage. We also do metal flashing, the metal that seals the joints around chimneys, walls and vents, along with siding, gutters and downspouts.

After a storm, customers have had us replace the roof, siding and gutters in one project. When you want to know what shape your roof is in, we inspect it and give you a detailed report on its condition. For businesses, we do commercial flat roofing, and one commercial customer had us inspect their roof.
