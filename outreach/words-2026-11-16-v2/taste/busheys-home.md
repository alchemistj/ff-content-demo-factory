### How a replacement goes

1. **In-home visit.** Your consultant looks at what you have now for moisture damage, rot, structural concerns, and anything that costs you energy or doesn't work right, and gives you the full picture before you decide anything.
2. **Design, selections and budget.** You go through window profiles, glass, hardware finishes and how the work will be done. Your consultant talks through the full cost and how to budget for it, so there are no surprises at signing.
3. **Measuring.** We come back and measure every opening, allowing for any variance in your existing frames. Those measurements go straight into your custom order.
4. **Ordering.** When you order, you get a detailed timeline, regular updates while your order is made, and confirmed installation dates.
5. **Installation.** Our own employees protect your home, remove the old units and install the new ones.
6. **Afterward.** A service call, a seasonal adjustment, a question years from now: you call us.
