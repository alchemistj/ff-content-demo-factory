**Buttons:** View the Demo Site / See What Changed

### Why we built this site

Read your Google reviews and one story keeps coming back: the customer was told to buy something big, and your electrician found a smaller answer. One of them put it this way:

I was told by two companies I needed a new electrical panel. … These guys are honest and have done good work.

— Sabine B.

Your site never says that. Your twelve services appear as bare names, such as "Electrical panel upgrade", with no word on repair versus replacement. Your reviews also show jobs the site never mentions, such as cameras and a whole-house rewire, and help the same day. So the demo starts with finding the real problem first.

### The demo, page by page

**1. Homepage**

**Current site problem**

  - The top of the page has no headline, so nothing tells a visitor or Google "electrician in Fort Wayne".
  - Fort Wayne appears only in the address at the bottom, and the name in your browser tab never says it. The pages say "in the community" and "in the area".
  - Services are bare names, and no review appears on the page.

**What the demo fixes**

  - A headline and browser tab name that say Electrician in Fort Wayne, IN.
  - Each service in plain words, plus when a new panel really is the answer.
  - Reviews matched to the work, including the cameras and the whole-house rewire.

**A full build would also add** Google and AI tools can only repeat what's written down. We get every service, credential and what makes you different out of your head and onto your site and Google profile, so they all tell the same story.

**2. Electrical Troubleshooting and Repair**

**Current site problem**

  - It shares one page with panels and lighting, under the headline "Electrical System Upgrades & Repairs in the Community".
  - Troubleshooting gets one sentence, with nothing on what usually goes wrong.
  - Nothing covers sparks, a burning smell or a breaker that keeps tripping. Your reviews say help came the same day, and the page doesn't.

**What the demo fixes**

  - Its own page with a Fort Wayne headline.
  - What usually fails, in plain words: a loose connection, a worn outlet, an overloaded circuit, a tripped GFCI outlet.
  - A section on sparks, heat and burning smells that says what to do first.
  - A line on same-day visits, which is what reviews describe.
  - Five reviews about real repairs.

**A full build would also add** A check of what your competitors offer, so services you didn't think to mention still get their own pages.

**3. Lighting Installation**

**Current site problem**

  - Lighting is one sentence about "custom lighting packages", and jobs customers describe, like three-way switches, are missing.

**What the demo fixes**

  - A Fort Wayne headline and its own page.
  - What lighting jobs involve: lampposts, three-way switches, wafer lights, ceiling fans.
  - Six reviews tied to the work.

**A full build would also add** Pages planned from what Google actually shows for each search, so every page targets something people really look for.

**4. Contact**

**Current site problem**

  - There is no contact page; the address and hours sit at the very bottom of the home page.
  - "Do you offer emergency electrical services?" has no answer, and a customer was helped after hours.

**What the demo fixes**

  - A contact page with phone, address and email at the top, and the hours just below.
  - A section for when something can't wait, so after-hours callers know to call.

**A full build would also add** Call and form tracking that shows which pages and towns your leads come from.

### What a full build includes

This demo is the first five pages.

**Gets you found**

  - Every service on your website and your Google profile, telling the same story
  - Each service gets its own page or shares one, based on what Google shows for that search
  - Every town you serve, on your website and your Google profile
  - Built so Google and AI tools can read every page

**Earns their trust**

  - Reviews on every page, matched to the service
  - About and owner pages that build your authority with Google and AI tools, so they know who stands behind the work
  - A strip at the top of every page with your rating and what makes you different

**Turns visitors into calls**

  - Scores 91 or higher on Google's mobile speed test at launch, in Google's "good" range.
  - A call-and-book bar that stays on screen on phones, where most of your visitors are. That's why the 91 matters: a slow page lets them down
  - A contact form that blocks spam

**Keeps working after launch**

  - Mobile page speed tested every 30 minutes, with a re-check within 5 minutes if a page slows down.
  - Your site watched around the clock for downtime.
  - Google indexing and your Google visibility checked every day.
  - Menus, contact links and forms tested in a real browser twice a day.
  - New team members, booking systems and new services, updated for you
  - Updated when Google and AI tools change how they read websites
  - You never have to know what to ask for
