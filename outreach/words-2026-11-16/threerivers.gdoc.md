### The demo, page by page

**1. Homepage**
Why it matters: This is what Google reads to learn who you are.
Current site problem
- The top says Reliable Service. Locally Owned. with no electrician and no Fort Wayne, so Google can't tell what you do or where.
- Five services appear as one-word tiles like Residential and Solar, with nothing on what's inside.
- No reviews appear, though customers praise your crew by name and you have 4.9 stars on Google.
What the demo fixes
- A headline that says Electrician in Fort Wayne, IN, right at the top.
- Each service in plain words, and the two big jobs linked to their pages.
- Reviews placed beside the work they describe.
A full build would also add Google and AI tools can only repeat what's written down. We get every service, credential and what makes you different out of your head and onto your site and Google profile, so they all tell the same story.

**2. Electrical Panel Upgrades**
Why it matters: Where panel upgrade searches in Fort Wayne land.
Current site problem
- Panels are one of nine bullets on a page whose headline has no town.
- Meter base and riser are another bullet, with nothing on what they are.
- The only hint of when to replace is a panel that is 20+ years old.
What the demo fixes
- Electrical Panel Upgrades in Fort Wayne, IN, on its own page.
- What a 100 to 200 amp upgrade involves and why older houses need one.
- Meter base and riser explained, plus signs a panel needs replacing.
A full build would also add Pages planned from what Google actually shows for each search, so every page targets something people really look for.

**3. Whole-Home Rewiring and Breaker Repair**
Why it matters: Tripping breakers send people searching for an electrician.
Current site problem
- Rewires and breaker replacement are single bullets.
- "Outdated wiring" is on a list, with no word on what it is.
- Your reviews about outlets, breakers and a hard-to-wire house appear nowhere.
What the demo fixes
- A Fort Wayne page for rewiring and breaker repair.
- What old wiring looks like and why breakers trip.
- Those reviews beside the work.
A full build would also add A check of what your competitors offer, so services you didn't think to mention still get their own pages.

**4. Contact**
Why it matters: It's where a visitor becomes a call.
Current site problem
- No contact page; "Make an Appointment" opens a link outside your site.
- Your estimate fee isn't on the site, and one 1-star review is about it.
- Your address appears only in the name in your browser tab.
What the demo fixes
- Phone, email and address at the top.
- The estimate fee stated, with the reason.
- What to have ready when you call.
A full build would also add Call and form tracking that shows which pages and towns your leads come from.
