### Air conditioning questions

**When should I have my A/C checked?**

In spring, before the summer heat arrives. A check ahead of the heat costs less than an emergency repair.

**Is my A/C leaking refrigerant?**

Delayed or longer cooling cycles and ice on your evaporator coils are the signs of a leak. If you think yours is leaking, we'll inspect it.

**What should I do with my A/C in winter?**

You don't need to do any preventative work. To keep it in good condition, put a tarp over it in fall and winter to keep snow and leaves out. Take the tarp off before you turn the condenser on again. If the tarp stays on while the air conditioning runs, the unit can overheat and be damaged.

**Do you work on ductless systems and chillers?**

Yes. We repair, install and maintain ductless splits, and we inspect, repair and install chillers, as described above.
