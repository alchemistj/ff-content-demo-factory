**Thin call**

- **Proof:** there is enough real proof for a proof-led short page. Furnace has the one strong story (new igniter, part on the truck, done in 35 minutes on New Year's Eve), plus Kevin S. and David R. AC has four decent quotes.
- **Own substance:** the cuts removed the filler, and what remains shows how little Griffin's own material supports. Each page is about 170 to 190 words, against 1100 to 1500 for the Jon's references. The only Griffin-specific claim on either page is 24/7, and the home page already carries it. Griffin's site has no repair content, brands or licenses to add, and this demo does not need padding.
- **Furnace as the one that could stand:** it has the strongest specific proof, and Chelsea W. covers replacement. Even so it is only a review page with a thin intro, so it is marginal.
- **AC:** do not publish it as a service page. At 186 words of mostly quotes and two repeated FAQ answers, it adds nothing the home page doesn't say. At best it is a section on the home page.

Recommendation: either flag Griffin as too thin for two service pages, keeping home, a single furnace page, contact and strategy, or have the owner supply real content for both pages on the onboarding call. I would not send the current two-page version.
