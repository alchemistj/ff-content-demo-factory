# Too-thin check, 8 prospects (2026-11-15)

Rule tested: ASSIGNMENT.md, "Demo copy (Josh's standing rules, 2026-11-15)": every demo needs 2 strong service pages built only from the business's real material. Never pad. Review quotes plus generic trade copy do not make a strong page. Calibration: Griffin = TOO THIN; ComfortTechs = BORDERLINE (real pages, generic).

Method: read facts.md, audit.md, usp.md and reviews.json for each. Review counts are my keyword pass over reviews.json (on-topic AND specific detail, not one-line praise). The folders hold word counts and content notes for the business's own pages, not the full page text, so "specifics" are what facts.md/audit.md record.

## fortwayne-hvac/jdkuhn: TOO THIN

**Why:** the usp.md lead is two reviews and sits below the gate (15). Each page would be review quotes around one line any competitor could print. This is the Griffin pattern.
