# Trade avatar: Wichita residential plumbing

Built 2026-11-13 (weekend shift, ff-architect #651 method) and reused across Wichita plumbing demos. Method: `docs/writer-guides/AVATAR-AND-USP.md`, section 1. Structure modeled on `avatars/hvac.md` and `avatars/roofing.md`; no facts carried over.

**Evidence base:** one folder, `review-data/jons-plumbing` (59 reviews, a Springfield, Missouri area plumber; reviewers mention Marshfield). It is the only plumbing review set we have, and it is not Wichita. Quotes are word for word, spelling kept. Anything not quoted is marked (inferred), and emergencies drawn from general trade knowledge are marked (trade knowledge). This file describes the buyer, not any one company: never put a quote here on a demo for a business other than the one it came from.

---

## 1. The buyers

**A. The homeowner (primary).** Owns the house, pays the bill, and is the one standing in the water. Messaging: someone comes today, a straight diagnosis, a fair bill.

**B. The new owner of an older house.** Not a separate payer, but a distinct moment: the plumbing problem shows up right after closing. "I just bought this house and already had issues" (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Carol J.`). "New to the area." (`review-data/jons-plumbing, wendy P.`) They have no plumber to call. Messaging: you are the plumber they don't have yet.

**C. The landlord or property manager (inferred).** Not in the reviews. They call for a tenant, want one call to handle it, and want a clean invoice. Treat all messaging for this buyer as unproven.

**D. The referrer.** One reviewer was sent by a client and now refers others: "I was referred to him by a client and I have and will continue to refer people to him as well." (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Richard A.`) Realtors and inspectors are the likely version in Wichita (inferred).

## 2. The primary avatar

**"Wet-Floor Warren and Tina," 35 to 65, Wichita homeowners** (inferred composite). A ranch or two-story from the 1960s to 1990s in Wichita or a nearby suburb, with a water heater near the end of its life and pipes nobody has looked at. They don't have "a guy." Reviewers describe older houses and water heaters that are "REALLY old" (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Janet H.`), and finding a plumber by recommendation: "Jon’s plumbing came highly recommended" (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Rex`). In Wichita they would also search Google or ask a neighbor (inferred).

## 3. The identity iceberg

- **What they say publicly:** "We have a leak. We just need somebody out here." (inferred)
- **What they admit privately:** "I don't know if this is a $100 problem or a $5,000 one, and I'm scared of being told the worst." (inferred)
- **What they won't admit to anyone:** "I ignored that old heater and the drip under the sink, and I don't know what's in the account for this." (inferred)

## 4. Parking-lot moments

**Moment 1: "The pipe let go."** (trade knowledge, with details from reviews) A January morning in Wichita after a hard overnight freeze. The kitchen faucet gives a trickle, then nothing, and a sound comes from the crawlspace or an exterior wall. Warren finds water on the garage floor. He shuts the main off and stands there with a phone in his hand. Similar real moments: "Had a water line break at a T during the heavy snow and sub zero weather.. they came out within a couple of hours" (`review-data/jons-plumbing, David E.`); "fixed my busted pipe after a cold snap in the weather" (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Reese J.`). He calls the first number and wants a person, a time, and no speech about how busy they are.

**Moment 2: "The basement drain is gurgling."** (trade knowledge) Sunday evening, guests due. Water backs up in the floor drain or the lowest tub when someone runs the washer. A sewer backup is dirty water, not clean water, and the house smells of it. Tina is already googling cleanup and wondering what is covered (she shouldn't be told; see section 10). Real version of the first visit going partway: "Dalton came over to clear a clog in our sewer system. He was extremely helpful and knowledgeable of our problem. Although he was unable to fix our problem on the spot" (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Kevin B.`).

**Moment 3: "No hot water."** (trade knowledge, with details from reviews) Cold shower on a work morning, or a puddle under the water heater. "Dalton came out as soon as we called after we lost our hot water." (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Cheryl W.`) The old heater is rusted at the bottom, the quote may be for a new one, and the kids need to get ready for school.

**Moment 4: "The water bill, or the warm spot."** (trade knowledge, no review backing) The bill doubles with no change in habits, a floor feels warm, or the meter spins with every fixture off. A leak in a line under a slab won't show as a drip, and Warren doesn't know who finds it or what it costs.

## 5. Top 5 fears, ranked by emotional intensity

1. **Water keeps coming in and nobody can come until tomorrow.** The water is off, the house is damp, and the plumber says next day. "had to turn off the water to stop the leak ... the best they could do would be the next day. That would not work for .so I thank them and keep calling around." (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Marvin K.`, a 4-star review.)
2. **A plumber who says he'll come and doesn't.** "Gave me the run around for 2 days." "Didn't even call me to say he wasn't coming." (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Jeffrey`, the only 1-star.)
3. **Paying a lot for something that wasn't necessary or isn't fixed.** The same reviewers praise "more than fair" prices and "honest" recommendations, which tells us what they feared: "He has been very honest in his recommendations" (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Richard A.`); "Thought his price was very fair for everything that was done." (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Mike F.`)
4. **The problem comes back and they pay again.** "fixed a problem that two other plumbers never fixed" (`review-data/jons-plumbing, carl F.`). The reverse reassurance: "when he had to make a second visit he did not even charge me labor" (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Parker D.`).
5. **Looking clueless.** They want it explained without being talked down to: "Were sooo patient and explained everything to me." (`review-data/jons-plumbing, wendy P.`)

## 6. Top 5 objections (surface, then the fear underneath)

1. **"Can you come today?"** → "If not, my house keeps flooding." (fear 1)
2. **"Will you actually show up?"** → "I'll wait around and get no call." (fear 2)
3. **"What's this going to cost, and is it really needed?"** → "I can't tell a real repair from an upsell." (fear 3)
4. **"Do I need a whole new water heater?"** → "I'm scared of the big bill, but I also don't want a repair that quits in a month." (fears 3, 4; inferred, from the heater replacement reviews)
5. **"What if it leaks again?"** → "Once you're paid, I'm on my own." (fear 4)

## 7. Top 5 wants

**Practical:** a real person answers the phone; someone comes the same day, or early the next morning; the problem found and explained; a fair price; a second visit that doesn't cost twice.

**Emotional:** relief; being told the truth; not feeling stupid; having "a guy" at last ("Jon is our “go-to” plumber", `review-data/jons-plumbing, Clare R.`); a plumber who stands behind it ("the stood behind their work...", `review-data/jons-plumbing, David E.`).

## 8. Past bad experiences

- **The one who couldn't fit you in.** "the best they could do would be the next day." (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Marvin K.`)
- **The fix that didn't hold.** "a problem that two other plumbers never fixed" (`review-data/jons-plumbing, carl F.`).
- **The long-running problem.** "fixed a sewer issue that had plagued our property for several years" (`review-data/jons-plumbing, Nick D.`).

## 10. Phrases to use and to avoid

**Use:** "tell us where the water is coming from and where your shutoff is" · "we'll find it and explain it before we touch your wallet" · "a real person answers" (only if true for the business) · "we'll tell you if it's a repair or a replacement" · "we call you back" · "if we have to come twice, we'll tell you how that's handled" (only with the business's own terms).

**Avoid (hard nos):**
- Invented stats, prices ("$89 service call"), guarantees ("same-day guaranteed") or warranties unless that business's facts.md proves them.
- "24/7" or "emergency" unless the business confirms it answers at night.
- Licenses, certifications, brand status, awards or years in business not in facts.md. Reviewer-stated years ("the last 7 years") describe Jon's, not the demo's.
- Any claim about insurance coverage, cleanup, health or legal outcomes. Never tell a sewer backup customer what insurance will pay, or whether the water is safe.
- Dollar figures or "no charge" offers from reviews used as the company's own.
- Corporate filler: "premier," "industry-leading," "full-service solutions."
- Water puns ("don't drain your savings") in the hero. The reader is scared, not amused (inferred).

## 11. Tone and triggers

**Tone:** plain, calm, quick to the point, neighborly. Short sentences. Talk like the plumber who explained it kneeling by the water heater, not like a sales office.

**Triggers that land:** a named person who answers (the reviews name Jon and Dalton again and again); speed stated as a plain fact, not a promise; "we'll tell you if it can wait"; the bill matching what you were told; "we stood behind it" (only if true).

## 12. What's thin

- **The plumbing evidence is thin and off-market.** All 59 reviews are one company in the Springfield, MO area. 57 are five-star, one is 4-star, one is 1-star. No Wichita voice at all. Real Wichita plumbing reviews should be pulled later, from several companies, before any demo leans on this file.
- **The emergencies are mostly trade knowledge.** Freeze burst, sewer backup and slab leak scenes are not backed by Wichita reviews. Only the freeze break (David E., Reese J.) and a sewer clog (Kevin B., Nick D.) appear in the reviews, and only briefly. Slab leaks do not appear at all.
- **Fears are inferred from praise.** With one 1-star and one 4-star, most "fear" evidence is what happy customers were relieved about.
- **Money is nearly invisible.** Reviews say "fair" and "reasonable" with no numbers.
- **Landlords and referrers:** one referral mention (Richard A.), no landlord voice.
- **An owner call would sharpen:** the real after-hours policy; how fast a burst-pipe call actually gets answered in a freeze; whether they handle sewer main work or only clearing; how they treat a second visit; and what a Wichita customer typically asks first.
