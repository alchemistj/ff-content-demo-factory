# Independent thinking authority

Research, prescription, and writing are independent thinking stages. Each stage contributes useful ideas to the next. The later model may adopt, improve, combine, or set aside earlier recommendations using its own judgment.

## Three input kinds

Keep these separate in every runtime input.

### 1. Evidence and facts

Original source material, review text and attribution, business identity, source references, and the distinction between **confirmed information** and **inference**.

Editorial recommendations do not become facts because an earlier model wrote them. Source authenticity and factual limitations remain meaningful.

### 2. Approved decisions

The page plan and other decisions a human explicitly approved for this prospect: page jobs, routes, target intents, business scope, and any specifically reserved human decisions.

Human approval of a page plan does **not** silently turn accompanying suggestions into locked editorial decisions.

### 3. Recommendations to consider

Research observations, possible differentiators, useful customer language, potential review uses, proposed angles, section ideas, and suggested emphasis.

Carry useful ideas forward with their source references and short reasons. Later stages receive the original research evidence and the prescriber's recommendations, not only a compressed summary of a summary.

Recommendations use may-language:

- "You may consider emphasizing…"
- "This review may support…"
- "One useful direction could be…"

May-language belongs to advice between agents. Customer-facing business copy should remain direct and confident where the evidence supports it.

A writer does not need to complete an administrative explanation for every ordinary editorial choice.

## Ownership

- The researcher owns research judgment.
- The prescriber owns the proposed page plan and its rationale.
- The writer owns how the approved page jobs become persuasive copy.
- The human owns editorial acceptance of website copy.

The writer may evaluate the relevance of source-backed reviews and choose their use. An unfinished editorial classification is not, by itself, a reason to withhold available source material from a capable writer.

## What is not an instruction

Do not treat recommended first reviews, suggested angles, or section ideas as mandatory. Do not run a second model as editor, judge, scorer, or approval gate. Mechanical validation checks software and artifact integrity only: package shape, approved routes, audience distinction, and faithful quotation of any review the writer chose to use.

Taste and evidence selection belong to writer judgment and the human review.
