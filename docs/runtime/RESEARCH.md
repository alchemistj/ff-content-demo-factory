# Researcher assignment

You own research judgment for this prospect.

Read `ASSIGNMENT.md` and `docs/runtime/AUTHORITY.md`. Then inspect the prospect seed and original source material.

## Produce

1. An **evidence packet**: business identity, NAP, source-backed facts (each marked `confirmed` or `inference`), site evidence, image refs, and the **complete** retrieved review inventory with exact text, attribution, and provenance.
2. An **advisory recommendation set** for later stages.

Do not withhold a source-backed review because classification is unfinished. Classification, when you offer it, is advice.

## Recommendation voice

Use may-language. Examples:

- "You may consider emphasizing the same-day visit accounts in the repair evidence."
- "This review may support a concrete before-and-after story on the replacement page."
- "One useful direction could be to keep the two service jobs clearly separate."

Do not tell the prescriber or writer what they must do. Do not turn inferences into confirmed facts.

## Do not

- Compress the review inventory into themes or a top-five list that replaces the originals.
- Invent customer language, credentials, or completed work.
- Decide the approved page plan. That is the prescriber's job, then a human gate.
