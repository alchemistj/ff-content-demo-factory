# Fluid Frame Demo Homepage Guide

The homepage is the business front door. It should quickly tell a local customer who this business is, what it does, where it works, why it is trustworthy, which service path fits, and what to do next.

It is broader than a service page and should not duplicate service-page depth.

Read `FLUID_FRAME_DEMO_WRITING_GUIDE.md`, the finished service pages, and the homepage examples before writing.

## Continuity

The homepage writer receives the completed service pages. Sound like the same business. Summarize those services without copying their paragraphs. Route into them. Preserve the distinctions they already established.

Do not reopen their strategy or rewrite them inside the homepage.

## Intent

The homepage normally owns the broad company/category + primary market intent. Do not make the H1 compete with a dedicated service page unless the prescription says otherwise.

Window Dudes is a useful exception to study: Springfield window-repair writing lives on `/` because that is the canonical homepage job on that site. Do not copy that routing onto a prospect whose repair work belongs on a service page.

## What to draw from the examples

Window Dudes `/`, SRA `/springfield/`, and Greene Planet `/` all introduce a Springfield company without becoming a second service page.

Notice how each one:

- Makes a specific case in the opening
- Routes into built services
- Places quotations beside company-level benefits (trust, honesty, presence, documentation)
- Ends on a real next step

Greene Planet’s `/springfield/` redirects to `/`. That root page is the Springfield homepage. SRA’s example is `/springfield/`, not the statewide `/`.

## Body jobs

Use judgment on order and format. Cover the jobs that matter:

- Identity: who, what, whom they help, where
- Services: the confirmed set, with built pages easy to find
- Differentiation from real evidence — not website-quality claims
- Confirmed service area
- Ownership, team, or history only when verified and useful
- A clear final action

If a homepage section starts answering the same decision at the same depth as a service page, shorten it and link onward.

## Hero

A short orientation, a literal company/category + location heading, two or three specific trust sentences, and primary + secondary CTAs with a real commitment gradient. Not a slogan template.

## FAQ and length

Use FAQ when it helps visitors choose a path, understand coverage, or decide how to contact the business.

Example homepage lengths are demonstrations. Do not add generic paragraphs to match a number. Packet word targets and FAQ counts are advisory.

## Finish

Make the path to call or contact obvious. Use the same offer and contact hierarchy as the rest of the site.

## SEO metadata

Write the final SEO title and meta description for this homepage route as part of the page. Follow the general guide. The website builder will consume those words rather than inventing them.
