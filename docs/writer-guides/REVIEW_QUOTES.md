# Review quotes on demo sites

How to quote and place real Google reviews on a demo. Josh approved these rules on 2026-10-26.

## Trim, never reword

1. **Use the reviewer's words exactly, but pick which ones.** Take one to three whole sentences from a review. Never change a word, not even to fix grammar or spelling.
2. **Whole sentences, in their order.** When you skip sentences between two picked ones, mark the gap with " … " so it is honest that something was left out.
3. **Never change the meaning.** Don't cut a "not" or a "but," and don't lift the one kind line out of a mixed review. Only 5-star reviews with words are quoted.
4. **Pick the sentences that fit the page.** Commercial lines go on the commercial page, move-in cleaning lines on the residential page.
5. **Long enough to tell its story.** Most quotes run one to four sentences. A review with a real story can run longer, about 100 words, when the story is the proof (Josh, 2026-11-09; see the Evolve and Window Dudes homepages).
6. **Keep the full review in the data file.** The site's data keeps the whole pulled review, and a test checks that each quoted piece appears in it word for word.

Long reviews used to be skipped. Now trim them instead: a strong review that names an employee, a job or a result is worth two or three of its sentences.

## The name under the quote

First name and last initial, like "Rachel D." When the reviewer's Google name isn't a person's name (a single letter, a username or a business name), the caption reads **Google review**. Never guess a name.

## Where reviews go

- **The first review sits right under the top of every business page,** after the hero and trust bar, so a visitor sees proof within the first screen of scrolling.
- Then place each review right after the paragraph it proves. Two to five on a page, where they earn their spot; a review is never a spacer between sections (Josh, 2026-11-09).
- Never the same review twice on one page. With only a few reviews, one review can appear on more than one page.
- No stars, ratings or review counts next to a quote.

## Example

Danielle S. left a 97-word review about Scott handling a property manager's apartment turnovers. It used to be skipped as too long.

Quoted on the commercial page:

> "Scott has been an incredible resource and support to us, especially during our busiest time! He is reliable, timely, and provides quality service in addition to keeping up with the quantity we assign. … Take a Break brings integrity and results."
>
> Danielle S.
