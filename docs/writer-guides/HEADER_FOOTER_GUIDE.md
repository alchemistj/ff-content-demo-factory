# Fluid Frame Demo Header & Footer Guide

Write the global business chrome so every business-facing route feels like one coherent production website.

Header and footer belong to the prospect’s business — not Fluid Frame, the audit, or the demo.

Read `FLUID_FRAME_DEMO_WRITING_GUIDE.md` and the `_chrome.md` files in `examples/approved-copy/` before writing.

## Inputs

Use the approved route list, confirmed business identity and NAP, finished service names, confirmed hours and offers, and the same voice as the pages.

Do not invent a link, service, offer, or contact detail to make the chrome feel fuller.

## Header

Use the confirmed business name and available verified logo. Do not invent a slogan, badge, or “trusted” claim.

Keep navigation on real built routes.

- Logo and Home point to `/home` on Content Demo Factory sites (the Strategy Overview at `/` is not ordinary business navigation).
- The reference sites use their own live routing (`/` for Window Dudes and Greene Planet homepages; SRA’s Springfield example homepage is `/springfield/`). Learn voice and density from them; follow the current prospect’s approved routes when writing a demo.
- Include only routes that actually exist.
- Use the approved business action and verified contact hierarchy for the primary CTA.
- Do not add Free Estimate, 24/7, Emergency, Same-Day, Financing, or Warranty unless that exact concept is supported.

## Footer

The footer should reinforce who the business is, what it does, where it serves, how to contact it, and where the key pages are. It is a trust/navigation anchor, not a second homepage.

Include confirmed identity, phone, email, address, hours, service links, service-area summary, and brief verified credentials when they exist. Group them so they scan. Do not force a four-column layout.

Never sell the website (“professional website experience,” “clean mobile experience,” “this mockup”).

## Reviews in chrome

Page quotation rules do not apply to header/footer. Primary proof belongs in page content beside the benefit it supports. Do not invent star badges, ratings, or review counts in chrome.

## Consistency

Use the same business name, phone hierarchy, service names, service-area language, CTA language, and capitalization as the finished pages.
