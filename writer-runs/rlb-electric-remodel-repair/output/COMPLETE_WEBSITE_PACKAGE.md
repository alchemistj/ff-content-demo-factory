## When a Panel Upgrade Makes Sense

An undersized electrical panel shows several signs:

- Breakers trip repeatedly during normal use
- You're adding major power draws (new AC, electric vehicle charging, workshop)
- The panel itself is aging and needs updating
- You're planning a kitchen or bathroom remodel
- You want to upgrade to smart or automated panel technology for better safety and control

Older panels lack the capacity and features modern homes need. New panel technology offers better monitoring, safety features, and the ability to manage power more efficiently. RLB inspects your panel and usage to determine whether an upgrade is necessary—or whether a simpler solution works better.

## Why Power and Outlets Stop Working

**A single outlet or light stops working** — usually a tripped breaker, failed outlet, or loose wire. Simple to fix.

**Multiple outlets lose power** — suggests a breaker or main panel issue. Takes longer to diagnose, but RLB finds it.

**Breakers trip repeatedly** — could be an overloaded circuit, a short, or a failing breaker. We test to know.

**A switch or fixture fails** — the device itself, loose connections, or wiring. We check everything.

**Power cuts out or flickers** — usually a problem at the utility, main panel, or branch circuit. We narrow it down.
