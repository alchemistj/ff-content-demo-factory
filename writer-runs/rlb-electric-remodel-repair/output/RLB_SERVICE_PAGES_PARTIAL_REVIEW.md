## When a Panel Upgrade Makes Sense

An electrical panel that's undersized for your home's demands can show several signs:

- Breakers trip repeatedly, even with normal use
- You're adding significant power draws (new AC unit, electric vehicle charging, workshop equipment)
- The panel itself is aging and needs updating alongside new capacity
- You're planning a kitchen or bathroom remodel that requires new circuits

A panel upgrade isn't an emergency most of the time, but it's worth addressing before it becomes one. RLB Electric will inspect your panel and your actual power usage to determine whether an upgrade is necessary or whether a simpler solution—like adding circuits or addressing a specific problem—makes more sense.

## Why Outlets, Lights, and Power Stop Working

Residential electrical problems usually fall into a few categories:

**A single outlet or light stops working** — often a tripped breaker, a failed outlet, or loose wiring. Usually simple to diagnose and fix.

**Multiple outlets lose power** — suggests a breaker issue, a broken wire, or a problem at the main panel. Diagnosis takes longer, but RLB has the experience to find it.

**Breakers trip repeatedly** — sometimes a circuit is overloaded. Sometimes there's a short. Sometimes the breaker itself is failing. We test to know.

**A switch or fixture fails** — could be the device itself, loose connections, or a wiring problem. We check everything.

**Power cuts out or flickers** — usually a problem at the utility connection, the main panel, or a specific branch circuit. We can narrow it down.
