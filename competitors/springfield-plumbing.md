## Part 2: Most common claims among the five

- **Licensed and insured** (or licensed/bonded): 4 of 5 (Mr. Rooter, Benjamin Franklin, Roto-Rooter via license number only, Paschal, Precise). Insurance is explicit on 3 of 5.
- **24/7 availability**: 3 of 5 (Mr. Rooter, Benjamin Franklin, Roto-Rooter).
- **Local or independent ownership**: 4 of 5 describe it as "independently owned," "locally owned," or "franchise." Only Precise is "locally owned" in third-party copy. None uses "family-owned" on its homepage.
- **Pricing**: Mr. Rooter is the only one with a flat-rate or upfront pricing claim on its homepage. Benjamin Franklin says "honest pricing." Paschal says "fair pricing." No one publishes a price list.
- **Guarantee language**: 4 of 5 mention some guarantee or satisfaction promise (Benjamin Franklin has the most specific: a $5/minute late-arrival payment). None states full terms on the homepage.
- **Years in business**: 2 of 5 (Mr. Rooter "Over 50 Years"; Paschal "since 1968"). Precise uses "years of hands-on experience" without a number.
