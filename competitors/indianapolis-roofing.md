# Indianapolis, IN residential roofing competitors

Researched 2026-11-15. Five companies: Premier Roofing Company, Bone Dry Roofing, Indy Roof & Restoration, Kingdom Roofing Systems, Stay Dry Roofing.

## Method and limits

- Searches run: "roofing company Indianapolis IN residential roof replacement", "Kingdom Roofing Systems Indianapolis website", "Stay Dry Roofing Indianapolis residential roof replacement website".
- Each company's own homepage (or the location page for Premier) was fetched with WebFetch and read for claims. Quoted wording is verbatim from the fetched page. Customer-review text is labelled as such.
- Selection is by search visibility and directory presence, not a confirmed Google local ranking. Review counts appear only where cited.
- "Not stated" means not on the fetched page. It does not mean the company lacks the service.
- Premier Roofing Company and Bone Dry Roofing use multi-city site templates; the Indianapolis pages are read as the Indianapolis offer.
