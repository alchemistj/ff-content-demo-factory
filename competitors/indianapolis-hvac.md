## Method and limits

- Searches run: "HVAC company Indianapolis IN AC repair furnace", "Airtron Indianapolis HVAC AC repair website", "Masters Heating and Cooling Indianapolis website family owned", "Efficient Systems Inc Indianapolis HVAC website".
- Company homepages fetched with WebFetch; quoted wording is verbatim from the fetched page. Directory and review text is labelled.
- Selection is by search visibility, Carrier/Bryant dealer presence and directory counts, not a confirmed Google local ranking.
- "Not stated" means not on the fetched page.
- Two entries need a check before use: Masters (the site's footprint spans Indiana, Michigan and Ohio, and it is unclear whether it is the same entity as the Indianapolis Carrier dealer listing) and Efficient Systems (no company site found).
