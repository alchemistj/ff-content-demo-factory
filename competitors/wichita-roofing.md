# Wichita, KS residential roofing competitors

Researched 2026-11-12. Five companies, excluding River City Roofing, Flawless Roofing Co, ICT Roofing & Exteriors and Douglas Fry Roofing.

## Method and limits

- Searches run: "roofing company Wichita KS", "roof replacement Wichita", "hail damage roof Wichita".
- Google local results (map pack ranking and review counts) could not be read directly. Companies were picked from search-result visibility and review counts shown on the company's own site or a listing page. The ranking is therefore not a confirmed Google local ranking.
- Review counts appear only where cited below. "not found" means the page or search results did not show it.
- Pages were read as fetched; wording may differ on live pages.
