## Method and limits

- Searches run: "roofing company Tulsa OK residential roof replacement", "Billings Roofing and Solar Tulsa website residential roof replacement", "Green Country Roofing Tulsa residential roof replacement", "Kennedy Roofing Solutions Tulsa website residential".
- Company sites fetched with WebFetch and quoted verbatim where the page was readable. Directory, GAF and review text is labelled.
- Selection is by search visibility and directory presence, not a confirmed Google local ranking.
- Two companies have no readable own site (Billings, Green Country) and one site returned only a title (Kennedy). Their entries rest on directories and are marked as such. Treat them as weaker evidence.
- "Not stated" means not on the fetched page.
