## Window Repair in Springfield — Diagnose First, Fix Right

Window repair is where most Springfield homeowners start — and where most window companies let them down. You call about a drafty window and get a quote for full replacement. You call about a fogged pane and get told the whole unit needs to go. That's not how we work.

We inspect the frame, glass, hardware, and sealing before we recommend anything. Here's what we're looking for when Springfield homeowners call us for window repair:

### Fogged or Cloudy Glass Between Double Panes

The IGU seal has failed. In most cases we replace only the glass unit — not the frame. Significantly less expensive than full replacement.

### Drafts Around a Closed Window

Could be failed weatherstripping, a warped sash, a shifted frame, or failed flashing directing water into the wall. We check all of it.

### Window That Won't Open, Close, or Lock

Usually a hardware problem — worn balances, a broken crank, a failed latch. We repair the hardware and save you the cost of a new window.

### Water Getting in Around the Frame

This is where the inspector credential matters most. Water intrusion can mean failed caulk, failed flashing, or years of moisture in the wall cavity. We find the actual source.

### Broken Glass

Single-pane breakage, impact damage, stress cracks — we cut replacement glass to your exact frame dimensions, usually in a single visit.

### Soft, Rotted, or Damaged Window Frames

Springfield's humidity, storms, and older homes can be hard on window frames. Soft wood, swelling, peeling paint, or gaps around the frame usually point to moisture damage — and we'll tell you whether it can be repaired or if replacement makes more sense.
