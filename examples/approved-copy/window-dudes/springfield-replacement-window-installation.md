## Signs Your Windows Need Full Replacement

Replacement makes sense when the frame is compromised, moisture has reached the structure, the sash is warped beyond correction, the window will not lock safely, or the unit no longer seals. If only the glass or hardware failed, repair may save you money.

## Frequently Asked Questions

- How do I know if I need replacement windows or just a repair?

  If your frame is solid and only the glass unit or hardware has failed, repair is usually the smarter and cheaper call. We only recommend full replacement when the frame is compromised, moisture has gotten into the structure, or the window can no longer function safely.
- How long does replacement window installation take?

  A single window often takes about 30 to 45 minutes. A full house with eight to ten windows is usually done in one day. We pull one window at a time so your home is never wide open for long.
- Will new windows lower my energy bills in Springfield?

  Yes, when installation is done correctly. ENERGY STAR-rated double or triple-pane windows with Low-E coatings can reduce heat transfer, but the installation quality is critical.
