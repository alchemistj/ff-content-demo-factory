## Frequently Asked Questions

- Can fogged double-pane glass be cleaned or repaired without replacing the unit?

  No. Fog between panes means the hermetic seal has failed and moisture is inside the sealed unit. The fix is replacing the insulated glass unit itself, usually without removing the window frame.
- Does Window Dudes do auto glass or windshield repair?

  No. Window Dudes specializes in residential window and door glass. We do not repair windshields, car windows, or automotive glass.
- Can you replace broken window glass without replacing the whole window?

  Often, yes. If the frame is sound, we can usually replace the glass or insulated glass unit without replacing the full window.
- Do you repair patio door glass?

  Yes. Window Dudes repairs and replaces patio door and exterior door glass panels for Springfield homes.
