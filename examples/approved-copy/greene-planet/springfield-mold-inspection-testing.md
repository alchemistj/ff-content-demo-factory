## When Lab Testing Makes the Difference

A dark spot could be common, manageable mold, or it could be something that needs aggressive containment — and the two look nearly identical without a lab result. When testing is warranted, samples go to an accredited third-party lab (never handled by Greene Planet), and the results belong to you. That's how you get a real answer instead of a guess. Lab analysis identifies what was collected and, in many cases, how significant the findings are compared to normal indoor conditions. That independent documentation can also be valuable when you're working with a physician, an insurance carrier, or a home buyer.

### Can mold testing tell me whether I actually need remediation?

Yes. Mold testing compares indoor spore counts against outdoor baseline readings — if indoor counts are within normal range relative to outdoor levels, active indoor mold generation is unlikely even if there's a visible spot somewhere. If counts are significantly elevated, something inside the building is generating spores. David interprets the results honestly, including when they point toward less remediation than the initial concern suggested.
