## Foundation Repair

Foundation cracks and settling in Springfield homes are driven by Ozark clay soil that expands when wet and shrinks when dry, stressing foundation walls and slabs every cycle. Seal the crack without correcting the moisture behind it and it reopens within a season.

Greene Planet identifies the drainage and grading conditions feeding the movement, then seals cracks to flex with normal soil cycles instead of cracking again at the next freeze-thaw. The same clay moisture often connects to a related crawl space or basement mold issue, so both get addressed in one visit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if I need mold remediation or just cleaning?

If mold covers more than 10 square feet, is in your crawl space or HVAC system, or keeps coming back after cleaning, you need professional remediation. Surface cleaning doesn't address the moisture source, which is why mold returns. David will inspect your home and tell you honestly what you're dealing with — and whether you actually need a full remediation or something simpler.

### What makes dry ice blasting different from other mold removal methods?

Dry ice blasting uses frozen CO2 pellets to remove mold from structural wood without adding moisture or leaving chemical residue behind. Unlike sanding or chemical treatments, it cleans surfaces without damaging them and creates less mess during the mold remediation process.

### Why does Greene Planet recommend independent environmental testing?

We recommend independent environmental testing because the company diagnosing the problem should not also be the company verifying the results. Independent testing removes conflicts of interest and gives homeowners unbiased documentation before and after mold remediation.

### How much does mold remediation cost in Springfield, MO?

Mold remediation costs vary based on the size of the affected area, the source of moisture, and the type of work required. Every situation is different, which is why Greene Planet provides free inspections and explains exactly what needs to be done before work begins.

### How long does mold remediation take?

Most mold remediation projects take anywhere from one day to several days, depending on the size of the problem and the moisture conditions involved. During your inspection, David will give you a realistic timeline for your specific situation.
