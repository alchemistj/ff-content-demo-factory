## The demo, page by page

Each card below is one page of the demo site we built. On the left is what may be holding the current site back. On the right is how the demo starts solving it.

### 1. Homepage

`/home`

The first impression — and the page Google relies on to understand who this business is and where it serves.

#### Current site problem

- Title is "Home | High Efficiency HVAC" — it doesn't tell Google what city or service this is for.
- Hero has no primary call-to-action button above the fold, so visitors aren't guided to call or request a quote.
- Trust signals like reviews, BBB accreditation, and certifications aren't visible on first load.

#### What the demo fixes

- Title targets "HVAC Company in Springfield, MO" — what local customers actually search for.
- Clear "Call now" and "Get a quote" CTAs are placed above the fold and visible immediately.
- BBB Accredited and Rheem Preferred Diamond Dealer badges are surfaced near the hero so trust is established right away.

#### Full build would also add

In the full build we also align the homepage with the Google Business Profile, confirm every product and service is reflected on the site, and ship with a 91+ mobile performance score so the homepage loads fast and represents everything the business actually does.

### 2. AC / Cooling Service Page

`/ac-installation-repair-springfield-mo`

The page that should be ranking for AC repair and installation searches in Springfield.

#### Current site problem

- Page title is "Cooling | High Efficiency HVAC" — it misses the phrase "AC repair Springfield MO" entirely.
- There's no dedicated meta description written for this page, so Google fills in a snippet that may not match buyer intent.
- Thin content makes it harder for Google and customers to understand the full scope of cooling services offered.

#### What the demo fixes

- Page title targets "AC Repair & Installation — Springfield, MO" to match how locals actually search.
- A unique meta description is written specifically for this page and its keywords.
- Full-length content covers install, repair, and maintenance as separate sections so each one has a chance to rank.

#### Full build would also add

In the full build we also confirm every cooling service offered (install, repair, maintenance, replacement, mini-splits) is reflected on the site, lock down internal linking from the homepage and trust page, and verify Google Search Console + Core Web Vitals after launch so the AC page steadily gains authority.

### 3. Furnace Installation & Repair

`/furnace-installation-repair-springfield-mo`

The page that should be ranking for furnace install and repair searches in Springfield.

#### Current site problem

- Current URL is /heating — no keywords and no city, so it's invisible to anyone searching "furnace repair Springfield MO."
- Only a handful of words on the page — not enough for Google to understand what the page is actually about.
- No dedicated meta description and no H1 targeting furnace-specific searches.

#### What the demo fixes

- URL slug targets the exact search phrase: /furnace-installation-repair-springfield-mo.
- Full-length page covers furnace install, repair, and maintenance as separate sections.
- Meta description and H1 are written specifically for furnace keywords paired with the Springfield location.

#### Full build would also add

In the full build we also add seasonal content blocks, financing CTAs, and SEO migration protection so the existing furnace traffic carries over to the new URL instead of resetting — plus a clean, page-builder-free codebase that doesn't drag down load time.

### 4. Contact / Get a Quote

`/contact`

The page where interest turns into a phone call or a lead — and where local SEO signals are reinforced.

#### Current site problem

- Contact info is buried — there's no persistent phone number in the navigation on every page.
- A Gmail address (highefficiencyhvac@gmail.com) is used instead of a branded business-domain address.
- Social links point to generic Wix or Twitter pages rather than the actual business profiles.

#### What the demo fixes

- Phone number is visible in the header on every page, so calling is always one tap away.
- Professional contact form includes a service-type selector so leads come in pre-qualified.
- Map embed and consistent NAP (name, address, phone) reinforce local SEO signals on the contact page.

#### Full build would also add

In the full build we also lock down NAP consistency across the Google Business Profile, BBB, and major directories, add an owner and trust page to back up the contact details, and ship a Monthly Visibility Snapshot so the owner can see how often Google is surfacing the site after launch.

## Overall summary

### The big-picture shifts the new site fixes

Zooming out from the page-by-page improvements above, these are the five foundational signals search engines and customers use to understand who High Efficiency HVAC is. Here's the underlying state today — and what the new build is set up to shift.

- **SEO Title:** "Home | High Efficiency HVAC" — No keywords
- **Meta Description:** "From heating to cooling, install to repair… come check us out!" — No city. No CTA.
- **H1 Tag:** "Quality Comfort. Designed to last." — No service or location keywords
- **Service page word count:** "~90 words total on /services" — 300+ needed to rank
- **Page builder:** "Wix.com" — Slower load, weaker SEO control
