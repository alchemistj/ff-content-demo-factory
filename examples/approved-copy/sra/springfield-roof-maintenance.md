## What a Maintenance Visit Covers

SRA provides free, twice-yearly roof maintenance visits for Springfield-area homeowners, documenting shingle condition, flashing, ventilation, and drainage, so small issues get caught early, not after they've already cost you money.

Here's why:

A roof doesn't fail all at once. It fails one small, ignorable element at a time: a nail backing out, a cracked pipe boot, algae holding moisture against the decking.

SRA's twice-a-year visits check:

**Shingle condition** Granule loss, curling, cracking, or missing tabs.

**Flashing and seals** Around chimneys, vents, skylights, and valleys, where most leaks start.

**Attic ventilation** Poor airflow bakes shingles from underneath and shortens their life.

**Gutters and drainage** Clogged or sagging gutters push water back under the roofline.

**Soft spots or sagging** Early signs of decking damage you can't see from the ground.

You get the findings in plain language, whether that's “you're good for another season” or “this flashing needs attention now.” SRA doesn't manufacture urgency to sell a job. An honest “not yet” costs nothing and earns the next call.

## Built for Ozarks Weather

Springfield roofs work harder than roofs in milder climates. Winter freeze-thaw cycles expand moisture trapped under compromised shingles, widening small cracks into real ones.

Spring and early summer bring the hail and straight-line wind this region is known for — damage that can look cosmetic from the driveway and be structural underneath. Summer humidity feeds the algae and moss that hold water against the deck long after a storm passes.

None of that is unusual. It's the normal wear pattern for this part of Missouri — and exactly why a roof that looked fine at move-in can be quietly aging faster than you'd expect.

## Repair, Replace or Maintain?

Not every roof issue needs a new roof, and not every fix should be a patch. Isolated damage on a roof with plenty of life left is a repair.

Widespread wear, multiple leak points, or a roof near the end of its expected lifespan usually makes replacement the smarter financial call over repeated patch jobs.

Our [roof repair page](/springfield/roof-repair/) breaks down exactly how to spot the difference. Roof Maintenance is all about keeping a healthy roof healthy, not diagnosing damage that's already happened.
