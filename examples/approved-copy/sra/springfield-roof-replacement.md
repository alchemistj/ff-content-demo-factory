## What the Job Looks Like

A typical Springfield roof replacement follows a clear sequence:

1. 1

Landscaping and the driveway get protected with tarps before anything else starts.

2. 2

The crew strips the old roofing down to bare deck.

3. 3

Decking gets inspected for soft spots, rot, or old water damage.

4. 4

Any damaged wood is replaced, not covered over.

5. 5

New underlayment, flashing, and ventilation components go in.

6. 6

New shingles or panels are installed per the manufacturer's exact pattern.

7. 7

Full cleanup follows, including a magnetic sweep for stray nails.

The installation schedule depends on roof size, pitch, weather, access, and any deck repairs uncovered during tear-off. SRA gives you a realistic timeline before work begins.

## Repair or Replace

Replacement often makes sense when:

Shingles are cracked, curling, or missing across multiple areas, not just one spot

Damage or deterioration is spread across multiple sections of the roof

The roof’s age and overall condition make repeated repairs less practical

Decking is soft, discolored, or sagging in more than a small area

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does a roof replacement cost?**

The price depends on roof size, pitch, material, access, existing layers, and deck repairs. SRA provides a project-specific estimate instead of relying on a broad regional average.

**Should I wait for a different season to replace my roof?**

Availability changes through the year, but active leaks or storm damage should not be delayed for a possible seasonal price difference. SRA can explain the current schedule and the risk of waiting.

**How do I know whether my roof needs replacement?**

Widespread shingle damage, recurring leaks, soft decking, failing flashing, and repeated repairs can point toward replacement. A full inspection is the most reliable way to compare repair and replacement.
