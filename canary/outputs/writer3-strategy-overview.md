## Why we built this site

Swifts Roofing can meet Springfield homeowners on four pages that match completed jobs already in the review record, instead of a generic “roofing services” site.

Home is the company: finished roofs on Springfield-area houses, with Jared Swift named on the work. Roof Replacement is the page when the roof over the house is coming off. Roof Repair is the page when the roof stays and a leak, seal, flue, or broken tile needs work. Contact is where someone calls or writes once they already know the shop.

That is the conversion path. A homeowner who needs a local roofing company lands on Home. A homeowner who already knows it is a full reroof, or work on the roof that is staying, goes to the matching service page. A homeowner who only needs the number, email, or Hillcrest address uses Contact. Every public page points the call to (417) 771-0477.

Trust is built from named completed work, not from ratings, review counts, hours, or warranties the record does not support. ## Conversion and trust strategy

The site is meant to sort the caller before the phone rings.

- **Company first.** Home answers “who is this roofing company in Springfield?” with completed roofs and Jared on the job. Secondary links send a full reroof to Replacement and keep-the-roof work to Repair.
- **One decision per service page.** Replacement is for the roof coming off. Repair is for the roof staying. Each page says the other job lives elsewhere, so a leak does not get sold as a reroof, and a worn-out roof does not get sold as a seal.
- **Call is the action.** Primary CTA on every public surface is (417) 771-0477. Contact adds email. There is no form, booking widget, or chat in this words package.
- **Proof sits next to the claim.** Reviews are not dumped in a testimonial block. Home uses three completed-roof quotes in non-adjacent sections. Replacement uses three reroof quotes. Repair uses two keep-the-roof quotes. Contact uses none.
- **Hedge what the record cannot carry.** Evening length on Neal’s install, Hoffman’s same-day swing-by, and Josh Baird’s price praise stay tied to those jobs. Owens Corning is Preferred, not Platinum. Licensed/$2M is stated as the company states it, then “ask Jared what that covers.” Estimates are free because the owned site says so.

The intended click path is Home → the matching service page → call, or Contact → call. Inspection, tarping, hail, commercial, and insurance-claim work are not given URLs, so they cannot intercept that path.

## Quality standard this package was written to

- Word count is diagnostic only. The former hard 800-word floor is revoked. No page passed because of length. Advisory useful-body counts already recorded: Home 625, Replacement ~887–892, Repair ~779–781, Contact 203.
- Each public section should add a distinct homeowner decision, scope fact, process expectation, differentiator, or proof point. Do not pad. Do not cut useful evidence to hit a number.
- Grade A service pages need 3 reviews (1 lead + 2 support). Grade B needs 2 (1 lead + 1 support). Contact is review-ineligible. Strategy Overview is review-ineligible.
- Reviews on a page are not adjacent and are not a clumped testimonial dump. Quotes are contiguous excerpts of the sealed written texts.
- Public copy must read like a local roofing site, not an audit memo. Timing caveats stay next to the named job and are not repeated as factory language.
- If evidence cannot support a claim, the claim stays off the page. That is why hours, Aaron Avenue, Platinum, SLAs, and unpublished services are absent.
