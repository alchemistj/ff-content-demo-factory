About
Our Company
Envision Electric LLC is proud to be a full-service electrical company specializing in new and existing residential, commercial, and industrial installations. We are fully licensed and insured with over ten years of experience in the Lincoln, Omaha, and surrounding areas. We take pride in providing our customers with a quality finished product at a competitive price. We give free detailed quotes so let us help you envision the possibilities of your next electrical project.
Our Core Values
What sets us apart from the rest
Quality
Our motto is “value for your money.” We do not take shortcuts and pride ourselves in quality and reputable work. We get the job done right the first time and on time.
Safety
We provide all of the necessary equipment needed to complete your project safely. From fall protection, PPE, lock out/tag out procedures and weekly safety meetings, we strive to maintain an excellent safety record.
Professionalism
Envision’s employees are polite, reliable, and honest. You can ensure that appointments will be made and kept on time in addition to continued communication throughout. Cleanliness and professionalism is guaranteed throughout the duration of your project.
Teamwork
We take a teamwork approach to meet all your electrical needs with you, the client being the most important part of the team. Our goal is to create a trusting long-term relationship with all our clients.
Meet the Owner
Envision Electric is a locally-owned electrical company founded by Tom McEvoy. Tom grew up in the small town of Malvern, Iowa and moved to Lincoln, Nebraska in 2008 to attend college. After attending southeast community college in Milford he joined and completed his apprenticeship program with IBEW Local 265 in Lincoln. Tom has 12+ years of electrical experience on residential, commercial, and industrial job sites and felt as though he had gained enough experience and knowledge to pursue his lifelong goal of opening his own electrical company. Tom is married to his wife Dezi McEvoy who is a mental health practitioner at Monarch Counseling. Together they enjoy traveling, boating, hiking, spending time with family and their little bundle of joy, Hallie Mae McEvoy.
