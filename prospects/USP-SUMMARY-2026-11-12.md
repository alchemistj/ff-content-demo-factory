# Wichita demos: lead claim per demo (#651, step A)

Method: `docs/writer-guides/AVATAR-AND-USP.md`. Avatars: `avatars/roofing.md`, `avatars/hvac.md`. Competitors: `competitors/wichita-*.md`. Score = Appeal × Exclusivity. A claim needs 16 or more to lead.

Recalibrated after QA (11/12): owner or named-person access claims are capped at exclusivity 3, and a braid scores as its strongest single part.

**Thin spots to confirm on owner calls:**
- Douglas Fry: whether they use subcontractors (Bobo P.'s 1-star review says a sub did the work), and whether "no commissioned salespeople" (site-only claim) is current. Tile and DaVinci scores 16 for tile-roof homes and deserves its own section, not the hero.
- Flawless: the terms of the class 4 offer.
- ICT: how often Juan, Brady or Arturo meets the adjuster.
- Air Capital: whether they show the failed part (the reviews say "explained").
- Dream Makers: the start year, whether there's a 24/7 line, and whether Nate can honor the proposed text-a-photo offer.
- Integrity: the price-first lead is inferred from "Please call for quote"; confirm with Sam.
- ICT: how Juan, Brady and Arturo work a short insurance offer.
- River City: each half of its lead claim rests on one review.

Competitor caveats: no Google local pack was available, so rankings come from directory review counts. Two HVAC competitors (Crockett, Air Source) are listed from directories only.
