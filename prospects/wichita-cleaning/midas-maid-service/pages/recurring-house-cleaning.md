## Why Regular Visits Work

Dirt gets harder the longer it sits. Grease on a stove top wipes off in week one and needs scraping by month three. Soap scum, dust on baseboards and film on bathroom mirrors work the same way.

A regular visit catches every surface in the easy stage, so each clean is lighter than a one-off clean of the same house.

It also means you aren't saving the house up for a big reset before company arrives. The reset happens a little at a time.
