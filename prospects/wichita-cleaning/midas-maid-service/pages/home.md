## Why Most Cleaning Services Get Dropped

Almost nobody drops a cleaner after the first visit. The first clean is easy, because everyone is on their best behavior.

It's the fifth or sixth visit where it goes wrong. The shower gets a quick wipe instead of a scrub, and a new person does the kitchen differently.

Or nobody tells you the day moved, and you spend the afternoon wondering if anyone is coming.

None of those is a disaster. Together they turn a service that was supposed to give you time back into one more thing you have to watch.

That's the moment people quietly cancel. It's rarely about price. It's that the service stopped being something they could forget about.
