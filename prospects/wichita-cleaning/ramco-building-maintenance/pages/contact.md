# Contact Ramco Building Maintenance

The fastest way to reach us is to call. If you'd rather write, send the form and tell us about your building.

**Buttons:** Call (316) 262-7900 | Send the Form

## Call or Send the Form

Call (316) 262-7900. You can also fill out the form on this page with your name, phone number, email and zip code, and a note about what you need. We don't publish an email address, so the form is the way to write.

## What to Have Ready

You don't need all of it, but it helps to know:

- What kind of building it is: office, plant, bank, school, medical or something else
- Roughly how big it is, and whether it's one location or more
- When people are in it, so we know when cleaning can happen
- What isn't working with your current cleaning, or what has changed
