## The idea
Harris began in 2015 with one building, a $45 vacuum and Jordan Harris as the only employee. It now has supervisors, a sales manager and the crews of a much larger company, but Jordan and his brother Lance still run it. You get the capacity of a big company and owners who answer for the result: "resources and staff like a Mega Company, yet a personal touch like the Mom & Pop" (Casey C.).
