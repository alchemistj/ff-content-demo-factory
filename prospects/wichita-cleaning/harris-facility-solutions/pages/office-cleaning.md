## What Goes Wrong With Office Cleaning

Most office cleaning problems aren't dramatic.

The trash isn't emptied on Monday. The restroom runs out of towels before a client visit. The vacuum lines are crooked and the kitchenette sink is still wet from Thursday.

Each one is small. Together they tell your staff and your visitors that nobody is paying attention.

## Why It Happens

A building is cleaned well when the same people learn it. They know which door sticks, which office keeps its blinds closed and where the supplies live.

When the crew changes every month, none of that carries over, and the quality drops back to a first-day job each time. A supervisor who walks the building matters for the same reason, because someone has to catch what a checklist can't.

## What We Clean

An office cleaning plan usually covers the same core jobs:

- Emptying trash and recycling
- Vacuuming carpet and dust-mopping or mopping hard floors
- Cleaning and restocking restrooms
- Wiping desks, counters, kitchenettes and break rooms
- Cleaning glass at entries and doors

How often depends on your building. A busy front office with clients walking through needs more than a back office with six desks. We build the plan around your traffic, not a standard package.

## Choosing an Office Cleaner

When you compare cleaning companies, the price is the least useful number. Ask these instead:

- Who will be in my building, and will it be the same people each week?
- Who is the supervisor, and can I call that person directly?
- What happens if something is missed?
- Who owns the company, and will they answer?
