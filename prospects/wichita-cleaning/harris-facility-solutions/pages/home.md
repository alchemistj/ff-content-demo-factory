We clean offices on a regular schedule, so the building looks the same on Friday as it did on Monday.

## Floor Care

Hard floors tell on a building. Finish wears thin in the walkways, grit gets ground into the shine, and by spring the lobby looks tired no matter how often it's mopped.

We strip and refinish vinyl composition tile (VCT), scrub, burnish, and top-scrub-and-refinish between strippings. Jorge Campos, one of our supervisors, specializes in stripping and waxing.

A floor that is cared for on a schedule costs less than one that has to be rescued. See the details on our [floor stripping and waxing page](/floor-stripping-and-waxing-wichita-ks/).
