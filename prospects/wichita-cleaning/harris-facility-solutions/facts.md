### Same company as Harris Dream Clean?
**Verdict: very likely the same business run by the same two owners under two names. The legal link is NOT confirmed.**

Confirmed by primary or site sources:
- The Harris Facility Solutions site itself names Jordan Harris as its Owner & CEO and calls him a "co-owner of Dream Clean" and "Owner and CVO of Dream Clean" (https://harrisfac.com/meet-the-team/).
- The same origin story appears on both sides: the $45 vacuum from an unemployment check in 2015, one building, one employee. The HFS services page says "Harris Facility Solutions was launched in 2015. At that time, we cleaned one location and our owner, Jordan Harris, was the only employee." (https://harrisfac.com/services/). KMUW (2020-06-05) and KWCH (2023-03-21) tell the same story about Harris Dream Clean.
- The 2023 SBA award: the SBA's own 2023 state list names "Jordan Michael Harris" of "Harris Dream Clean, LLC, Wichita, KS" as the Kansas honoree (https://legacy.sba.gov/article/2023/mar/14/administrator-guzman-announces-2023-state-small-business-persons-year). The HFS home page shows "U.S. Small Business Person of the Year, Kansas" (https://harrisfac.com/) and the team page says "In 2023, Jordan was honored as the United States SBA Small Business Person of the Year" (https://harrisfac.com/meet-the-team/).
- The awards: the HFS blog post (https://harrisfac.com/thanks-to-expertise-com-for-the-award/, dated 2019-10-21) says "Harris Facility Solutions LLC was awarded the Thumbtack award for Best Cleaning Group in Wichita, Kansas, in 2015, and the Thumbtack Top Pro Award in 2017." The Expertise.com listing for "Harris' Dream Clean, LLC" carries the same Thumbtack wording (https://www.expertise.com/ks/wichita/office-cleaning, read 2026-11-09). The blog post's Expertise link also ends in "#HarrisDreamCleanLLC".
- Same magazine feature: the HFS home page shows "SUMMER 2023 Wichita Business Brothers Go From Basketball to Dream Clean" (https://harrisfac.com/). An Issuu copy of the 2023 Wichita Business issue is titled "BROTHERS GO FROM TO BASKETBALL DREAM CLEAN" (search result only, not opened).

Not confirmed:
