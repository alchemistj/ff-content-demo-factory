## What a Regular Clean Leaves Behind

A house can look clean and still not be. Dust settles on door frames and the tops of cabinets. Grease films the top of the range hood. Baseboards, wall hangings and the corners of floors only get attention when someone has the time.

You may be living with it without noticing, until you do. Then it is all you see.

A deep clean goes after those places. It is slower than a regular visit, and it should be.

## Why It Comes First

If you want a weekly, bi-weekly or monthly schedule, a deep clean is typically where it starts. In most cases it is required.

The reason is simple. If the first visit has to catch up on months of buildup, the regular clean never gets to the details. If the house starts clean, every visit after it can keep it that way.
