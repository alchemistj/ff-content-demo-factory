## When You Can't Keep Up

It is not laziness. Work, kids, caring for a parent, a bad back: there are a lot of reasons a house gets away from you.

Cleaning is also the one chore that never finishes. The week you get ahead, the week after undoes it.

A recurring clean takes it off your list. You come home to a house that is already done, and you keep your weekend.

## Why the Same Person Matters

A house has habits. The drawer that sticks, the cat that sleeps on the good chair, the floor that can't take the strong cleaner.

A new person each visit learns none of it. They clean what they can see and leave. You are back to explaining it again, or living with it.

When the same person comes every time, the house gets better over time instead of staying the same. That is what a schedule with me is for.

## Weekly, Bi-Weekly or Monthly

**Weekly.** The most thorough upkeep, and it carries the biggest discount. A good fit for homes with kids or pets, where the mess comes back every few days.

**Bi-weekly.** The most popular. Two weeks is long enough to notice the house, and short enough that nothing builds up.

**Monthly.** Light maintenance. It works well when you want a regular reset between bigger cleans.

If you aren't sure, start with bi-weekly. There are no contracts, so you aren't locked into the choice.

## How to Choose

Think about how fast your house gets messy. A family with kids and a dog is back to hair and fingerprints in days, so weekly keeps up with it. A couple who are out all day may only need the bathrooms and floors refreshed every other week.

Monthly suits a house that stays tidy on its own and needs a deeper reset now and then. Spring and fall cleaning are there too, when you want a seasonal reset on top of your schedule.

If you can't decide, tell me about your house and who lives in it. I'll tell you what I would do.
