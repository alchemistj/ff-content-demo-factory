OUR STORY
STARTED LOCAL.
STAYED LOCAL.
The Way Construction was founded in Evansville in 2018. What started as a small roofing operation has grown into one of the area’s most recognized exterior contractors, with a team that handles roofing, siding, gutters, and storm damage restoration across the Tri-State.
The 2-Star President’s Club is GAF’s top honor, earned by fewer than 1% of contractors in any given year. Beneath it sits GAF Master Elite, the certification that requires active licensing and insurance, strong homeowner ratings, and ongoing product training. We hold both.
We stay in the Tri-State because this is home. Our crew is local, our customers are neighbors, and our reputation is built one job at a time.
Our commitment to this community goes beyond construction. Learn more about our faith and mission on The Why page.
HOW WE WORK
WHAT WE HOLD OURSELVES TO.
These are not taglines. They describe how we approach every job, from the initial call to the final walkthrough.
HONEST ASSESSMENTS
We tell you what the roof needs, not what drives the largest invoice. If a repair will do, we say so. If a full replacement is the right call, we explain exactly why.
CLEAN JOB SITES
Debris, nails, and material are cleaned up before we leave each day. At the end of the project, a magnet sweep is standard procedure. We take care of the property we’re working on.
NO SUBCONTRACTOR SURPRISES
The crew that shows up is our crew. We do not hand your job to an unfamiliar subcontractor. You get the team you hired.
BACKED BY WARRANTY
As a GAF Master Elite contractor, we can offer the Golden Pledge Warranty on qualifying roofing projects, covering both materials and workmanship for up to 25 years.
RESPONSIVE COMMUNICATION
You hear from us before the job starts, during the job if anything changes, and after the job is done. We do not go quiet when questions come up.
INSURANCE EXPERIENCE
