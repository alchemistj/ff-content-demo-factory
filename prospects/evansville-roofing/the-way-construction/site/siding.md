WHY IT MATTERS
YOUR SIDING IS YOUR HOME’S FIRST LINE OF DEFENSE.
Damaged or aging siding allows moisture into your wall cavity, which leads to rot, mold, and insulation loss. New siding seals the envelope, improves energy efficiency, and protects the structure underneath.
We use quality materials and take the time to install housewrap, flashing, and trim correctly. The work behind the siding matters as much as what you can see.
Stops moisture infiltration into the wall cavity
Improves thermal performance and comfort
Eliminates pest entry points at gaps and joints
Increases resale value and curb appeal
