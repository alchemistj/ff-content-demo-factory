WHAT’S INCLUDED
EVERY REPLACEMENT INCLUDES.
We don’t cut corners on the layers you can’t see. Each replacement covers everything from decking to ridge cap.
FULL TEAR-OFF
All existing shingle layers are removed down to the decking. No layovers. This lets us inspect the decking and address any damaged or rotted sections before anything new goes on.
DECKING INSPECTION AND REPAIR
Once the old roof is off, we walk every square foot of decking looking for soft spots, water damage, and failing fasteners. Damaged sections are replaced before installation begins.
UNDERLAYMENT AND ICE AND WATER SHIELD
Proper underlayment covers the full roof deck. Ice and water shield is applied at all eaves, valleys, and penetrations where water is most likely to back up.
GAF SHINGLES
We install GAF Timberline shingles — the most-installed shingle in North America. Available in multiple profiles and colors, with wind and impact resistance ratings built in.
FLASHING AND VENTILATION
All existing flashing is inspected and replaced as needed. Proper ridge ventilation is confirmed or corrected to protect against heat and moisture buildup in the attic.
SITE CLEANUP
We magnet-sweep the yard, haul off all debris, and leave the property clean. You get a completed job, not a cleanup project.

FREE STRATEGY SESSION AND PRICING
