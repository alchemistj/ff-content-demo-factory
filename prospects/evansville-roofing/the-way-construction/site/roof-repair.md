WHAT WE REPAIR
WE REPAIR IT AT THE SOURCE.
Water is often showing up inside the house in a different location than where it entered the roof. We trace it back to the actual breach before any repair work begins.
ACTIVE LEAKS
We locate the entry point, assess any decking damage underneath, and make the repair. You get a photo record of what we found and what we did.
MISSING AND DAMAGED SHINGLES
Wind, impact, and age can loosen or remove shingles, leaving the underlayment and decking exposed. We match the replacement shingles as closely as possible and seal properly.
FLASHING FAILURES
Flashing at chimneys, skylights, dormers, and walls is one of the most common sources of leaks. We remove, reset, and reseal flashing that has lifted, corroded, or separated.
RIDGE CAPS
Ridge cap shingles bear the most weather exposure and fail before the rest of the roof. We replace deteriorated or missing ridge caps and reseal the ridge line.
PIPE BOOTS AND PENETRATIONS
Rubber pipe boots crack and separate over time, especially in temperature extremes. We replace failed boots and reseal all roof penetrations including vents and gas lines.
VALLEYS AND EAVE EDGES
Valley flashing and drip edge failures can allow water to run behind gutters or through valley seams into the decking. We repair or replace these sections when they are contributing to water intrusion.
REPAIR OR REPLACE
HOW WE DECIDE WHICH PATH IS RIGHT.
We give you an honest assessment based on what we actually find. A repair that buys five more years may be the right call. A repair that just delays the inevitable is not a service to you. Here is how we think about it.
REPAIR MAKES SENSE WHEN…
THE DAMAGE IS ISOLATED
The leak or damage is confined to one area
The rest of the roof is in good condition
The roof is less than 15 years old
The decking beneath the damaged area is sound
The failure is a specific component (pipe boot, flashing, single section)
REPLACEMENT MAKES SENSE WHEN…
THE ROOF IS FAILING OVERALL
The roof is 20+ years old with widespread granule loss
Multiple areas are leaking or showing active deterioration
The decking is significantly damaged or rotted
A layered roof has reached the legal or structural limit for additional layers
The cost of repair approaches or exceeds replacement value over time
