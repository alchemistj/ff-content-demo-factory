## Business in one line
A single-office Peoria roofer at 202 SW Center St with a metal-roofing specialty (six real metal pages on the site) and 36 near-identical town pages. Only 10 of its 48 Google reviews have written text, and most are one line. No owner, years, hours, email, license or warranty terms are in the folder (facts.md).

## Core customer insight (three lines)
- **What the buyer doesn't know:** which kind of metal roof (standing seam, corrugated, metal shingles) suits the building, and what a complete quote should name.
- **What most companies do:** treat metal as an add-on. The competitor scan found no metal-roofing claim at any of the five.
- **What Pioneer does instead:** metal roofing is the specialty, for homes and commercial buildings, with a page for each kind on its own site.

## Gate result
Top claim: "metal roofing is the specialty," **12**. **Below gate** (16+ needed). The demo leads with it as the best honest claim, and the strategy page carries "The offer we'd build with you" (below). Nothing in the reviews can lift a claim over the line: they are one line each.

## The offer we'd build with you (proposal, scored the same way)
A one-page metal roof spec handed to the owner before the work starts: the kind of panel and finish, how it is fastened, what goes underneath, how the edges, valleys and vents are handled, and what the roofer and the manufacturer each stand behind. Appeal 4 (objections #3 and #4: locked in before understanding), Exclusivity 4 (none of the fetched competitor sites offers one). Score **16**. Not something Pioneer does today. Terms are theirs to set. The service page's five "questions to ask any roofer" are the draft of this sheet.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Peoria homeowners handed a metal roof quote they can't read, Pioneer Roofing is the shop that works in metal, so the panel, the fasteners and the details are named before you sign.
- **B, method:** Unlike roofers who treat metal as an add-on, Pioneer has a page for each kind: standing seam, corrugated, metal shingles, residential and commercial.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You know which metal roof you are buying and why.

**Pick: B.** It is the only claim that rests on Pioneer's own site rather than one review, and it matches the six metal pages. A is the hero's second paragraph.
