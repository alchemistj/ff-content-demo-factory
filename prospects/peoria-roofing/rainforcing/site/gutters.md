Common Signs Your Gutter System Needs Attention
Water Pooling at the Foundation: Soil erosion or basement dampness indicating improper downspout drainage.
Fascia or Soffit Rot: Water backing up behind the gutter, rotting the supporting wood structure.
Sagging or Separation: Fasteners pulling free from the roofline due to heavy ice or water accumulation.
Storm & Hail Dents: Crushed downspouts or cracked aluminum from severe weather.
