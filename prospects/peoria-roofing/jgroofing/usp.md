# USP: JG Roofing and Company, Peoria IL

Method: `docs/writer-guides/AVATAR-AND-USP.md` section 2. Appeal is scored against `avatars/roofing.md` (a Wichita avatar, reused for the trade; the fears and wants are the same). Exclusivity is scored against the five companies in `competitors/peoria-roofing.md`: River City Roofs, Kreiling, Zeller, Messing, Blue Line. Only River City, Kreiling and Messing have sites we could read. Zeller and Blue Line are directory-only, so exclusivity is scored conservatively.

## Business in one line
A single-office Peoria roofer at 1010 W Northcrest Ave with a thin Wix site. Its 61 written Google reviews (59 five-star) name Amanda in 19 and Jose in 22, and 18 mention insurance or a claim. Owner is not stated on the site (facts.md), so no owner-access wording is used.

## Core customer insight (three lines)
- **What the buyer doesn't know:** how a hail claim works, in what order, and what the roofer's part is in it.
- **What most companies do:** say "we work with your insurance" (Messing states it; River City only through a review) and leave the homeowner to work out who does what.
- **What JG does instead, in reviews:** Amanda sits down and explains each step; Jose looks at the roof and says what it needs. The same two names run through the reviews from the first call to the last day.

## Gate result
Top claim: "Amanda walks you through the insurance claim, step by step," **15**. **Below gate** (16+ needed). The demo leads with it as the best honest claim, and the strategy page carries "The offer we'd build with you" (below). Same position as the other 15-point demos in this batch.

## The offer we'd build with you (proposal, scored the same way)
A one-page claim and job timeline, handed to the homeowner before the tear-off: the adjuster visit, what the insurance company sends, who to call, the date, the timeframe and the cleanup, in order. Appeal 5 (fears #1 and #5, objection #1), Exclusivity 4 (none of the 3 fetched sites offers one; River City's install timeline is "not said"). Score **20**. Not something JG does today: reviews show Amanda explaining the steps out loud. The page says what it leaves out (coverage and settlement belong to the insurer). Terms are theirs to set.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Peoria homeowners staring at a hail claim they don't understand, JG Roofing is the roofer where Amanda explains each step and Jose says what the roof needs, so you are never left guessing what comes next.
- **B, method:** Unlike roofers who say "we work with your insurance" and stop there, JG splits it in two: Jose looks at the roof, and Amanda walks you through the claim and what to expect.
- **C, emotional outcome:** Someone is keeping track, so you don't have to learn roofing and insurance in the same week.

**Pick: A.** It names fear #1 in the buyer's words and has the strongest review support (Daniel G., Rose H., Kayla T.). B is close and is the hero's second line.

## What's thin
- **The lead is below gate (15).** Insurance help is claimed by Messing and is table stakes in the Wichita scoring (E 1 there). It is held at 3 here because only one of three fetched sites states it and none names who does it.
- **Who Amanda and Jose are.** Reviews say "owners" (Teresa C., carl1978) but facts.md lists the owner as Unverified, and the site email is amanda@. The copy names them as the people customers deal with, never as owners, and uses "we". Ask on the call.
- **Claim outcomes.** Several reviews say JG got "more than I expected" covered. The copy never promises an outcome and never interprets a policy.
- **One-review specifics** (nail sweeps, dumpster on wheels, a siding match proved, three inspections) appear only as quotes in the reviewers' words.
- **Not used because facts.md marks them Unverified or Conflict:** GAF certification (not checked on the locator), "BBB A+ accredited" (no BBB profile found), "licensed, insured, bonded", the 2018 founding year and "family-owned" (search snippet only), Google rating and count, the two Angi review counts.
- **Phone.** Site visible number and Google say (309) 567-8236; the Call or Text tel links dial (309) 316-3652. The demo uses the Google number; the conflict is the first `confirm` item.
- **Service page.** Roof replacement was chosen on about 30 on-topic reviews (a roof replaced, new roof or re-roof), against about 5 for repair. The site has no service pages of its own.
- **Service area.** The site says "Peoria, and All of Central Illinois" and names no towns, so the demo names Peoria only.
