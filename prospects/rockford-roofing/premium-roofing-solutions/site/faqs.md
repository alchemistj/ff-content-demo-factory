How long does a roof installation or replacement take?
The duration of a roof installation or replacement depends on factors like the size and complexity of the roof, weather conditions, and the type of roofing material used. Typically, a roof can be replaced in a day, but in extreme cases, it could take three to five days. For more detailed information, please reach out to us.
How can I identify if my roof has problems?
Common signs of roofing issues include:
Missing, cracked, or curling shingles
Water stains on ceilings or walls
Sagging roof deck
Granules accumulating in gutters
If you notice any of these signs, it’s advisable to contact a roofing professional for an inspection.
What roofing materials do you recommend?
The choice of roofing material depends on various factors, including your budget, aesthetic preferences, and the local climate. Common options include asphalt shingles, metal roofing, clay tiles, and cedar shakes. We can guide you through the best options to fit your needs.
