Roof Inspection Services – Keep Your Roof in Top Condition
At Premium Roofing Solutions LLC, we offer thorough roof inspection services in Wisconsin and Illinois.  From Green Bay, Madison, Lake Geneva, Beloit and Rockford IL to identify potential problems before they turn into costly repairs. Our experienced roofing professionals inspect every aspect of your roof to ensure it’s in excellent condition.
Why Regular Roof Inspections Are Important
Regular roof inspections help to:
Catch Small Problems Early: Address minor issues before they become major repairs.
Extend the Life of Your Roof: Proper maintenance and inspections can help extend the lifespan of your roof.
Ensure Storm Readiness: Get peace of mind knowing your roof is ready to withstand heavy storms.
What Our Roof Inspection Covers
Our comprehensive roof inspection includes:
Shingle & Tile Condition
We inspect for missing, damaged, or worn-out shingles and tiles.
Leak Detection
We look for signs of leaks, water damage, or other moisture-related problems.
Flashing & Gutters
We check the condition of your roof’s flashing, gutters, and other components that protect against water infiltration.
Structural Integrity
We assess the overall structural condition of your roof, ensuring it’s solid and stable.

FAQs About Roof Inspections
How often should I get a roof inspection?
It’s recommended to get a roof inspection at least once a year or after major storms.
How long does a roof inspection take?
A typical roof inspection takes about 1-2 hours, depending on the size of the roof.
