Do I Really Need to Replace My Gutters With My Roof?
A Question Every Smart Homeowner Asks…
You’re replacing your roof — or at least thinking about it.
And somewhere along the way, someone asks:
“You planning to do the gutters too?”
And now you’re wondering…
“Do I need to?”
It’s a smart question. Because gutters are part of your roof system. But that doesn’t mean they always need replacing.
Let’s walk through the smart way to decide:
When You Should Do Both Together
If your gutters are sagging, rusted, or clogging often — this is the perfect time to replace them. Why?
You already have a crew on-site
Your fascia boards are exposed
You avoid paying for two separate projects
It ensures your entire water drainage system works as one
A brand-new roof and old, damaged gutters don’t mix. It’s like putting bald tires on a new car.
When You Might Not Need New Gutters
Now, if your gutters are still doing their job — draining water, holding their shape, no visible wear — then no, we won’t recommend replacing them.
In fact, we’ll work around them. Carefully.
Because we don’t sell what you don’t need.
