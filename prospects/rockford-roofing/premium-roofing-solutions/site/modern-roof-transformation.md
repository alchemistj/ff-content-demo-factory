Modern Roof Transformation
Project Overview
Premium Roofing recently completed a transformative roofing project for a home with an older shingle system that had sustained damage over time. The roof experienced multiple leaks due to age, water intrusion, and wind lift caused by severe weather. Our team stepped in to address these issues and deliver a stunning update that enhanced both the functionality and aesthetics of the property.
Key Challenges
Leaking Areas
Significant leaks were present, especially near the chimney and other vulnerable points.
Water-Damaged Decking
Several sheets of roof decking required replacement due to water damage.
Wind Damage
High winds had lifted portions of the older shingles, compromising the roof’s integrity.
Our Approach
Decking Replacement
Removed and replaced all damaged decking sheets to restore structural integrity.
Enhanced Protection
Installed additional protective materials in critical areas prone to leakage, such as around the chimney.
Premium Shingle Installation
Upgraded the roof with CertainTeed Moire Black shingles, known for their durability and elegant appearance.
Gutter and Wrap Updates: Complemented the roof update by installing a sleek black gutter system and black garage door wraps for a cohesive modern look.
Final Result
Improved Functionality
The roof is now leak-free, secure against wind damage, and designed to last for years to come.
Stunning Aesthetics
The homeowner selected Moire Black shingles, which paired perfectly with the black gutter system and garage door wraps, giving the property a fresh, contemporary appearance.
Customer Satisfaction
The client was thrilled with the color upgrade and overall craftsmanship of the project.
