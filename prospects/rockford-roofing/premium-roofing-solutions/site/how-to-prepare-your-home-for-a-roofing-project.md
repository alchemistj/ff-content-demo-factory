How to Prepare Your Home for a Roofing Project
Reduce delays and keep your project stress-free
Protect your home, vehicles, and landscaping from accidental damage
Know what to expect before the crew shows up
You’re excited about getting your new roof. Maybe a little nervous too. After all, it’s a big project. You might be wondering: “Is there anything I should do to get ready?”
The answer is yes. And we at Premium Roofing Solutions want to help you be fully prepared so that everything goes smoothly.
Step 1: Clear the Way for Equipment and Workers
Roofing is a labor-intensive job, and the crew will need room to work efficiently. That means making space:
Move vehicles out of the driveway and garage
Remove patio furniture, grills, and outdoor decor near the house
Keep gates unlocked and pets inside
The more access we have, the faster and safer the job gets done.
Step 2: Protect Fragile Items Inside Your Home
During a roof tear-off and install, there will be some vibration — especially on older homes. Take a few minutes to:
Remove picture frames and fragile items from shelves
Take down hanging wall art or mirrors
Cover attic items with drop cloths
You’d be surprised how many homeowners thank us later for this tip.
Step 3: Talk to Your Neighbors
Roofing can be noisy. It’s courteous to give your neighbors a heads-up the day before the crew arrives. Let them know the project should last a day or two and that you’re working with a professional crew (that’s us!) who respects the neighborhood.
Step 4: Prepare Kids and Pets
Young children and pets may be startled by the noise. Make arrangements if needed:
Plan indoor activities or off-site visits
Keep pets in a quiet room or at a friend’s for the day
Roofing isn’t a quiet process — but it’s fast and efficient with the right team.
Step 5: Know the Process
Our team will walk you through every step before we begin:
Arrival time and crew size
Where materials will be placed
How cleanup is handled
We’ll answer your questions and set expectations so there are no surprises.
