Cedar Shake Roof Replacement: The Look You Love, Built to Last
If you’re a homeowner with a Cedar Shake roof, you already know the charm and character it brings to your home. But over time, even the most beautiful natural cedar roofs wear down—exposing you to costly repairs, moisture issues, and reduced energy efficiency.
At Premium Roofing Solutions, we help homeowners like you replace old Cedar Shake roofing with modern Cedar Shake replacement shingles—giving you the timeless wood look you love, with the durability and performance of advanced roofing technology.
Why Replace Your Old Cedar Shake Roof?
Natural Cedar Shake roofs are beautiful—but they don’t last forever. Here’s why many homeowners are choosing to upgrade:
Aging & Deterioration: If your cedar shingles are curling, splitting, or have moss and rot, it’s time for a replacement.
Maintenance Costs: Cedar Shake requires ongoing upkeep. Modern replacements offer long-term performance with far less maintenance.
Insurance Requirements: Some insurance companies now limit or increase premiums on homes with aging wood shake roofs.
Better Performance: Today’s engineered Cedar Shake shingles provide enhanced wind, impact, and fire resistance—without sacrificing appearance.
Modern Cedar Shake Replacement Shingles: Best of Both Worlds
We install Cedar Shake-style replacement shingles that replicate the rich texture of hand-split cedar—but with better protection, longevity, and value.
You can choose from:
Synthetic Cedar Shake Shingles
Made from engineered materials (such as composite or polymer), these shingles resist fading, cracking, and warping. They offer the beauty of cedar with Class A fire resistance and 50+ year lifespans.
Treated Real Cedar Shingles
Prefer to stay with natural wood? We offer pressure-treated or fire-retardant Cedar Shakes that are longer-lasting and code-compliant—especially for regions with strict fire or weather regulations.
Impact-Resistant Options
For homeowners in hail-prone or high-wind areas, we offer impact-rated replacement shingles that combine rustic style with advanced protection.

FAQs About Cedar Shake Roof Replacement
Can I keep the cedar look with a replacement shingle?
Absolutely! Modern shake-style shingles offer the same authentic wood grain textures with better durability.
Do replacement Cedar Shake shingles need maintenance?
Synthetic options require very little maintenance. Treated natural wood options need occasional cleaning and inspection.
How long will my new Cedar Shake roof last?
Many of our replacement systems are backed by 30–50 year warranties and built to outlast traditional cedar.
Is it worth upgrading from real wood to synthetic shake?
Yes—especially for homeowners who want less upkeep, better insurance coverage, and long-term value.
