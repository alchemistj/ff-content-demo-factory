Why Regular Roof Inspections Are Crucial
A roof doesn’t send you a calendar invite when it’s time to inspect it. It doesn’t ring your phone to warn you about loose shingles or a slow-forming leak. But make no mistake—your roof is talking to you. And if you live in Wisconsin or Northern Illinois, where winter snow meets spring storms and summer heat, listening to your roof isn’t just wise… it’s essential.
At Premium Roofing Solutions, we understand that roof inspections in Wisconsin & Illinois are the first step to catching a small problem before it becomes a big problem. And what we’ve learned over the years is this: the costliest repairs usually stem from the smallest overlooked issues. A roof inspection may not sound exciting, but it could be the smartest home investment you make this year.
So why are roof inspections so important—especially here in the Midwest? Let’s walk you through it.
The Hidden Threats You Can’t See from the Ground
Most homeowners only think about their roof when something goes wrong—a leak in the attic, a stain on the ceiling, or a shingle blown off in a storm. But by the time you spot those symptoms, the damage is already done.
A professional roof inspection uncovers what your eyes (and even binoculars) can’t:
Soft spots indicating weakened decking beneath the shingles
Worn or missing flashing around chimneys, vents, and skylights
Clogged or disconnected gutters leading to water pooling at the base of your roof
Minor granule loss that signals shingles are aging prematurely
Hidden hail or wind damage that may qualify for an insurance claim
It’s not about fixing problems after they arise—it’s about spotting and stopping them before they cost you thousands.
Northern Illinois & Wisconsin Climate: Beautiful but Brutal on Roofs
Living in the midwest means enjoying four distinct seasons—but your roof pays the price.
Winter Snow & Ice Dams: As snow builds up and melts unevenly, it can back up under your shingles and cause interior leaks.
Spring Storms: Thunderstorms, hail, and high winds test every seam and shingle.
Summer Heat: UV rays break down asphalt shingles over time, causing brittleness and fading.
Fall Leaves: Clogged gutters can lead to moisture buildup and rotting fascia boards.
If your roof isn’t inspected at least once a year, these seasonal shifts can slowly erode its integrity—without you even knowing.
How Often Should You Get a Roof Inspection?
Here’s what we recommend for homeowners:
Once annually (preferably in spring or fall)
After any major storm, especially hail or wind over 45 mph
Before selling or buying a home
If your roof is over 10 years old
Inspections don’t just protect your home—they provide peace of mind. And at Premium Roofing Solutions, we offer complimentary roof inspections for residents because we believe prevention is the best form of protection.
What to Expect During a Roof Inspection
A full inspection with our team is simple, straightforward, and thorough.
1. Visual Survey
We examine all visible areas of the roof, looking for loose, damaged, or missing shingles, discoloration, and sagging.
2. Structural Assessment
We check for signs of structural damage, such as warping in the roofline or soft spots in the decking.
3. Interior Check
We inspect your attic for signs of leaks, mold, poor ventilation, and insulation problems.
4. Flashing & Gutter Review
We verify that all flashing, vents, and gutters are securely installed and functioning correctly.
5. Condition Report & Recommendations
We provide a detailed report with photos, identifying any issues we found and offering repair or replacement suggestions—along with cost estimates.
Common Problems We Find in Roofs
From the hundreds of roofs we’ve inspected in this area, here are the most frequent issues:
Improperly sealed flashing around skylights or chimneys
Gutter blockages causing water to backflow under shingles
Nail pops and loose shingles from high winds
Algae streaks or moss growth due to trapped moisture
Heat blisters or cracked shingles from summer sun exposure
Ventilation issues leading to moisture buildup and mold in the attic
In most cases, homeowners had no idea these problems existed. Early intervention can mean the difference between a simple $250 flashing repair and a $12,000 full roof replacement.
