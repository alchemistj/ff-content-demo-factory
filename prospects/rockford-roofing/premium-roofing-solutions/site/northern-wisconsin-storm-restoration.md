Northern Wisconsin Storm Restoration
Project Overview
In northern Wisconsin, a beautiful home faced massive hail damage caused by 2-3″ hailstones during a severe storm. The damage extended to the shingles, James Hardie siding, and gutter system. Premium Roofing was called in to restore the home to better-than-pre-storm conditions with premium materials and expert craftsmanship.
Key Challenges
Hail-Damaged Shingles
The roof’s shingles were severely impacted, compromising the home’s protection against further weather events.
Siding Damage
The siding sustained significant dents and cracks from the hailstones.
Gutter System
The existing gutters were heavily dented and required replacement.
Our Approach
Impact-Resistant Shingle Installation
Replaced the damaged roof with Malarkey Class 4 Impact Shingles, providing superior durability and protection against future storms.
Siding Repair
Repaired and restored the James Hardie siding to its original strength and appearance.
Upgraded Gutters
Installed 6″ gutters to enhance the home’s water management system and ensure long-lasting performance.
Final Result
Enhanced Protection
The Malarkey Class 4 Impact Shingles provide unmatched resistance to hail and severe weather.
Beautiful Restoration
The home’s exterior was restored to a pristine condition, maintaining its charm while gaining enhanced durability.
Customer Delight
The homeowner was thrilled with the transformation and the peace of mind offered by the upgrades.
