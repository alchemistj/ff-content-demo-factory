Two-Story Home Transformation Roscoe, IL
Location: Roscoe, IL
Project: Comprehensive roofing and siding transformation featuring an upgrade to Malarkey Vista Class-4 (Brilliant Black) shingles and Ironstone Double Dutch Siding
Assessment: The roof and siding were damaged by weather over time. By accurately identifying damage, managing the insurance claim process, and delivering a turnkey renovation with premium products and expert project management
Outcome: Provided a seamless, zero-effort experience that enhanced both curb appeal and structural integrity
A two-story residence in Roscoe, Illinois faced exterior damage and was in need of a complete
overhaul to restore its appearance and structural integrity. The homeowners sought a solution
that not only addressed the underlying damage but also delivered a visually striking, durable,
and low-maintenance exterior. Premium Roofing Solutions LLC stepped in to provide a
seamless, premium-quality roofing and siding experience that exceeded expectations at every
stage.
The Challenge
Over time, the property’s existing roof and siding had been compromised by weather-related
wear and tear. Beyond the obvious cosmetic issues, the homeowners were concerned about
potential long-term structural problems and the hurdles associated with insurance claims and
contractor coordination. They required a partner who could:
Accurately assess the full extent of damage
Manage insurance negotiations on their behalf
Offer a hassle-free, high-quality roofing and siding replacement
Provide improved materials that could enhance both curb appeal and overall property
value
The Solution
Premium Roofing Solutions LLC delivered a comprehensive, turnkey approach that placed the
homeowners’ peace of mind at the forefront of the project.
Accurate Damage Identification
The PRS team conducted a thorough on-site inspection to evaluate both the roof
and siding. They identified the precise nature and extent of the damage, which served as
a critical foundation for a successful insurance claim and an effective remediation plan.
Insurance Claim Approval & Management
Navigating insurance claims can often be time-consuming and stressful. PRS handled
every aspect of the claim process—from filing the necessary paperwork to interfacing
directly with the insurance company—securing full coverage for the replacement. This
approach eliminated the burden on the homeowners, ensuring a smooth, zero-effort
experience on their end.
Premium Materials & Upgrades
In line with their commitment to exceptional quality, PRS upgraded the homeowners to
Malarkey Vista Class-4 impact-resistant shingles at no additional cost. This
complimentary upgrade not only improved the home’s weather resistance and durability
but could also potentially yield long-term insurance savings.
For siding, PRS introduced Ironstone Double Dutch Siding. This choice offered
enhanced strength, lower maintenance requirements, and a richly textured appearance
that complemented the Malarkey Vista Brilliant Black roofing shingles. The result was a
cohesive and visually stunning exterior that elevated the home’s overall curb appeal.
World-Class Project Management
Throughout the project, PRS maintained open and proactive communication with the
homeowners, guiding them through the timeline, product selections, and any necessary
follow-ups. The project was completed efficiently, on schedule, and with minimal
disruption to the homeowners’ daily lives.
Results & Benefits
Upon completion, the Roscoe residence showcased:
A high-performance roofing system, featuring Class-4 shingles known for superior
impact resistance and potential insurance discounts.
Durable, low-maintenance Ironstone Double Dutch Siding that enhanced the home’s
aesthetic and structural integrity.
The peace of mind that comes from having insurance claims managed by professionals,
ensuring a stress-free renovation process.
A heightened property value and a fresh, modern look that stands out in the
neighborhood.
Premium Roofing Solutions LLC delivered a comprehensive, top-tier roofing and siding
transformation for this Roscoe, IL home—on time and beyond expectations. By expertly
managing the insurance claim process, upgrading materials at no extra charge, and prioritizing
world-class service, PRS provided the homeowners with a seamless, stress-free experience
resulting in a gorgeous, resilient home exterior.
