Is It Time for a Roof Replacement?
Signs that you need a roof replacement include:
Frequent Leaks: If your roof constantly leaks, it’s time to consider a full replacement.
Old Age: Roofs older than 20 years may be due for a replacement to avoid unexpected problems.
Shingle Damage: Large patches of missing or curling shingles indicate significant wear and tear.
