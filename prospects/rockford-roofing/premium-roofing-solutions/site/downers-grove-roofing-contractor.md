Downers Grove, Illinois is a DuPage County village of about 50,000 residents, set roughly 20 miles west of downtown Chicago. The community traces its roots to 1832, when Vermont native Pierce Downer settled in a grove of oak trees that gave the village its name. One of its most treasured landmarks is the 1846 Blodgett House, which now stands on the Downers Grove Museum campus and is believed to have been a stop on the Underground Railroad. The village is also known for its historic Sears catalog homes, which arrived by rail as kits that included everything from pre-cut lumber to asphalt shingles. Many of these houses are now close to 100 years old.
