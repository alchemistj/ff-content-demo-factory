Why Roofing Isn’t a DIY Job (And What Could Go Wrong If You Try)
Discover the hidden risks of DIY roofing projects
Understand why cutting corners can cost you more
Learn what smart homeowners in Wisconsin do instead
You’re handy. You’ve fixed a few things around the house before. You’re even pretty good with a hammer.
So you start thinking: “How hard can roofing be?”
After all, it’s just shingles and nails, right?
That’s the thought process a lot of homeowners go through… right before they run into a headache they weren’t expecting.
And unfortunately, by the time they call Premium Roofing Solutions, the damage has already been done.
Let us save you the trouble — and the expense — by walking you through why roofing isn’t a weekend DIY job, and what can (and often does) go wrong.
Safety Is No Joke
Let’s get the obvious one out of the way: roofing is dangerous.
We’re talking about 15 to 30 feet in the air, carrying bundles of shingles, on an angled surface — sometimes with wind or heat working against you.
Falls from roofs are one of the top causes of home improvement injuries in the U.S.
And here’s the kicker: insurance may not cover it if it’s a DIY job.
You wouldn’t climb onto your chimney during a snowstorm… so why risk your safety trying to repair a roof you’ve never worked on?
What You Don’t Know Can Hurt You
Even if you manage to avoid falling, there’s a second risk: missing hidden damage.
Professional roofers know where to look for:
Soft decking or rot under the shingles
Incorrect or missing flashing
Improper attic ventilation causing future ice dams
Nail pops that lead to leaks months later
You can’t fix what you don’t see. And many DIY roofers only realize the problem after their “repair” fails… and the leak stains the ceiling.
No Warranty. No Recourse.
Most roofing materials come with long-term warranties.
But here’s what the fine print says: they’re voided if installed improperly.
That means if your DIY job starts failing in 2 years instead of 20, you’re stuck.
You’ll pay for materials twice — plus labor the second time around.
At Premium Roofing Solutions, we stand behind our work with both manufacturer warranties and our own craftsmanship guarantee. You don’t get that with a YouTube tutorial.
It’s Cheaper Until It’s Not
Yes, DIY seems cheaper upfront. But factor in:
Equipment rentals
Emergency repairs
Water damage
No resale value boost
Voided warranties
Higher insurance premiums if something goes wrong
Suddenly, that “cheap” fix isn’t saving you anything. In fact, it could be the most expensive thing you never budgeted for.
