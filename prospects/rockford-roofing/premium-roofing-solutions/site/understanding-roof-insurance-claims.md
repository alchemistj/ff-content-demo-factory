Understanding Roof Insurance Claims
The Storm Passed. Now What?
You walked outside after the wind died down.
Shingles in the yard.
Your neighbor’s got a roofing crew already.
And you’re left wondering:
“Should I call my insurance company?”
Here’s the real answer: Not yet.
If you’ve never filed a roof insurance claim before, the process can feel like a maze — with fine print, adjusters, and uncertainty.
That’s where we come in.
Here’s What Most Homeowners Don’t Know
Insurance adjusters don’t work for you — they work for the insurance company.
But we do.
At Premium Roofing Solutions, we start with a free inspection. We show you photos, document the damage, and help you decide if a claim is even worth filing. And if it is — we guide you through it.
We’ll meet with your adjuster.
We’ll help you get a fair estimate.
We’ll make sure nothing important gets missed.
How the Process Works
You call us first – no cost, no commitment
We inspect your roof, take pictures, and explain the damage
You decide – we’ll tell you if a claim makes sense
We meet with your adjuster and handle the roofing paperwork
Your roof gets repaired or replaced, typically only costing your deductible
And that’s it. No guesswork. No confusion. No fear of being taken advantage of.
If you think the storm did damage — let’s take a look before you make that call. It might just save you thousands.
