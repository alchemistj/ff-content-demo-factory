Metal Flashing
Project Overview
Premium Roofing Solutions was tasked with restoring the roofing system of a historic colonial-style home built in the 1930s, located in Rockford, IL. The home’s aged roofing system had multiple leaks, necessitating a complete roof replacement while preserving the architectural integrity of this historic property.
This complex project involved meticulous planning and seamless coordination across multiple trades to ensure all aspects of the home’s unique features were addressed, including custom dormer window trim replacement, chimney flashing, and the installation of modern gutter solutions.
Preserving Historic Architecture
Leaking Areas
The 8 dormer windows on the home featured rotted colonial-style wood trim that required precise replacement with modern materials while maintaining the historic look.
Custom Flashing for Chimneys and Mansard Roof Areas
The home featured 4 chimneys that demanded custom-fabricated flashing to prevent future leaks and ensure a watertight seal.
Integration with Other Trades
Coordinating efforts between trades for custom trim replacement, chimney flashing, and roofing work required careful project management.
Modernizing Gutter Systems
The installation of 6-inch white gutters with leaf protection needed to blend seamlessly with the home’s aesthetic while providing modern functionality.
Solutions and Execution
PVC Trim Replacement for Dormer Windows
The Premium Roofing Solutions team carefully removed and replaced the rotted wood trims on all 8 dormer windows. Using durable PVC material, the trims were fabricated to replicate the intricate colonial-style design, preserving the home’s historical charm while ensuring long-lasting durability.
Custom Aluminum Flashing
To address water infiltration, all 4 chimneys were completely re-flashed with custom-fabricated aluminum. Our skilled technicians ensured precise installation to protect the home from future water damage.
Complete Roofing System Replacement
The old, leaky roof was replaced with a high-quality roofing system that not only provides superior protection but also complements the home’s historic aesthetic.
Modern Gutters with Leaf Protection
Our team installed 6-inch white gutters with built-in leaf guards. This modern system enhances water drainage efficiency while requiring minimal maintenance, an ideal upgrade for the property.
Seamless Trade Coordination
Premium Roofing Solutions coordinated with other trades to manage the custom trim work, roofing, and flashing installations efficiently, ensuring the project timeline was met and every detail was executed flawlessly.
Project Outcome
The historic home’s new roofing system and upgraded features restored its integrity, protected its structure from leaks, and maintained its architectural authenticity. The attention to detail in replicating colonial-style elements and the use of modern materials ensures the property will stand the test of time while preserving its historic charm.
Key highlights include:
Preservation of the historic colonial-style look with PVC trim replacement.
Watertight protection for 4 chimneys with custom flashing.
Efficient and aesthetically pleasing water management with 6-inch white gutters and leaf guards.
A fully restored roofing system that complements the home’s historic appeal.
