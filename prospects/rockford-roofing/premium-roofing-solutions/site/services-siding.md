FAQ About Siding
How long does siding last?
With proper maintenance, siding can last anywhere from 20 to 30 years, depending on the material used.
What are the best siding materials?
Vinyl, wood, fiber cement, and metal are popular choices, each offering unique benefits in durability, cost, and appearance.
Can siding improve my home’s energy efficiency?
Yes! Insulated siding helps regulate indoor temperatures, reducing heating and cooling costs.
How often should siding be cleaned?
Siding should be cleaned at least once a year to remove dirt, mold, and mildew buildup.
Do you offer siding repairs?
Yes, we provide repair services for cracked, warped, or damaged siding to maintain the integrity of your home’s exterior.
