FAQs About Roof Repair
How do I know if my roof needs repair?
Look for missing shingles, leaks, or sagging areas. If you notice any of these signs, it’s best to get an inspection to avoid bigger problems.
How long does a roof repair take?
Most roof repairs can be completed within a day, but more extensive damage may require additional time.
