Loves Park, Illinois is home to about 23,000 residents and sits along the east bank of the Rock River north of Rockford. The city takes its name from Malcolm Love, a Rockford industrialist who bought 236 acres along the river in 1901 and turned the land into a gathering place for his friends. For years, Loves Park was the largest unincorporated town in the country until residents voted to become a city in 1947. Today, the city is known for having no municipal property tax, since it funds local services through sales tax.
