Your Guide to Roof Replacement
When it comes to protecting your most valuable asset—your home—few things matter more than your roof. A solid, well-installed roof keeps your family safe, your energy bills low, and your property value strong. But how do you know when it’s time to replace your roof in Lake Geneva, Madison or Rockford, IL? And what should you expect during the process?
At Premium Roofing Solutions, we know that a roof replacement isn’t just a construction project—it’s a turning point for your home’s longevity and curb appeal. In this guide, we’ll walk you through everything you need to know about replacing your roof in Wisconsin or Northern Illinois area, including the signs to watch for, material options, costs, and why choosing a trusted local contractor matters.
Is It Time to Replace Your Roof?
Your roof speaks to you—just not in words. Instead, it shows signs that it’s nearing the end of its life. Here are the most common signals:
Your roof is over 20 years old. Most asphalt shingle roofs have a lifespan of 20–25 years. If yours is approaching that age, it’s time to consider replacement.
Shingles are curling, cracking, or missing. If you notice damage, especially after a storm, your roof could be compromised.
Moss, mold, or dark streaks. These aren’t just cosmetic issues. They signal trapped moisture, which can rot the wood beneath your shingles.
Leaks or water stains inside your home. If you’re seeing signs of water infiltration, the roof is no longer doing its job.
Granules in your gutters. Asphalt shingles shed granules as they deteriorate. If you’re cleaning your gutters and finding lots of shingle grit, take note.
It’s easy to delay action—but waiting too long can lead to major interior damage, mold growth, and costly structural repairs.

Roofing Materials
Because of the Midwest’s harsh winters, high winds, and fluctuating temperatures, not all roofing materials perform equally well.
Here are a few top options:
Architectural Asphalt Shingles
These are a favorite for homeowners because of their affordability, style, and wind resistance.
Metal Roofing
Increasingly popular, metal roofs offer longevity (up to 50+ years), energy efficiency, and incredible durability against hail and wind.
Designer Shingles
Mimic the look of wood shake or slate while offering modern performance at a lower cost.
If you’re unsure which is right for your home, we’ll help you weigh the pros and cons during your consultation.
How Much Does a Roof Replacement Cost?
We know this is the million-dollar question (although thankfully, it won’t cost that much).
In the Midwest, roof replacement costs vary depending on:
The size and pitch of your roof
The materials you select
Whether your existing decking needs repair or replacement
Removal and disposal of your current roof
On average, most residential roof replacements range between $8,000 and $25,000. Premium Roofing Solutions offers financing options and flexible payment plans to make it easier on your budget.
