Winter Roof Maintenance Checklist for Wisconsin Homeowners
When winter hits in Wisconsin, your roof is your first line of defense — but it’s also the part of your home that takes the most abuse. Snow, ice dams, wind, and temperature swings can all shorten your roof’s lifespan if it’s not properly maintained.
Here’s a simple winter checklist to help protect your roof before and during the cold months:
Pre-Winter Prep
Clean out your gutters to prevent ice damming
Check for damaged or missing shingles before the snow flies
Trim overhanging branches to avoid damage from falling limbs
Inspect flashing and sealant around chimneys and vents
During Winter
Watch for ice dams forming on the edges of your roof
Use a roof rake after heavy snowfalls (especially if more than 6” of snow accumulates)
Keep attic ventilation clear — proper airflow helps prevent ice buildup
What to Avoid
Don’t use salt or de-icing products on your shingles
Don’t try to chip ice with sharp tools — it can damage your roof
Don’t ignore small leaks — they can become major problems fast
Winter in Wisconsin is no joke, but with a little prevention, your roof can handle it like a champ.
