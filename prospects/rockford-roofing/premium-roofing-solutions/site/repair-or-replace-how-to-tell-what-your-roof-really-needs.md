Repair or Replace? How to Tell What Your Roof Really Needs
Midwest homeowners know the struggle — one day it’s blazing sun, the next it’s hail or a heavy downpour. With our unpredictable weather, your roof takes a beating year after year. So when problems show up — missing shingles, small leaks, or curling edges — the big question is: Do you just repair it, or is it time for a full roof replacement?
At Premium Roofing Solutions, we believe in giving homeowners honest answers, not quick sales. Here’s how to tell what your roof really needs — and what to expect every step of the way.
1. How Do You Know If You Need a Full Roof Replacement or a Repair?
It starts with a thorough inspection. We look for the telltale signs of wear — missing shingles, curling edges, cracked flashing, or heavy granule loss. These symptoms often show up after a Midwest storm or years of sun exposure.
If most of your roof is over 20 years old, a replacement is often the smarter long-term move. Patching an aging roof can buy you time, but it’s like putting a Band-Aid on a worn-out tire — it might hold for a season, but not for long.
When the damage is limited to one area, a well-done repair can absolutely extend your roof’s life. That’s why a professional inspection matters — it helps you make the right call without guesswork.
2. How Long Will a Roof Replacement Take?
Most residential roof replacements in the Midwest are completed in one to three days, weather permitting. Of course, it depends on your roof size, materials, and layout.
At Premium Roofing Solutions, we give you a clear project timeline before work begins, so there are no surprises. Our crews show up early, stay organized, and clean up every nail and scrap before calling the job complete. We treat every home like it’s our own — and our customers notice.
3. How Should You Prepare Your Home for a Roofing Crew?
You don’t have to do much — we handle nearly everything from setup to cleanup.
Still, a few small steps go a long way:
Clear vehicles from your driveway so our crew and equipment have space.
Move fragile items or valuables from the attic (vibrations can shift things slightly).
Cover outdoor furniture or plants near the home’s edge if you’d like an extra layer of protection.
Once that’s done, you can relax — we’ll take care of the rest and keep your property tidy throughout the project.
4. What Happens If It Rains Mid-Project?
Midwest weather can turn on a dime, and we plan for that. If rain shows up in the middle of your project, we pause work and securely tarp your roof to keep everything dry and protected.
Safety and quality always come first — our crew won’t rush through a wet roof. We resume work as soon as conditions allow, ensuring your new roof goes on right the first time.
5. Will Homeowner’s Insurance Cover Roof Damage?
If your roof damage is caused by hail, wind, or storm impact, your homeowner’s insurance may cover repair or replacement costs.
Premium Roofing Solutions helps you document the damage properly, take photos, and prepare the claim paperwork. We’ve guided hundreds of Midwest homeowners through the process — and we’ll make sure you get the reimbursement you deserve.
Conclusion
A roof is more than shingles — it’s your home’s first defense against our Midwest elements. Whether you need a simple repair or a full replacement, the key is to act before small problems turn costly.
If you’re unsure where your roof stands after this season’s storms, Premium Roofing Solutions can take a quick look and give you honest feedback — no pressure, just straight answers from a team that truly cares about your home.
