How Our Milton Roofing Process Works
A properly executed roofing project in Milton follows a defined sequence that can't be compressed without consequences. Our process is the same whether we're handling a standard residential replacement or a full storm restoration after a severe Rock County weather event.
Initial inspection that evaluates the full system — shingles, underlayment, decking, ventilation, flashing, and gutters — not just the visible surface damage
Complete tear-off so decking can be physically checked for delamination and soft spots rather than estimated through a visual-only inspection
Ice-and-water shield installed at all eaves, valleys, and penetrations before any underlayment or shingles go down — the sequence that prevents the most common failure modes
Ventilation verification against Wisconsin code minimums and actual attic square footage, with corrections made where the existing system falls short
Final walkthrough with the homeowner covering every completed element, with written documentation of all materials installed and their warranty terms
