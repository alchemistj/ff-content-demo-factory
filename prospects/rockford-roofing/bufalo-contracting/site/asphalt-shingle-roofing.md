Asphalt Shingle Roofing
Durable Roofing Built for Midwest Weather
Asphalt shingle roofing installation and replacement from Bufalo Contracting in Rockford & Loves Park, Illinois.
Your roof in Rockford or Loves Park, IL, faces high winds that lift shingle edges, heavy rain that tests flashing seals, and temperature swings that cause materials to expand and contract. Asphalt shingle roofing provides a balance of durability, weather resistance, and curb appeal for homes throughout the area. Whether your current roof is sagging from age or you need a full tear-off after storm damage, a properly installed shingle system keeps water out and handles the seasonal extremes common in Rockford.
Bufalo Contracting installs architectural shingles and 3-tab shingles designed to withstand wind speeds and moisture exposure typical of Northern Illinois. The work includes removing old materials down to the deck, installing waterproof underlayment, and sealing flashing around chimneys, vents, and valleys. Proper ventilation is integrated to prevent heat buildup that shortens shingle life and contributes to ice dam formation. You receive guidance on color and style selection to match your home's exterior and neighborhood character.
Request an asphalt shingle roofing estimate today with Bufalo Contracting in Rockford to review material options and timeline for your project.
How shingle installation protects your home
Installation begins with a complete tear-off of existing shingles, felt paper, and damaged decking if needed. In Rockford and Loves Park, IL, older homes often have multiple layers of shingles that must be removed to inspect the wood underneath. New underlayment is rolled out and fastened, followed by starter strips along the eaves and rake edges to prevent wind uplift.
When the work is finished, you see straight shingle lines with no gaps or lifted edges. Water flows directly into gutters without pooling in valleys or behind flashing. Your attic stays cooler in summer because ridge vents allow hot air to escape, and your heating bills drop in winter because the roof deck no longer warms unevenly.
The service includes disposal of old roofing materials and a site cleanup that leaves your yard free of nails and debris. It does not include structural framing repairs or siding replacement, though any damaged wood decking discovered during tear-off is addressed before new shingles are installed. Proper installation extends shingle life and reduces the likelihood of leaks during freeze-thaw cycles.
