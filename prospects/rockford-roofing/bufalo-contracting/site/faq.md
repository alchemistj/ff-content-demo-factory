FAQ
How do I know if my roof needs repair or full replacement?
If your roof has widespread damage, multiple leaks, or is over 20 years old, replacement is often more cost-effective than repeated repairs. Minor localized damage like a few missing shingles can usually be repaired. At Bufalo Contracting, we assess the extent of damage and age to recommend the best solution for your Rockford home.
What roofing materials work best in Rockford's climate?
Asphalt shingles are popular in Rockford due to their durability against freeze-thaw cycles, wind, and hail common in northern Illinois. Metal roofing also performs well in heavy snow and ice conditions. At Bufalo Contracting, we help select materials with comprehensive warranties suited to local weather patterns.
How long does a typical roof replacement take?
Most residential roof replacements are completed in one to three days, depending on size, complexity, and weather conditions. Commercial projects may take longer based on building size and roofing system type. Our team works efficiently to minimize disruption while ensuring quality installation.
What are the signs of hail damage on a roof?
Hail damage appears as dents or bruises on shingles, cracked or missing granules, and damaged flashing or vents. You may also notice dents on gutters and siding. At Bufalo Contracting, we provide storm damage inspections and assist with insurance claims to document hail impact accurately.
Can ice dams really damage my roof?
Yes, ice dams trap melting snow, causing water to back up under shingles and leak into your home, damaging insulation, ceilings, and walls. They form when heat escapes through the roof, melting snow that refreezes at the eaves. Professional removal prevents costly interior water damage during harsh Rockford winters.
What's the difference between residential and commercial roofing?
Residential roofs are typically sloped with shingles or tiles, while commercial roofs are often flat or low-slope using membrane systems like TPO, EPDM, or modified bitumen. Commercial projects require different materials, drainage solutions, and installation techniques. Both benefit from quality materials and expert installation for long-term performance.
How do energy-efficient windows reduce heating costs?
Energy-efficient windows feature Low-E coatings and insulated frames that minimize heat transfer, keeping warm air inside during winter and reducing furnace use. This improved insulation can lower energy bills significantly in cold climates like Rockford. Proper installation ensures maximum thermal performance and draft elimination.
When should I schedule storm damage repairs?
Schedule repairs immediately after a storm to prevent further damage from water intrusion, exposed materials, or compromised structural integrity. Prompt action also helps with timely insurance claims. At Bufalo Contracting, we offer emergency storm restoration services including temporary repairs and complete property restoration.
Do gutter guards really prevent clogs?
Quality gutter guards significantly reduce debris buildup by blocking leaves and twigs while allowing water to flow through. They minimize maintenance needs but may still require occasional cleaning. Seamless gutters combined with guards provide optimal protection against water damage to your foundation and landscaping.
What's the biggest mistake homeowners make with roof maintenance?
Ignoring minor issues like small leaks or missing shingles is the most common mistake, allowing problems to escalate into costly structural damage. Regular inspections after severe weather help catch issues early. At Bufalo Contracting, we recommend annual roof checks to extend lifespan and maintain warranty coverage.
How does new siding improve energy efficiency?
Modern siding adds an insulating layer that reduces thermal bridging and air infiltration, helping maintain consistent indoor temperatures. Insulated vinyl or fiber cement siding can lower heating and cooling costs. Proper installation with quality underlayment maximizes energy savings and enhances your home's curb appeal.
Will insurance cover wind damage to my roof?
Most homeowner policies cover sudden wind damage like torn-off shingles or structural harm from falling branches, minus your deductible. Documentation through photos and professional inspection is essential. At Bufalo Contracting, we assist with insurance claims, providing detailed assessments to support your storm damage restoration needs.
Do you help file the insurance claim, or is that something I handle on my own?
We handle the entire process with you — from documenting damage and scheduling an adjuster inspection to reviewing the settlement and completing approved repairs. Our team has guided hundreds of Rockford-area homeowners through hail, wind, and storm claims, so you're never navigating the paperwork alone. Most clients are surprised by how straightforward the process is when an experienced contractor leads it.

Bufalo Contracting handles both residential and commercial roofing, which means the same experienced team that repairs a family home's storm damage also manages larger flat-roof and multi-unit commercial projects across Rockford. That breadth of service — combined with deep expertise steering clients through insurance claims — is why local property owners consistently turn to us first.
Who should I call in Rockford when I need storm damage roof repairs handled quickly and correctly?
A contractor with hands-on storm damage experience and a proven track record guiding homeowners through the insurance process is the right call. Bufalo Contracting has worked with Rockford-area property owners on hail, wind, and severe weather repairs, managing both the physical restoration and the claims paperwork so repairs move forward without unnecessary delays.
What does a professional roof inspection include, and when should I schedule one?
A professional roof inspection covers shingle condition, flashing, vents, gutters, and a check for moisture or damage in the attic. You should schedule one after any significant storm and before winter arrives to catch small problems before they become costly repairs. Learn more on our roof repairs page.
How often should Rockford homeowners get a roof inspection?
Most roofing professionals recommend a thorough inspection at least once a year, plus an additional check after any major hail or wind event. Rockford's freeze-thaw cycles and severe storm seasons make regular inspections especially important for catching early signs of wear before they lead to leaks.
Can a roof inspection catch problems before they turn into major repairs?
Yes — a thorough inspection identifies failing shingles, compromised flashing, and early attic moisture before those issues escalate into structural damage or interior leaks. Catching problems at the inspection stage almost always costs significantly less to fix than waiting until visible damage appears inside your home.
