Metal Roofing
Long-Lasting Roofing That Handles Snow Loads
Metal roofing installation and upgrades from Bufalo Contracting in Rockford & Loves Park, Illinois.
When snow piles up on your roof in Rockford or Loves Park, IL, and temperatures swing from below zero to thawing within days, metal roofing sheds snow and ice instead of trapping it. Standing seam metal roofs and metal panels are designed to withstand high winds, heavy snow loads, and the freeze-thaw cycles that crack or lift other roofing materials. If you want a low-maintenance option that lasts decades without needing frequent repairs, metal roofing provides long-term performance for both residential properties and select commercial buildings throughout the area.
Bufalo Contracting installs metal roofing systems with interlocking seams and concealed fasteners that prevent water infiltration and resist wind uplift. The material reflects sunlight, which reduces attic heat buildup in summer and lowers cooling costs. Metal roofing is popular in Northern Illinois for homes with steep pitches and for property owners who want to avoid the cost and disruption of frequent re-roofing. Installation includes underlayment, flashing, and trim work that matches your home's exterior.
Schedule a metal roofing consultation with Bufalo Contracting in Rockford to review panel styles, colors, and installation requirements for your property.
What makes metal roofing a lasting option
Installation begins with measuring your roof and ordering panels cut to fit the exact length of each slope. In Rockford and Loves Park, IL, metal roofs are often installed over existing shingles if the deck is sound, which reduces tear-off costs and waste. Panels are fastened to purlins or directly to the deck, and vertical seams are crimped together to form a watertight barrier.
After installation, you see clean lines running from ridge to eave with no exposed fasteners to rust or leak. Snow slides off the roof instead of melting and refreezing at the edges. Your attic stays cooler in summer because the reflective surface deflects heat rather than absorbing it, and you no longer worry about shingles lifting during windstorms.
Metal roofing requires minimal maintenance compared to asphalt shingles and does not need replacement for 40 to 60 years under normal conditions. The service does not include structural reinforcement if your roof framing cannot support the weight of metal panels, though most residential roofs in the area handle the load without modification. Proper installation ensures seams remain sealed and expansion joints accommodate temperature-related movement.
