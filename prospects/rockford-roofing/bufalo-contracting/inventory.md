### Conflicts

1. **Phone numbers.** The site shows one number. BBB search results for the same business list four: (779) 368-0256, (779) 368-0349, (815) 494-0793, (855) 528-3256. Needs owner confirmation.
2. **Second address.** The site lists only 3466 Colony Bay Dr. BBB search results also list 810 S Main St, Belvidere, IL 61008. The site title says "Rockford & Belvidere, IL" but has no Belvidere office.
3. **Years.** home says "In 2024 ... 15 years of being in business." Home, about and blog pages say "For over a decade." deck-building says "For over 15 years." BBB says "accredited since 2011" (not checked). The founding year is never stated. Needs owner confirmation.
4. **Replacement time.** FAQ: "Most residential roof replacements are completed in one to three days." roof-replacement and roof-installations: "The process typically takes several days." Reviews: "replaced in one day" (Kim M.).
5. **Decks.** Some pages' header says "Roofing, Siding, Windows, Gutters, Decks, & Storm Restoration." Other pages say "...Gutters, & Storm Restoration." The deck page exists, but decks are not in the main menu.
6. **Complaints.** commercial-roofing claims "zero customer complaints." `reviews.json` contains one 1-star review about unreturned calls and an unfixed leak.
7. **Guarantee wording.** Footer: "We guarantee exceptional craftsmanship and on-time completion." commercial-roofing: "Guaranteed Work." service-area template: "comprehensive warranties." No terms anywhere. Do not use as a guarantee.
8. **Staff names.** Site testimonials name Chip Limberg, Zach Reidenbach and Dillon Hagstrom. `reviews.json` names Brandon Jones, Dillon/Dillion Hagstrom, Eddie/Ed Dudek, Damon Carley, Shawn, Tony, Jason, Javier, Rob Ebany and Christian. "Chip" appears in `reviews.json` only as a first name (Greg U.); the surname Limberg does not appear there. Spelling varies (Dillon/Dillion, Eddie/Ed/Edie, Zach/Zack).
9. **Scale claims.** "over 10,000 roofs" (home H1) and "hundreds of Rockford-area homeowners" (faq) and "transformed hundreds of homes" (service-area template). Not contradictory, but none is sourced.
10. **Service-area template.** The 16 `service-area-*` pages use the same template text ("Owens Corning," "Forest City," "hundreds of homes"). Treat as unverified.
