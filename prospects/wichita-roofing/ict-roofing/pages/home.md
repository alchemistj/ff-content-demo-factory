## What Hail Actually Does to a Shingle

Most of it you can't see from the ground. Hail bruises a shingle: it knocks the protective granules loose and cracks the asphalt mat underneath.

The shingle still looks like a shingle. But the bare spot ages fast in the sun, and the crack opens up over the next few winters. That's how a roof that "looked fine" after the storm leaks two years later.

The ground gives you clues. Dented gutters, dented vents and flashing, and piles of granules at the bottom of your downspouts all mean hail hit hard enough to matter. A person on the roof, or a drone overhead, confirms it.

## Questions Before You Call

**Is the inspection really free?**
Yes. We get on the roof or put a drone over it, photograph what's there and walk you through it. If what you thought was damage is just normal wear, we'll tell you that and you owe nothing.

**Do I have to file an insurance claim?**
No. It's your policy and your decision. We'll tell you what we find, and if the damage doesn't justify a claim, we'll say so.

**Will insurance pay for a whole new roof?**
It depends on your policy and on what the adjuster finds, and no roofer can promise an outcome. What we do is document the damage, meet the adjuster, and push back if the offer covers only part of a roof that's damaged all over.

**What's my share of the cost?**
Your deductible. Many policies set a separate, higher deductible for hail and wind, so check your policy for that number before the adjuster visits.

**How long does a roof replacement take?**
A lot of our roofs are done in a single day. A big or complicated roof can take longer.
