## Gate result
Top claim: "We stay in it when insurance offers less," **20** (was a tie with the founder-and-adjuster braid at 20; the braid now scores 15). Passes gate (16+). Describe actions only, never a claim outcome.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners whose insurance offer comes back short, ICT is the roofer that stays in it with you: the photos, the paperwork and the talk with the adjuster, so you aren't doing it alone.
- **B, method:** Unlike roofers who say they "assist" with claims and step back, ICT keeps working with you when the offer comes back short, with Juan, Brady or Arturo on your roof alongside the adjuster, so nothing the storm did goes unseen.
- **C, emotional outcome:** When the offer comes back short, you aren't alone with a letter. Someone at ICT is still working it with you.

**Pick: A.** It names the avatar's #1 fear (a claim cut short) in the moment it happens, and the proof (Kristi I., Crystal E.) is a review fact no competitor describes. B keeps the founder-and-adjuster detail for the method section, where it supports the lead instead of carrying it.

## What's thin
- "We stay in it" rests on Kristi I. and Crystal E.; ask Juan what he actually does when an offer is partial, and confirm it never reads as promising a result.
- "On the roof with the adjuster" rests on Debbie L. and a few similar reviews; ask Juan how often he personally attends and how often Brady or Arturo go. Now a supporting row (15), not the lead.
- Owner access (Juan climbs the roof) is capped at E 3: any owner-run shop can say it. It supports the method section only.
- Drone and leak-finding are one or two reviews each: ask whether they are standard.
- Competitor scan could not read Google rankings; Dingers and Premier on-roof practice was "not stated", not confirmed absent. Premier's "you don't chase the insurance company. We do." is close to the lead, so exclusivity 4 is the most it can hold.
- Warranty, financing, certifications and BBB status are unverified (facts.md), so no claim here uses them.
