Costliest first:

3. None of their 69 five-star Google reviews on the site (homepage has a few unattributed testimonials).
4. "About" opens the insurance page; no About page or owner story.
5. Report card: tap-to-call and email work (credit); forms have no field labels; leftover /cart link.
