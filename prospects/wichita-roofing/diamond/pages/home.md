## Gutters and Downspouts

Gutters get ignored until they overflow at the edge of the roof, sag away from the house or dump water next to the foundation.

Water that doesn't leave the roof edge soaks the fascia behind it and the soil under it. Gutters that pull away usually mean the fasteners or the board behind them have given out.

We install gutters, repair them and fix drainage problems. When a roof is being replaced, doing the gutters in the same job means one crew, one cleanup and a matching edge along the whole house.

## Questions Before You Call

**Do I have to file an insurance claim before you look at my roof?**
No. You can call about a roof or gutter problem whether or not insurance is involved. Whether to file is your decision, and we'll tell you what we see.

**Will my insurance pay for a new roof?**
We can't say. Your policy and your insurance company decide that. What we can do is be at the adjuster's visit and make sure the damage on your roof is seen.

**How do I know if I need a repair or a whole new roof?**
Damage in one place on an otherwise sound roof is a repair. Damage across several slopes, or a roof that keeps leaking after repairs, points to replacement. We'll tell you which one you have.

**Can you do the roof and the gutters together?**
Yes. Doing both in one job means one crew and one cleanup.
