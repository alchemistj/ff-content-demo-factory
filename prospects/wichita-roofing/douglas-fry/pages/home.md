## What We Find When We Get Up There

Most roof calls start with something you see on the ceiling or in the yard. Here is what usually causes it, and what we do about it.

**A brown stain on the ceiling under a tile roof that looks fine** Tile sheds water, but the underlayment beneath it is what keeps the house dry, and it wears out long before the tile does. If the underlayment has failed, we lift the tile, put down new underlayment and lay your same tile back.

**Cracked or slipped tiles after hail or wind** A broken tile lets water reach the underlayment, and a near-match replacement looks like a patch and may not seal like the original. We order tile that matches, and Jason takes it up with your insurer when a storm did the damage.

**A leak that came back after someone else fixed it** The last repair probably patched the spot above the stain, while the water got in several feet uphill and ran along the deck. We check the whole roof, find where the water really enters and fix that.

**Water around a chimney, skylight or sky tube** Flashing is thin metal shaped to that exact spot, and it loosens with age or gets smeared with caulk. We rework the flashing instead of sealing over it.

**Shingles in the yard after a wind storm** Wind gets under shingles whose sealant strip never bonded or has aged out, and once a tab lifts, the next gust tears it off. We replace what's missing and check the rest for creased tabs and hail bruising.

**Cedar shakes split, curled or missing** Wood shake dries, splits and rots, and a loose shake lets water straight through. Very few roofers have the skill to repair wood shake, and we do.

## Questions Before You Call

**Can my roof be repaired, or do I need a new one?**

It depends on how far the damage spreads. A leak at one valley, one flashing or one patch of underlayment is a repair. Broken tile or bruised shingles across most of the roof, or leaks showing up in more than one room, point to replacement. From the ground, look for granules piling up at the downspouts and cracked tile in more than one area.

**Do you work with insurance companies?**

Yes. Jason works with your insurer from the first call to the finished roof. We price from what's on your roof, not just from the paperwork, and we'll tell you if the claim covers less than the roof needs.
