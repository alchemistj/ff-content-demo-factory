## Tile Roof Repair

Concrete and clay tile are tough, and they still leak. The tile isn't the weak point. The underlayment beneath it, the valleys and the flashing are.

For a cracked or broken tile, we replace it with a tile that matches. For a failed valley or flashing, we rebuild that detail. For a worn-out underlayment, we lift the tile in the affected area, put down new underlayment and relay the same tile.

That lift-and-relay work is slow and takes a crew that has done it many times, because tile that's walked on wrong cracks. It's also what keeps an old tile roof out of the dumpster.

## Valleys, Flashing and Sky Tubes

Water goes where a roof changes direction or where something pokes through it. That makes valleys, chimney flashing, skylights and sky tubes the usual suspects.

Flashing is thin metal that has to be shaped and sealed to that exact spot. It ages, loosens and gets sealed with caulk by whoever came before. We rework flashing from eave to rake, around chimneys and around skylights, and replace what has worn out instead of smearing over it.

Valleys carry the most water on the roof, so they wear first. A deteriorating valley is a repair worth doing before it becomes a ceiling.

## Metal Roof Leaks

Metal roofs leak at the places where metal is joined or fastened: seams, penetrations, flashings and exposed screws that have backed out. A standing seam roof hides its fasteners, so a leak there usually means a seam, a flashing or a damaged panel.

We repair metal roof leaks, including odd shapes that other roofers couldn't get to hold.

## Repair Questions

**How do I know if I need a repair or a new roof?**
If the leak is at one valley, flashing or underlayment and the rest of the roof is sound, it can usually be repaired. If damage is spread across the roof, a replacement may make more sense.

**Why did my roof leak after someone else fixed it?**
Because the repair addressed where the water showed up, not where it got in. It's the most common reason a repair doesn't hold.

**Can you work on a roof another contractor installed?**
Yes. We've traced and fixed leaks on roofs other roofers put on.
