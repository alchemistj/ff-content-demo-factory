## What to Have Ready

You don't need to know what is wrong. Tell us what you can see: a stain on the ceiling, a missing shingle, a roof the storm hit.

A few things help us help you:

- Your address
- The kind of roof you have: tile, shingle, shake, metal or something else
- Where the leak or damage is, and when you noticed it
- Whether a storm is involved, and whether you've called your insurance company

If you've had a roofer work on it before, tell us. A leak that came back after a repair tells us where not to look.

## What Happens Next

One of us comes out, gets on your roof and tells you plainly what we found. If it needs a small repair, we say so. If it needs more, we explain why.

Then you get a written estimate with the scope of work, the warranty and the price, so what we told you on the roof is what's on the paper.

If hail or wind caused the damage, Jason can work with your insurance company from the first call to the last nail.
