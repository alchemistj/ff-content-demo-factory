## Gate result
Top claim: "No commissioned salespeople or project managers," **20**. It passes gate (16+). It is the only 20; the owner braid dropped to 16 (strongest part), and the leak-finding row and the tile-roof segment row also score 16.

## Three USP statements
- **Option A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners who are tired of roofers who send a salesman, then vanish, Douglas Fry Roofing is the family-owned roofer with no commissioned salespeople or project managers, so the person quoting your roof isn't working on commission.
- **Option B, method:** Unlike roofers who pay commissioned salespeople to quote the job, Douglas Fry Roofing has none: Doug or Jason Fry is the one you reach and the one who looks at your roof, so the person who diagnoses the leak is the person who answers for it.
- **Option C, emotional outcome:** Nobody is working a commission on your roof. You call, a Fry picks up, and you are not in the dark.

**Pick:** Option B. It states the lead (no commissioned salespeople) as the contrast, answers the "storm guy" fear without attacking anyone, and keeps the owner access as the supporting proof. Option A reads like most roofers' copy.

## What's thin
- **Subcontractors.** Bobo P. (1-star) says a subcontractor did the roof and Jason left after five minutes. Ask the owners whether they use subs. If yes, "an owner on the roof" needs a narrower wording ("an owner inspects and quotes"). It is the main risk to the top claim.
- **Owner on the roof vs. on the phone.** The site says owners are always available. Reviews show Jason on roofs. Ask how many jobs Jason or Doug personally walks.
- **Competitor scan limits.** Exclusivity rests on page wording, not Google or calls. "Not found" may miss a competitor who does it.
- **Insurance.** Reviews describe Jason negotiating claims. We cannot promise outcomes. Ask what he does at the adjuster visit (on the roof? photos?), since Dingers and Premier already meet adjusters.
- **Free estimate, financing, second phone number, certifications** are unconfirmed (facts.md questions 3, 4, 1, 6) and stay out of the lead claim.
- **Years in business:** sources disagree (30, 40, 1981). Not used here.
- **Lead is the site's own claim.** "No commissioned salespeople" comes from the homepage and FAQ only (facts.md §2). Confirm it with the owners before it leads. Competitor exclusivity (0 of 5) rests on page wording, so a competitor may say it somewhere the scan did not read.
- **Tile and DaVinci: does it deserve its own demo angle?** Yes, as a segment angle, not the hero. For a tile-roof homeowner it scores 16 (A 4 x E 4): DaVinci is claimed by 0 of 5, shake repair was not found, and the site shows decades of tile work and real tile job photos. It does not lead because most Wichita homes are not tile or DaVinci (not measured) and the avatar does not profile that buyer. Build it as a second section or page for tile, DaVinci and shake owners; ask the Frys what share of their jobs is tile and whether the avatar needs a tile-roof variant. The tile pages also have audit problems (the "-copy" pages, filenames showing as text).
