## Gate result
Top claim: the owner braid, "An owner gets on your roof and answers the phone," scores 20. It passes gate (16+). Rows 2 and 3 also score 20, and rows 4 and 5 score 16.

## Three USP statements
- **Option A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners who are tired of roofers who send a salesman, then vanish, Douglas Fry Roofing is the family-owned roofer where Doug or Jason Fry answers the phone and gets on your roof, so you talk to the person who does the work.
- **Option B, method:** Unlike roofers who pay commissioned salespeople to quote the job, Douglas Fry Roofing puts an owner on your roof and on your phone, so the person who diagnoses the leak is the person who answers for it.
- **Option C, emotional outcome:** You call, and a Fry picks up. Someone who knows roofs has this, and you are not in the dark.

**Pick:** Option B. It names the contrast with commissioned selling, which answers the "storm guy" fear without attacking anyone, and the "so" is checkable in the reviews. Option A reads like most roofers' copy.

## What's thin
- **Subcontractors.** Bobo P. (1-star) says a subcontractor did the roof and Jason left after five minutes. Ask the owners whether they use subs. If yes, "an owner on the roof" needs a narrower wording ("an owner inspects and quotes"). It is the main risk to the top claim.
- **Owner on the roof vs. on the phone.** The site says owners are always available. Reviews show Jason on roofs. Ask how many jobs Jason or Doug personally walks.
- **Competitor scan limits.** Exclusivity rests on page wording, not Google or calls. "Not found" may miss a competitor who does it.
- **Insurance.** Reviews describe Jason negotiating claims. We cannot promise outcomes. Ask what he does at the adjuster visit (on the roof? photos?), since Dingers and Premier already meet adjusters.
- **Free estimate, financing, second phone number, certifications** are unconfirmed (facts.md questions 3, 4, 1, 6) and stay out of the lead claim.
- **Years in business:** sources disagree (30, 40, 1981). Not used here.
