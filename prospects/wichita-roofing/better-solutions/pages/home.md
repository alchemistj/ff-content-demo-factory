## Questions Before You Call

**Is the estimate really free?**
Yes. The estimate is free, and so is a roof inspection. You don't owe anything for us to look.

**Do I need a whole new roof?**
Not always. If the damage is in one place and the rest of the roof is sound, that's a repair, and I'll tell you so. If the shingles are failing across the roof, a repair only buys a little time.

**Will you talk to my insurance company?**
I'll walk the roof with you and your adjuster and explain the work. What the policy covers, and what it pays, is decided between you and your insurer.

**What if it rains on the day?**
Roofs don't go on in the rain. We move the day and I call you to set a new one.

**Do you do more than roofs?**
Yes: siding, gutters, fences and concrete, as listed above. Roofing is where most of our work is.
