## When a Roof Is Past Repairing

A roof wears out slowly, and then all at once. The signs usually show up together:

- Shingles curling, cracking or missing across more than one slope
- Bare patches, and gritty granules washing into your gutters
- Leaks in more than one spot, or one that keeps coming back
- Daylight, or dark staining, in the attic
- Hail or wind damage over the whole roof, not one corner

The reason a repair stops working is simple. Old shingles go brittle, and a brittle shingle cracks when it's lifted, so a patch turns into a new place for water to get in.

If the damage is in one spot and the rest of the roof is sound, that's a [repair](/roof-repair-wichita-ks/), and I'll say so. A replacement is for the roof that's done.

## What a Replacement Involves

A new roof is more than new shingles. Here is what a proper replacement covers, from the first day to the last:

1. **Protect the house.** The yard, plants and anything against the walls get covered before the tear-off starts.
2. **Tear off the old roof.** The old shingles and underlayment come off down to the deck.
3. **Check the deck.** Soft or rotted boards get replaced, because nothing holds up on a bad deck.
4. **Make it watertight.** New underlayment goes down, and flashing and vents are set around chimneys, walls and pipes.
5. **Lay the shingles.** The new roof goes on from the bottom up.
6. **Clean up.** The yard and the driveway get cleaned, and the old roof leaves with us.

## Comparing Quotes

If you have more than one quote, make them match up. Ask each roofer the same questions:

- Does the price include tearing off the old roof, or does it go over it?
- What happens if the deck is rotted, and what does each board cost?
- Are flashing, vents and drip edge replaced, or reused?
- Are gutters in the price, or separate?
- Who cleans up, and how?

A low number that leaves these out isn't low. Ask me the same questions, and I'll answer every one.
