## Gate result
Top claim: free class 4 upgrade, downspouts and gutter guards included, **20**. **Passes gate (16+).** Caveat: the offer is the site's own words ("only company" unchecked) and its current terms need Ravi's confirmation before it leads anything.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point.** For Wichita homeowners who just had hail take their roof and don't want the same shingle back, Flawless is the roofer that includes a free class 4 impact-resistant upgrade, downspouts and gutter guards, so the new roof is tougher than the old one at no extra line on the bill.
- **B, method.** Unlike roofers who sell Class 4 as an add-on, Flawless puts the upgrade, the downspouts and the gutter guards in the job from the start, with Ravi filing the claim and meeting your adjuster, so you know what you're getting before the crew shows up.
- **C, emotional outcome.** The roof goes on in a day, tougher than the last one, and the next storm doesn't start the worry over.

**Pick: A.** It names the buyer's moment and the exact differentiator, and the claim is the one that scored 20. B's "no add-ons" framing is stronger once Ravi confirms the terms.

## What's thin
- **Offer terms.** Ask Ravi: is the free class 4 upgrade, downspouts and gutter guards offer current, on every replacement or only claim jobs, and who verified "only company"? (facts.md §13)
- **Class 4 on the roof itself.** Reviews say "impact resistant" and "50 year"; the manufacturer and rating are unnamed.
- **Owner on the roof.** Reviews show Ravi on the job, but how often, and whether he sells and crews himself, needs a call.
- **Claims.** Reviews say "get it approved" and "maximum amount"; we may only say he files the claim and meets the adjuster.
- **Competitor gaps.** Scan covered pages only; Google rankings and Air Capital's reviews were unread.
- **Negatives.** Three low reviews cite repeated sales calls and no follow-through (Jamie S., Matt S., Whitney); the ownership claim needs honest handling.
