## When It's Time for a New Roof, Not Another Patch

Shingles don't fail all at once. They go brittle, curl at the edges, lose their granules and let go of nails, and one day a stain shows up on a ceiling.

A patch fixes the spot you can see. It does nothing about the shingle next to it, which is the same age and wearing out the same way.

When the damage is across the whole roof, or the roof is simply old, replacing it costs less over time than patching it every spring. If the problem is one leak in one place, that's a [roof repair](/roof-repair-wichita-ks/), and I'll tell you so.

## From Storm Claim to Approved Roof

In Wichita, hail ends a lot of roofs years before age would. When it does, your homeowner's policy may pay for the replacement, so the claim is usually the first decision, not the last.

Here's the path.

You call us. We inspect the roof for free and document what we find. We file the claim with your insurance company, and we work with your adjuster to get the roof approved.

The adjuster is at your house once, for a short visit. What's been documented before they arrive, and who walks the roof with them, often decides how the claim goes.

You don't have to get on the roof, learn the paperwork or chase anyone down. You tell us when the adjuster is coming, and we take it from there.

## What You Pay

On an insurance replacement, your policy decides what the claim pays, and your deductible is yours. We'll tell you where the claim stands as it goes, so the amount you owe isn't a surprise at the end.

If there's no claim, you pay for the roof, and the price comes from your roof, not a number off a chart. These are the things that move it:

- **Size and pitch.** More squares of roof means more material, and a steep roof is slower and harder to work on.
- **Layers.** Every old layer has to come off before the new roof goes on.
- **Decking.** Wet or rotted boards under the shingles get replaced, and nobody knows how many until the old roof is off.
- **The shingle.** Which one you pick changes both the price and the look.

Anyone who quotes a roof over the phone hasn't seen yours. You get the number after the inspection, with each of those things explained.

Downspouts and gutter guards come with the replacement. Hail beats up gutters as much as shingles, and a new roof draining into old, clogged gutters wastes half the job.

## Choosing the Shingle

The roof is the biggest single piece of your house you can see from the street. Color and style are the part of this job you'll live with every time you pull into the driveway, so take your time with them.

What the shingle is made to survive matters as much as how it looks. We put a class 4 impact-resistant shingle on your replacement as a free upgrade, so you're not choosing between the look you want and a roof that stands up to the next storm.

## Before You Call

**Is the inspection free?** Yes. It's how every job starts.

**How soon after a storm should I call?** Soon. Most policies set a time limit for filing a storm claim, and damage documented right after the storm is easier to prove than damage found a year later.

**Do I have to be home?** Be there for the inspection if you can, and for the adjuster's visit. Tell me when the adjuster is coming and I'll meet them.

**Can I replace the roof without a claim?** Yes. If there's no storm damage, there's no claim to file. You get a free estimate for the new roof.

**Does the gutter work cost extra?** The downspouts and gutter guards come with the roof replacement. New gutters themselves are a separate service: installation, repair and cleaning.
