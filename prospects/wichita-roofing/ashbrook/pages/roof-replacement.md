## How You Know It's Time

A roof doesn't fail all at once. It gives you signs, and most of them can be seen from the yard.

Shingles curl up at the edges or crack across the middle. Granules, the rough gritty coating, wash down into your gutters and collect at the bottom of the downspouts. Leaks show up in more than one place.

One bad spot is a repair. Wear across the whole roof is a replacement, and patching it just means paying twice.

Hail changes the picture. It bruises shingles where you can't see it from the ground, and a roof that looks fine can leak a couple of years later.

If you're not sure which one you have, I'll go up and look. If it's only a repair, I'll tell you, and you can read about [roof repair](/roof-repair-wichita-ks/).

## What Replacement Day Looks Like

The crew shows up early. Landscaping gets protected first, and then the old roof comes off.

With the old shingles gone, we look at the deck underneath. Soft or stained wood needs replacing, not covering, because a new roof on a weak surface hides the problem until it fails.

Then the underlayment, flashing and vents go in, and the new shingles go on. Many of our roofs are finished in a single day.

Before the crew leaves, the yard gets checked for nails and material.

## Replacement With an Insurance Claim

When a storm is what finished your roof, a replacement comes with a claim. That means photos, an adjuster's visit and a scope of work, with paperwork in between.

I document the roof and explain what I find in plain words. I can meet the adjuster at the roof if you'd like.

A claim that comes back short of what the roof shows isn't always the last word.

There's a process for asking the insurance company to look again, and I'll walk you through it. It's your policy and your decision. I don't promise what an insurance company will pay, because no roofer can.

## What Drives the Price

I can't give you a price over the phone, and no honest roofer can. Several things set it.

- The size of the roof, and how steep it is.
- How many layers of old shingles are on it.
- The condition of the deck once the old roof is off.
- Flashing, vents and anything that has to be redone around chimneys or skylights.
- Whether the gutters come along.

I look at the roof, then give you a price. You'll know what's in it and why.
