## Is It Time, or Is It a Repair?

Walk the yard and look up. Shingles curling at the edges, bare patches where the surface has worn away, and shingle grit washing out of your downspouts all say the same thing: the roof is worn out, not just damaged.

A roof like that can be patched, but the patch buys you a season, not a decade. If the whole slope is going, the money is better spent on the roof itself.

The opposite is just as true. One failed flashing or a bad vent boot is a [roof repair](/roof-repair-wichita-ks/), and replacing a sound roof because a stain showed up is money you didn't have to spend.

That's why we look before we quote: in the attic and on the roof, not from the driveway. Our job is to tell you which one you have.

## What Comes Off Before Anything Goes On

Replacement means more than new shingles. The old roof comes down to the decking, and that's where the real condition of the house shows.

Decking that's soft, stained or rotted from years of slow leaks gets replaced, not shingled over. New shingles nailed into bad wood loosen and fail early, and no one can see it once the roof is finished.

Underneath the shingles go the parts you'll never look at again: the underlayment, the flashing at the chimney, walls and valleys, and the vents that let the attic breathe. Reusing old flashing is how a brand-new roof ends up with an old leak.

Ventilation matters more than most people expect. A hot, wet attic cooks shingles from underneath and shortens the life of the roof you just bought.

## Choosing the Material

We install composition shingles, metal roofing and tile. They are different roofs for different priorities.

**Composition shingles** are what most Wichita homes wear. They cost the least up front, come in plenty of colors, and any roofer in town can repair them. Asphalt shingles generally last 20 to 30 years, less on a hot slope or after hail.

**Metal** costs more to install and lasts considerably longer. It sheds snow and rain quickly and holds up well in wind. It's also louder in a downpour unless the roof is built with that in mind.

**Tile** is heavy and long-lived. The roof framing has to be able to carry it, so it's a decision to settle before anyone orders material, not after.

Kansas weather decides some of this too. Hail is hard on every material, and wind lifts any shingle that wasn't nailed right. Tell us what matters most (price, years, looks or hail) and we'll tell you which roof fits.

## What Decides the Price

Every roof quote is built from the same few things. Understanding them helps you compare quotes fairly.

**Size and pitch** More squares of roof means more material and labor. A steep roof is slower and costlier to work on safely.

**Material** Composition shingles, metal and tile run at very different prices. Shingles cost the least up front; metal and tile cost more and last longer.

**Decking repair** Rotted wood can only be found once the old roof is off. A good estimate explains how that's handled before the work starts.

**Layers and details** Old layers to tear off, chimneys, skylights and valleys all add labor. A simple, single-layer roof costs less to replace than a cut-up one.

We give you a detailed estimate after we've seen the roof. A number given over the phone, before anyone has looked, is a guess.
