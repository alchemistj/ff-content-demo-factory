## Gate result
Top claim: row 1, "Find It First. Check It After." at 20. It passes gate (16+). Row 2 also scores 20 and row 3 scores 16, so the hero can lead with the braid.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point.** For Wichita homeowners who have a leak and don't know whether they need a repair or a whole roof, River City Roofing is the roofer that gets into the attic and onto the roof first and finds where the water is really coming from, so you pay for the problem you have.
- **B, method.** Unlike roofers who quote from the driveway and disappear after the crew leaves, River City Roofing has Anthony check the cause before the quote and come back after the job, so nobody is guessing and nobody goes silent.
- **C, emotional outcome.** You call one person, he finds the real problem, and he checks on you after. You never feel in the dark about your own roof.

**Pick: B.** It names the contrast with the roofers customers fear (fear #4 and #5) and states the method in the order a customer lives it. Both halves come straight from named reviews.
