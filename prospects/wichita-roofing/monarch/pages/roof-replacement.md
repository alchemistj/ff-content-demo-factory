## How You Know It's Time

Most asphalt shingle roofs last somewhere between 20 and 30 years, less where hail and hot summers are hard on them. The signs are usually easy to spot once you know to look.

Shingles curl or crack. Bare patches show where the granules have worn off. The gutters fill with grit, a ceiling stains after rain, or shingles keep blowing into the yard.

One of those isn't always the end of the roof. If it's one slope or a few shingles, [roof repair](/roof-repair-wichita-ks/) may be all you need. Cesar will tell you which it is.

## Choosing Your Shingles

The shingle you pick changes how long the roof lasts. A basic three-tab shingle is the cheapest and the thinnest. Architectural shingles are thicker, hold up longer and look better.

In hail country there's a third option, Class 4 impact-resistant shingles, built to take hail without cracking. We've installed them. Some insurance carriers discount premiums for them, so ask yours.

Cesar walks you through the choices and the cost of each, and you pick the color you can live with.

## What a Good Replacement Includes

Whoever you hire, a replacement done right has these parts. Ask any roofer to confirm each one before you sign.

- The old roofing comes off down to the deck, so the wood underneath can be checked.
- Soft or rotted decking is replaced, not covered over.
- New underlayment goes down, along with flashing at the chimneys, walls and vents.
- Ventilation in the attic is checked, because a hot attic cooks shingles from below.
- New shingles are nailed in the pattern the manufacturer calls for.

## Why It Matters What's Under the Shingles

Most roof failures start in the details, not in the shingles. A leak at a chimney is usually flashing, and a roof that ages early is often an attic that can't breathe.

That's why we want to look at the whole roof before we price it.
