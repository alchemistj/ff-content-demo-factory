## Roof Repair

A few shingles in the yard after a windy night, or a drip over the dining room, does not always mean a new roof.

Water travels. It runs along the deck and drops somewhere far from where it got in, so the stain on your ceiling is rarely under the problem. We find where the water is really coming in, fix that, and tell you honestly if the rest of the roof has years left.

## When Hail or Wind Hits

After a Kansas storm, the roof often looks fine from the driveway. Hail bruises shingles and loosens their granules, and the damage shows up months later as a leak.

If your insurance company is involved, the adjuster's visit is the moment the damage gets written down. We look at your roof first, tell you plainly whether there's damage, and go to the adjuster's inspection.

We talk with your insurance company for you and send the paperwork in. What an insurer pays is between you and them, and nobody honest can promise it. Our part is making sure they see everything that's on your roof.
