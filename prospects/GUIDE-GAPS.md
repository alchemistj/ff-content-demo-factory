# Guide gaps

Architects add a line here when a guide is missing something or contradicts itself: what, which guide, the example. The guide owner folds it in.

- **Reusing a review across pages.** REVIEW_QUOTES.md ("Where reviews go") says "With only a few reviews, one review can appear on more than one page". docs/words-package.md ("What the checker enforces", item 2) and WRITER-BRIEF.md say each review is used once. Example: 1st Call (Wichita HVAC) has 48 five-star reviews, fine; a 7-review business like Azteca HVAC could not fill four pages under the once-only rule. Which wins? (wichita-hvac Architect, 2026-11-09)
- **File name mismatch.** STRATEGY_PAGE_GUIDE.md ("Lines to pick from") and words-package.md point to `full-build-lines.md`; the file is `docs/writer-guides/FULL_BUILD_LINES.md`. (wichita-hvac Architect, 2026-11-09)
- **No field for the no-website subheading.** STRATEGY_PAGE_GUIDE.md ("The approved subheading") says to start with "A website" when the business has no website. words-package.md has no field or flag for it, so the template can't know which version to show. Example: 1st Call Heating & Cooling (wichita-hvac/firstcall), whose Google website link shows a domain-for-sale page. (wichita-hvac Architect, 2026-11-09)
- **Retell check (8) flags business facts.** scripts/check-words-package.mjs check 8 fails standard hours ("8:00 AM to 5:00 PM" matches a review's "5:00 pm", Epic HVAC) and the business's own site wording when a reviewer used the same words (Alpha HVAC: "heating and air conditioning" equipment, a quote from their /heating page). Writers end up rewording confirmed facts to get past it. Could hours, phones and facts.md quotes be exempt? (wichita-hvac Architect, 2026-11-09)
- **"Get a Free Estimate" button vs unconfirmed offers.** The v2 rule (top of every page ends with **Buttons:** Call … | Get a Free Estimate) promises free estimates. For 3 of 4 Wichita HVAC prospects (Cook's, 1st Call, Epic) facts.md does not confirm free estimates. I told writers to use "Request Service" there. Is that right, or is the button label fixed chrome? (wichita-hvac Architect, 2026-11-09)
