# Guide gaps

Architects add a line here when a guide is missing something or contradicts itself: what, which guide, the example. The guide owner folds it in.

- **Storm/hail damage as a service page (roofing).** KEYWORD_GUIDE.md section 5 (Roofing) says avoid "Storm Damage Repair" as a service page (too narrow), but `prospects/wichita-roofing/BRIEF.md` step 5 offers "roof repair or storm and hail damage repair" as the second page. Wichita roofers lean heavily on hail claims. Example: ICT Roofing & Exteriors has an Insurance page and leads with claims. For now the Wichita roofing plans use Roof Replacement + Roof Repair and cover hail/insurance inside them. (Wichita roofing Architect, 2026-11-09)
- **Review pull workflow reports failure after success.** `.github/workflows/review-pull.yml`: the pull step ends with `PIPESTATUS[1]: unbound variable`, so the run shows red, yet the reviews are committed to `review-data`. Also, queuing several pulls at once cancels all but the newest pending run (concurrency group), so pulls must be queued one at a time. Example: runs 67 to 70 on 2026-11-09. Not a writer guide, but Architects trip on it. (Wichita roofing Architect, 2026-11-09)

- **"Google review" caption: guide allows it, checker rejects it.** REVIEW_QUOTES.md ("The name under the quote") says a non-person reviewer name is captioned "Google review"; `scripts/check-words-package.mjs` only accepts "First L." matching a five-star reviewer. First-name-only reviewers ("Deanna", "Stephanie") fail too. Example: Flawless Roofing lost JayWalk, DIYNINJAGUY, Deanna and Stephanie, four of its 18 five-star reviews. (Wichita roofing Architect, 2026-11-09)
- **Report-card status words undefined.** words-package.md shows `"status": "Needs work"` but no guide lists the allowed values. Writers are using "Good", "Missing", "Needs work". (Wichita roofing Architect, 2026-11-09)
- **Lowercase reviewer names rejected.** The checker name rule is case-sensitive, so real five-star reviewers like "amanda C." (ICT Roofing, the best one-day-roof and insurance review) cannot be quoted. REVIEW_QUOTES.md does not say whether to capitalize the caption ("Amanda C."). Example: `prospects/wichita-roofing/ict-roofing/reviews.json`. (Wichita roofing Architect, 2026-11-09)
- **Service-page minimum vs thin evidence.** The checker requires 750+ rendered words per service page, while SERVICE_PAGE_GUIDE.md says example lengths are "not required word counts" and the writing guide says not to pad. With 7 reviews and a thin site, the writer filled with general advice ("replace when repairs keep coming back"). Example: `prospects/wichita-roofing/rivercity/words.json`. Should small-evidence prospects get a lower floor? (Wichita roofing Architect, 2026-11-09)
- **page-to-words.mjs crashes on a paragraph after a list or FAQ.** A plain paragraph that follows a numbered list or a `**Question?**` answer inside the same `##` section throws `Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'push')` (line 73: `cur.body` was deleted). Multi-paragraph FAQ answers hit it often. When it crashes partway, `words.json` keeps the old pages, so check-words-package then reports confusing "too thin" and "review already used" fails. Example: rivercity home, flawless roof-repair, douglas-fry roof-replacement. (Wichita roofing Architect, 2026-11-09)
- **check-page and check-words-package disagree on review reuse.** check-page passes a review that appears on two pages; check-words-package fails it ("already used on /home/"). The writer brief says 2 to 5 reviews per page. With few reviews these collide: River City has 7 written reviews for 4 pages, so 2+ per page means reuse. Which rule wins for thin-review prospects? (Wichita roofing Architect, 2026-11-09)
- **check-page passes a homepage that check-words-package fails for not linking its service pages.** Example: flawless and douglas-fry home.md. (Wichita roofing Architect, 2026-11-09)
