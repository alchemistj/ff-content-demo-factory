Method: `docs/writer-guides/AVATAR-AND-USP.md` section 2. Appeal is scored against `avatars/hvac.md` (a Wichita avatar, reused for the trade). Exclusivity is scored against the 5 competitors in `competitors/peoria-hvac.md`: AAA Northgate One Hour, Meister, AbsoLutes, A&A, Josh Phillips HVAC. Four of the five have no own site we could load, so "not found" below means not in directory or dealer text, and exclusivity is scored conservatively.

## Core customer insight (three lines)
- **What the buyer doesn't know:** what an after-hours call will cost, until the bill shows up.
- **What most companies do:** say "24/7" or "emergency service" (3 of 5 do) and leave the price for the driveway.
- **What TSI did for one customer:** told the caller, up front, that a night visit costs much more than waiting until morning, and let the caller choose (Bob W.).

## Business in one line
TSI is a single-location Peoria HVAC company, in business since 2004 per its site, with real after-hours calls in its reviews: a Sunday heat wave (Brad S.), a 10 PM furnace call (Linda K.), an after-hours AC call priced up front (Bob W.).

## Gate result
Top claim: "after-hours call answered, with the cost of the visit told up front," **16**. **Passes gate (16+),** just. The weekend-and-no-pickup claim is 15 and goes in the hero's second line only through the Brad S. review, not as a headline.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Peoria homeowners whose AC quits after hours, TSI is the company a caller reached and was told upfront what a night visit costs versus waiting for morning, so you decide before anyone drives out.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that say "24/7" and leave the price for the doorstep, TSI's callers describe a person who answers after hours and lays out the cost first.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You call after hours, you hear what it costs, and you decide. No surprise on the doorstep.

**Pick: A,** written on the pages as what one customer saw. It names the scariest moment and the price half that no competitor states. B is the outreach angle only if Josh wants it.

## What's thin
- **The lead rests on one review (Bob W.).** The copy says what he heard, and tells the reader to ask what a visit costs. It never says TSI quotes the price first as policy. Ask the owner: do you quote the after-hours price on the call, and what is it?
- **Emergency line.** The site says "24/7 After Hours Emergency Service" and "Emergency Repair 24/7", and office hours are Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Whether the emergency line is the same number is open (facts.md open question 7). The copy says "after-hours emergency service" and never "24/7."
- **Phone.** The demo uses the Google number (309) 699-3333, which is also the number in the site's static HTML. A script on the live site swaps it to 309-324-5317 in a browser (facts.md, re-check 2026-11-15), so no phone-conflict claim is made anywhere.
- **Owner.** No owner is named anywhere (facts.md). The copy says "we" and "TSI."
- **Ameren, years, licenses, BBB, warranty, financing:** the site says "union-trained, certified, and licensed technicians" and "Trust A+ BBB Rated Company", but facts.md marks license numbers and the BBB rating Unverified, and no warranty or financing is stated. None is used. The promotions ($500 and $250 off) have no year and are not used.
- **One 2-star review** (William O., 2023-12-27) is about a commercial maintenance bill and an unanswered discount request. It is not quoted. Do not claim "no complaints."
- **Competitors are "not found," not "absent."** Only One Hour has a loaded site, and its fetch was truncated.
