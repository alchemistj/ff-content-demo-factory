## Gate result
Top claim: "looks for the fix before anyone talks about a new system," **20**. **Passes the gate (16+).** The follow-up row also passes at 16. The speed row is 15: close, but below.

## Core customer insight (three lines)
- **What the buyer doesn't know:** whether "you need a whole new system" is a diagnosis or a sales pitch.
- **What most companies do:** quote the replacement first. Four of five Lincoln competitors lead with free estimates on replacements or install financing.
- **What ComfortTechs does instead:** finds what failed, says so, and talks about a new system only if it is real, as Kim H., Bryce R. and Brendan M. each saw.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Lincoln homeowners who've just been told they need a whole new furnace or AC, ComfortTechs looks for the fix first and lays out your options if replacement is real.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that quote the replacement, ComfortTechs finds what failed, tells you, and only then talks about a new system, with a free estimate if you need one.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You're not left wondering whether you were sold something you didn't need.

**Pick: A.** It names the #2 fear at the exact moment it hits, and no Lincoln competitor states it. B is the outreach angle. The speed and after-hours stories go in the subline and the service page.

## What's thin
- **Hours conflict (Conflict in facts.md).** The AC page says "24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and 365 days a year"; the contact page says Mon to Sat, 7 to 5, Sunday closed. The demo prints no hours and says "call and ask". The owner replied "We are available 24/7 for reasons just like this" to Robin B., but that is one reply. Do not use "24/7" until the owner says which is true.
- **Founding year conflict.** The homepage says "since 2007". An owner reply to Joe C. says "since I started the company in 2006", and the footer says 2022. The copy uses no year.
- **The repair-first row rests on three reviews.** Kim H. (2020), Bryce R. (2017) and Brendan M. (2023). Not a lot, and some are old. Ask the owner how a "you need a new system" second opinion usually goes.
- **Owner not named.** The email is jory@comforttechs.com and several reviews say "Jory", but facts.md marks his role as Unverified. The demo says "we".
- **Claims facts.md marks Unverified and the copy therefore skips:** NATE-certified, licensed and insured, BBB membership, American Standard "specialist" or dealer status, full warranties, the financing lender.
- **Two one-star reviews (2019)**, both disputed by the owner's reply. Not used.
- **Residential and commercial are mixed** in the reviews (Dustin J., Cynthia H. are businesses). The demo is residential-led and keeps commercial as one section.
