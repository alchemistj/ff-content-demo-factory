Replacing your Air Filters
by genr8admin | Sep 17, 2019 | Tips | 0 comments
It’s important to replace your filters every 90 days for better efficiency. If you use your unit more than normal or if your unit is older, your filter may need to be changed more often. Clean air filters promote good airflow which helps your furnace heat more efficiently. A clean filter also reduces the amount of dust circulating in your home. A good way to judge if your air filter needs to be replaced is to see if it is covered in dust.
