Who we are
Roettger’s Heating and Cooling was established in 2022 with the goal of providing exceptional HVAC service, replacement, and maintenance for our customers. Backed by more than 10 years of hands-on, high-quality experience, we are committed to delivering the right solutions to meet your comfort needs and always with your budget in mind.
For me, this business is about more than just heating and cooling—it’s about building lasting relationships. We value transparency, clear communication, and treating every home or business as if it were my own. Whether we are completing a quick tune-up, troubleshooting a complex issue, or installing a full system replacement, you can count on us to get the job done right the first time.
Outside of work, I enjoy spending time with my wife, Jessica, who also grew up here in Lincoln, and our two boys. As a family, we care deeply about our community and strive to make every customer feel like part of the Roettger family.
