Frequently Asked Questions
How often should I service my HVAC system?
We recommend scheduling professional HVAC maintenance twice a year; once in the spring for your air conditioner and once in the fall for your furnace or heating system. Regular tune-ups improve efficiency, extend the life of your equipment, and help prevent costly breakdowns.
What are signs my HVAC system needs attention?
Common warning signs include strange noises, uneven temperatures, weak airflow, higher energy bills, or unpleasant odors. If you notice any of these issues, contact Roettger’s Heating & Cooling right away. Catching problems early helps save money on major repairs.
How do I know if I need to replace my system instead of repairing it?
If your system is more than 10 years old, needs frequent repairs, or isn’t keeping your space comfortable, replacement may be the smarter investment. We offer honest recommendations and free estimates so you can choose the best solution for your home or business.
Can HVAC maintenance really lower my energy bills?
Yes! A clean, well-tuned HVAC system runs more efficiently, using less energy to heat and cool your space. This can lead to noticeable savings on your monthly utility bills and better overall performance.
Do you offer emergency HVAC service?
Absolutely. We know heating and cooling issues don’t always happen at convenient times. Contact us for fast, reliable service when you need it and we’ll work quickly to get your system running again.
Do you offer financing for new systems?
Yes, we offer flexible financing options to make new HVAC systems more affordable. This allows you to get the comfort you need now while making manageable monthly payments that fit your budget.
What brand of equipment do you install?
We proudly install Comfortmaker® HVAC equipment. Comfortmaker is an ICP-manufactured brand known for its reliability, energy efficiency, and long-lasting performance. Because it’s locally supplied, we can get equipment quickly, which means faster turnaround times for new installations and replacements. Choosing Comfortmaker ensures you get a high-quality system that’s built to keep your space comfortable for years to come..
Why choose Roettger’s Heating & Cooling?
Because we combine expert knowledge with honest pricing and friendly, local service. Whether it’s a simple tune-up or a full system replacement, we treat your home or business as if it were our own.
