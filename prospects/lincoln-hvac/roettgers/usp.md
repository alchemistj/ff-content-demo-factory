## Business in one line
Roettger's Heating and Cooling is a small, locally owned Lincoln HVAC company where customers say Matt, the owner, answers the phone and does the work, often with Kaden as the technician (66 pulled reviews, all five-star, mostly May to November 2026). Its site says "Speak directly with Matt for fast answers and service scheduling."

## Gate result
Top claim: the braid, **16**, scored as its strongest part, the heads-up text. **Passes the gate (16+), just.** The heads-up row also scores 16 on its own. The speed row is 15 and the honest-recommendation row is 15.

## Core customer insight (three lines)
- **What the buyer doesn't know:** when the technician will actually show up, and whether the person on the phone is the person doing the work.
- **What most companies do:** claim 24/7 and leave the wait unexplained. Four of five Lincoln competitors say 24/7 or emergency.
- **What Roettger's does instead:** Matt takes the call, the technician texts or calls ahead, and the work usually starts the same day, as six reviewers describe the heads-up.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Lincoln homeowners whose AC quit on a hot afternoon, Roettger's is the company where you talk to Matt, get a text when the technician is on the way, and are not left guessing.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that say 24/7 and leave you waiting, Roettger's answers the call, tells you when the technician is coming, and explains what failed before the work starts.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You know who's coming and when, and you're not sitting in a warm house wondering.

**Pick: A.** It meets the #3 fear ("nobody will come") with the one thing six reviewers mention and no competitor states. B is a close second for the outreach message. The speed stories go in the subline and the service page.

## What's thin
- **The text is stated by six reviewers, not by the site.** The site says nothing about it. Ask Matt whether the heads-up text or call is standard on every visit, and whether he sends it himself or Kaden does.
- **Years conflict (Conflict in facts.md).** The about page says "established in 2022" and also "more than 10 years of hands-on, high-quality experience". The copy uses no year.
- **No address, no hours on the site (Unverified in facts.md).** The demo prints neither. The FAQ says emergency service is offered, with no hours.
- **Financing, warranty, license, insurance and brands:** the financing line in facts.md (a Wisetack program with example payments) is marked Unverified, and no warranty, license or insurance claim is on the site. The copy skips all of them. One review names Rheem; the site names no brand.
- **Owner role.** "Matt the owner" comes from Amelia P. and "hands on owner operator" from Cory S. The site says only "Speak directly with Matt." Matt's surname is inferred from the business name. The copy says "Matt".
- **Technician names.** Kaden appears in many reviews (also spelled Caden and Kaleb in a few). The demo doesn't name him in copy; the quotes do.
- **Review pull:** 66 of 169 reviews pulled, all five-star, mostly the last six months. Older reviews were not seen.
- **Competitors are "not found," not "absent."**
