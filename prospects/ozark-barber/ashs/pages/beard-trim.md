## When a Beard Needs a Trim

You can usually tell. One side is longer than the other, stray hairs run down the neck and the edge along your cheek has blurred.

Hair grows at different speeds on different parts of the face, so a beard loses its shape faster than the hair on your head. A beard that is the right length can still look unfinished.

A trim fixes that without changing how your beard looks. The length stays, the surface evens out, and the edges come back.

## Trim, Cut and Shaping: Which One Do You Need?

These three terms get mixed up, so here is how we use them.

**Beard trim.** Maintenance. Your beard keeps about the same length. We even out the surface, clean up stray hairs and tidy the edges.

**Beard cut.** A change in length. If the beard has grown past where you like it, or you want to go from full to short, a cut takes real length off and gives you a new starting point.

**Beard shaping.** The lines. We set the cheek line and the neckline so the beard has clear edges.

Most men want a mix.

A long beard may need a cut and then shaping. A short one may only need a trim. Tell us what you're after when you sit down and we'll build the right combination.

## Which One to Book

- The length is right but it looks messy: a beard trim.
- It's longer than you want, or you want a new length: a beard cut.
- The length is fine but the edges aren't clean: beard shaping.
- You want a different look overall: a cut with shaping.

If you can't tell which is which, show up and say so. We'll look at the beard and tell you what it needs.

## Getting Ready for Your Appointment

Come in with the beard you have. You don't need to comb it into shape or trim it first. A beard trimmed at home the night before is harder to shape, because the uneven spots hide the way it naturally grows.

A photo of the look you want helps a lot. Point out any cowlick, patchy spot or thin side so we can work around it.

## Patchy and Thin Spots

Many beards run thick at the chin and thin out on the cheeks. A beard like that looks best with a length and a line chosen around the patches, not in spite of them.

We look at where the beard is full and where it's sparse, then pick a shape that makes it look full.

## Questions About Beard Work

**Can I get a beard trim without a haircut?**
Yes. Book it on its own or add it to a haircut.

**What's the difference between a trim and a cut?**
A trim keeps your length and cleans up. A cut takes the beard shorter.

**Can you shape a beard that has grown out?**
Yes. If it has also grown past the length you like, we cut it down and then shape it.

**How often should I come in?**
It depends on how fast you grow and how sharp you like the edges. Many men with a shaped beard come every few weeks, and you can plan your schedule with us after your first visit.
