## When You Book, Tell Us

- The cut or service you want: haircut, fade, kids' or teen cut, beard trim.
- If you want a haircut and a beard trim, say both so we set aside the time.
- If it's a child's first haircut, say so.
- If you're bringing more than one person, book back to back times and let us know.

A photo of the cut you want is the fastest way to get it right. If you can't find the shop, call and we'll point you in.
