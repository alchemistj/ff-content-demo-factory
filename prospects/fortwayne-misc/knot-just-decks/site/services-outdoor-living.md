Your backyard should feel like an extension of your home — a place to relax, entertain, and enjoy the outdoors. We design and build custom outdoor living spaces that turn ordinary yards into functional retreats.
Decks, pergolas, seating areas, lighting — tailored to your lifestyle. We start by understanding how you'll use the space, then design features that fit. Hosting family or enjoying quiet evenings, we build spaces that work for both.
Many homeowners begin with a deck design & build project, then add enhancements like pergolas, privacy walls, or lighting. Outdoor living spaces can also be paired with composite decking or wood decking for a cohesive look and long-lasting performance.
Your outdoor living upgrade can work hand-in-hand with other exterior improvements, including garage floor coatings, gutters, and flagpoles. Our team coordinates projects so your home's exterior feels polished and intentional.
We believe great outdoor spaces start with thoughtful design and quality materials. Knot Just Decks brings craftsmanship, creativity, and attention to detail to every project, ensuring your investment adds both enjoyment and value to your home.
We build outdoor living spaces in Fort Wayne, New Haven, Huntertown, Defiance, Columbia City, Angola, Warsaw, Coldwater, and Southern Michigan — retreats that inspire.
