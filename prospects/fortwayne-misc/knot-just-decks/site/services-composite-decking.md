As one of Trex’s top-designation Platinum installers, our team is trained and authorized to maximize Trex’s 25‑year limited fade and stain warranty for every composite deck we build.
Composite decking gives you the beauty of wood without the maintenance. We install premium systems engineered to resist fading, moisture, insects, and warping — even in the Midwest's changing climate.
Composite boards are engineered from recycled materials and durable polymers — they won't splinter, rot, or need yearly staining. Available in a wide range of colors, textures, and finishes to match any home.
We build with proven framing techniques and manufacturer-approved methods, ensuring proper ventilation, drainage, and structure so your deck performs for decades.
Composite decks pair well with wood decking and outdoor living upgrades — visit our wood decking and Epoxy vs Polyurea pages to compare options for your project.
Your deck should be as functional as it is beautiful. We guide you through every option — railing styles, board patterns, finishes — to build a low-maintenance space that fits your life. We build all year long, not just in spring.
We install composite decking in Fort Wayne, New Haven, Huntertown, Defiance, Columbia City, Angola, Warsaw, Coldwater, and Southern Michigan — outdoor spaces built to last.
