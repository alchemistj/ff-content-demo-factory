Common Deck Mistakes
Building a deck is a major investment. Avoid these common mistakes through proper design, materials, and installation.
Get Expert Guidance
⚠️
Improper Footing Depth
Footings that aren't deep enough can shift during freeze-thaw cycles, causing structural damage to your entire deck.
⚠️
Poor Drainage Planning
Without proper drainage, water pools under your deck causing rot, erosion, and foundation issues over time.
⚠️
Incorrect Board Spacing
Boards that are too tight or too loose lead to warping, buckling, and trapped moisture that accelerates decay.
⚠️
Wrong Material Choice
Choosing materials without considering climate, maintenance, and longevity leads to premature replacement. Compare composite vs wood.
⚠️
Skipping Permits
Building without proper permits can result in fines, forced removal, and issues when selling your home.
⚠️
DIY Without Experience
Deck building requires structural knowledge. Our professional process ensures safety and code compliance.
