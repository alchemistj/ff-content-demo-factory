Industrial-Grade Garage Floor Coatings
Knot Just Decks is a Penntek Certified Installer — trained and authorized to apply Penntek’s premium polyurea and polyaspartic coatings, engineered for Midwest temperature swings and a lifetime of daily use.
Your garage floor takes more abuse than almost any surface in your home — hot tires, oil, salt, moisture, freeze-thaw. Bare concrete can't keep up. Knot Just Decks installs professional coating systems that transform cracked concrete into a seamless, durable surface.
We install high-performance polyurea floor coatings designed for residential garages, basements, workshops, and commercial spaces. Unlike epoxy DIY kits, our coatings are professionally prepared, moisture-tested, and applied for long-term adhesion — preventing peeling, bubbling, and discoloration.
Not sure which system is right? Our Epoxy vs Polyurea Floors comparison breaks down curing time, UV resistance, durability, and maintenance. Most homeowners choose polyurea for fast installation and superior longevity — we'll help you pick.
Garage floor coatings also add value. A clean, glossy, slip-resistant floor instantly improves your home's appearance and functionality. Many homeowners pair coatings with our deck design & build services or outdoor living upgrades to fully enhance their property. If you're planning a full exterior refresh, our team can help coordinate your garage, deck, and pergola projects together.
Knot Just Decks is known across the Midwest for quality craftsmanship, honest guidance, and clean, professional installations. Every project starts with proper surface prep, crack repair, and moisture mitigation so your coating performs for years — not months.
We install garage floor coatings in Fort Wayne, New Haven, Huntertown, Defiance, Columbia City, Angola, Warsaw, Coldwater, and Southern Michigan — protecting and enhancing homes with results that last.
