TREXPRO PLATINUM CONTRACTOR
Knot Just Decks holds Trex’s highest contractor designation — reserved for elite installers with proven craftsmanship, training, and customer-first service.
A custom deck is more than an outdoor platform — it's where families gather, friends connect, and memories are made. Knot Just Decks specializes in deck design and build services that combine craftsmanship, durability, and personalized style to create outdoor spaces that truly feel like home.
Every project begins with a consultation to understand your goals, lifestyle, and budget. Our designers help you choose layouts, materials, railings, lighting, and stairs that fit your space and complement your home's architecture. Once your plan is finalized, our skilled installers bring your vision to life using proven construction methods and premium materials.
We build both composite decking and wood decking, giving you options based on maintenance preferences, appearance, and longevity. Composite decks offer low upkeep and modern durability, while wood decks provide classic warmth and natural beauty. If you're unsure which is right, our team will walk you through the pros and cons so you can make a confident decision.
Your deck can also become the foundation for a complete outdoor living space. Many homeowners add pergolas, privacy walls, seating areas, and lighting to extend their living area beyond the home. We also coordinate decks with garage floor coatings and other exterior improvements for a cohesive property upgrade.
To help homeowners plan properly, we provide a clear deck installation process and highlight common deck mistakes to avoid costly issues. From permitting and inspections to final walkthroughs, we handle every detail so you can enjoy a stress-free experience.
We design and build decks in Fort Wayne, New Haven, Huntertown, Defiance, Columbia City, Angola, Warsaw, Coldwater, and Southern Michigan — outdoor spaces built to last.
