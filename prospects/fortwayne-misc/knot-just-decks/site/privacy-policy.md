Knot Just Decks, Inc. ("Company," "we," or "us") values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website knotjustdecks.com (the "Website") and related online services (collectively, the "Services"). By accessing or using our Website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy and our Terms and Conditions of Use. We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on the Website, and your continued use of the Services after changes are made constitutes your acceptance of those changes. If you have questions, contact us using the information in Section 6.
1. Information We Collect
We collect two types of information:
a. Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
PII includes data that identifies you as an individual, such as your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, IP address, device identifiers, and geolocation. We collect this when you fill out forms, request quotes, or contact us. You may choose not to provide some of this information, but doing so may limit your ability to use certain features of the Services. We do not share your PII with third parties except in the following situations:
With your consent
To fulfill the reason you provided it (e.g., to deliver a quote)
With service providers who assist with our operations
When required by law or legal process
In the event of a business transition (e.g., merger or sale)
Where otherwise stated in this Policy
Your information may be stored or processed outside of your country of residence. While we take precautions, those jurisdictions may have different data protection standards.
b. Aggregate Information
This is non-identifying data such as the pages you visit, referring URLs, browser type, and IP address. We use this information in aggregate form to improve the Website, analyze traffic, and tailor the user experience.
2. How We Use Your Information
We may use your PII to:
Provide quotes or services
Respond to inquiries
Improve our Website and Services
Send marketing communications (if you've opted in)
Troubleshoot technical issues
Enforce our Terms of Use or comply with legal obligations
Cookies and Tracking Technologies: We use cookies and other technologies (e.g., web beacons, Flash cookies, Google Analytics) to personalize your experience, analyze traffic, and remember your preferences. Cookies may store data like your IP address and browser type. You can manage or block cookies via your browser settings. If you disable cookies, parts of the Website may not function properly.
3. Third-Party Tracking
Some third-party service providers (such as advertisers or analytics partners) may use cookies or similar technologies on our Website. These providers may collect data about your browsing behavior across websites to deliver targeted content or ads. We do not control these third-party technologies. For questions about their practices, please contact them directly.
4. Your Choices
You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by updating your preferences or contacting us. You can also:
Adjust your browser settings to limit cookie usage
Request access to, updates to, or deletion of your personal data (see Section 6)
California residents may have additional rights under state law.

We may share your PII:

As part of a business transfer or restructuring
To comply with legal obligations
