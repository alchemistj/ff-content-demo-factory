NIFDA MEMBER DEALER
Knot Just Decks is a member of the National Independent Flag Dealers Association — ensuring the flags and flagpoles we install meet industry standards for materials, durability, and proper display.
A flagpole is a timeless way to show pride in your home, business, or community. Knot Just Decks offers professional flagpole sales and installation for residential and commercial properties across the Midwest.
We provide a range of aluminum and fiberglass flagpoles in multiple heights, finishes, and mounting styles. Our team helps you select the right size and placement to ensure proper visibility and long-term durability. Every installation includes secure footing and weather-resistant hardware.
Flagpoles are often installed alongside outdoor living spaces, pergolas, or entryway improvements. Many homeowners coordinate flagpole installations with deck design & build or garage floor coatings to complete their exterior upgrades.
We also offer maintenance guidance and accessories, including lighting kits, halyard systems, and flag replacements. Our goal is to make owning a flagpole simple, safe, and long-lasting.
From consultation to installation, Knot Just Decks ensures your flagpole is properly installed and compliant with local guidelines. We take pride in delivering reliable craftsmanship and professional service. We also service all residential & commercial flags and flagpoles.
We install flagpoles in Fort Wayne, New Haven, Huntertown, Defiance, Columbia City, Angola, Warsaw, Coldwater, and Southern Michigan — high-quality solutions for residential and commercial properties.
