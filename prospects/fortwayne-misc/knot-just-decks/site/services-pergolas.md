Custom Pergolas
Add architectural beauty and defined outdoor space with a professionally designed pergola that enhances your deck, patio, or backyard.
Get Your Free Estimate
A pergola adds structure, shade, and architectural beauty to any outdoor space. We design and install custom pergolas that anchor your deck, patio, or backyard — a defined zone for relaxation and entertaining.
Our pergolas are built with high-quality materials, engineered for the Midwest. Classic wood or low-maintenance composite — we help you choose a design that complements your home and outdoor living goals.
Pergolas pair perfectly with our deck design & build services and complete larger outdoor living spaces. Many homeowners choose to add lighting, privacy panels, or climbing plants to create a comfortable environment for dining, lounging, or hosting guests. When combined with composite decking or wood decking, a pergola becomes the centerpiece of your backyard.
Our process begins with a design consultation to understand your space, sun exposure, and functional needs. We then customize size, style, and layout to fit your vision. From permitting to final installation, our experienced team manages every detail so you can enjoy your new pergola without stress.
A pergola is also a smart investment. It increases property value, boosts curb appeal, and makes your outdoor space usable throughout more of the year. Homeowners often coordinate pergola installations with garage floor coatings, gutters, or other exterior upgrades to create a fully refreshed home.
We build pergolas in Fort Wayne, New Haven, Huntertown, Defiance, Columbia City, Angola, Warsaw, Coldwater, and Southern Michigan — outdoor structures as beautiful as they are functional.
