Accessibility Statement
This statement was last updated on May 2026.
We at Knot Just Decks are working to make our site www.knotjustdecks.com accessible to people with disabilities.
What web accessibility is
An accessible site allows visitors with disabilities to browse the site with the same or a similar level of ease and enjoyment as other visitors. This can be achieved with the capabilities of the system on which the site is operating, and through assistive technologies.
Accessibility adjustments on this site
We have adapted this site in accordance with WCAG 2.1 guidelines, and have worked to make the site accessible to the level of AA. This site's contents have been adapted to work with assistive technologies, such as screen readers and keyboard use. As part of this effort, we have also:
Used semantic HTML and landmark regions to support screen-reader navigation
Set the language of the site
Defined clear heading structures on all of the site’s pages
Added alternative text to images
Implemented color combinations that meet the required color contrast
Reduced the use of motion on the site, with respect for prefers-reduced-motion settings
Ensured all videos and files on the site are accessible
