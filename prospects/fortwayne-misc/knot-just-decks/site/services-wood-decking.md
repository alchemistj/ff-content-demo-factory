Composite or classic wood, the same Platinum-level craftsmanship goes into every Knot Just Decks build. Pressure-treated lumber, cedar, and specialty woods — framed and finished to last decades in Midwest weather.
Wood decking offers timeless beauty and natural warmth. We build custom wood decks using pressure-treated lumber, cedar, and specialty wood — durable and visually striking.
A properly built wood deck creates an inviting space for entertaining, relaxing, and family. Our team designs decks that match your home's architecture with correct spacing, drainage, and framing built to handle the Midwest climate.
While wood decks require more maintenance than composite, many homeowners prefer their authentic appearance and affordability. We help you compare materials by linking to our composite decking and deck design & build pages, allowing you to choose the right solution for your lifestyle and budget.
Wood decks also serve as the foundation for larger outdoor living spaces. Pergolas, seating areas, and lighting can be added to create a complete backyard retreat. Many homeowners coordinate deck upgrades with garage floor coatings and exterior improvements for a unified property enhancement.
We build decks that look beautiful today and stay strong for years. Clear guidance, expert installation, and maintenance recommendations — so you protect your investment. We build all year long, not just in spring.
We build wood decks in Fort Wayne, New Haven, Huntertown, Defiance, Columbia City, Angola, Warsaw, Coldwater, and Southern Michigan — outdoor spaces built to last.
