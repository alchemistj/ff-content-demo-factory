Polyaspartic vs Polyurea
Choosing the right garage floor coating is essential for long-term performance. Compare the two most popular professional options.
Get a Coating Quote
The two most popular professional garage floor coating systems are epoxy and polyurea. Both improve the look and durability of your concrete — but they differ in critical ways.
Epoxy is cost-effective with a glossy finish, but it cures slowly and is sensitive to temperature. Polyurea cures fast, resists UV damage, and handles moisture better — ideal for the Midwest climate.
Most homeowners prefer polyaspartic for its longevity and faster installation. Learn more on our garage floor coatings page and see why professional installation is critical to preventing peeling and chipping.
