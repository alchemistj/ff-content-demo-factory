Your roof is your home's first line of defense, protecting everything beneath it from Northeast Indiana's heavy snow, high winds, hail, driving rain, and harsh seasonal weather. Whether your roof has reached the end of its lifespan or has been damaged by a recent storm, Knot Just Decks provides dependable roofing solutions built to last.
We specialize in full roof replacements, roof repairs, storm damage restoration, insurance claim assistance, and emergency roof tarping for homeowners throughout Northeast Indiana, Northwest Ohio, and Southern Michigan. Every project begins with a complimentary roof inspection so you understand the condition of your roof before making any decisions.
As a full-service exterior contractor, we also understand how your roof works together with the rest of your home's exterior. A failing roof can lead to water intrusion, damaged gutters, deteriorating siding, and unnecessary wear on your outdoor living spaces. By evaluating your home's entire exterior system, we help ensure every component works together to provide lasting protection.
Whether you're replacing an aging roof or recovering from storm damage, our experienced team will guide you through every step—from inspection to final cleanup—with honest recommendations and quality workmanship.

Our Roofing Process
Our roofing projects follow the same proven customer experience that has made Knot Just Decks
a trusted name for home improvement throughout the region.
1. Free Roof Inspection
‍We thoroughly inspect your roof to evaluate its condition, identify damage, and determine the best solution for your home.
2. Honest Recommendations & Written Proposal
You'll receive a detailed written proposal outlining our recommendations, materials, pricing, and project scope—without pressure or hidden costs.
3. Planning & Scheduling
Once approved, we'll coordinate materials, permits (when required), scheduling, and answer any questions before installation begins.
4. Professional Installation
Our experienced crew removes damaged roofing as needed and installs your new roofing system using proven installation methods and quality materials.
5. Final Walkthrough & Warranty
After installation, we perform a final inspection, ensure the job site is thoroughly cleaned, and review your warranty information so you can feel confident in your investment.
Storm Damage & Insurance Claims
Storms can cause significant roof damage that isn't always visible from the ground. If you've experienced hail, high winds, fallen branches, or severe weather, our team can inspect your roof and help determine whether repairs or replacement are needed.
We regularly assist homeowners with insurance claims, providing thorough documentation and working with you throughout the process to help restore your home as efficiently as possible.
If your roof has suffered severe storm damage, we also offer emergency tarping services to help protect your home until permanent repairs can be completed.
