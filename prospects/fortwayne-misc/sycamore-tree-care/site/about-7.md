Why us?
Professionalism
We value good communication, honesty, and learning. If you contact us we will get back with you and if we say we'll do it, then it's done. Every member of our team goes to the best tree care conferences available to keep up to date on everything trees.
Joe Paden IN-3497B
#1 priority: Safety
Your safety, the trees safety, our safety. Those are the highest priority. We will do whatever it takes to make a situation safe for everyone involved. We all wear the property safety clothing and equipment. You can rest easy with us on your property!
Care for your trees
Sometimes trees need removed, but many times, trees can be saved and preserved for years of enjoyment. If we can help you keep your trees alive & healthy that's what we want to do.
Help the community
At Sycamore we want to see the tree canopy improved over the whole city with all the benefits that come from that. We also want to take youth from the community and set them up for success in life and arborculture. We want to see trees and youth grow up successful and healthy!
