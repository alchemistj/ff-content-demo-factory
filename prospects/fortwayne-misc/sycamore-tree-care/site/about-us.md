About Sycamore Tree Care
Founded in 2022 by Joe & Katie Paden and their good friend Jessica Carter (Webb).  The goal at Sycamore Tree Care was and is simple, help people be able to enjoy their trees and help youth in Fort Wayne that are struggling.
We believe that getting young people outside, working hard, and gaining skills can be massive for increasing their prospects in life.
​
Let us help you get your trees on track & help us get these students lives on track!
​
Joe has been in the tree industry for over a 12 years and is a Board Certified Master Arborist.

Our Passion is for Trees and Youth Development
Our experienced team doesn't just prune, trim, and remove trees; we also educate and mentor young people to help them build life skills and confidence. Our passion for both trees and youth development drives us to provide the highest quality tree service while making a positive impact on our community.
Our Tree Services
We offer a range of tree services to keep your trees healthy and beautiful, including pruning, trimming, diagnosis and treatment of diseases, and tree removal. We use the latest techniques to ensure the safety and health of your trees while minimizing disruption to your property.
Contact us today to learn more about our services and to schedule a consultation.
