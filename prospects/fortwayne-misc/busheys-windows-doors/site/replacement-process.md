OUR PROCESS
In-Home Visit
We evaluate
what you have.
Every Bushey’s relationship begins with an honest look at your current windows and doors. Your consultant will check for moisture damage, rot, structural concerns, and any energy efficiency or functional issues — giving you the full picture before you ever make a decision.
Design | Selections | Budget
We design the right
solution — together.
This is where the real conversation happens. Your consultant listens first, then helps you co-create a solution that fits your home, your style, and your budget. From window profiles and performance glass to hardware finishes and installation approach, every option is walked through clearly.
No smoke and mirrors on pricing. Your consultant will help you understand the full cost of your project and talk through how to budget for it — so there are no surprises when it’s time to sign.
Precision Measurement
We measure for a
perfect fit.
Once you’ve made your selections, Bushey’s will visit your home to take precise measurements of every opening — accounting for any variances in your existing frames. These measurements go directly into the order for your custom-fabricated windows and doors, ensuring a proper fit and a clean, finished look.
Scheduling | Fabrication
From order day to a
beautiful install.
When your order is placed, you’ll receive a detailed timeline — and we’ll keep you updated throughout fabrication with regular progress updates and confirmed installation dates. No wondering where things stand.
Before your windows ever leave for your home, they go through our advanced automated spray finishing process. Precision coating technology applies every paint and stain finish to the woodwork with a consistency and smoothness that simply can’t be replicated by hand — less waste, more uniformity, and a result that’s built to last.
Installation
We show up, and
we do it right.
On installation day, Bushey’s own employees arrive ready to work. They protect your home, remove the old units with care, and install each window and door with precision — using premium spray foam insulation, the right screws, and professional caulking for a truly airtight result. We take our time and do it right the first time, every time.
Long-Term Relationship
We’re still here
after installation day.
Bushey’s doesn’t disappear once the job is done. If you ever need a service call, a seasonal adjustment, or just have a question about your windows or doors years down the road — we’re here. That’s what it means to buy local from a team that stands behind their work.
