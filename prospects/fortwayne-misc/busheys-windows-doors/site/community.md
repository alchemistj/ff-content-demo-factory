Bushey’s has been a part of the Fort Wayne community since 1968. Giving back to the neighborhoods we serve isn’t something we do occasionally. It’s part of who we are.
A business with local commitments
When you choose Bushey’s, you’re supporting a locally owned, family-operated company that reinvests in Fort Wayne through partnerships, sponsorships, volunteer work, and community initiatives that strengthen the city we call home. From hunger relief to local festivals and fitness events, we show up for Fort Wayne the same way Fort Wayne has shown up for us for over five decades.

OUR PARTNERS IN THE COMMUNITY
Habitat for Humanity of Greater Fort Wayne
Habitat for Humanity of Greater Fort Wayne builds and renovates affordable homes in Allen County, partnering with volunteers and families to create safe, stable housing and stronger communities.
Run for Riley
Run for Riley is an annual charity 5-mile/5K race supporting Riley Children’s Foundation, raising funds for pediatric care, research, and programs that benefit children and families across northeast Indiana.
Military Families
Local initiatives that support military families through financial assistance, essential supplies, and community programs—often coordinated through organizations like Associated Churches in Fort Wayne.
Associated Churches Food Bank
Associated Churches of Fort Wayne & Allen County sponsors a network of neighborhood food banks in Fort Wayne that provides essential groceries and resources to families in need, helping address food insecurity throughout Allen County.
Proud to serve Fort Wayne
For over 55 years, Fort Wayne area homeowners have trusted Bushey’s with their homes. That trust means everything to us, and it’s what drives us to give back to the community that has supported us for generations. When you work with Bushey’s, you’re choosing a local team that’s invested in this community for the long haul.
