What is the difference between fiberglass and vinyl windows? Fiberglass windows, like those made with Marvin’s Ultrex® material, are significantly stronger than vinyl. They hold their shape better through temperature changes, which means a tighter seal and better energy efficiency over time. ## Energy Efficiency
What is Low-E glass and why does it matter? Low-E, or low emissivity glass, has a thin metallic coating that reflects heat and filters harmful UV rays. It helps keep your home warmer in winter and cooler in summer, reducing the strain on your heating and cooling systems and lowering your energy bills year-round. It’s one of the most impactful upgrades you can make when replacing windows in a Fort Wayne area home. 

## Energy Efficiency
What is argon gas in windows? Argon is a safe, odorless, colorless gas that is used between panes of glass in place of regular air. It is denser than air, which makes it a better insulator, reducing heat loss and improving the overall energy efficiency of your windows. 

## Energy Efficiency
What does U-Value mean for windows and doors? U-Value measures how well a window or door insulates by rating the rate of heat transfer. The lower the U-Value, the better the product is at keeping heat inside during winter and outside during summer. It’s one of the most important numbers to look at when comparing replacement windows or doors for a Fort Wayne area home. 

## Energy Efficiency
What is Solar Heat Gain Coefficient (SHGC)? Solar Heat Gain Coefficient measures how much heat from sunlight passes through a window or door. A lower SHGC means less unwanted heat enters your home, which helps keep it cooler and more comfortable during warm Indiana summers. 

## Energy Efficiency
What is R-Value, and how does it differ from U-Value? R-Value measures a material’s resistance to heat flow. The higher the R-Value, the better the insulation. For windows and doors specifically, U-Value and SHGC are the more commonly used and reliable measurements of energy performance. 

## Energy Efficiency
What is ENERGY STAR® certification? ENERGY STAR® is a joint program of the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency and the U.S. Department of Energy. Windows and doors that earn ENERGY STAR® certification meet strict energy efficiency guidelines that can help Fort Wayne area homeowners save on utility bills and reduce their environmental impact.

## Installation
What is the difference between an insert and a full-frame window replacement? An insert replacement fits a new window into your existing frame, preserving your interior trim and reducing installation time. A full-frame replacement removes everything down to the rough opening and installs all-new framing, casing, and window. Bushey’s offers both options and will help you determine which is the right fit for your home during your free consultation.

## Maintenance
Why are my windows sweating or fogging up? Condensation on the inside of your windows is usually caused by excess indoor humidity meeting a cooler glass surface, which is common during Indiana winters. Fogging between the panes of glass, however, typically indicates a failed seal and may mean it’s time to consider replacement. Bushey’s can help you determine the cause and the best solution during a free consultation.
