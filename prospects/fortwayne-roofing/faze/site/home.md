Frequently Asked Questions About Roofing
Let's cut through the confusion and get you the answers you need. Below, we've addressed the most common questions about our exterior services, process, and what you can expect when working with Faze.
What signs indicate I need a roof replacement or repair?
Your roof will usually give you some pretty clear warning signs when it needs attention. The most obvious red flags are missing or curled shingles, granules showing up in your gutters (they look like dark, coarse sand), or water stains on your ceiling that seem to appear after it rains. If your roof is pushing 20-25 years old, or you're noticing daylight peeking through your attic boards, it's definitely time to give us a call and let us take a look.
Do I need to replace my entire roof or can I just repair it?
it really depends on what's going on up there. If you're just dealing with a few damaged shingles or a small leak, a repair job might be all you need to get back in shape. But if your roof is getting up there in age and you're seeing widespread issues, or if more than 30% of your roof is damaged, replacing the whole thing is usually the smarter long-term investment. Either way, we'll come out and give you our honest assessment without trying to sell you more than you need.
Will new siding improve my home’s energy efficiency?
You bet it will! Think of new siding like putting a warm winter coat on your house. Modern siding materials come with way better insulation than the stuff they used even a decade ago, and when we install it, we can add an extra layer of insulation underneath. Most of our customers tell us they notice a real difference in their energy bills after we've put new siding on, especially during those brutal summer and winter months.
How often should gutters be cleaned and maintained?
If you want to keep water flowing where it should (and not where it shouldn't), you're going to want to show your gutters some love at least twice a year. Fall is super important because of all those leaves, and spring to clear out any winter debris that's built up in there. However, if you've got tall trees hanging over your house, you might need to bump that up to three or four times a year.
How do I know if I need new windows?
If your energy bills are creeping up while your comfort level's going down, your windows might be trying to tell you something. Pay attention to things like condensation between the panes, drafts you can feel from across the room, or windows that stick when you try to open them. These are all signs that your windows are ready for replacement.
