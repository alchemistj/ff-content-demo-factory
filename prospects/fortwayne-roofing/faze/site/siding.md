Common Signs You Need Siding Repair from a Professional Contractor
Siding Is Warped or Pulling Away
Siding that's starting to bubble out or pull away from your house isn't just an eyesore, it's your home trying to tell you there's trouble brewing underneath. Water is sneaky, and once it finds a way behind your siding, it can cause thousands in damage before you know it.
Rising Energy Costs
If your energy bills keep climbing even though your habits haven't changed, your siding might be waving the white flag. Modern siding helps keep your heating and cooling where it belongs - inside your home.
Water Stains On Interior Ceiling or Walls
Think your siding's color is just showing its age? Think again. Those patchy, faded spots usually mean your siding's protective properties are breaking down, leaving your walls vulnerable to moisture damage.
Interior Warning Signs
When your interior walls start showing moisture spots or your wallpaper starts peeling, that's your house screaming for help. These signs mean water has already breached your siding's defenses and is making itself at home in your walls.
Cracks, Holes, or Missing Sections
Those tiny cracks in your siding might seem harmless, but they're likely inviting water to come in and wreck your home's structure. By the time you notice interior damage, that little crack has already caused big trouble.
Sneaky Dry Rot
Finding spongy spots or fuzzy growth on your siding? That's like discovering the tip of an iceberg. What you can't see is usually much worse. Rot moves fast once it gets started, so this isn't something you want to wait on.

Frequently Asked Questions About Siding
Can I install new siding over existing siding?
While it's possible to install new siding over old in some cases, it's usually not the best approach for long-term protection of your home. Installing over existing siding can hide underlying damage and moisture issues that could cause serious problems down the road. We recommend removing old siding first so we can inspect and repair any hidden issues, ensure proper installation, and give you the best results for your investment.
Do I need to replace my insulation when installing new siding?
Siding replacement presents the perfect opportunity to upgrade your home's insulation since we'll have direct access to your walls. During our inspection, we'll check your existing insulation for damage, moisture, or inadequate coverage that could be affecting your home's energy efficiency. If your current insulation is in good shape we can leave it, but many homeowners choose to upgrade to modern insulation options during siding replacement to maximize their energy savings.
How long does siding installation take?
For most homes, a complete siding replacement takes about 1-2 weeks from start to finish, though this can vary depending on your home's size and the type of siding you choose. We work efficiently but never rush, ensuring every detail is handled properly and your property is cleaned up at the end of each work day. Our siding installation team will give you a clear timeline before work begins and keep you updated throughout the project.
