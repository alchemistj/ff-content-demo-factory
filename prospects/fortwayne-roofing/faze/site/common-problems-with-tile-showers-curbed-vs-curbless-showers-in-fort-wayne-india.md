Common Problems with Tile Showers: Curbed vs Curbless Showers in Fort Wayne, Indiana (2026)
Tile showers are one of the most popular upgrades in bathroom remodeling.
They offer:
Luxury appearance
Premium feel
Strong durability
Long-term value
But despite all their benefits, tile showers aren’t perfect.
Like any product, there are potential downsides homeowners should understand before investing.
At Faze Construction, we believe the best remodel decisions happen when homeowners fully understand both the benefits and the challenges of each product.
For this article, we’ll use a common shower size as our example:
3x5 shower remodel
This applies to both:
Curbed walk-in showers
Curbless walk-in showers
Let’s talk about the most common problems with tile showers.
Problem #1: Poor Grout Can Fail Over Time
One of the biggest failure points in tile showers is grout.
If low-quality grout is used, over time it can:
Crack
Chip
Fade
Stain
Deteriorate
Once grout begins to fail, water infiltration becomes a major concern.
Water can seep into weak points and eventually create:
Moisture damage
Mold
Waterproofing failures
Structural issues
This is one of the biggest reasons some tile showers fail prematurely.
At Faze Construction, this is why we require epoxy grout as a standard procedure.
Epoxy grout offers major advantages:
Superior strength
Excellent stain resistance
No fading
Better moisture protection
Longer lifespan
It costs more upfront.
But it dramatically improves long-term performance.
This is one reason our tile showers cost more than many competitors.
We prioritize long-term durability over short-term savings.
Problem #2: Curbless Showers Have More Water Control Challenges
Curbless showers are one of the most requested upgrades in bathroom remodeling.
They offer:
High-end appearance
Modern design
Better accessibility
Aging-in-place functionality
But curbless showers do come with added complexity.
The biggest challenge?
Water control.
A curbed shower naturally helps contain water inside the shower area.
That makes water management easier.
A curbless shower relies entirely on:
Proper floor slope
Drain placement
Waterproofing
Installation precision
If any of these are done incorrectly, water control issues can occur.
Problem #3: Curbless Showers Cost More on Concrete Foundations
This is something many homeowners don’t realize.
If your home has a concrete slab foundation, building a curbless shower becomes more complex.
Why?
Because the shower floor must be recessed lower than the surrounding floor to allow water to flow properly toward the drain.
That often requires:
Concrete cutting
Additional prep work
Floor modifications
Drain adjustments
This increases both labor and project cost.
Curbless showers are not always the most practical option for every home.
Problem #4: Tile Showers Are a Premium Investment
Tile showers are built for homeowners who want long-term quality.
They are not typically the best fit for:
Rental properties
Quick flips
Short-term ownership
Why?
Because tile showers are premium investments.
A properly built tile shower can realistically last decades—often 50 to 70 years or more.
That makes them ideal for:
Forever homes
High-value properties
Long-term homeowners
If you’re planning to move soon or want a lower-cost solution, other shower options may make more sense.
Problem #5: Tile Showers Cost More Upfront
This is the obvious one.
Tile showers cost more than fiberglass or acrylic systems.
Why?
Because they require:
Demolition
Waterproofing
Shower pan systems
Tile installation
Grout systems
Precision labor
Custom upgrades like:
Benches
Niches
Premium tile
Glass packages
increase cost further.
The result is a premium product—but at a premium price.
Curbed vs Curbless: Which Is Better?
The answer depends on your goals.
Choose a Curbed Shower If You Want:
Better water control
Lower project cost
Easier installation
Strong long-term performance
Curbed showers are often the best balance of luxury and practicality.
Choose a Curbless Shower If You Want:
Luxury design
Accessibility
Modern aesthetics
Aging-in-place functionality
Curbless showers offer incredible design and functionality—but require more planning and investment.
Who Is a Tile Shower Best For?
Tile showers are best for homeowners who value:
Longevity
Luxury
Customization
Premium finishes
Long-term ROI
They are not always the best fit for everyone.
The best bathroom remodel depends on:
Budget
Lifestyle
Home value
Long-term plans
That’s why product selection matters.
Choosing the Right Shower Matters
At Faze Construction, our goal isn’t to sell every homeowner the most expensive option.
Our goal is to help homeowners choose the right solution for their needs.
Sometimes that’s a fiberglass shower.
Sometimes it’s a premium curbed tile shower.
Sometimes it’s a fully custom curbless luxury shower.
It all depends on what fits your home and goals.
