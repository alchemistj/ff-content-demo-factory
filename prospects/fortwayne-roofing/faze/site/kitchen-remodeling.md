Kitchen Remodel FAQs
How long does a typical kitchen remodel take?
Most kitchen remodels take between 4-6 weeks from demolition to final touches, though larger projects might need a bit more time. We'll give you a detailed timeline before we start so you know exactly what to expect. During construction, we set up a temporary kitchen space and work efficiently to minimize disruption to your daily routine.
What's a realistic budget for a kitchen remodel?
While every project is unique, most of our kitchen remodels fall between $30,000 and $80,000, depending on the scope of work and materials you choose. Some homeowners start with smaller updates around $15,000, while others invest $100,000+ in complete luxury renovations. We'll help you understand costs upfront and can often suggest ways to get the look you want while staying within your budget.
Can I use my kitchen during the remodel?
While your kitchen won't be fully functional during the remodel, we help you set up a temporary kitchen space with essential appliances like a microwave, coffee maker, and mini-fridge. We also work in phases to maintain access to your sink and refrigerator as long as possible, and keep one countertop usable whenever we can. Most importantly, we clean up thoroughly at the end of each day to keep your home livable throughout the project.
Should I pick my appliances before or after designing the layout?
It's best to have your appliances selected early in the design process. Knowing exact dimensions helps us create a more efficient layout and ensures everything fits perfectly. We can recommend reliable appliances that match your cooking style and budget, or design around specific models you've already fallen in love with.
