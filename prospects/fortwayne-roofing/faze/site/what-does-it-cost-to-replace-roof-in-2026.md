What Does it Cost to Replace a Roof in Fort Wayne Indiana 2026?
June 26, 2026
How much should a new roof cost?
 One of the most common questions we get at Faze Construction
“How much should a new roof cost?”
The short answer?
For a typical asphalt shingle roof in Fort Wayne, Indiana, most homeowners can expect to spend anywhere from $19,000 to $37,000+, depending on the roof system they choose and the complexity of the project.
For premium systems like metal roofing, costs can range even higher.
Let’s break it down.
Average Roof Replacement Cost in Fort Wayne (2026)
For reference, a 30-square roof equals approximately 3,000 square feet of roofing area.
Entry-Level Asphalt Shingle Roof
Average Cost: $19,000–$25,000
This is the most common roof replacement option for homeowners looking for a dependable, budget-conscious solution.
Entry-level asphalt shingles are ideal for homeowners who:
Need a practical replacement
Plan to sell in the next 5–15 years
Want to keep upfront costs lower
These systems typically include:
Tear-off of old roofing materials
New underlayment
Ice & water protection
New shingles
Basic ventilation upgrades
Standard flashing work
Typical warranties for entry-level systems range from 10–15 years, depending on installation quality and manufacturer.
Premium Class 4 Impact-Resistant Shingle Roof
Average Cost: $28,000–$37,000
If you want better durability, longer life, and better storm resistance, upgrading to a Class 4 impact-resistant shingle is often the smartest long-term investment.
Benefits of Class 4 shingles include:
Highest impact resistance available for asphalt shingles
Better hail protection
Higher wind resistance
Longer lifespan
Reduced maintenance
Potential insurance premium discounts
Many homeowners in Indiana choose Class 4 systems because of the amount of wind, hail, and storm activity we experience.
Typical warranties on Class 4 systems are often 30–50 years, depending on the manufacturer and installation package.
For homeowners planning to stay in their home long-term, this is often the best value.
Metal Roofing Systems
Average Cost: $40,000–$50,000+
For homeowners wanting maximum durability and long-term protection, metal roofing is often considered the gold standard.
Metal roofing offers:
Exceptional lifespan
Superior weather resistance
Energy efficiency
Minimal maintenance
Strong resale value
Class 4 impact ratings on many systems
Most properly installed metal roofing systems can last 40–70+ years.
While the upfront investment is higher, many homeowners choose metal because it may be the last roof they ever install.
What Factors Affect Roof Replacement Cost?
Not all roofs cost the same—even if they’re the same size.
Several factors can significantly impact pricing:
Roof Steepness
Steeper roofs require more labor, additional safety equipment, and more installation time.
Number of Layers
If your home has multiple layers of shingles, removal becomes more labor-intensive and costly.
Roof Complexity
More valleys, dormers, skylights, chimneys, and penetrations increase labor and material costs.
Ventilation & Decking Repairs
Poor ventilation or damaged wood decking can add cost but are critical to long-term roof performance.
Product Selection
Better underlayments, upgraded shingles, and premium accessories improve performance—but increase upfront cost.
Cheap Roof vs. Quality Roof
One of the biggest mistakes homeowners make is shopping for the cheapest roof.
A lower price often means compromises in:
Installation quality
Materials
Ventilation
Flashing details
Warranty coverage
The reality is this:
The cheapest roof is rarely the cheapest roof in the long run.
A properly installed roof system protects your home, improves efficiency, and saves money over time.
