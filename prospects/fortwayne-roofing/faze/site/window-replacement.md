When To Get New Windows
Signs You Need To Replace Your Windows
Drafts or Leaks
Drafts coming through your windows are a clear sign your window seals are failing and costing you money on energy bills.
Condensation
When you spot moisture trapped between glass panes, it means your window's seal has failed and is letting outside air compromise your home's efficiency.
Difficult to Open/Close Windows
Windows that stick, jam, or won't stay open are telling you their mechanisms are wearing out and need professional attention.
Rot or Damage
Soft, crumbling wood or visible water damage around your window frames signals serious issues that typically require full replacement.
Increased Energy Bills
A sudden spike in your heating or cooling costs often points directly to windows that have lost their insulating properties.
Age
After two decades of Indiana weather, even the best windows start losing efficiency and could be costing you more than replacement would.

COMMON CHOICES FOR WINDOW REPLACEMENT
Window Styles For Your Home
Double-Hung Windows
The classic choice that never goes out of style. Both sashes move up and down for easy cleaning and maximum ventilation control. Perfect for bedrooms, living rooms, and any room needing traditional charm.
Casement Windows
Hinged on the side and opening outward with a crank, these windows provide excellent ventilation and unobstructed views. They're especially popular above kitchen sinks or in hard-to-reach areas.
Picture Windows
Fixed windows that don't open but provide maximum natural light and uninterrupted views. Ideal for living rooms or any space where you want to frame a beautiful view.
Sliding Windows
Smooth horizontal operation makes these windows ideal for wide openings. Their simple operation and unobstructed views work great in modern homes and rooms overlooking patios or gardens.
Bay & Bow Windows
Create a dramatic focal point while adding extra interior space and natural light. These windows extend outward from your wall, perfect for creating reading nooks or window seats.
Awning Windows
Hinged at the top and opening outward, these windows are great for ventilation even during light rain. Perfect for bathrooms or paired above larger windows for extra airflow.

Frequently Asked Questions About Window Replacement
How long does window replacement take?
For most homes, we can replace all windows in just 1-2 days, though larger homes or custom installations might take a bit longer. Our crews work efficiently on one window at a time to minimize your home's exposure to the elements. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start and make sure your home is secured and cleaned up at the end of each work day.
What's the difference between full-frame and pocket window replacement?
A full-frame replacement involves removing the entire window down to the rough opening, which is ideal when there's damage around the frame or you want to change window sizes. A pocket installation keeps your existing frame and just replaces the window itself, which is faster and less expensive but only works if your current frames are in good condition. We'll assess your situation and help you choose the best approach for your home.
Is it cheaper to replace all your windows at once?
While you can replace windows one at a time, doing them all at once typically saves you money in the long run. You'll benefit from package pricing, reduce installation costs since our crew only needs to come out once, and start saving on energy bills immediately. Plus, your windows will age at a consistent rate, maintaining a consistent look across your home. However, we understand budget constraints and can also work with you to develop a phased replacement plan if needed.
How much can I really save on energy bills with new windows?
While exact savings vary by home, most of our customers see a noticeable drop in their energy bills after installing new windows. Modern energy-efficient windows can reduce heating and cooling costs by 15-25% on average. The biggest difference shows up during Indiana's extreme temperature months, when your HVAC system doesn't have to work as hard to maintain comfortable temperatures.
When is the best time of year to replace my windows?
While we install windows year-round in Indiana, many homeowners prefer spring or fall when temperatures are mild. That said, our installation teams are trained to work efficiently in any weather, using special techniques and barriers to minimize heat loss during winter installations or cooling loss during summer. We'll help you choose the best time based on your schedule and needs.
