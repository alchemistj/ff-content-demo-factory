Clogged Gutters

Leaves, twigs, and other debris can block water flow, causing overflow that pools around your foundation—one of the leading causes of structural damage. Regular cleaning and gutter guards help prevent this problem.

Plant Growth

If your gutters have become a mini garden, it’s a sign they’re holding moisture and debris for too long. These growing roots can work their way under shingles, into siding, and around windows, leading to costly repairs.

Ice Dams

Harsh Indiana winters bring ice buildup that can trap water in your gutters, forcing it under your shingles and into your home. Properly installed gutters and insulation can help prevent these damaging ice dams.

Leaky Gutters

Cracks, holes, and separated seams can allow water to seep into your home’s siding, foundation, or even interior walls. If you’re noticing leaks, it’s time to repair or replace damaged sections before they cause major issues.

Poor Installation

Incorrect slope, misaligned downspouts, and poorly sealed joints can cause water to pool where it shouldn’t. Our expert installation ensures your gutters are properly pitched, securely fastened, and positioned to direct water safely away from your home.

Sagging Gutters

Gutters that sag or pull away from your home can’t direct water effectively, putting your roof, walls, and landscaping at risk. Reinforcements or replacements can get your system back in shape and working as it should.

Frequently Asked Questions
What Gutter Material Is Best For My Fort Wayne Home?

For most Fort Wayne homes, seamless aluminum gutters offer the perfect balance of durability, cost effectiveness, and style. These gutters resist rust, handle our varied Indiana weather conditions beautifully, and come in a wide range of colors to match your home. They're also lightweight enough to prevent stress on your fascia boards while being strong enough to stand up to heavy rainfall.

What are the signs that I need new gutters rather than just repairs?

If you notice multiple cracks, rust spots, or separating seams, it's usually more cost effective to replace your gutters than continue making repairs. Watch for signs like sagging sections, water marks beneath the gutters, or gutters pulling away from your house. When your gutters are over 20 years old and showing these signs, replacement typically makes more sense than ongoing repairs.

Should I replace my gutters and downspouts at the same time?

Yes, replacing both your gutters and downspouts together ensures your entire water management system works perfectly as one unit. Your downspouts handle 100% of the water your gutters collect, so mixing old downspouts with new gutters often leads to flow problems and can even void some warranty coverage. Installing a complete system also allows us to properly size everything to work together efficiently.
