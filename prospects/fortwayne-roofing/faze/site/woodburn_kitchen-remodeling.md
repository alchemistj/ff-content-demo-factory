Kitchen Remodel FAQs
How long does a typical kitchen remodel take?

Most kitchen remodeling projects in Fort Wayne take between 4 to 8 weeks from demolition to completion, depending on the scope of work and complexity of your design. Custom cabinets typically take 6 to 8 weeks to arrive after ordering, so we use this time to handle other aspects of preparation and construction. Our team creates a detailed timeline at the start of your project and coordinates all trades efficiently to minimize disruption to your home life while ensuring quality results.

What's a realistic budget for a kitchen remodel?

The cost of a kitchen remodel in Fort Wayne typically ranges from $35,000 to $85,000, with the final price depending on your choices for cabinets, countertops, appliances, and the extent of layout changes. Simple updates like new cabinets and countertops fall on the lower end, while complete renovations with high end materials and structural changes require a larger investment. We work closely with you to understand your priorities and create a design that maximizes value within your budget while achieving the kitchen of your dreams.

Can I use my kitchen during the remodel?

While your kitchen won't be fully functional during the remodel, we help you set up a temporary kitchen space in another room with essentials like a microwave, coffee maker, and refrigerator. We can often keep your existing sink connected until the last possible moment, and we work in phases to minimize disruption to your daily routine. Our team cleans up thoroughly each day and maintains clear pathways through your home to keep the space as livable as possible during construction.

Should I pick my appliances before or after designing the layout?

We strongly recommend selecting your major appliances before finalizing your kitchen design because their specific dimensions will impact cabinet layouts and electrical planning. Knowing exactly which refrigerator, range, and dishwasher you want allows us to create perfect fits for each piece and plan for proper ventilation and utility connections. This approach prevents costly adjustments later and ensures your kitchen functions exactly as intended when complete.
