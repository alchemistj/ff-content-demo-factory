What Does Siding Replacement Cost in Fort Wayne, Indiana? (2026)
One of the most common questions homeowners ask when considering exterior remodeling is:
How much does new siding cost?
The answer depends on several factors, including the material you choose, the size of your home, and the complexity of the installation.
At Faze Construction, we help homeowners throughout Fort Wayne understand the true cost of siding replacement so they can make informed decisions.
Let’s break down what siding costs in Fort Wayne, Indiana in 2026.
Average Siding Replacement Cost in Fort Wayne
For reference, siding projects are typically measured in squares.
One square equals:
100 square feet of siding coverage
For this article, we’ll use a 30-square siding project as our baseline.
That equals approximately:
3,000 square feet of siding coverage
Vinyl Siding Cost
Vinyl siding remains the most popular siding option for homeowners in Fort Wayne because of its affordability, durability, and low maintenance.
Standard Vinyl Siding
Average Cost: $25,000–$36,000
Standard vinyl siding is ideal for homeowners looking for:
Strong value
Low maintenance
Good curb appeal
Affordable exterior upgrades
This is typically the most budget-friendly siding option.
Premium Vinyl Siding Cost
If you’re looking for a more premium look, upgraded vinyl products are available.
This includes:
Board and batten
Insulated vinyl siding
Vinyl shake siding
Premium Vinyl Siding
Average Cost: $60,000–$70,000
These products offer:
Better curb appeal
More design flexibility
Improved energy efficiency
Premium appearance
They cost significantly more but create a much more upscale exterior finish.
Metal Siding Cost
Metal siding is becoming more popular among homeowners wanting a modern, durable exterior.
Metal Siding
Average Cost: $70,000–$80,000
Metal siding offers:
Excellent durability
Strong weather resistance
Modern appearance
Minimal maintenance
While it’s one of the most expensive siding options upfront, it performs extremely well over time.
Wood Siding Cost
Wood siding provides a timeless, natural look that many homeowners love.
Wood Siding
Average Cost: $60,000–$70,000
Wood siding offers:
Natural beauty
Premium curb appeal
Classic architectural appearance
However, wood requires more maintenance than vinyl or metal and typically comes at a higher installation cost.
What Impacts Siding Replacement Cost?
Not every siding project costs the same—even if two homes are similar in size.
Several factors can significantly impact pricing.
1. Tear-Off vs Overlay
One major factor is whether old siding needs to be removed.
Questions include:
Are we tearing off existing siding?
Are we installing over existing material?
Is there hidden damage underneath?
Removing existing siding increases labor and disposal costs.
2. Insulation & Moisture Barriers
Some homeowners choose to install:
House wrap
Insulated backer board
Weather-resistant barriers
These improve:
Energy efficiency
Moisture protection
Overall performance
But they also increase project cost.
3. Metal Wrap Work
Wrap work refers to aluminum trim wrapping around:
Windows
Doors
Fascia
Exterior wood trim
The more wrap work needed, the higher the labor and material cost.
This can be a major variable in siding projects.
4. Wood Rot Repairs
Before siding is installed, the underlying wall structure must be inspected.
Any:
Wood rot
Soft spots
Water damage
must be repaired to create a proper nailable surface.
This is critical for long-term siding performance.
5. Home Height & Complexity
Multi-story homes typically cost more.
Additional factors include:
Number of stories
Number of wall faces
Dormers
Bump-outs
Architectural complexity
More complexity means more labor.
6. Color Selection
Many homeowners don’t realize siding color can affect price.
Darker colors typically cost more than lighter colors.
In many cases, darker colors can add:
10–20% to project cost
This is especially true with premium siding lines.
