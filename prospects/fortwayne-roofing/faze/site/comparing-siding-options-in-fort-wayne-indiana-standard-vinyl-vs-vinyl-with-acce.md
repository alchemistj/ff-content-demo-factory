Comparing Siding Options in Fort Wayne, Indiana: Standard Vinyl vs Vinyl with Accents vs Insulated Siding with Masonry (2026)
One of the biggest questions homeowners ask during a siding consultation is:
Which siding option is right for my home?
The answer depends on your priorities.
Are you looking for:
The most cost-effective option?
Better curb appeal?
Maximum energy efficiency?
Premium long-term performance?
At Faze Construction, we help homeowners throughout Fort Wayne compare siding systems based on cost, appearance, durability, and long-term value.
In this article, we’ll compare three of the most popular siding options for a 30-square siding project.
For reference:
1 square = 100 square feet
30 squares = 3,000 square feet of siding coverage
Let’s break down the options.
Option 1: Standard Vinyl Siding
This is the most common siding option for homeowners in Fort Wayne.
Standard vinyl siding offers a strong balance of:
Affordability
Durability
Low maintenance
Curb appeal
Cost for 30 Squares
$30,000–$45,000
Typical pricing ranges from:
$1,000–$1,500 per square
Benefits of Standard Vinyl Siding
Standard vinyl siding typically includes:
Full siding replacement
Moisture barrier / house wrap
Trim accessories
Long-term exterior protection
Benefits include:
Low maintenance
Strong durability
Improved curb appeal
Moderate energy improvements
Most premium vinyl systems, such as Exterior Portfolio products, include a double lifetime warranty.
That warranty often transfers to the next homeowner, which can improve resale value.
Best For:
Budget-conscious homeowners
Rental properties
Homeowners wanting strong value without premium pricing
This is usually the best option for homeowners looking for a major exterior upgrade at the lowest investment.
Option 2: Standard Vinyl Siding with Masonry or Premium Accents
This option is ideal for homeowners who want stronger curb appeal and higher marketability.
You still get the value of standard vinyl siding—but with premium accent upgrades.
Common accent options include:
Tando stone
Versetta stone
Masonry veneers
Vinyl shake
Board and batten
These accents are typically installed on:
Front elevations
Entryways
Gables
Feature walls
Cost for 30 Squares with Accents
Standard Vinyl + Accents
$35,000–$55,000
Accent packages typically add:
$5,000–$10,000+
depending on scope and materials.
Benefits of Masonry & Accent Systems
One of the biggest advantages of adding accents is increased curb appeal.
Masonry veneers offer:
Premium appearance
Better marketability
Strong weather resistance
Long lifespan
Products like Tando stone and Versetta stone are extremely durable and often last as long—or longer—than the siding itself.
This creates a high-end look without fully upgrading the entire home exterior.
Best For:
Homeowners focused on resale
Homeowners wanting better curb appeal
Families wanting a premium exterior look without going fully premium
This is often the sweet spot for homeowners wanting both value and aesthetics.
Option 3: Insulated Siding with Masonry Accents
This is the premium option.
This system is built for homeowners who want:
Maximum energy efficiency
Premium appearance
Superior impact resistance
Long-term performance
This is more than just a cosmetic upgrade.
It’s a performance upgrade.
Cost for 30 Squares
Insulated Siding + Masonry
$70,000–$85,000
This is the highest investment tier.
But it also offers the strongest overall performance.
Benefits of Insulated Siding
Insulated siding helps improve your home’s thermal barrier.
That means less heat transfer through exterior walls.
Benefits include:
Lower cooling costs
Lower heating costs
Improved indoor comfort
Better wall insulation
Increased impact resistance
This is especially valuable in Fort Wayne where homes experience:
Hot summers
Cold winters
Major seasonal temperature swings
Insulated siding can significantly reduce thermal loss compared to standard siding systems.
Why Add Masonry to Insulated Siding?
Combining insulated siding with masonry accents gives homeowners both:
Performance
and
Curb Appeal
You get:
Energy efficiency
Premium appearance
Strong durability
Higher resale value
This combination gives homeowners the best of both worlds.
Best For:
Premium homeowners
Long-term homeowners
Homeowners prioritizing efficiency and luxury
This is the ideal system for homeowners wanting the best exterior package available.
Which Siding Option Is Best?
The best option depends entirely on your goals.
Choose Standard Vinyl If:
You want affordability and strong long-term value.
Choose Vinyl + Accents If:
You want stronger curb appeal and better resale appeal.
Choose Insulated + Masonry If:
You want premium performance, luxury appearance, and maximum efficiency.
There’s no universal “best” siding.
There’s only the best siding for your home, goals, and budget.
