How Much Does a Shower Remodel Cost in Fort Wayne, Indiana? (2026)
One of the most common questions homeowners ask when considering a bathroom remodel is:
How much does it cost to remodel a shower?
The answer depends on several factors, including:
Existing bathroom condition
Demolition requirements
Water damage repairs
Type of shower system
Materials and upgrades
At Faze Construction, we help homeowners throughout Fort Wayne understand what goes into the true cost of a bathroom remodel.
For this example, we’ll use a common bathroom layout:
Bathroom Size: 8x8
Existing Tub: 3x5 cast iron tub
This is a very common setup in many Fort Wayne homes.
Let’s break down the costs.
Step 1: Demolition & Cast Iron Tub Removal
The first cost to consider is demolition.
Cast iron tubs are extremely heavy and difficult to remove.
Unlike fiberglass tubs, cast iron tubs usually cannot be removed in one piece.
They often need to be broken apart and removed section by section.
This process requires careful demolition to avoid damaging:
Plumbing
Drain systems
Flanges
Surrounding framing
Typical cast iron tub removal costs:
$2,000–$3,000
Step 2: Subfloor & Structural Repairs
Once demolition is complete, the next step is inspecting the bathroom structure underneath.
In many older bathrooms, we commonly find:
Water damage
Rot
Soft subfloors
Mold issues
This is especially common around older tubs.
Before a new shower system can be installed, the substrate must be repaired.
Typical subfloor repair costs:
$1,500–$3,000
At this point, total prep costs typically range from:
$4,000–$5,000+
depending on demolition complexity and repair needs.
Option 1: Fiberglass Shower Unit
Fiberglass shower systems are the most budget-friendly option.
These systems offer a clean, modern appearance at a lower price point.
Benefits include:
Lower cost
Faster installation
Low maintenance
Multiple storage and shelving options
Fiberglass systems can still be highly customizable with:
Shelving
Niches
Seating
Storage upgrades
However, compared to premium systems, fiberglass tends to have shorter lifespan.
Over time, fiberglass can:
Crack
Fade
Wear down
Fiberglass Shower Cost
Shower system cost:
$10,000–$12,000
Prep and demolition:
$4,000–$5,000
Total Project Cost:
$14,000–$17,000
Best For:
Budget-conscious homeowners
Rental properties
Quick bathroom upgrades
Option 2: Tiled Walk-In Shower (Curbed)
A tiled walk-in shower offers a major upgrade in both appearance and durability.
This is where homeowners begin moving into premium bathroom remodeling.
These systems require:
Waterproofing systems
Shower pans
Tile installation
Grout/seal systems
Optional upgrades include:
Bench seating
Custom niches
Premium tile
Upgraded glass
These additions can significantly improve both luxury and functionality.
Curbed Walk-In Shower Cost
Base tiled shower:
Starting around $18,000
Luxury upgrades:
$4,000–$5,000+
Prep and demolition:
$4,000–$5,000
Total Project Cost:
$22,000–$32,000
Best For:
Homeowners wanting luxury
Families wanting durability
Long-term homeowners
Option 3: Curbless Walk-In Shower
Curbless showers are the highest-end shower remodel option.
This is where the shower floor transitions seamlessly into the bathroom floor with no step-up curb.
Curbless showers offer:
Luxury appearance
Easier accessibility
Better aging-in-place functionality
Modern high-end design
These are among the most sought-after bathroom upgrades today.
Why Curbless Showers Cost More
Curbless systems require more labor and preparation.
Floor slope and drainage become extremely important.
If the bathroom is built on a slab foundation, costs increase further because additional concrete work may be required.
Slab prep can add:
$3,000+
The shower system itself also costs more due to:
Waterproofing complexity
Drain systems
Premium tile work
Custom glass
Curbless Shower Cost
Typical total cost:
$35,000–$45,000
depending on:
Tile selection
Drain style
Glass package
Bathroom structure
Best For:
Luxury homeowners
Aging-in-place planning
Homeowners wanting premium design
Which Shower Option Is Best?
The best shower remodel depends on your priorities.
Choose Fiberglass If:
You want affordability and fast installation.
Choose a Curbed Tile Shower If:
You want a balance of luxury, durability, and value.
Choose a Curbless Shower If:
You want the highest-end look, functionality, and long-term performance.
There’s no one-size-fits-all answer.
The best shower depends on your:
Budget
Style
Functional needs
Long-term goals
