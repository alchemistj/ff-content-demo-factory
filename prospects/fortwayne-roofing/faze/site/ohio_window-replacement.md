HOW TO KNOW ITS TIME TO HIRE A WINDOW REPLACEMENT COMPANY
Signs You Need To Replace Your Windows
Drafty or Leaking Windows

Air sneaking through gaps around your windows or water appearing on your windowsills are clear signs your window seals have failed. These issues will only get worse with time and need professional attention.

Foggy Window Panes

When you spot a cloudy haze between your double pane windows, it means the seal has broken and allowed moisture inside. This not only looks unsightly but also means your windows aren't insulating properly anymore.

Difficult to Open/Close Windows

Windows that stick, jam, or won't stay open point to worn out hardware or damaged frames. Struggling with hard to operate windows isn't just frustrating, it can also be a safety concern.

Visible Deterioration

Wood decay, warping, or cracking around your windows signals serious structural issues that need immediate attention. These problems often extend deeper than what's visible on the surface.

Rising Energy Costs

When you notice your heating and cooling bills climbing unexpectedly, inefficient windows might be the culprit. Modern windows can dramatically improve your home's temperature control and reduce those monthly energy costs.

Aging Windows

Just like other parts of your home, windows have a lifespan. If yours are approaching the 20 year mark, upgrading to today's energy efficient models will improve your comfort and save you money in the long run.

Double-Hung Windows

Double-hung windows are a timeless choice that blends beauty and function. With two sashes that slide up and down, they make it easy to control airflow and allow for simple cleaning from inside your home. Their adaptable design complements any space but works especially well in bedrooms and living areas.

Casement Windows

Casement windows open outward with a crank, giving you full top-to-bottom airflow. They’re especially handy in hard-to-reach areas like above kitchen counters or in tight spaces where traditional windows might be difficult to operate. Plus, their design provides clear, beautiful views of your outdoor space.

Picture Windows

If you love natural light and want to highlight your home’s surroundings, picture windows are the way to go. With no moving parts, they provide an uninterrupted view while brightening up your space. They’re perfect for living rooms or any area where you want to maximize outdoor visibility.

Sliding Windows

Want to bring more of the outdoors in? Sliding windows offer wide, unobstructed views and glide open smoothly from side to side. Their sleek, modern look makes them perfect for rooms facing a backyard, garden, or scenic landscape.

Bay & Bow Windows

Looking to add charm and extra space to a room? Bay and bow windows extend outward, creating a cozy alcove perfect for a reading nook, breakfast spot, or extra seating. These windows flood your space with natural light while adding depth and character to your home.

Awning Windows

Need ventilation even when it’s raining? Awning windows open outward from the bottom, creating a shield that keeps rain out while allowing fresh air in. Their smart design makes them ideal for bathrooms, basements, or above other windows to improve airflow throughout your home.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does window replacement take?

While we can install windows in any season, many Ohio homeowners prefer the mild temperatures of spring or fall for their window replacement projects. These seasons offer ideal conditions for the caulk and sealants to cure properly, and you won't have to worry about excessive heat loss or gain during installation. However, our experienced team has special procedures to protect your home when installing windows during summer heat or winter cold, so we can successfully complete your project in any weather.

When is the best time of year to replace my windows?

We typically recommend scheduling your window replacement during spring or fall, when Ohio's weather is most moderate. During installation, there's a brief period where your home is exposed to the elements. In Ohio's hot summers or freezing winters, this means your HVAC system has to work harder. While our team works efficiently, those mild spring and fall temperatures create ideal conditions and help keep your energy bills in check.




However, we understand that window problems don't always wait for perfect weather. Our skilled team has the expertise to ensure proper installation in any conditions. We've handled window replacements through every extreme of Ohio's weather, so you can trust us to get the job done right.

Is it cheaper to replace all your windows at once?

Replacing all your windows at once is typically more cost effective than doing them in phases because you'll save on labor costs and often receive better pricing on materials for larger projects. Many window manufacturers offer bulk pricing discounts, and we can pass these savings directly to you when you replace multiple windows. Additionally, completing all your windows at once means you'll start enjoying the energy savings and comfort benefits immediately rather than dealing with a mix of old and new windows.
