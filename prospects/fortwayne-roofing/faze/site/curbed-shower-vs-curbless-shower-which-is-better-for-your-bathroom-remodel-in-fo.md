Curbed Shower vs Curbless Shower: Which Is Better for Your Bathroom Remodel in Fort Wayne, Indiana? (2026)
One of the biggest decisions homeowners face during a bathroom remodel is choosing between a curbed shower and a curbless shower.
Both are excellent walk-in shower options.
Both offer:
Modern design
Better accessibility
Long-term durability
Luxury appearance
But each option has different advantages depending on your budget, lifestyle, and long-term goals.
At Faze Construction, we help homeowners throughout Fort Wayne determine which shower style is the best fit for their home.
Let’s compare the two.
What Is a Curbed Shower?
A curbed shower includes a raised threshold or lip at the shower entry.
This curb is typically around:
4 inches high
The curb creates a barrier between the shower floor and the bathroom floor.
This helps keep water contained inside the shower area.
Curbed showers can be designed with:
Glass doors
Fixed glass panels
Fully open walk-in layouts
They remain one of the most popular walk-in shower designs.
Benefits of a Curbed Shower
Curbed showers offer several advantages.
1. Lower Cost
A curbed shower is usually the more cost-effective option.
Because the curb helps control water, installation is simpler compared to a curbless system.
This makes curbed showers a great option for homeowners wanting:
Luxury appearance
Strong performance
Lower project cost
2. Better Water Control
This is one of the biggest advantages of a curbed shower.
The curb acts as a containment barrier.
This helps prevent water from escaping into the bathroom.
This is especially helpful if:
Hair clogs develop
Drains slow down
Water backs up
The curb gives added protection against overflow.
For homeowners prioritizing water control, curbed showers are often the better choice.
3. Strong Long-Term Performance
Curbed showers are highly durable and perform extremely well when installed properly.
They provide an excellent balance of:
Functionality
Durability
Cost efficiency
What Is a Curbless Shower?
A curbless shower removes the raised threshold entirely.
The shower floor transitions seamlessly into the bathroom floor.
There is no step-up or curb.
This creates a clean, modern appearance and makes the bathroom feel larger and more open.
Curbless showers have become increasingly popular in luxury bathroom remodeling.
Benefits of a Curbless Shower
Curbless showers offer several unique advantages.
1. Premium Luxury Appearance
Curbless showers create a very high-end look.
They offer:
Sleek design
Modern aesthetics
Open-concept feel
Premium visual appeal
This is one of the most luxurious shower designs available.
2. Better Accessibility
Curbless showers are ideal for accessibility.
Because there is no step or lip, entry and exit are easier.
This makes them excellent for:
Aging in place
Wheelchair accessibility
Limited mobility
Long-term safety
They pair extremely well with:
Benches
Grab bars
Slip-resistant flooring
For homeowners planning for long-term independence, curbless showers are a strong choice.
3. Increased Home Appeal
Curbless showers are highly desirable in today’s remodeling market.
They often add significant appeal to:
Luxury homes
Modern homes
High-end remodels
They can improve marketability and buyer appeal.
Why Curbless Showers Cost More
This is one of the biggest considerations.
Curbless showers usually cost more because installation is more complex.
Water control depends entirely on:
Floor slope
Drain location
Waterproofing
Installation precision
There is very little room for error.
Foundation Type Matters
The cost difference often depends heavily on your foundation type.
Wooden Foundation
Curbless showers are more cost-effective on wood-framed floors.
This is because the floor can often be modified more easily.
Slab Foundation
Curbless showers are much more expensive on concrete slab foundations.
Why?
Because the concrete usually needs to be cut or ground down to recess the shower floor.
This creates proper slope toward the drain.
That additional labor can significantly increase cost.
If curbless design isn’t a must-have, curbed showers are often the more cost-effective option.
Lifespan: Curbed vs Curbless
When installed properly, both systems are extremely durable.
A professionally installed tile shower can last:
50–85 years or more
The key factor isn’t curb vs curbless.
The key factor is installation quality.
That includes:
Waterproofing
Drain setup
Tile installation
Grout systems
This is why proper installation matters so much.
Which Shower Is Right for You?
The best shower depends on your goals.
Choose a Curbed Shower If You Want:
Lower cost
Better water control
Easier installation
Strong long-term value
Choose a Curbless Shower If You Want:
Premium aesthetics
Better accessibility
Luxury design
Aging-in-place functionality
There is no universal “best” option.
There’s only the best option for your home, lifestyle, and budget.
