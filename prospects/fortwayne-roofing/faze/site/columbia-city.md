FAQs

Below are some of the most common questions we receive from Fort Wayne residents.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes, we work on all types of commercial buildings, like apartments, shops, and offices in Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana. We coordinate with property managers and business owners to work during off-hours to avoid disturbing your business.

What time of year is best for roof replacement?

We install roofs all year in Indiana, but many prefer spring or fall for mild weather. Our crews work efficiently in any season, and winter can sometimes mean faster scheduling and better prices.

How do I know if my gutters need to be replaced vs. cleaned?

If your gutters are sagging, pulling away from the house, or leaking a lot, they probably need to be replaced. We can inspect and give you an honest assessment. Sometimes a good cleaning and minor repairs are enough, but if you see rust or seams coming apart, replacement might be better.

What type of siding is best for Indiana weather?

For Indiana’s climate, we recommend fiber cement or vinyl siding. Both handle temperature swings and moisture well. Vinyl is low maintenance and a great value, while fiber cement is very durable and can look like real wood.

Will new windows really help lower my energy bills?

Yes, modern energy-efficient windows can lower your bills, especially if replacing old, single-pane windows. Most customers see a difference in comfort right away and a 15-25% reduction in heating and cooling costs with quality windows and proper insulation.
