Shingle Roof vs. Metal Roof: Which Is Better for Your Home in Fort Wayne, Indiana? (2026)
One of the most common questions homeowners ask when replacing their roof is:
Should I choose asphalt shingles or metal roofing?
The truth is, both are excellent roofing systems—but they serve different types of homeowners.
At Faze Construction, we install both shingle and metal roofing systems throughout Fort Wayne, Indiana, and one of the biggest things we help homeowners determine is which roof makes the most sense for their budget, goals, and long-term plans.
Let’s compare them.
Cost Comparison: Shingle Roof vs Metal Roof
For most homeowners, cost is the first major deciding factor.
For a typical 30-square roof (approximately 3,000 square feet of roofing area) in Fort Wayne:
Asphalt Shingle Roof
Average Cost: $19,000–$25,000
Average project cost: ~$22,000
Metal Roof
Average Cost: $40,000–$50,000+
Average project cost: ~$40,000
That means in many cases, a shingle roof costs roughly 50–75% less upfront than a metal roof.
For homeowners looking for the best balance of affordability and performance, shingles often win.
Why Shingle Roofs Are Often the Better Fit
For many Fort Wayne homeowners, asphalt shingles are the better overall fit.
Here’s why.
1. Lower Upfront Investment
Shingle roofing gives homeowners a high-performing roofing system at a much lower upfront cost.
This makes shingles ideal for:
Budget-conscious homeowners
Rental properties
Investment properties
Homeowners planning to sell within 5–15 years
You can still get excellent performance without spending metal-roof money.
2. Easier and More Affordable Repairs
One of the biggest advantages of shingles is repairability.
If damage occurs from:
Wind
Hail
Storm debris
Individual shingles can often be repaired or replaced without major reconstruction.
Metal roofs can be more difficult and more expensive to repair depending on the panel type and damage location.
This makes shingles more flexible when it comes to long-term maintenance.
3. Strong Storm Protection Options
Many homeowners assume metal automatically offers better protection.
That’s not always true.
Modern asphalt shingles now come in:
Class 3 impact-resistant options
Class 4 impact-resistant options
Class 4 shingles provide some of the highest impact resistance available in the asphalt roofing category.
They also offer:
Better hail resistance
Stronger wind performance
Potential insurance discounts
Longer product life
For Fort Wayne homeowners dealing with hail and wind exposure, upgraded shingles are often an excellent option.
4. Excellent Warranty Options
Today’s premium shingles offer significantly better warranties than many homeowners realize.
Many architectural and impact-resistant shingle systems come with warranties up to 50 years.
Metal roofing typically starts around:
50-year warranty
and can extend to:
Lifetime
Double lifetime
While metal wins on longevity, premium shingles still provide exceptional long-term protection at a much lower price point.
5. Strong Return on Investment (ROI)
Roof replacement is one of the most valuable home improvement projects for resale value.
In Fort Wayne, we typically see:
Shingle Roof ROI
Approximately 70% return on investment
Example:
Spend $22,000
Potential equity return: ~$15,000–$16,000
Metal Roof ROI
Approximately 65% return on investment
Example:
Spend $40,000
Potential equity return: ~$26,000
Metal still provides strong value—but shingles often produce a better ROI percentage because of the lower upfront cost.
This makes shingles especially attractive for homeowners thinking about resale.
When Metal Roofing Makes More Sense
Metal roofing still has major advantages.
It may be the better option if you want:
Maximum durability
Longer lifespan
Minimal maintenance
Premium appearance
Greater energy efficiency
Metal roofing is often ideal for homeowners planning to stay in their home long-term and wanting a “last roof” solution.
Which Roof Is Better for Indiana Weather?
In Fort Wayne, your roof needs to handle:
Heavy rain
High winds
Snow
Ice
Freeze-thaw cycles
Hail storms
Both shingle and metal roofing perform well when installed properly.
The most important factor is not just the product.
It’s the installation.
Even the best roofing material will fail if the roofing system underneath is installed incorrectly.
That includes:
Decking
Moisture barriers
Flashing
Ventilation
Fastening patterns
Installation quality matters just as much as product quality.
Final Verdict: Why Shingles Are the Better Choice for Most Homeowners
While both roofing systems are excellent, asphalt shingles are often the best fit for the average homeowner in Fort Wayne.
Why?
Because they offer:
Lower upfront cost
Easier repairs
Strong warranties
Excellent ROI
Great storm protection
For most homeowners, shingles provide the best overall balance of affordability, performance, and value.
