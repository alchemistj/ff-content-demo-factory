Roof Repair Vs. Replacement
Consider Roof Replacement If:
Your roof is 20+ years old
Your roof has multiple leaks, extensive shingle damage, or other major damage
You are looking to upgrade to more energy-efficient roofing
The cost of needed repairs exceeds 50% of a new roof replacement﻿
Consider Roof Repair If:
Your roof is less than 10 years old & was properly installed
Issues are minimal/isolated, for example, a single leak or limited shingle damage
Spot repairs can effectively fix the issue and extend the life of your roof﻿

WHAT YOUR ROOF IS TELLING YOU
Signs Your Roof Needs To Be Replaced

Keep an eye out for these common warning signs that its time for a full roof replacement:

Curled Shingles

When your shingles start curling up at the edges like old potato chips, it's a clear signal your roof is losing its fight against Indiana weather.

Missing Granules

Noticing dark patches on your roof or black granules in your gutters? That’s your shingles losing their protective coating.

Broken or Missing Shingles

Finding pieces of shingles scattered across your yard after storms is your roof's way of waving a red flag for help.

Sagging Roof

A roof that's starting to sag in spots isn't just an eyesore, it's a serious warning that your home's structure could be at risk.

Water Stains on Interior Walls

Those mysterious water stains creeping across your ceiling are usually your first clue that your roof has sprung a leak somewhere.

Moss or Algae Growth

When moss starts turning your roof into a green carpet, it's trapping moisture that can wreck your shingles.

FAQs
How Much Does a New Roof Cost?

This is usually everyone's first question, and we wish we could give you an exact number right off the bat. The truth is, every roof is different. Factors like size, pitch, material choices, and whether we need to fix any underlying issues all affect the final cost. But here's what we can tell you: we offer flexible financing options to make it manageable, and we'll always give you a detailed, honest estimate after looking at your specific situation.

How long does it take to replace a roof?

Most residential roofs take 1-3 days from start to finish, though larger or more complex projects might need a bit more time. And we get it. Having your roof replaced isn't exactly a quiet process, which is why we work efficiently but never rush. Our crews show up early, work hard, and clean up thoroughly at the end of each day, so you're not living in a construction zone any longer than necessary.

What time of year is best for roof work?

Here's the thing about Indiana weather, it doesn't always cooperate with perfect timing. While spring and fall tend to be ideal with their mild temperatures, we actually install and repair roofs year-round. Our crews are trained to work safely and effectively in all conditions, and sometimes winter installations can mean faster scheduling and better pricing. The best time for your roof work really depends on your situation. If you're seeing warning signs of problems, don't wait for "perfect" weather to get it fixed.
