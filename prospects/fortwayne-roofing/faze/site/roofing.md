What Are The Different Types Of Roofing
Shingle Roofing
Asphalt shingles are the most common type, offering excellent protection and style at a price that won't break the bank. Our selection of architectural and dimensional shingles comes in a variety of colors and styles to match any home's character.
Metal Roofing
Built to last up to 50 years or more, metal roofs offer durability against Indiana's toughest weather while helping cut your energy bills. They're a perfect blend of strength and style that can transform your home's appearance.
Tile Roofing
Tile roofing brings a timeless, elegant look while providing exceptional durability and natural insulation. These distinctive roofs are perfect for homeowners looking to make a bold architectural statement that will last for generations.
Slate Roofing
Nothing says luxury quite like a natural slate roof, with its unique character and century-long lifespan. Each slate tile is a piece of natural art that creates an unmistakable look of sophistication.
Flat Roofing
Modern flat roofs are engineered with advanced materials that ensure perfect waterproofing while creating usable outdoor space. They're ideal for both contemporary home designs and additions like covered porches.
Wood Shake Roofing
Wood shake roofs bring natural warmth and rustic charm that gets even better with age. These hand-split cedar shakes add distinctive texture and character that no other roofing material can match.
Low Slope Roofing
Low slope roofs require special attention to waterproofing and drainage, and we've mastered the techniques to keep them leak-free. Our expert team uses premium materials and proven installation methods to ensure your low slope roof performs perfectly for decades.
Signs Of Roof Damage
Cracked or Curled Shingles
If you spot shingles that are cracked, curled up at the edges, or missing altogether, your roof's protective barrier is compromised. Dark patches mean you're losing granules, which is a clear sign your shingles are past their prime.
Roof Is 20+ Years Old
Even if everything looks okay, a roof that's pushing 20-25 years old is near its end. Getting ahead of problems with an inspection now can save you from dealing with a roofing emergency later.
Water Stains On Ceiling or Walls
Water stains on your ceiling or walls are red flags that your roof might be failing. If your attic feels unusually damp or you spot sunlight peeking through the boards, you've got potential trouble spots that need addressing.
Granules In Gutters
Next time it rains, check your gutters. If they're filled with what looks like black sand, those are granules from your shingles. A few granules are normal, but if you're seeing a lot, your shingles are wearing out faster than they should.
Higher Energy Bills
If your energy bills are rising without explanation, your roof's insulation might be compromised. A damaged or aging roof can let your expensive conditioned air escape right through the top of your house.

Roof Repair Vs. Replacement
Consider Roof Replacement If:
Your roof is 20+ years old
Your roof has multiple leaks, extensive shingle damage, or other major damage
You are looking to upgrade to more energy-efficient roofing
The cost of needed repairs exceeds 50% of a new roof replacement
Consider Roof Repair If:
Your roof is less than 10 years old & was properly installed
Issues are minimal/isolated, for example, a single leak or limited shingle damage
Spot repairs can effectively fix the issue and extend the life of your roof

FAQs
How Much Does a New Roof Cost?
This is everyone's first question, and while we wish we could give you an exact number right now, the truth is that every roof is unique. The cost depends on factors like your roof's size and slope, the materials you choose, and whether there's any underlying damage that needs to be fixed. But here's what we can tell you: we offer flexible financing options to make a quality roof replacement fit your budget, and we'll always give you a detailed, honest estimate after inspecting your specific situation.
How long does it take to replace a roof?
Most residential roof replacements take 1-3 days from start to finish, though larger or more complex roofs might need a bit more time. Our crews work efficiently but never rush. We believe in doing the job right the first time, which includes thorough cleanup at the end of each day. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start, so you know exactly what to expect, and we stick to our schedule unless severe weather forces a delay.
How do I know it’s time to replace my roof?
Your roof will usually give you some pretty clear warning signs when it's ready for replacement. If your shingles are curling, cracking, or missing, you're finding granules in your gutters, or you're seeing water stains on your ceilings, these are all red flags that shouldn't be ignored. Age is another big factor. If your roof is approaching 20-25 years old, it's smart to start planning for a replacement before you run into emergency issues. Give us a call for a free inspection, and we'll give you an honest assessment of whether you need a full replacement or if repairs might buy you a few more years.
