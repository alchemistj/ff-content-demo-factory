Shower Glass for Bathroom Remodels: What Fort Wayne Homeowners Need to Know Before Choosing a Glass Door (2026)
July 28, 2026
Shower Glass Problems Fort Wayne, IN
If you're remodeling your bathroom, one of the last—and most exciting—decisions you'll make is choosing your shower glass. It completely changes the look of a bathroom, making it feel larger, brighter, and more luxurious.
However, not every shower can have every type of shower glass.
This is one of the biggest misconceptions homeowners have during a bathroom remodel, and it's something we encounter regularly at Faze Construction throughout Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana.
Understanding what's possible before your remodel begins can save time, money, and disappointment later.
Types of Shower Glass
Today's homeowners have more options than ever before.
Frameless Hinged Shower Doors
These are the most popular option for luxury bathrooms.
They offer:
Clean modern appearance
Minimal hardware
Easy cleaning
Open feel
Custom sizing
These work exceptionally well on both curbed and curbless showers.
Sliding Shower Doors
Sliding glass doors are ideal when bathroom space is limited.
Benefits include:
Great for smaller bathrooms
No swing clearance required
Modern appearance
Excellent water containment
Because these doors slide on tracks, they require continuous mounting surfaces across the opening.
Neo-Angle Shower Glass
Neo-angle enclosures are commonly used in corner showers.
These maximize floor space while still providing:
Full glass enclosure
Elegant appearance
Better use of small bathrooms
Clear vs. Frosted Glass
Homeowners also choose between different glass finishes.
Clear Glass
Bright appearance
Makes bathrooms feel larger
Shows off decorative tile work
Frosted Glass
Increased privacy
Softens visibility
Popular in shared bathrooms
The Most Common Shower Glass Mistake
Recently we remodeled a bathroom for a homeowner in Fort Wayne who wanted something very specific.
They wanted an ADA-friendly walk-in soaking tub with a full custom tile surround extending all the way to the ceiling.
We were able to:
Remove the existing fiberglass surround
Eliminate an overhead bulkhead
Relocate plumbing
Install a recessed shampoo niche
Tile the shower from floor to ceiling
The remodel turned out beautifully.
However...
The homeowner also wanted a custom glass enclosure.
Unfortunately, this is one situation where shower glass simply isn't practical.
Why Shower Glass Doesn't Work with Walk-In Tubs
A walk-in tub is very different from a traditional shower.
Instead of stepping over a curb, the homeowner opens a waterproof door built directly into the tub itself.
Because of this design, there isn't a continuous structure for shower glass to mount properly.
Glass enclosures require secure attachment points.
Typically those include:
Tile walls
Mortar
Continuous framing
Solid fiberglass surrounds
Sliding doors require a continuous top and bottom track.
Hinged doors require a structurally secure mounting surface capable of supporting the weight of the glass.
A walk-in tub interrupts that entire system because the tub itself contains a functioning waterproof door.
Adding another glass door above it would create two independent doors occupying the same opening.
Technically, you could install partial glass panels, but they would provide very little benefit while making access significantly more difficult.
In most situations, it simply isn't the right design.
When Shower Glass Works Best
Custom shower glass performs exceptionally well in:
Traditional curbed showers
Curbless walk-in showers
Frameless shower systems
Full tile shower surrounds
Luxury bathroom remodels
These installations provide the continuous mounting surfaces needed for safe, durable operation while also creating the clean, modern appearance homeowners love.
Every Bathroom Is Different
One of the biggest mistakes homeowners make is assuming every Pinterest photo can be recreated in every bathroom.
The reality is that every project has unique structural considerations.
Before recommending shower glass, we evaluate:
Shower layout
Accessibility needs
Door swing clearances
Wall construction
Tile layout
Waterproofing system
Mounting locations
Long-term durability
Sometimes a different solution actually creates a better bathroom.
Our goal isn't just to install products—it's to design a bathroom that functions beautifully for years to come.
