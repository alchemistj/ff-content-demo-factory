Why Atlas Pinnacle Is My Favorite Roofing Shingle for Fort Wayne Homeowners
By Faze Construction | Fort Wayne Roofing Contractor
If someone asked me:
"What's the best roofing shingle for the money in Fort Wayne?"
My answer is almost always the same:
Atlas Pinnacle.
Not because it's the cheapest.
Not because it's the most expensive.
But because I believe it delivers the best balance of price, performance, warranty, appearance, algae resistance, and long-term value available to homeowners throughout Fort Wayne, New Haven, Huntertown, Leo-Cedarville, Auburn, Columbia City, Huntington, Decatur, Bluffton, Roanoke, Grabill, Ossian, Kendallville, Warsaw, Angola, and Northeast Indiana.
After installing hundreds of roofing systems, Atlas Pinnacle has become one of my favorite shingles to recommend for homeowners who want the most value for every dollar they spend.
Comparing Atlas's Three Main Asphalt Shingles
Atlas offers three primary architectural shingles you'll commonly find in Northeast Indiana.
• Atlas ProLam
• Atlas Pinnacle
• Atlas StormMaster
Each serves a different homeowner.
The question is:
Which one gives you the best return on investment?
Let's compare them.
Atlas ProLam
Best for Tight Budgets
Atlas ProLam is Atlas's entry-level architectural shingle.
It's designed for homeowners looking for dependable protection while keeping the initial investment lower.
Typical features include:
Limited Lifetime Warranty
Approximately 15-year wind warranty
10-year algae resistance
Standard architectural appearance
Around 110 MPH wind rating
If your primary concern is affordability, ProLam is a solid option.
But in my opinion, it's missing several features that dramatically increase long-term value.
Atlas Pinnacle
The Best Bang for Your Buck
This is where Atlas really shines.
Atlas Pinnacle takes everything homeowners like about ProLam and improves nearly every category without a dramatic jump in price.
Typical benefits include:
Limited Lifetime Warranty
Extended manufacturer warranty options through certified installers
Up to 50-year algae protection
Up to 130 MPH wind rating (depending on installation method and warranty)
Class 3 impact resistance
Scotchgard™ Protector to help resist black streaks caused by algae
Larger selection of colors
Better overall curb appeal
The biggest improvement isn't necessarily the wind rating.
It's the algae protection.
Atlas incorporates copper-containing granules that help prevent the black streaking commonly seen on roofs throughout Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana.
That means your roof looks newer longer.
Atlas StormMaster
Maximum Impact Protection
StormMaster is Atlas's premium architectural shingle.
It includes many of the same features as Pinnacle while adding one major upgrade:
Class 4 Impact Resistance
This means it performs significantly better against hail impacts than a standard Class 3 shingle.
StormMaster also includes:
Limited Lifetime Warranty
50-year algae resistance
High wind performance
Premium impact protection
Enhanced durability for hail-prone areas
If hail resistance is your highest priority, StormMaster is an excellent choice.
Why I Recommend Atlas Pinnacle Most Often
Here's where the value equation really changes.
Let's use simple numbers.
If a ProLam roof costs 100%, then:
Atlas Pinnacle generally costs about 10–15% more
Atlas StormMaster may cost roughly 20–30% more than Pinnacle, depending on the project
For that relatively small increase over ProLam, Pinnacle gives homeowners:
✓ Much better algae protection
✓ Better warranty options
✓ Better curb appeal
✓ Higher wind performance
✓ Longer expected service life
✓ Better resale appeal
In my experience, that's one of the best investments a homeowner can make.
Is StormMaster Worth the Extra Cost?
Sometimes.
Sometimes not.
If you live in an area that regularly experiences severe hail—or you specifically want the added protection of a Class 4 roof—StormMaster can absolutely make sense.
But if your goal is balancing:
Cost
Warranty
Appearance
Longevity
Performance
it's hard to beat Atlas Pinnacle.
That's why it's one of our most frequently installed shingles.
Where Can You Buy Atlas Shingles in Fort Wayne?
Atlas shingles are available through several professional roofing distributors serving Northeast Indiana.
These include suppliers such as:
Modern Builders Supply (MBS)
ABC Supply
Reese Wholesale
Many retail home improvement stores may also carry Atlas products.
However, there's an important difference.
Buying shingles yourself is not the same as receiving a certified roofing system installation.
Many enhanced manufacturer warranty options require installation by a contractor who meets Atlas's certification requirements and follows the approved installation system.
Always ask your contractor:
Which Atlas shingle are you installing?
Which distributor are you purchasing from?
Are you certified to offer Atlas enhanced warranty programs?
Will my warranty be registered after installation?
Why Installation Matters More Than the Shingle
Even the best shingle can fail if it's installed incorrectly.
A roofing system is more than shingles.
It includes:
Ice and water barrier
Synthetic underlayment
Flashing
Ridge ventilation
Starter shingles
Ridge caps
Fasteners
Pipe boots
Drip edge
Proper attic ventilation
When these components work together according to manufacturer specifications, homeowners receive the protection they're paying for.
