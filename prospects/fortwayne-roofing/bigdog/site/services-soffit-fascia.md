Over time, Fort Wayne's weather takes a toll on soffit and fascia. Rain, snow, ice, and UV exposure cause wood fascia to rot, aluminum soffit panels to loosen, and paint to peel. Damaged fascia can't support gutters properly, leading to sagging and overflow. Damaged soffit panels allow moisture and pests into your attic space.
Big Dog Roofing repairs and replaces soffit and fascia as standalone services or as part of a larger roofing or gutter project. We offer both aluminum and vinyl options that resist rot, require minimal maintenance, and look clean for decades. Vented soffit panels ensure proper airflow through your attic, which is essential for preventing moisture buildup and ice dams.
We frequently bundle soffit and fascia work with roof replacements and gutter installations for the most efficient and cost-effective approach. When everything is done together, you get a completely refreshed roof edge that looks great and performs even better.

Most soffit and fascia repairs start with a water movement problem. Fort Wayne homes get heavy rain, freeze-thaw cycles, ice at the eaves, and wind-driven storms that can expose weak spots around gutters, drip edge, trim, and attic ventilation.
