Signs Your Gutters Are Hurting the Roof
Gutter problems do not stay in the gutter. Overflow and bad drainage can rot fascia boards, push water behind shingles, flood landscaping, and send water toward the foundation. During roof and gutter inspections, we look for the symptoms that usually show up before a bigger repair bill.
Overflow marks
Black streaks, washed-out mulch, and stained siding usually mean water is jumping the gutter instead of moving through the downspout.
Sagging sections
Sagging gutters hold water, stress fasteners, and pull against fascia. This often gets worse after snow and ice loads.
Rot behind the gutter
Soft fascia, peeling paint, or gaps behind the gutter can signal long-term water intrusion that should be fixed before new gutters go on.
