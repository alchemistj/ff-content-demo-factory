Roofing Challenges in Roanoke
Indiana's climate creates specific roofing challenges for Roanoke homeowners. Here's what we see most often — and how Big Dog Roofing addresses each one.
Wind and Storm Exposure
Roanoke's position in the open terrain of southern Allen and northern Huntington counties means homes face significant wind exposure during severe thunderstorms. High winds can lift and tear shingles, especially on older installations that used fewer fasteners than modern standards require. We install every roof with six-nail patterns and high-wind-rated materials.
Aging Homes with Original Roofing
Many established Roanoke homes still have their original roofing systems from the 1970s and 1980s. These roofs have often exceeded their expected lifespan and may have hidden issues like rotted decking or inadequate ventilation. Our free inspections identify exactly what's needed so you can make an informed decision about repair versus replacement.
Hail Damage
Indiana's hail season can affect Roanoke multiple times per year. Even moderate hail can crack and bruise shingles in ways that aren't visible from the ground but significantly reduce your roof's protective capability. We offer free post-storm inspections, document visible damage, and can prepare a contractor estimate or meet with an adjuster when requested. Coverage decisions remain with the carrier under the policy.
Seasonal Temperature Swings
Roanoke experiences temperature swings of 100+ degrees between summer highs and winter lows. This thermal cycling causes roofing materials to expand and contract repeatedly, which can loosen fasteners and crack aging shingles over time. Quality materials and proper installation techniques mitigate this stress.
