LOCAL GUIDE
Metal roofing can be a strong option for Huntertown homeowners who want long service life, clean curb appeal, and strong storm performance. It is not the right fit for every roof or budget, but it deserves a real comparison when you are planning a replacement.
By Big Dog Roofing · Published April 30, 2026 · Updated August 9, 2026
Where metal roofing performs well
Metal can make sense for homeowners planning to stay long term, roofs with certain low-slope sections, homes exposed to wind or trees, and owners who want a roof with less frequent replacement over time.
How it compares with architectural shingles
Architectural shingles remain the practical choice for many Huntertown homes because they cost less upfront and perform well when installed correctly. Metal costs more, but can deliver a longer lifespan and different maintenance profile.
What to ask before choosing metal
Ask about panel type, fastening method, underlayment, ventilation, trim details, noise expectations, snow shedding, and how transitions will be flashed. The details matter as much as the material.
