Common Roofing Issues for Ossian Homeowners
Even newer homes aren't immune to Indiana weather. Here are the challenges we see most in the Ossian area.
Hail Damage on Newer Shingles
Many of Ossian's homes were built in the last 20 to 30 years with standard architectural shingles that are now entering the middle of their lifespan. These roofs are especially vulnerable to hail — a single severe storm can knock years off a roof's remaining life by cracking shingles and dislodging protective granules. After a hailstorm, the damage often isn't visible from the ground, which is why a professional inspection matters. Big Dog Roofing provides free post-storm inspections, documents visible damage, and can prepare a contractor estimate or meet with an adjuster when requested. Coverage decisions remain with the carrier under the policy.
Wind Exposure on Rural Properties
The farmhouses and rural homes surrounding Ossian sit in open Wells County terrain with minimal wind protection. Straight-line winds during severe storms can lift and tear shingles, especially along ridges and edges. We install high-wind-rated shingles with enhanced nailing patterns on all Ossian area roofs, providing extra protection where the landscape doesn't offer any natural shelter from the elements.
Farmhouse & Outbuilding Roofing
The rural properties around Ossian often include not just a main house but also barns, workshops, and outbuildings that need roofing attention. Older metal roofs on these structures develop issues at fasteners and seams, while some older farmhouses still have original roofing that needs complete replacement. Big Dog Roofing handles both residential and agricultural roofing projects, including standing-seam metal installation for properties that want maximum longevity.
Builder-Grade Material Limitations
Some of the newer homes in Ossian's subdivisions were built with builder-grade roofing materials that prioritize cost over performance. These entry-level shingles often show premature wear — cracking, granule loss, and poor wind performance — within 15 to 20 years. When it's time to replace, we upgrade Ossian homeowners to premium architectural shingles that offer significantly better protection, longer warranties, and improved curb appeal.
