Storm Damage Roof Repair in Fort Wayne, IN
Fort Wayne storm damage roof repair for hail, wind, active leaks, missing shingles, and insurance claim documentation. We inspect the damage, tarp when needed, and explain the next step clearly.

After a major storm, thousands of Fort Wayne homeowners discover roof damage — some obvious like missing shingles, and some hidden like hail bruising that weakens shingles from within. Either way, untreated storm damage leads to leaks, mold, structural damage, and dramatically shorter roof lifespan. Getting a professional inspection immediately after a storm is critical.
Big Dog Roofing provides free, no-obligation storm damage inspections. We document visible conditions with high-resolution photos and videos from ground level and on the roof so you have a clear record to share with your insurer. Your insurance carrier decides coverage, scope, and payment under your policy.
We can explain our inspection findings, prepare a roofing estimate, and meet the adjuster when requested. We do not interpret coverage or promise a claim outcome; your deductible, exclusions, depreciation, and approved scope depend on your policy and the carrier’s decision.

What Our Storm Inspection Documents
A good storm inspection is more than a quick look from the driveway. We photograph the slopes, gutters, vents, ridge caps, soft metals, and interior leak signs so you have a clear record before the damage gets worse or the insurance deadline gets closer.
Hail impact evidence
We look for bruised shingles, granule loss, dented soft metals, and collateral damage that helps confirm storm activity.
Wind damage patterns
Creased, lifted, missing, or unsealed shingles can lead to leaks even when the roof still looks mostly intact from the street.
Claim-ready photos
You get practical documentation for the adjuster, plus a plain-English explanation of whether repair, replacement, or monitoring makes sense.
