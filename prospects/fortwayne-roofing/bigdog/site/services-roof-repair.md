Common Roof Repairs We See Around Fort Wayne
Most roof leaks begin at weak points, not in the middle of a perfect shingle field. That is why our repair inspection focuses on the detail work that usually fails first: pipe boots, chimney flashing, wall flashing, valleys, ridge caps, and fasteners around roof penetrations.
Pipe boot leaks
Rubber pipe boots dry out and crack over time. If caught early, this is usually a targeted repair instead of a full roof replacement.
Flashing failures
Chimneys, skylights, sidewalls, and valleys need watertight flashing. A small gap can create ceiling stains long before the roof looks bad from the ground.
Wind-lifted shingles
Fort Wayne wind can break the shingle seal and expose nails. We check nearby shingles too so the repair does not stop one row short.
