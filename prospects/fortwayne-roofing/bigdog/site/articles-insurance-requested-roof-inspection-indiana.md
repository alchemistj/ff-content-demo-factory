What to Do When Your Insurer Requests a Roof Inspection
An underwriting or renewal inspection is not the same as a storm claim. Read the notice, document the roof, answer the request, and keep every decision in writing.
By Big Dog Roofing · Published August 26, 2026

An insurance company may ask for roof photographs, an inspection, proof of repairs, or replacement information while underwriting a new policy or reviewing a renewal. That is different from filing a storm-damage claim. Start by reading the actual notice: who sent it, what condition or document is requested, the response deadline, and what happens if the request is not satisfied.
A roofer can document visible conditions and provide a repair or replacement scope. The insurer decides its underwriting and policy action. Do not accept a promise that one inspection or one repair will guarantee coverage or renewal. Indiana Department of Insurance guidance says the policy controls and warns that an insurer is not required to renew a policy at the end of its term, subject to applicable rules.

Identify Which Insurance Process You Are In
New-business underwriting, a renewal review, a post-inspection repair request, cancellation, nonrenewal, and a property claim are different processes. The notice should identify the action and effective dates. If it is unclear, call the number on the policy or official carrier website and request a written explanation.
Do not open a damage claim merely because underwriting wants current roof information. A claim should be based on a covered event you intend to report under the policy, not used as a substitute for a maintenance inspection. Ask the carrier how it wants the requested underwriting evidence delivered.
Order a Report That Answers the Notice
Give the inspector a copy of the request with personal and policy information redacted as appropriate. The report should label roof planes, material, approximate age if known, layers, shingles, flashing, penetrations, valleys, ventilation, repairs, interior symptoms, and any unsafe or inaccessible areas.
If a condition can be repaired, the scope should say where it is, what work is proposed, and what is not included. If replacement is recommended, ask for the condition evidence behind that recommendation. A generic estimate without photographs may not answer the insurer's question.
Document Repairs From Before to After
Photograph the condition before work, the repair in progress when practical, and the completed area. Keep the signed scope, material information, paid invoice, permit record if applicable, and contractor contact information. Ask the insurer exactly which documents it accepts as proof.
Do not hide, crop, or mislabel a condition. If the requested repair reveals a larger issue, stop and update the scope. Send a factual package and retain the delivery confirmation.
Get the Carrier's Answer in Writing
After submitting, ask the insurer to confirm receipt and whether the requirement is satisfied. Record the date, representative, reference number, and any remaining item. If an important statement is made by phone, summarize it in a secure written message and ask the carrier to correct anything inaccurate.
For questions about Indiana insurance rights or a notice you cannot resolve with the carrier, the Indiana Department of Insurance publishes consumer guidance and contact options. It can explain regulatory processes; it does not replace your policy or give a contractor authority to interpret coverage.
A Five-Step Response Plan
Read the whole notice. Mark the action, reason, evidence requested, response date, and effective date.
Verify the sender. Use policy documents or the carrier's official contact channel before sharing information.
Match the inspection to the request. Get labeled evidence, not a sales-only estimate.
Complete and document agreed work. Keep before-and-after photos, invoices, and required permits.
Confirm the outcome. Ask the carrier in writing whether anything else is required.
Use Property Evidence, Not Northwest-Side Assumptions
North and west Fort Wayne ZIP areas contain housing from very different construction eras. ACS estimates for ZCTA 46808 lean much older than 46845, but an insurer's question applies to the specific property and policy. A ZIP code cannot establish a roof's age, condition, maintenance history, or insurability.
If the notice cites an inspection photo, ask for the location and finding. Roof edges, trees, additions, and complex slopes can look different from a street image than they do during a close inspection. Respond with address-specific, date-labeled evidence.
What the Inspection Should Show
For responding to an insurer's roof inspection request, a useful report should locate and photograph the conditions that affect the decision instead of offering only a repair-or-replace conclusion.
the specific condition named in the notice
roof material, layers, and supported age range
each roof plane and any inaccessible area
shingles, seams, or panels
flashing, penetrations, and roof edges
repairs and prior patches
interior or attic moisture evidence
dated photographs and a clear recommendation
Mistakes That Make the Problem Harder
treating an underwriting request as an automatic claim
missing the notice or policy effective date
sending an unlabeled sales estimate as the only evidence
accepting a contractor's guarantee about insurer action
Frequently Asked Questions
Is an insurance-requested inspection the same as a claim inspection?
No. It may be part of underwriting or renewal rather than a reported loss. Read the notice and confirm the process with the insurer.
Can a roofer certify that my insurer will renew the policy?
No. A roofer can document roof conditions and work. The insurer makes underwriting and renewal decisions under the policy and applicable rules.
What if I cannot meet the deadline?
Contact the insurer immediately, explain the specific issue, and ask in writing whether an extension or alternate documentation is available. Do not assume one will be granted.
Should I send the full roofing estimate?
Send what the insurer requests through its approved channel. Protect unnecessary personal information and retain an exact copy and delivery confirmation.
