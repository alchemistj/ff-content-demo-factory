What Affects Roof Replacement Cost in Fort Wayne?
Several factors determine where your project falls within these ranges:
Roof size. Roofing is priced per square (100 square feet). A typical Fort Wayne ranch home is 15 to 25 squares. Larger two-story homes can be 25 to 40+ squares.
Roof pitch and complexity. Steeper roofs require more safety equipment and labor time. Complex roofs with dormers, valleys, hips, and multiple penetrations cost more than simple gable roofs.
Material choice. Standard 3-tab shingles cost less but only last 15 to 20 years. Architectural shingles (what we recommend) last 25 to 30 years and carry better warranties. Designer and premium shingles add cost but offer enhanced aesthetics and durability.
Decking condition. When we tear off the old roof, we inspect every square inch of decking. Rotted or damaged plywood needs to be replaced before new roofing goes on. Most homes need some decking work — we document it with photos so you see what you're paying for.
Layers to remove. If your home has two layers of old shingles (the maximum allowed), the tear-off takes longer and generates more debris, increasing labor and disposal costs.
Ventilation and accessories. Upgrading ridge vents, adding soffit vents, or installing new pipe boots and flashing adds to the total but significantly extends your new roof's performance and lifespan.
How to Get the Best Value on Your Fort Wayne Roof Replacement
A roof replacement is a major investment, and getting the best value means more than just finding the lowest price. Here's what smart Fort Wayne homeowners consider:
Get multiple quotes — but compare apples to apples. Make sure each quote includes the same scope: full tear-off, quality underlayment, ice and water shield, proper flashing, and equivalent-grade shingles. A quote that's significantly lower is usually cutting corners somewhere.
Ask about warranties. Material warranties cover the shingles themselves. Workmanship warranties cover the installation. Big Dog Roofing provides a 15-year craftsmanship warranty on every project in addition to the manufacturer's material warranty.
Verify the crew. Ask who will supervise the work, how the crew is trained, and who is accountable after completion. Our crews are trained and supervised to the Big Dog standard on every job.
Check reviews and references. Online reviews tell you what to expect. Read homeowner reviews →
