NORTH ANTHONY AREA, FORT WAYNE
Storm Damage Claim to Full Replacement in 9 Days
After a spring hail event, the homeowner had granule loss on multiple slopes, damaged ridge caps, and active water staining in the attic.
Scope: Full tear-off, 29-square GAF architectural shingle installation, upgraded ridge ventilation, flashing replacement
Insurance: Claim approved with supplement for code-required ventilation updates
Outcome: Completed in one production day with full site cleanup and final walkthrough photos

LEO-CEDARVILLE, IN
Leak Source Isolation and Targeted Repair Plan
Homeowner reported recurring leaks near a chimney chase despite prior patch work from another contractor.
Scope: Flashing rebuild, deck patch at affected area, shingle and underlayment replacement on damaged slope
Why this mattered: Repair addressed root cause instead of applying another short-term sealant patch
Outcome: Leak-free through subsequent heavy rain events and winter freeze-thaw cycle
