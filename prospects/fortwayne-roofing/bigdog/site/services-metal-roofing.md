Big Dog Roofing installs both standing seam and corrugated metal roofing systems for homes across Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana. Standing seam metal roofs feature concealed fasteners and clean vertical lines that give your home a modern, high-end look. Metal roofing can be a strong fit for Fort Wayne's snow, wind, rain, and temperature swings. Standing Seam vs. Corrugated Metal Roofing
Most Fort Wayne homeowners compare two metal options: standing seam and corrugated or exposed-fastener panels. Both can be good roofs when installed correctly, but they fit different homes, budgets, and long-term goals.
Standing seam
Concealed fasteners, clean vertical lines, strong weather performance, and the longest service life. This is the premium choice for homeowners who want the most durable metal system.
Corrugated metal
A more accessible metal option with exposed fasteners and a traditional panel look. It still needs correct flashing, underlayment, and fastener placement to perform in Indiana weather.
