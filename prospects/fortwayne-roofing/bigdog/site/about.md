OUR STORY
Where Hard Work Meets Heart
Welcome to Big Dog Roofing — where craftsmanship, honesty, and heart come together to protect the place you call home.
We are a locally rooted roofing company — specializing in roof replacement, repairs, and gutter services — proudly based out of Fort Wayne's historic Electric Works campus. From our home inside this revitalized landmark, we carry forward a tradition of hard work, innovation, and community — values that guide us in every project we take on.
Our team works with homeowners on roofing, siding, gutters and gutter guards across Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana. We've grown because neighbors trusted us to do the right thing, and that trust means everything to us.
Every project — big or small — gets treated like it's being done for family. Because around here, being local matters. We're not a fly-in franchise or a storm-chasing outfit. We're part of the Fort Wayne community, and the people we serve are the same people we shop beside, cheer with, and raise our families around.
When you choose Big Dog Roofing, you're choosing a team that shows up, follows through, and stands behind every shingle, screw, and handshake.
