Common Roofing Issues for Bluffton Homeowners
Century-old homes, river-corridor moisture, and Indiana storms create a unique set of roofing challenges in Bluffton.
Century-Old Roof Structures
Bluffton has an unusually high number of homes built before 1930, particularly around the downtown core. These older structures often have original roof framing with smaller rafters, skip sheathing designed for wood shakes, and decades of patched repairs layered on top of each other. When we replace a roof on one of these Bluffton homes, we do a thorough structural evaluation first — checking rafters for sagging, replacing rotted decking, and ensuring the structure can properly support modern roofing materials.
Wabash River Humidity
Bluffton sits along the Wabash River, and homes near the river corridor experience elevated humidity levels that accelerate roof deterioration. Moss, algae, and lichen thrive in these damp conditions, trapping moisture against shingle surfaces and shortening their lifespan. We recommend algae-resistant shingles for Bluffton homes near the river and can treat existing growth to extend your current roof's life when replacement isn't yet necessary.
Severe Weather Exposure
Wells County's flat terrain provides no natural protection from the severe storms that sweep across Indiana every spring and summer. Hail damage is a regular concern, and the straight-line winds that accompany derecho events can tear shingles from even well-maintained roofs. Big Dog Roofing provides free inspections, documents visible damage, and can prepare a contractor estimate or meet with an adjuster when requested. Coverage and settlement decisions remain with the carrier under the policy.
Inadequate Attic Ventilation
Many older Bluffton homes lack proper ridge-and-soffit ventilation systems. Without balanced airflow, attics trap heat in summer (baking shingles from below) and moisture in winter (promoting ice dams and condensation damage). We address ventilation as part of every roof replacement — adding ridge vents, soffit intake, and baffles to create the airflow your roof needs to perform at its best.
