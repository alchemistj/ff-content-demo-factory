When to Call for Emergency Roof Repair
Not every roofing issue is an emergency, but these situations require immediate professional attention:
Active water intrusion. If water is coming through your ceiling or walls during or after a storm, your roof has a breach that needs immediate containment. Turn off electricity in the affected area, move valuables away from the leak, and call us.
Fallen tree or large debris. A tree limb or other heavy debris that has punctured or displaced your roofing materials creates an opening that will cause rapid, escalating damage with every passing hour.
Missing large sections of roofing. If a storm has stripped away a significant area of shingles or flashing, your underlayment and decking are exposed to the elements. Temporary tarping is critical to prevent water damage to your home's interior.
Structural sagging or collapse. If your roofline is visibly sagging or you notice bouncing or softness when walking in the attic, there may be a structural issue that requires immediate assessment for safety.
After a confirmed hail or tornado event. Even if you do not see obvious damage, a prompt professional inspection can document the roof's condition and identify damage before it worsens. Follow the notice and mitigation requirements in your own policy.

Insurance Claims for Emergency Repairs
Emergency roof damage caused by wind, hail, or a fallen tree may qualify for coverage, depending on the cause, policy terms, exclusions, and carrier decision. Wear, maintenance, and pre-existing damage are commonly treated differently.
Big Dog Roofing can inspect the roof, photograph observed damage, provide a written repair estimate, and explain the construction scope to you or an adjuster. We do not interpret your policy or decide coverage, pricing allowances, or settlement amounts; those decisions belong to you and your insurer.
Before filing, review your policy and deductible and contact your carrier with coverage questions. We will give you a clear roofing assessment so you can make an informed decision.
