The Problem
The home had already been patched once, but water was still showing up near the chimney chase during hard rain. The previous work treated symptoms, not the leak path.
Our inspection found aging flashing details, compromised seal lines, and a localized area of deck damage that needed to be opened up and rebuilt correctly.
The Scope
Remove and rebuild chimney flashing details
Patch damaged decking at the affected area
Replace underlayment and shingles on the impacted slope
Water-test and review with the homeowner before closeout
Targeted repair work focused on the actual leak source, not another temporary patch.
Repair plan centered on preserving the roof where possible while fixing the vulnerable section correctly.
Why This Project Matters
Not every leak means the homeowner needs a full replacement. In this case, the higher-value move was a focused repair that dealt with the flashing assembly and damaged deck area directly.
The result: a smaller project, a solved problem, and a homeowner who did not have to buy a roof they did not need yet.
