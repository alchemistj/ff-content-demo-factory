The Situation
The homeowner noticed bruising, ridge-cap damage, and granule loss after a hail event moved through Fort Wayne. There were also early signs of attic staining, which turned a routine inspection into a time-sensitive claim situation.
The most important first step was documentation. We photographed the affected slopes, soft metals, ridge details, and ventilation conditions so the adjuster conversation started with evidence instead of guesswork.
The Scope
Full tear-off and 29-square replacement using GAF architectural shingles
New underlayment, drip edge, flashing, and ridge components
Supplement approval for code-required ventilation updates
One production day plus final photo walkthrough and cleanup
Job-site documentation used during claim review and final production planning.
Final aerial confirmation after installation, cleanup, and homeowner walkthrough.
Why This Project Matters
This is the kind of project where speed helps, but clean communication helps even more. The homeowner needed a local contractor who could document the damage well, meet the adjuster, catch the ventilation issue, and still execute the install cleanly once the claim moved.
That combination is what turns a stressful storm claim into a straightforward roofing project.
