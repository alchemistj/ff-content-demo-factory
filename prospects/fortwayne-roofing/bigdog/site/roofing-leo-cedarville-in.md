Roofing Challenges in Leo-Cedarville
Indiana's climate creates specific roofing challenges for Leo-Cedarville homeowners. Here's what we see most often — and how Big Dog Roofing addresses each one.
Wind Exposure in Open Terrain
Leo-Cedarville sits in a largely open agricultural landscape where straight-line winds from summer thunderstorms can reach damaging speeds with little obstruction. Homes along the eastern edge of town and rural properties along Schwartz Road are especially vulnerable. We install high-wind-rated shingles with six-nail patterns on every Leo-Cedarville project for maximum uplift resistance.
Hail Damage from Spring and Summer Storms
Northeast Indiana's severe storm season regularly delivers hail events that bruise and crack asphalt shingles. Hail damage is often invisible from the ground but can shorten a roof's service life. After major storms, Big Dog Roofing offers free damage inspections, documents visible conditions, and can prepare a contractor estimate or meet with an adjuster when requested. New Construction Warranty Transitions
Many newer homes in Leo-Cedarville were built during the subdivision boom of the last decade. As builder warranties expire, homeowners need a trusted local roofer for inspections, maintenance, and repairs. We understand how these homes are built and can identify early signs of wear before they become costly problems.
Freeze-Thaw Cycles and Ice Dams
Indiana winters put roofs through repeated freeze-thaw stress. Ice dams form when attic heat melts snow on the upper roof and it refreezes at the eaves, forcing water under shingles. Proper ventilation and ice-and-water shield underlayment are essential — both are standard in every Big Dog Roofing installation.
