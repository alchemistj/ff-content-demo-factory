A roof replacement involves a complete tear-off of your existing roofing materials down to the deck, inspection and repair of the underlying decking, installation of new underlayment, drip edge, flashing, ice and water shield, and your chosen roofing material. We don't cut corners — every component matters.
Fort Wayne's climate puts serious demands on roofing systems. Between severe thunderstorms, hail events, heavy snow loads, and freeze-thaw cycles, a properly installed roof is your home's most important line of defense. That's why we use premium materials like GAF Timberline HDZ shingles, engineered specifically for high-wind markets like ours.
Most Fort Wayne roof replacements cost between $7,000 and $22,000 for asphalt shingles, depending on roof size, pitch, complexity, and materials. We explain the proposed scope, materials, and any allowances in your written estimate. If hidden damage changes the scope, we discuss it before additional work.

When Replacement Makes More Sense Than Another Repair
A repair is the right call when the damage is isolated and the roof still has useful life left. Replacement starts to make more financial sense when repairs keep stacking up, shingles are brittle across multiple slopes, or the roof is already near the end of its service life.
Age and wear
Fort Wayne asphalt roofs often need closer attention after 15 years because wind, hail, humidity, and freeze-thaw cycles speed up granule loss and seal failure.
Multiple leak points
A single pipe boot leak can be repaired. Water stains in different rooms, repeated shingle blow-offs, or widespread flashing failure usually point to a bigger system problem.
Storm or insurance work
When hail or wind affects several slopes, we document the damage and help you compare repair, replacement, and insurance claim options before you decide.
