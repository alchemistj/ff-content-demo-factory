Common Roofing Issues for Decatur Homeowners
Adams County's rural landscape and historic housing stock create specific roofing challenges that require experienced hands.
Aging Farmhouse Roofs
Adams County is home to hundreds of older farmhouses, many dating back 80 to 100+ years. These homes often have original roof framing with undersized rafters, inadequate ventilation, and multiple layers of shingles that have been applied over the decades. When we work on a Decatur farmhouse, we inspect the entire roof structure — not just the shingles — to identify decking rot, ventilation problems, and structural concerns that need to be addressed during replacement.
Metal Roof Maintenance
Many rural properties and agricultural buildings in the Decatur area use metal roofing, which is an excellent long-term choice. However, older metal roofs can develop issues at seams, fasteners, and penetrations. Rust, loose screws, and failed sealant are the most common problems we address. For homes considering a switch from shingles to metal, we install standing-seam metal roofing that eliminates exposed fasteners entirely.
Storm Damage Across Open Farmland
The flat, open farmland surrounding Decatur gives severe storms room to gather strength. Hail and straight-line winds hit Adams County hard, and rural properties with no tree cover or surrounding structures take the full force. After a major storm, we can inspect and document visible damage, prepare a contractor estimate, and meet with an adjuster when requested. Coverage and claim decisions remain with the carrier under the policy.
Moisture & Condensation in Older Homes
Many of Decatur's heritage homes were built before modern building science understood the importance of proper vapor barriers and attic ventilation. This leads to moisture problems — condensation on the underside of the roof deck, mold growth on sheathing, and premature shingle failure from trapped humidity. We address these root causes during every roof replacement, not just the symptoms.
