Roofing Challenges in Huntertown
Indiana's climate creates specific roofing challenges for Huntertown homeowners. Here's what we see most often — and how Big Dog Roofing addresses each one.
New Construction Transitioning Off Builder Warranties
Huntertown's building boom means many homes are now reaching the 5 to 10 year mark where builder warranties expire and original roofing materials begin showing their first signs of wear. We inspect newly built homes to identify early issues — like improperly installed flashing or inadequate ventilation — before they become expensive problems.
Severe Thunderstorms and Hail
Northern Allen County roofs can face hail, wind, and wind-driven rain during Indiana severe weather. After a storm, we inspect for lifted or creased shingles, hail impacts, damaged soft metals, and leak entry points, then provide photos you can share with your insurer.
Open Terrain Wind Exposure
Many newer Huntertown subdivisions are built in previously open agricultural land where young trees provide minimal wind protection. Roofs in these areas face higher wind loads during storms. We install all Huntertown roofs with high-wind-rated shingles and enhanced fastening patterns to ensure maximum performance.
Indiana's Freeze-Thaw Cycles
Huntertown winters bring repeated freezing and thawing that stress roofing materials. Ice dams can form along the eaves and force water under shingles, causing interior leaks and damage. Proper attic ventilation and ice-and-water shield underlayment prevent these issues — both are standard on every Big Dog installation.
