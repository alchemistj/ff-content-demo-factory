What's Included in Our Free Roof Inspection
When Big Dog Roofing inspects your roof, we don't just glance at it from the driveway. Our inspections are comprehensive, covering every component of your roofing system from the ridge to the foundation line.
Here's exactly what we check during a free roof inspection in Fort Wayne:
Shingle condition — cracking, curling, granule loss, missing shingles, and hail bruising

When Should Fort Wayne Homeowners Get a Roof Inspection?
We recommend scheduling a professional roof inspection in the following situations:
After a major storm. Hail, high winds, and fallen debris can cause damage that isn't visible from the ground. Insurance claims have time limits, so getting an inspection promptly after a storm protects your coverage.
If your roof is 15 years old or older. Most asphalt shingle roofs in Fort Wayne last 25 to 30 years. Once you pass the halfway mark, regular inspections help you plan ahead instead of reacting to emergencies.
Before buying or selling a home. A roof inspection gives buyers confidence and gives sellers documentation of their roof's condition. We provide inspection reports that can be shared with real estate agents and buyers.
If you see warning signs. Granules collecting in gutters, daylight visible through the attic, water stains on ceilings, or shingles that look worn or uneven are all signals that it's time for a professional assessment.
Every 2 to 3 years as routine maintenance. Catching small issues early prevents expensive repairs later. A 20-minute inspection today could save you thousands down the road.
