- **Solar panel removal and reinstall** during a roof replacement (solar-panel-removal-reinstall-roof-replacement, solar-panels-and-your-roof-fort-wayne). Unusual for a roofer, and a real service page.

- **Financing** (financing, articles-roof-financing-fort-wayne). The financing page names no lender and says "subject to the provider's terms and approval". The financing article says "Big Dog Roofing offers financing plans." Conflict of tone, see below.
