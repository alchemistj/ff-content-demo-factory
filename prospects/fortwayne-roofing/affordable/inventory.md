## Lead candidates (USP method)

**Avatar caveat:** `avatars/roofing.md` is built on Wichita reviews. There is no Fort Wayne roofing avatar. Appeal is scored against the Wichita avatar's ranked fears and wants (insurer and claim fears, a dishonest or pushy roofer, a silent roofer, a leak). Fort Wayne reviews show price, speed and no pressure more than insurance, so Appeal may be understated on price. Exclusivity is scored against `competitors/fortwayne-roofing.md` (five companies, homepage-only check).
