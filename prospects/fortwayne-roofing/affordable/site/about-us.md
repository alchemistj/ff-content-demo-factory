Our Story
Affordable Roofing is our family owned business that has been providing quality work in Fort Wayne and surrounding areas since 2012. We initially specialized in Roofing, Siding, Windows and Gutters but over the years we continued to grow and Roofing and Gutters became our main area of work. Now, our family owned business is doing over 200 projects a year. We’ve completed over 1,500 projects since opening and pride ourselves on providing exceptional work at an affordable price. We have an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau, are a preferred contractor with Owens Corning, require no money down, and provide financing if needed.
