Keeping Your Fort Wayne Home Dry: When It's Time for a New Roof
Keeping Your Fort Wayne Home Dry: When It's Time for a New Roof
Jan 15, 2026 · 2 min read
Your roof is your home's first line of defense against rain and melting snow. When shingles wear out, water can find its way into the decking, attic, and ceilings. Here is what Fort Wayne homeowners can watch for, what a full roof replacement covers, and how gutters fit into the project.
Signs Your Roof May Be Ready for Replacement
Northeast Indiana winters, spring storms, and summer heat all take a toll on asphalt shingles. From the ground, look for:
Curling, cracked, or missing shingles.
Bare spots where the protective granules have worn away, or granules collecting at the bottom of downspouts.
Water stains on ceilings or in the attic after heavy rain.
Sagging areas along the roofline.
A roof that is near the end of its expected life.
If you notice several of these, an in-person inspection can tell you whether a roof replacement is the right next step.
What a Full Roof Replacement Covers
A replacement starts by tearing the old roof down to the deck so any damaged wood can be found and replaced. A new roof then goes on as a complete system: underlayment and ice and water barrier, flashing and drip edge, proper ventilation, starter and ridge shingles, and the shingle line and color you choose. Every quote from Affordable Roofing spells out the scope, and cleanup and haul-away are part of the job.

Where Gutters Fit In
A new roof sheds water to the edge, and your gutters carry it away from your siding and foundation. If your gutters are sagging, rusted, or pulling away from the fascia, you can add new gutters to your roof replacement so the whole system works together. Just ask about it during your estimate.
Keep an Eye on Your Roof Between Seasons
Look over your roof from the ground after major storms.
Check your attic for damp insulation or daylight showing through.
Keep branches trimmed back from the roofline.
Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water can drain.
Avoid climbing onto your roof or using a ladder when conditions are unsafe. Leave roof inspections to a professional.
