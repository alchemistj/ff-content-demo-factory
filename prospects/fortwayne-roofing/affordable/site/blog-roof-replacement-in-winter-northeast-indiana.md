Can You Replace a Roof in Winter in Northeast Indiana?
By Affordable Roofing · Published Oct 19, 2026

Owens Corning has specific cold-weather steps for that, including hand-sealing the shingles around the roof's perimeter. What changes in winter isn't whether a roof can go on. It's how it goes on. Shingle manufacturers write cold-weather instructions because asphalt shingles act differently in the cold:
They're stiffer. Owens Corning's installation instructions say to "use extra care in handling shingles when the temperature is below 40°F."
They don't seal right away. Owens Corning explains that its shingles have "a factory-applied strip of thermally activated sealant," and "direct exposure to the sun's energy activates the sealant." In cold weather, shingles "may not immediately seal until warmer temperatures are present" (Owens Corning cold weather technical bulletin).
They may take longer to lie flat. The same bulletin notes that shingles installed in cold weather "may take longer to lay flat."
None of that means a winter roof is a bad roof. It means the crew has to follow the cold-weather steps.

What's different about installing shingles below 40°F?
Owens Corning's cold-weather bulletin applies when "the air and surface temperatures are at or below 40°F (4°C)." In northeast Indiana, that covers a lot of days from late fall through early spring. The bulletin recommends these steps:
Leave a small gap at the shingle ends. Field shingles get a slight gap, no more than 1/16 inch, at the end joints. Shingles expand when it warms up, and the gap keeps them from buckling against each other.
Hand-seal every shingle around the perimeter. On architectural (laminated) shingles, Owens Corning calls for four 1-inch dabs of ASTM D4586-compliant asphalt roof cement. They go about 1 inch from the leading edge and get pressed into place. That protects the edges, where wind grabs first, until the seal strip activates.
Don't overdo the cement. The bulletin warns that too much roof cement can make shingles blister, and that it shouldn't squeeze out past the edge.
Handle bundles gently. Cold shingles are less forgiving, so loading, carrying and bending all need more care.
The Duration installation instructions repeat the perimeter hand-sealing and the end gap for cold-weather installs (Owens Corning Duration installation instructions). Following them is part of installing the shingle the way the manufacturer requires.
Question to ask any roofer quoting a winter job: "What do you do differently when it's below 40 degrees?" If the answer doesn't include hand-sealing the perimeter, keep asking.

Will my new shingles seal in winter?
They will. It just may take until the weather warms up. Owens Corning lists the things that affect how fast the sealant activates: geographic location, roof slope, which direction the roof faces relative to the sun, and the average daily outdoor temperature during and after installation.
In plain terms, a south-facing slope on a sunny February afternoon may start sealing much sooner than a shaded north slope. Until the strip activates, the hand-sealed perimeter is what holds the edges against wind. That's why we don't treat that step as optional.
When should you wait for better weather?
Some days aren't roofing days, in any month:
Snow or ice on the roof. The deck has to be clean and dry before anything goes down. Owens Corning's instructions tell installers to "prevent the wood deck from getting wet before, during and after installation."
Wet or freezing-rain forecast. Once the old roof is off, the deck is open until the new underlayment goes on. That's why the weather forecast matters for any tear-off, and why we never leave a roof open.
Deep cold snaps. Crew safety and handling the shingles both get harder. A few days' wait is better than rushing.

Frequently asked questions
Can you replace a roof in the winter in Indiana?
Yes. Asphalt shingle roofs can be installed in winter if the deck is dry and the crew follows the manufacturer's cold-weather steps. Below 40°F, Owens Corning calls for small end gaps and hand-sealing the perimeter shingles with asphalt roof cement. We don't do many winter roofs, but when a homeowner needs one, we do it, usually in a single day.
What temperature is too cold to install shingles?
Owens Corning doesn't set a single cutoff temperature. Its cold-weather recommendations kick in when air and surface temperatures are at or below 40°F (4°C), and they include extra handling care, end-joint gaps and perimeter hand-sealing. Snow, ice, wet decking and safety are the practical limits we watch on winter jobs.
Will shingles seal if they're installed in cold weather?
Yes, once it warms up. The factory seal strip is thermally activated by the sun. In cold weather, it may not seal until warmer temperatures arrive. Location, slope, which way the roof faces and daily temperatures all affect timing. Hand-sealing the perimeter protects against wind until then.
