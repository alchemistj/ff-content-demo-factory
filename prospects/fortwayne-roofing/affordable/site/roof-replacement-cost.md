What's the average cost of a new roof in Fort Wayne?
Across the roof replacements we do, the average comes out to about $10,000. That number covers a wide mix of homes: small ranches, big two-stories, homes with detached garages, and roofs that needed new decking.

What makes a roof cost more or less?
These are the factors that move the price on our quotes, roughly in order of how much they matter.
1. Roof size. This is the biggest factor. Roofers measure in squares, and one square is 100 square feet of roof surface. A bigger roof needs more shingles, more underlayment and more labor. The roof area is always larger than your home's floor area, because of overhangs and slope.
2. Pitch (steepness). A steep roof takes longer to work on safely and needs more safety setup. A low, walkable ranch roof usually costs less to install than a steep two-story roof of the same size.
3. How many layers come off. We tear every roof down to the deck. If there are two layers of old shingles instead of one, there's more labor and more disposal.
4. Decking condition. Nobody can see the wood under the shingles until the tear-off. If boards or sheets are soft or damaged, they have to be replaced before the new roof goes on. Sometimes that's a few sheets, and sometimes the whole deck. We'll tell you up front how decking is handled on your quote.
5. Ventilation. A roof needs balanced intake and exhaust airflow through the attic. If your home needs ridge vent or other ventilation updates, that's part of the job and part of the price. It's also part of installing the shingles the way the manufacturer requires.
6. Shingle line. We install mostly Owens Corning Duration shingles, plus Oakridge and many other brands. Different lines are priced differently. Our Duration vs Oakridge guide explains what you get with each.
7. Roof complexity. Valleys, dormers, skylights, chimneys and multiple roof sections all add flashing work and cutting time. A simple gable roof is the least expensive shape.
8. Gutters and extras. Many homeowners replace gutters together with the roof. New seamless gutters, downspouts and guards are a separate line on the quote, so you can decide on them.

What warranty comes with a new roof?
There are two kinds of warranty on a new roof, and you should understand both before you sign:
Shingle (manufacturer) warranty. Owens Corning shingles carry a Limited Lifetime warranty from Owens Corning. The actual warranty document spells out the details, limitations and requirements.
Workmanship warranty. This covers the installation itself. Every roof we install comes with our standard 5-year workmanship warranty. For an additional cost, you can upgrade to a 10-year workmanship warranty backed by Owens Corning.
We'll explain both on your estimate, and your contract will list which one you chose.
How long does a roof replacement take?
Most of our roofs are torn off and installed in one day. Bigger or steeper roofs, or a full decking replacement, can add time. Even so, a full-deck job we posted in February 2024 was finished in a single day. We never leave a roof open. What comes off gets covered the same day.
