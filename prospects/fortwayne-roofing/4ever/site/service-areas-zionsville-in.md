The village and the rest of Zionsville
Zionsville is two roofing markets. The old village has brick streets and houses that go back well over a century — steep pitches, dormers, porch roofs at a lower slope, additions tied in at different times, sometimes plank decking under the shingles. On those houses the field of the roof is the easy part. The valleys, sidewalls, and chimney are where the money and the leaks are, and a quote that treats it like a simple gable is not being careful.
Outside the village it is newer — estate lots with big custom roofs, and subdivisions from the 1990s on with sound decking and modern framing. Those are more straightforward, but the large custom homes can carry as much flashing detail as anything in the village.
Weather, as it shows up on the ladder
Boone County's roof problem is spring hail and straight-line wind more than the freeze-thaw the northern counties get, though ice at the eaves still happens and the membrane there still matters. Hail bruises asphalt and starts its decline. Metal stays watertight; a heavy stone can dent a flat pan cosmetically. A metal shingle or textured profile hides that better. Ask your carrier how they treat metal at renewal before counting on anything.
What fits here
On the older village homes, a four-side interlocking metal shingle keeps the period look and removes the exposed nail heads that failed last time; it wants 3/12 or steeper. Mechanically seamed standing seam is what I trust on low-slope porch roofs and long simple runs. Exposed-fastener panel is for the outbuilding, not the house — every screw's washer ages. Stone-coated I am cautious about; it manages water after it gets under the panel. Wooded lots hold moisture and debris, which chews through asphalt granules and is mostly a cleaning question on coated metal.
Covenanted neighborhoods need color and profile approved in writing before material is ordered. We supply the documentation.
When it is the wrong call
Selling in a few years. A rental. One leaking flashing on a sound roof — that is a repair, and we do not take repair calls. Wanting shingles again. Shopping the lowest number this year.
