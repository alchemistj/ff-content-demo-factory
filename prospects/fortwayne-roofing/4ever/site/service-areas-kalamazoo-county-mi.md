What that means for a roof
The median structure here went up around 1975. That puts most of the stock in the post-war-to-eighties range: simple planes, trussed roofs, decking that is usually sound. These are the most straightforward metal installs we do, and the ones where the cheapest option is a genuine option rather than a false economy.
None of that decides your roof. Pitch, the details around chimneys and walls, and what you expect from the roof do — and those are things we look at rather than infer from a county average. The five systems each explain where they fail as well as where they fit.
