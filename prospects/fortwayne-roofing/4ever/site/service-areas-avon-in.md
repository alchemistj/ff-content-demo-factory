Avon's roofs are reaching the end of their first cycle
Avon grew fast, and most of it grew at once. FEMA's structure inventory puts the typical home here at about 1999, across roughly 6,000 single-family homes. A builder-grade asphalt roof from that era is at or past the age where it starts failing, which is why so many Avon homeowners are looking at a roof for the first time.
That is a good position to make the decision from. The decking is usually sound and the framing is modern, so these tend to be straightforward installs.
Approval comes before color
Much of Avon is inside an HOA, and architectural guidelines vary. Some were written before metal roofing looked the way it does now. Get the profile and color approved in writing before any material is ordered. We provide product data and samples for the board, and I would rather spend two weeks on approval than have you arguing with an association after the roof is on.
Choosing between the two common systems
On these houses the choice is usually between standing seam and a metal shingle. Standing seam has clean lines and very few penetrations through the panel itself. Metal shingles look closer to a traditional roof, often pass HOA review more easily, and hide hail dents better. They cost about the same as mechanically seamed standing seam and need at least a 3/12 pitch. Neither is maintenance-free.
Who should not do this
If you expect to move within a few years, metal is hard to justify. If you are shopping on the lowest price, we are not the right call. If the problem is one leak on a sound roof, that is a repair, and we do not take repair calls.
