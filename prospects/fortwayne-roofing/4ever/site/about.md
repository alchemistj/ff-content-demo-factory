About 4Ever Metal Roofing
One trade, done since 2016, across Indiana, Ohio and Michigan.
What we do, and what we do not
We install metal roofs on homes. Standing seam, interlocking metal shingles, stone-coated steel and — where the pitch allows — exposed-fastener panels. We do not do windows, siding or gutters, and we do not take standalone repair calls. Doing one thing is how the details get learned, and the details are what decide whether a metal roof lasts.
Close to 2,000 metal roofs since 2016. Two offices, in Fort Wayne and Noblesville. Crews that are manufacturer-certified, which is what lets the manufacturer warrant the workmanship as well as the material.
Light tan standing seam metal roof with an octagonal cupola, aerial view
How we think about advice
Education comes before persuasion. The purpose of this site is not to convince you to buy a metal roof. It is to help you make an informed decision — even if that decision is not to move forward, or not to work with us. If something we write removes information to make a decision feel easier, it is wrong.
So every system gets the same treatment: what it is, what it does well, where it fails, who it fits, who it does not. If a system needs maintenance, we say so. If it performs differently by climate or pitch, we say so.
Materials
Steel shingles from EDCO and Vicwest, stone-coated steel from Tilcor, and standing seam coil from Platinum Metals that we roll-form on site — finished with Sherwin-Williams or AkzoNobel coil coatings. The manufacturer pages carry their warranties, color cards and documents, so you read the same paperwork we do.
