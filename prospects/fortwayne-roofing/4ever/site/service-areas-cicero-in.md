Cicero is the closest town to our Noblesville office
Cicero sits just north of Noblesville along Morse Reservoir, and it is the shortest drive our Noblesville crews make. FEMA's structure inventory counts about 1,900 single-family homes in town, and the typical one was built in the early 1990s. That puts a lot of Cicero roofs at the age where the original asphalt is done or close to it.
Open water and open ground change the wind load
What I watch for here is wind. Homes on the reservoir and on the larger rural lots west of town have nothing slowing the wind down before it reaches the roof. On asphalt, that shows up as lifted tabs and shingles in the yard after a spring storm. On metal, it shows up in the details: how the panels are fastened at the eaves and rakes, how the ridge is closed, and whether the installer followed the uplift requirements for an exposed site.
Here's the tradeoff. A properly fastened metal roof handles wind far better than asphalt, but a poorly fastened one fails at the edges first, and it fails in big pieces. The material is not the protection. The installation is.
Outbuildings and pole barns
A lot of Cicero properties have a detached garage or a pole barn as well as the house. Exposed-fastener panels are a reasonable choice for an outbuilding, but the screws carry rubber washers that wear out, so it is a roof you inspect and re-tighten over the years. We will not put exposed fastener on anything under a 4/12 pitch. On the house itself, most people end up choosing between standing seam and a metal shingle.
When I tell Cicero homeowners not to do this
If the roof has one bad pipe boot or a single flashing leak and the rest of it is sound, that is a repair, and we do not take repair calls. If you expect to sell within a few years, good asphalt is the better use of the money.
If you are on the lake or on acreage you plan to keep, the question is simpler: do you want to replace this roof again in fifteen years? Our Noblesville office covers Cicero directly. Now you have the context to decide.
