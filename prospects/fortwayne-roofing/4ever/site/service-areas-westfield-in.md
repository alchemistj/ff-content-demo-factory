The newest housing stock we work on
Westfield has grown faster than almost anywhere else we serve, and it shows on the roofs. A lot of what I see is original builder-grade asphalt on a house less than twenty years old — sometimes less than ten. That surprises people. A shingle that was installed to a price does not always get to its rated life, and a couple of hail seasons can shorten it further.
The good news is underneath. Decking is sound, framing is modern, ventilation is usually close to right. These are clean tear-offs and straightforward installs.
Hail and straight-line wind
This part of the state gets spring storms more than hard winters. Hail bruises asphalt and starts its failure clock; on metal a large stone can leave a cosmetic dent and the roof stays tight. A textured profile or a metal shingle hides a dent better than a flat pan. Straight-line wind lifts shingle tabs; a concealed-clip system has nothing for it to get under. Before you count on an insurance break, ask your carrier directly how they treat metal on renewal — some do, some do not.
Here's the tradeoff on a young house
If the house is new, you are deciding whether to put a fifty-year roof on a home you may or may not keep for fifty years. If you are staying, metal ends the cycle before it starts — you buy one roof instead of three. If you are in a starter home you will leave in a few years, asphalt is the rational choice and I will say so. Rental and flip properties, same answer.
Systems, and the HOA
Most of Westfield is covenanted. Get the color and profile approved in writing before anything is ordered; we supply product data and samples, and it is worth the two weeks. The four-side interlocking metal shingle usually has the easiest time with a board, because it reads as a shingle roof; it needs 3/12 or steeper. Standing seam fits cleaner modern elevations and is what goes on a low-slope porch or sunroom. Exposed-fastener panel belongs on a barn or shed, not the house. Stone-coated I am cautious about, because it manages water after entry rather than keeping it out.
