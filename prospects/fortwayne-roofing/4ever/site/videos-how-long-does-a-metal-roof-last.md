How Long Does a Metal Roof Last
It depends on the system. An exposed fastener roof with annual maintenance runs 30 to 35 years. A concealed fastener roof can go 50 to 70 before it needs paint.
How long a metal roof lasts depends on the type of system you install. An exposed fastener system, with annual maintenance, is generally going to last you 30 to 35 years. A concealed fastener system, with little to no maintenance, can last 50 to 70 years before it needs paint.
A permanent metal roof installed properly is going to last between 50 and 70 years. What that means is no more re-roofing every 15 to 20 years.
