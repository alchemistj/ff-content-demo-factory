Brownsburg homes are built to stay in
Brownsburg has the highest median household income of the Hendricks County towns we serve, by the Census figures, and FEMA's structure inventory puts the typical home at around 1998, across nearly 8,800 single-family homes. In practice that means a lot of families who have been in the same house for twenty years and plan to stay, living under a roof that was never meant to last that long.
That is exactly who metal roofing is for, and it is also where I slow the conversation down.
The one-time decision deserves the most care
If you are only going to do this once, the details matter more than the brand on the panel. How the standing seam is formed matters: a mechanically seamed panel is crimped together on the roof, while a snap-lock panel simply clips. They look alike from the street and behave differently in wind and over decades. Flashing at walls, valleys and chimneys is where most metal roofs that leak are leaking.
Here's the tradeoff. A roof done once and done carefully costs more than asphalt today. Over the time most Brownsburg owners stay, it is usually the roof you do not have to buy again.
What metal will not do
It is not maintenance-free. Sealants age, debris collects in valleys, and anything mounted to the roof later needs to be flashed correctly. Hail can dent a flat panel even though the roof stays watertight. Expect to look at it once a year, the way you would any roof.
When I would tell you not to
If you are selling soon, if the decision is mainly about price, or if the leak is one detail on a sound roof, metal is not the right call. We do not take repair work.
