Privacy policy
How 4Ever Metal Roofing, LLC handles what you send through this site. Short, because we collect little.
What we collect
When you send a message or request a quote, we keep what you typed — name, phone, email, address and message — and the page you sent it from, so we can reply. Our server keeps ordinary access logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and troubleshooting.
What we do with it
We use it to reply to you and, if you ask for a quote, to schedule and prepare it. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone except the services that deliver our email and, if you choose financing, the lender you apply with.
Cookies and tracking
This site sets no advertising cookies and loads no third-party trackers. Embedded videos come from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain, which does not set cookies until you press play.
Your choices
Ask us what we hold about you or ask us to delete it: info@4evermetalroofing.com.
