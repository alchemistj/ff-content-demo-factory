Why we install their products
AkzoNobel makes the paint on the coil before it is ever a roof panel. The one we buy from them is Ceram-A-Star 1050, their silicone-modified polyester, and it goes on most of the homes we do — exposed fastener and standing seam both. It is an excellent product for residential applications and it performs. It is also the only AkzoNobel paint we offer.
Here is the honest comparison. SMP is a step down from a PVDF fluoropolymer, which is the finish that holds color and gloss the longest. It is not much of a step down anymore, and it is far more affordable across a whole roof. That is the trade, and for most homeowners it is a good one.
Sherwin-Williams WeatherXL is the other coil coating you will find on our panels, and the two are close. When you are comparing quotes, find the coating name on each one, then read what its warranty actually covers before you read the price.
