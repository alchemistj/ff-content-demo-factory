What that means for a roof
The median structure here went up around 1960. That puts most of the stock in the post-war-to-eighties range: simple planes, trussed roofs, decking that is usually sound. These are the most straightforward metal installs we do, and the ones where the cheapest option is a genuine option rather than a false economy.
