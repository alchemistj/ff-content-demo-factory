Pendleton: the old town, the new streets, and the farms
Pendleton has a downtown of older homes around the falls — steeper pitches, porches, additions, old decking — ringed by newer subdivisions on their first or second asphalt roof, with farm ground and screw-down barns beyond. I see all three kinds of roof here, and they want different answers.
On the older houses, the tear-off is where the scope gets decided: soft decking at the eaves, sidewall flashing done in caulk, a chimney that has been patched. That belongs on the quote as a line item before anyone climbs a ladder.
What the weather does
Madison County gets central Indiana's spring hail and straight-line wind; hard winters matter less, though ice at the eaves is still a Midwest fact. Hail bruises asphalt and starts its failure clock. Metal stays watertight; a large stone can cosmetically dent a flat pan, and a metal shingle or textured profile hides that better. Out past town the wind has a long run across open fields, and a concealed-clip panel gives it nothing to work on. Ask your carrier how they treat metal at renewal before counting on anything.
The barn is not the house
The exposed-fastener panel on a Pendleton pole barn is an honest product and we install a version of it. On the house you plan to keep it is usually the wrong call: every screw's washer ages and needs attention around year ten to twelve. On the house, a four-side interlocking metal shingle where you want a shingle look and have 3/12 or better, or mechanically seamed standing seam for long runs and low-slope porch roofs, keeps every fastener out of the weather. Stone-coated I am cautious about — it manages water after entry.
Covenanted subdivisions need color and profile approved in writing before material is ordered; we supply the documentation.
When it is the wrong call
Leaving in a few years. A rental or flip. One leaking detail on a sound roof — a repair, and we do not take repair calls; a repair contractor is the right call. Wanting shingles again. Shopping the lowest number this year.
