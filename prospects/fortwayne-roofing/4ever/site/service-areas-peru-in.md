What that means for a roof
The median structure here went up around 1939. That is old enough that most of these houses are on their third or fourth roof, and old enough that the decking is the thing I want to see before anyone quotes a system. Steeper roofs and real geometry are common, which suits an interlocking shingle or a mechanically seamed standing seam and rules out very little.
None of that decides your roof. Pitch, the details around chimneys and walls, and what you expect from the roof do — and those are things we look at rather than infer from a county average. The five systems each explain where they fail as well as where they fit.
