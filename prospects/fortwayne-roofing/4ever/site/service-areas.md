Where we work, and how far we will drive
Two offices, three states. Fort Wayne covers northeast Indiana, northwest Ohio and southern Michigan; Noblesville covers the Indianapolis metro. If your town is not on the list, ask — the list is where we have worked, not a fence.
How far is too far
Our Fort Wayne office works within roughly a 100-mile radius, and Noblesville within about 40 — which covers the Indianapolis metro. Beyond that we still take calls, but the honest answer changes with the job: a full re-roof justifies a longer drive than a small one does, and a commercial or agricultural building justifies more still.
What we will not do is quote a job we cannot service afterward. A roof we install is one we expect to be called back to in ten years, and that only works if we can get there.
Why the local pages are different from each other
They are not the same page with the city name swapped. Roofs in Shipshewana are not the roofs in Carmel: the housing stock is a different age, the snow load is different, the HOA and review-board situation is different, and the systems that fit are different. Each page says what we actually see on roofs there and which system I would and would not put on them.
If you are outside all of it
Call anyway. If we are the wrong company for the distance, we will say so — and if we know somebody who does this work properly in your area, we will tell you who.
Two offices, one crew standard
Every ZIP code we serve is handled by whichever office is the shorter drive. Same systems, same manufacturers, same warranty either way.
