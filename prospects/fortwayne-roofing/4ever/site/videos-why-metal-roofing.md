Why Metal Roofing
For homeowners in their forever home who want a roof that outlasts them. Lifespan, energy, insurance, and what happens to asphalt shingles when they come off.
Metal roofing is for homeowners who are living in their forever home and want a system that outlasts them. Metal lasts anywhere from 50 to 70 years. Asphalt shingles last 15 to 20 years on average.
Metal roofing is going to provide energy savings, conservatively from 18% up to 30%. And because your house is more resistant to wind, fire and hail, annual insurance premiums should be reduced by anywhere from 10% up to 35%.
Asphalt shingles account for more than 20 billion pounds of landfill waste each year in the US alone, and they take over three centuries to decompose. Metal roofing is made with recycled material and is 100% recyclable. In short, metal roofing is about permanence.
