Shipshewana already knows metal
Most places I work, I spend the first ten minutes explaining that a metal roof does not have to look like a barn. In Shipshewana the barns are the point. The roofs on the outbuildings, the shops, and a good share of the houses are already metal. The conversation here is not whether metal; it is which metal, and on what.
The barn roof and the house roof
The screw-down panel on the outbuildings is an exposed-fastener system. Every screw goes through the panel with a rubber washer under it, and those washers wear out around year ten to twelve — sooner on the slope that takes the afternoon sun. On a barn or a shop, that is a fair trade for the lower cost. On a house you plan to keep, it means a fastener pass every decade or so and a roof only as good as six hundred small seals. We will not install it under 4/12.
The house systems hold the panel with concealed clips and locked seams, so nothing that seals the roof sits in the weather. A mechanically seamed standing seam is the one I trust most on a house — it handles the long runs and the low-slope additions farmhouses accumulate. An interlocking metal shingle fits a steeper roof where you want a traditional look. Stone-coated steel I am cautious about, because it manages water after it gets in.
What the weather does here
This is the northern tier, and the lake-effect snow is real — more of it than Fort Wayne gets, earlier, and it stays. The roof damage is still the freeze and thaw: meltwater refreezing at the cold eave and backing up. The membrane at the eave and the attic ventilation prevent that; the panel makes the consequences smaller. Metal sheds snow all at once, so snow retention above doorways and walks belongs in the quote.
When I tell Shipshewana homeowners not to do this
If you are selling in the next few years, the math does not close in time. If one flashing or one pipe boot is leaking on a sound roof, that is a repair — we do not take repair calls. If the smallest number this year is the question, the barn panel wins it and I will say so.
