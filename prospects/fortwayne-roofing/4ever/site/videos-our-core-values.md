Our Core Values
Craftsmanship, honesty, fit and discipline. What each one means on a roof, and why most roofs fail on the install, not the material.
The core values of 4Ever Metal Roofing are craftsmanship, honesty, fit and discipline.
Craftsmanship means we are obsessed with the details. Most roofs do not fail because of the material. They fail because of how they were installed and how much attention the contractor paid to each little detail: the wall flashings, the pipe boots, the underlayment, the screws.
Honesty means being straight with the homeowner, even when that means we walk away from the job and shake hands as friends. Fit runs both ways: not everybody is going to be a great fit as our customer, we are not going to be a great fit as everybody's contractor, and not every home makes sense for a metal roof. Discipline is the spine for the other three. Without it, it all falls apart.
