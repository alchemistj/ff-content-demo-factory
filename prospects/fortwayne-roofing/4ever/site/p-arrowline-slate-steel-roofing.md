ArrowLine® Slate Steel Roofing
The slate profile of EDCO's interlocking steel shingle line. Four-side lock, PVDF finish, Class 4 impact, 3/12 minimum pitch.
How I think about this one
Same lock, slate face. On a steep, cut-up roof it does a better job than a stone-coated tile of keeping water where it belongs, and it weighs a fraction of real slate. The finish is PVDF — read the warranty's fade and chalk numbers and compare them to anything else you are quoted.
What it costs, and why
Installed, most ArrowLine® Slate Steel Roofing roofs we put on land between $1,250–$1,700 per square. A square is 100 square feet of roof, and that figure includes tearing off one layer of shingles.
Where yours lands depends on the roof more than the product. A second layer to tear off, soft decking we only find once the old roof is open, a steep pitch, and a lot of valleys, dormers, chimneys and skylights all push it up. A simple, walkable roof with one layer sits near the low end.
This is a range, not a quote. I would not give you a real number without seeing the roof, and I would be careful with anyone who does. If the up-front number is the whole question, how a metal roof costs out over thirty years is the better place to start.
