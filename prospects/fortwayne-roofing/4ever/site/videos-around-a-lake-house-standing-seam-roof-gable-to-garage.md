Around a lake house standing seam roof, gable to garage
A full circle around a lake house standing seam roof, from the steep gable past the shed to where it steps down over the garage. No narration.
This pass goes all the way around the house, so you can see how the steep main gable steps down to the lower garage roof and how the hips and valleys meet at that transition.
That step-down is the kind of spot a standing seam roof has to be detailed carefully. The panels are formed to length; the transition is flashing work.
