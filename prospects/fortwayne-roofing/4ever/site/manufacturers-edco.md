Why we install their products
EDCO makes the interlocking steel shingles behind what we call the 4Ever Fortress system. I install theirs because of how the panel is built, not because of the name: a four-side interlock that keeps water out at the surface instead of counting on the underlayment to catch it later, a PVDF finish rather than a cheaper polyester, and a warranty document that actually says what it covers.
Here's the tradeoff. An interlocking shingle costs more than a screw-down roof and about the same as mechanically seamed standing seam. It wants a 3/12 pitch or steeper, and it is unforgiving of a crew that has not done it — start a course wrong and the mistake repeats across the roof. That is also why I like it: the product does not hide a bad install.
If you want a roof that reads as slate or shake from the street, this is the one I would put on my own house. If you like the look of vertical seams, standing seam is the simpler answer.
