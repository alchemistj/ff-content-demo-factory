EXPOSED FASTENER METAL
Metal durability, at a friendlier price.
Exposed-fastener metal (sometimes called ag-panel or R-panel) is fastened through the panel face with gasketed screws. It costs less than standing seam and installs faster, while still delivering the long life and weather resistance metal is known for.
It's a smart choice for outbuildings, barns, shops, and homeowners who want metal without the premium price.
