FLAT ROOFING
Flat roofs, sealed for the long haul.
Flat and low-slope roofs fail differently than pitched roofs, it's about seams, drainage, and membrane, not shingles. We install and repair single-ply TPO and EPDM, along with modified-bitumen systems, on homes, additions, porches, and commercial buildings.
Proper detailing at every seam and penetration is what keeps them watertight.
