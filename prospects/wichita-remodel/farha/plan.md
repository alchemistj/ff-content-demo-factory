## The idea
A remodel is five trades that have to arrive in the right order, and the house is only as good as how they hand off to each other. Geremy started as a tile installer and now runs every one of those trades under his own name, so you hire once, he stays on the job, and you call the same company for the next room. The reviews prove it: people come back for a second, third and "almost 20 years" of rooms.

## Bathroom page
Moment: an old box-store shower, cracked grout, a leak that showed up in the ceiling below. Reason: tile does not waterproof a shower, what is behind it does; a slow leak rots the floor before anyone sees it. What we do: Geremy began as a tile installer; walk-in showers, tile on walls and floors, custom vanities, in-house designer, carpentry in the same crew when damage turns up. Reviews: Eric B. (waterproofing was his number one screen, still perfect years later), Roger H. (floor rot and termite damage under the master shower, repaired), Cody F. (armoire turned into a bathroom sink cabinet), Ruth C. (custom vanity built for the master bath). Questions: how do you waterproof, what if you find damage, can I bring my own tile or fixtures (Eric B.: Geremy let them source it), do you design (in-house designer, Amy B.).

## Kitchen page
Moment: a kitchen whose floor plan, worn counters or tired cabinets make every meal harder. Reason: floor, cabinets, counters, plumbing and wiring all meet in the same room, and moving a wall or sink changes all of them; order matters (materials ordered early, no delays). What we do: new floors, cabinets, counters, appliances, rearranged layouts, quartz and granite, tile backsplashes, trim and paint, one company. Reviews: Detha K.
