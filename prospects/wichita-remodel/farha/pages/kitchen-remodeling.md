## Why a Kitchen Is Planned as One Project

Floor, cabinets, counters, plumbing and wiring all meet in the same room, and each one depends on the others. Cabinets can't be set until the floor and walls are ready.

Counters can't be measured until the cabinets are in. The sink and the stove need their plumbing and power where the new layout puts them.

Change the order and something gets torn out twice.

We run carpentry, flooring, plumbing, electrical, tile and paint ourselves, so the schedule is one schedule. Nobody waits on a different company to show up before the next step can start.

## What Goes Into a Kitchen Remodel

A full kitchen remodel replaces the floor, the cabinets, the counters and often the sink and faucet, then finishes with tile backsplash, trim and paint. If the layout changes, the plumbing and wiring change with it.

Because those are all trades we run, the work isn't split between a kitchen company and a separate company for each trade.

A good layout starts with how you cook. Where you prep, where the trash goes and who walks through the room while you're at the stove decide where things belong, more than any catalog does.
