## Why a Shower Fails

A shower rarely fails where you can see it. Grout cracks, caulk peels and a tile comes loose, but those are signs, not the cause.

The cause is water getting behind the tile. Tile and grout don't make a shower waterproof. A membrane or liner behind them does, and so does the way the floor of the shower is sloped and sealed.

When that layer is wrong, water soaks the wall and the floor underneath for years. The first sign can be a stain on the ceiling below.

That's why the first question to ask any contractor is how they'll waterproof your shower.
