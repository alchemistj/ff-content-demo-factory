## One Company for Every Trade

A remodel is never one job. It's carpentry, plumbing, wiring, tile and paint, and each one has to finish before the next can start.

Hire them separately and you become the project manager. You're the one calling the tile setter because the plumber ran late, or finding out the electrician was never told the wall moved.

We run every one of those trades ourselves. Our crews are licensed and insured in carpentry, tile and flooring, plumbing, electrical and painting, so you make one call and get one plan.

It also makes the next project easier. The crew that already knows your house is the crew that does your next room.

## Bathroom Remodeling

Most people call us about a bathroom for the same few reasons. The shower is a builder-grade box that's been there for decades, the grout keeps cracking, or a leak showed up in the ceiling below.

Almost every shower problem is a water problem. Tile looks waterproof, but what keeps water out of your walls and floor is what sits behind the tile.

That's where I started. I began as a tile installer, so I know where showers fail.

We build walk-in showers, tile walls and floors, and make vanities to fit the room.

A designer on our own team works with you one-on-one on the layout and the materials.

## Kitchen Remodeling

A kitchen that doesn't work usually isn't one bad thing. It's worn flooring, cabinets that don't hold what you own, counters with nowhere to set a hot pan, and a layout that makes two people trip over each other.

Those pieces all meet in the same room, so they're best planned together. Move a sink and the plumbing, the cabinets and the floor all change with it.

We do the floors, cabinets, counters and layout as one project, with one crew and one schedule. If the best fix is to rearrange the room, we'll show you how.
