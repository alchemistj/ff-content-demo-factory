# TWG Construction (The Window Guys), Wichita KS: facts

Sources: https://twgconstruction.biz/ (curl, server-rendered WordPress); `wichita/reviews/twg-construction.md` (32 Google reviews, 4.8); web lookups as cited.

**1. Owner: not established (uncertain).** No source names an owner. Owner replies are signed "Best Regards, Scott" (review by Carole, 2019-06-28). Reviewers name "Scott" as lead on jobs (Duane S., 2019-04-13) and "Brad Randall" as the person to ask for (Doug D., 2020-09-22). Neither is stated as owner. GuildQuality lists "Scott Cloud" as crew, role unstated (https://www.guildquality.com/pro/TWG-Construction-LLC). Kansas SOS search returned 403.

**2. Independent; years.** No franchise or dealer link seen; filed as "TWG Construction LLC" (GuildQuality, NAICS). Likely independent, unverified.
