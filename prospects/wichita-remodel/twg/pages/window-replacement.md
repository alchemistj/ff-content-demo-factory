## Why Windows Fail

Fog between the panes means the seal around the glass has failed. Air and moisture get in, the insulating space between the panes is gone, and the fog never clears.

A window that is soft or stained at the frame has had water behind it, and a rotted frame doesn't get better. A sash that sticks, a draft you can feel with the window shut or a room that never gets comfortable all point the same way. The window has stopped doing its job.

Hail adds one more cause. It cracks glass outright, and it can break the seal on a window that looks fine.

## Choosing a Replacement Window

Replacement windows come in a few basic styles.

A double-hung window slides up and down and is common in older houses. A casement swings out on a crank. A slider moves side to side, and a picture window doesn't open at all.

The right one depends on the room. A bedroom may need a window that opens wide enough to climb out of in an emergency. A big living-room window might be fixed glass.

Which windows bother you most, and why? Bring photos if you can. We'll look at the opening and the room, and talk through what fits.
