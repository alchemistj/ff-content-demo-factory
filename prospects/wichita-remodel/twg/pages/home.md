## Roof Replacement

Hail can ruin a roof you can't see from the driveway. It bruises the shingle and knocks the protective granules loose, and the leaks show up months later.

Other signs are shingles that curl or crack, tabs the wind keeps lifting, and gutters full of grit from the shingles. If your adjuster's report says replace, or you can see daylight in the attic, it's time.

We tear off the old roof, put on the new one and leave the yard clean. We've replaced metal roofs as well as shingle.

## Siding, Windows and Doors

Siding takes the same hail the roof does, and it dents, cracks and splits. Old siding that was never storm damaged still wears out, and we'll tell you which one you have.

When we install new siding, many of the options carry lifetime manufacturer warranties.

Windows are the other place hail does damage you don't see.

Cracked glass is obvious. A failed seal, with fog between the panes, is easier to miss. We replace windows with rotted frames, failed seals or storm damage, and the window page has the details.

We also install entry and security doors in vinyl, wood and fiberglass.
