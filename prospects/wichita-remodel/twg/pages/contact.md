## What to Have Ready

You don't need much. A few details help us start in the right place:

- What you noticed: a leak, fogged windows, damaged siding, or hail
- Photos of the damage, if you have them
- Your insurance claim number or the adjuster's report, if you've already filed

If you haven't filed a claim yet, call us anyway. We can look at the house and explain where you stand.

If you're not sure whether the damage is from hail or from age, that's a fine reason to call. We'll look at the roof, siding or windows and tell you what we see.
