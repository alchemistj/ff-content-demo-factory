## When a Roof Is Finished

Some roofs fail loudly, with a leak over the bed. Most fail quietly: shingles that curl at the edges, cracks, bare patches where the granules wore off, and tabs the wind keeps lifting.

Check the gutters after a storm. Gritty granules in the downspout mean the shingles are shedding the layer that protects them from sun and rain.

If you've had a leak in two places or more, patching rarely holds. The roof is wearing out everywhere, and you're paying to chase it.

## What Hail Does

Hail doesn't have to punch a hole to ruin a roof. It bruises the shingle and cracks the mat under the granules, and the damage stays quiet until the first hard rain.

That's why a roof can look fine from the driveway and still need replacing. It takes someone on the roof to see it.

Metal roofs take hail differently. They dent, and a bad storm can damage them enough to replace.
