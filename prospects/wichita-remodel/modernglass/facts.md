# Modern Glass Structures: research facts

Bottom line: Windows and doors are a small, unproven part of this business. The site and most reviews are about shower glass, mirrors, and storefront or custom glass. The BBB profile lists window categories, and one customer review says they bought 30+ windows. Treat residential window replacement as a possible line, not the core business. Verify before pitching window demos.

1. Owner. BBB profile lists "Bryan Chamale, Owner" and "Karen Chamale, Office Manager" (https://www.bbb.org/us/ks/wichita/profile/shower-doors/modern-glass-structures--0714-1000062059). Reviews name "Bryan and Eric" (review by Samantha M., 2026-09-25), "bryan was on time when it came to measuring" and "karen in the office" (review by Norma G., 2025-12-30). Not stated on the company's own site. The owner's replies to reviews are unsigned. Confidence: moderate (one directory plus review first names).

2. Independent or franchise. Independent. BBB lists entity type "Sole Proprietorship", business started 6/5/2024, BBB file opened 8/15/2024, so about 2 years operating (BBB profile above). No franchise or dealer link seen. Kansas Secretary of State record not found by search (not checked directly). Review by Alonso A. (2025-01-20) calls it a "newly opened place," and the owner reply does not dispute it.

3. Services in their own words. Site headline: "Custom Residential & Commercial Glass." Site copy: "From storefronts to residential projects." Tagline: "Shower Doors · Mirrors · Storefronts · Custom Projects." Nav: Custom Cut Glass, Mirrors, Shower Glass, Commercial Glass, Residential Glass Installation, Specialty Glass (https://modernglassstructures.com/, Playwright render). The word "window" does not appear in the visible site text. BBB categories: Shower Doors, Windows, Window Installation, Window Repair, Window Glass, Replacement Windows, Tempered Glass, Mirrors, Glass Cutting, Plate Glass, Glass Repair (BBB profile).
   Reviews that mention windows (25 ratings, about 14 with text):
   - Magnolia W. (2024-11-08): "we have bought over 30 windows, glass replacements and aluminum glass doors from them with no issues." This is the strongest window-sales evidence.
   - Jordan W. (2025-01-22): "I've worked with this family on many different repair and construction projects including Windows and shower doors over the past 7 years." The "7 years" conflicts with the BBB start date of 2024, so it may refer to a predecessor business.
