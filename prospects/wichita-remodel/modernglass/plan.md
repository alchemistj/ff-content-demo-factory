## The idea
Glass is cut for one opening and cannot be trimmed afterward, so the measurement is the whole job. The reviews say the same thing from the other side: Modern Glass comes out fast, measures, shows up when it said it would, and the work is clean and quick. A shop that answers the phone and measures right beats a cheap sheet of glass that doesn't fit.
