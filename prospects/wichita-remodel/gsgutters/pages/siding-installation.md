## When Siding Is Tired

Siding takes the Kansas wind, hail and summer sun before anything else on your house does.

When it's wearing out, you can see it. Panels fade or turn chalky, crack, warp or work loose, and trim around the windows and corners starts to split.

What you can't see is the wall behind it. A gap or a missing piece lets water in, and wet sheathing rots quietly for years before it shows inside.

## Repair or Replace

If the damage is one section, say a few panels a storm cracked or a spot where the trim has rotted, a repair may be all the wall needs. We repair siding and trim, and we'll tell you when that's the better choice.

If the siding is worn across the house, patching only buys time. New siding gives you a wall that's tight against the weather again.

Either way, you get a free estimate and a price before we start.

## What Decides the Price

Siding prices move with the size of the house and the number of stories. They move with how much trim there is, how many windows and corners the siding has to work around, and what the wall looks like once the old siding is off.

A plain wall goes quickly, and a good estimate accounts for that. A wall with a lot of cut-ins and a bad surprise behind it takes longer.

That's why we look at your house before we quote it. The estimate is free, and you'll know the price before the work starts.

## Why Trim Matters More Than It Looks

Most leaks in a sided wall start at an edge, not in the middle of a panel. The edges are the windows, the doors, the corners and the line under the roof.

New siding with old, cracked trim is a new wall with the same weak points. That's why we install siding and trim together.
