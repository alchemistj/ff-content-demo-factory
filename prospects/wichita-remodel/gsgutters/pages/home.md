## One Crew for the Whole Outside of Your House

Water is what wears out the outside of a house. It comes off the roof, runs past the edge, down the wall, around the windows and into the wood under the paint.

Gutters, siding, trim, paint and decks are all ways of keeping it out. When one of them fails, the water finds the next.

That's why we do all of it. The crew that hangs your gutters can see where else the water is getting in, so you hire one crew instead of five contractors.

We're insured, the estimate is free, and we take 10% off for veterans and retirees. We bring over eight years of experience to every job.

## Gutters

You notice it when it rains. Water pours over the edge instead of down the downspout, the siding below is streaked, and there's a puddle by the foundation.

Gutters fail in a few ordinary ways. They clog with leaves and shingle grit, and they sag when the fasteners let go.

A hailstorm dents them and splits the seams. A plugged downspout backs up the whole run, and the water goes over the edge and down the wall.

We hang new gutters and downspouts, repair the ones you have, clean them out and put screens on top to keep debris out. If the wood behind a gutter has gone soft, we replace it.

Gutter work moves fast, and you get a straight answer on what your house needs.

## Interior and Exterior Painting

Peeling paint on the trim and soffit is usually the first place a house shows its age. Paint is the skin over the wood at the edges of your house, and bare wood soaks up water.

Paint over rotted wood fails again in a year or two. So where the wood behind a gutter or along the trim has rotted, we replace it, and the paint goes on something solid.

We paint outside and inside, and a freshly painted house looks cared for. Paint the trim and doors while the crew is already there, and you won't need to call a painter later.
