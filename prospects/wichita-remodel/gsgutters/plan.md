## The idea
Water is what wears out the outside of a house, and gutters, siding, trim, paint and decks are all how you keep it out. One owner-led crew that hangs the gutters can see where the water is getting in and fix the whole path, so you hire Nelson's crew once instead of five contractors.

Reviews behind it: Lane P. ("keeping the water out of the house"), Trino B. (gutters, rotted wood around them, then trim and soffit paint), Luis R. (siding, paint, gutters, then back for a deck), Rob C. ("gutter work or any other home repairs"), Roxann S. (kitchen: "first and only call").
