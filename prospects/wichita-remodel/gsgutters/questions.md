2. Years in business. The site says "over eight years of invaluable experience", but the Birdeye profile and the Kansas record give no founding year. Which can we say?

4. Hours: the site footer says Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Is that current? We left hours off the demo until confirmed.
5. Email: the site shows info@gsguttersllc.com in the footer but the top-bar link opens Nelsongaldamez2020@gmail.com. Which should we show?
6. Street address: Google shows 1243 N Poplar St, Wichita, KS 67214. Is that open to customers, or a home or shop address we should keep off the site?
7. Service area: "90-mile radius" of Wichita. Which towns do they work in most, so we can name them?
8. Services to confirm: windows and doors (installed or only trimmed and painted?), floors, interior painting, and kitchen remodels (one review: "redid my kitchen"). Do commercial properties count? Anything else (soffit, fascia, garage doors, rotted-wood repair)?
9. Gutter details: seamless aluminum? Which gutter guard brands? Is there a warranty on gutters, siding or decks?
10. Do the veterans and retirees 10% discount and the free estimate apply to every service?
11. Do they want the "eco-friendly" and "ethical practices" wording from the old site kept? We left it out; no reviews back it.
12. Logo file and brand colors (not sampled). Photos of finished siding, decks and gutters for the gallery.
13. Who else is on the crew? One review credits "Nelson and his son"; we did not name anyone else.
