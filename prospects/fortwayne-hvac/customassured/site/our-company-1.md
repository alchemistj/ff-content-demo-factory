Where it all started!
Rod started in the HVAC trade over 35 years ago sweeping floors and has held every position along the way. During those years, Rod has earned certifications, accreditations, and training for customer service, installation, and technical services that he puts to use every day. Rod is very involved in all facets of the day to day operations of the company and directly oversees sales, installations and job scheduling for customers, as well as ongoing training process for all employees including himself. The other half of the husband-wife team, Kim, is also involved in all the day to day operations of the business. Kim handles the scheduling of service calls, billing, accounting, payroll, and various other office tasks. Kim has a degree in business and participates in training that helps keep Custom Assured running smoothly.
Whats next?
Second generation son, Casey, and his better half, Julie, and been meticulously working side by side with Rod and Kim to learn the ins and outs of the day to day operations at Custom Assured Mechanical. Casey, just like Rod, started in the shop pushing a broom, unloading deliveries, and even fabricating sheet metal for the installation teams since 2007.  Today, after obtaining his Technical Certificate from Ivy Tech, Casey manages the service department at Custom Assured Mechanical. Julie, with a degree in Communications from Purdue University Fort Wayne and a background in customer service, has applied her expertise to assist in the office and make sure that your customer experience at Custom Assured Mechanical runs smoothly from start to finish.
