“We earn our customers for a lifetime, not just a single job.”
Custom Assured Mechanical, Inc. is a family owned and operated company that specializes in the sale, installation, and service of Heating and Air Conditioning systems. Custom Assured started operations in 1998 and has grown over the years to come to reside in our current facility located on Independence Drive in Fort Wayne. We are a company that is big enough to offer an extensive lineup of services and products to meet any heating and cooling need you may have, yet small enough to offer you personal service.
As our name implies we tailor jobs to meet the needs of our customers, whether they are residential, commercial, or new construction clients. We pledge prompt, professional, and personal service during as well as beyond the installation or repair. Our service department is available around the clock. At Custom Assured Mechanical, the job isn't complete until you're satisfied. We earn our customers for a lifetime, not just a single job. It's more than a mission statement, we take it to heart-it's our reputation and our promise.
