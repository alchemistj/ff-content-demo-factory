# USP: Custom Assured Mechanical

Method: `docs/writer-guides/AVATAR-AND-USP.md` section 2. Appeal is scored against `avatars/hvac.md` (a Wichita avatar, reused for Fort Wayne; the fears and wants are the same trade). Exclusivity is scored against the five companies in `competitors/fortwayne-hvac.md`: Summers Plumbing Heating & Cooling, Rolf Griffin Service Experts, Custom Assured Mechanical itself (listed there as #3; its own homepage claims are the source for its row), PG Heating & Cooling, Titan Heating & Air Conditioning (site not reached, so not counted). Custom Assured's exclusivity is therefore scored against the four other companies.

## Business in one line
Custom Assured Mechanical is a Fort Wayne heating and air conditioning company run by owner Rod Schaefer and his wife Kim (facts.md section 2), with Google at 5.0 stars and 90 reviews, and a thin site. Of its 66 pulled reviews, 65 are 5-star and one is 4-star. About 22 mention twice-a-year or annual maintenance.

## Core customer insight (Ted's three lines)
- What the buyer doesn't know: a furnace or AC usually fails on its worst day because the small problems are quiet.
- What most companies do: show up when it breaks, with whoever is on the schedule.
- What this one does instead, per its customers: sends the same technician twice a year, and calls you when it's time.

## Gate result
Top claim: the same-technician-and-we-call-you braid, **16**. **Passes gate (16+),** just. Written as "we try to send the same technician" because the strongest wording comes from one reviewer (Samantha R.), though six reviewers support the pattern.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Fort Wayne homeowners tired of a stranger at the door each time, Custom Assured sends the same technician twice a year and calls you when it's time.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that wait for it to break, Custom Assured puts your furnace and air conditioner on a twice-a-year schedule with one technician who knows the system, and the call to book comes from them.
- **C, emotional outcome:** Lance is our guy. (Linda R.'s own words.)

**Pick: A.** It's the fear of calling a stranger when the system quits, and it is what several reviewers describe in their own words. B is the method section.

## What's thin
- **"Same technician" is a pattern in reviews, not a stated policy.** The site does not say it. Copy says "we try to." Ask Rod whether it is how they schedule.
- **"We call you" is also from reviews** (Gina W., Susan M., Nicole S.). The site does not describe it.
- **Hours.** Contact says Monday to Friday 9 to 4; the homepage says the service department is "available around the clock." Copy uses only the office hours and does not claim after-hours service except for the Custom Priority Club, which the site says includes emergency service.
- **Custom Priority Club.** Named on Our Services; customers call it a yearly service plan or annual priority club agreement. Whether it is still offered, and its terms and price, are not in the folder. No price is stated.
- **Guarantee terms, lender, licenses, certifications.** None stated. The 100% Satisfaction Guarantee appears in the trust bar and a financing paragraph as the site words it, on sales only.
- **The three homepage testimonials (Elizabeth, Kathy, Tom)** are not verified as real customers and are not used.
- **Office names.** Reviews name Julie and Kelly in the office, facts.md says Kim handles scheduling, and the site email is julie@customassured.com. The copy names only Rod and Kim, from the site.
- **Service page.** HVAC maintenance chosen on about 22 on-topic reviews and the Priority Club section of Our Services.
- **Contact form.** The site form uses GET and an invisible reCAPTCHA Enterprise (audit.md).
