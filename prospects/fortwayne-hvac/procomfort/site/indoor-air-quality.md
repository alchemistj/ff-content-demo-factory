What is Indoor Air Quality?
Indoor air quality is the measurement of dust, debris, and other pollutants in the air within your home or business. Several factors, like dust, allergens, and other pollutants, can affect indoor air quality. Poor indoor air quality can make a home uncomfortable and is a potential health hazard to occupants.
We offer indoor air services to help keep your home clean and dust free. Breathe with ease. Call Us Now!
Significance of Indoor Air Quality
Indoor air quality can directly impact our health and safety. If polluted, indoor air can be a health threat when we breathe. Such air exposes us to allergens, bacteria, and other microbes. Additionally, polluted indoor air can cause a foul odor in our homes.
Consequently, poor indoor air quality may have immediate and long-term side effects. Some of the health issues associated with poor indoor air quality and pollution include:
Respiratory diseases
Irritation of eyes, nose, and throat
Headaches
Fatigue
Aggravation of allergies
Heart disease
Cancer
Maintaining good indoor air quality can help you avoid these increased health risks within your home. Clean HVAC systems and air ducts are significantly more efficient and reduce emissions of gasses, thereby contributing to a cleaner, healthier environment.
How to Improve Indoor Air Quality
We can improve our home’s IAQ by frequently cleaning and changing the HVAC system air filters. In addition, we should ensure the HVAC system is regularly inspected and repaired. We can also allow more outdoor air to circulate inside the building when possible to improve ventilation.
