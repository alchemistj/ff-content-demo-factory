Mini-Split Installation
A mini-split air conditioning system is a ductless system with cold and hot coils outside the home or business. It provides the same cooling, heating, and dehumidification qualities as an air conditioning system but without requiring ductwork. The system is controlled from a central remote terminal.
Central Terminal
The central terminal is a module allowing you to control your mini-split air conditioning systems (or any other equipment). The central terminal runs on a dedicated power source and allows a technician to program and control every aspect of your system and monitor its performance. It can control multiple mini-split systems spread throughout a home or business and is the most convenient method of controlling your equipment.
Ductless Mini-split Air Conditioning Systems
The ductless mini-split system is an ideal choice for those with retrofitted homes or buildings where installing traditional split systems is impossible due to design limitations or lack of space. Ductless systems can be installed virtually anywhere and are designed to fit any shape and size area. The system includes a small compressor (it does not require electricity), which is mounted inside the building and provides ventilation to the outside coils. The centralized control unit can be installed in a closet, on a counter, or even mounted on the wall. The system also comes with an optional remote thermostat that allows you to adjust your temperature from anywhere in the home while out or away from your home.
