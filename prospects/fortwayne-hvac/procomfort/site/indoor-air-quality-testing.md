Reliable Indoor Air Quality Testing
Many of the contaminants that are able to get into your home come from outside. However, there are also some indoor sources that must be taken into account, which include everything from pets and mold to construction materials. If your home is poorly ventilated, indoor air contaminants can build up to dangerous levels.
Volatile organic compounds can be found in many consumer products that you might use throughout your home, which extend to rugs, shower curtains, paint, glue, and printers. These compounds have relatively low boiling points, which means that they can produce hazardous vapors when at room temperature. Mold is another contaminant that can be present in the wall cavities around your home. The spores that are produced from mold can worsen the quality of your indoor air.
The main component of our indoor air quality testing involves VOC tests. This type of test takes around two hours to be completed and allows us to collect an air sample that can identify the exact concentration of volatile organic compounds in your home.
As for mold, there are many different types that can get into your home, some of which are more hazardous than others. Laboratory testing is required to determine the exact type of mold, after which we can take steps to get rid of it and restore your indoor air quality.
If mold is present in your home, you might smell a somewhat musty odor. However, a professional assessment is needed to determine what remedies are required. Our mold tests include protocols for how to safely get rid of the mold around your home. If you attempt to do so yourself, the mold spores could be disturbed and spread all throughout the indoor air.
Benefits to Improving Your Indoor Air Quality
Exposure to indoor air contaminants can cause many health issues over an extended period of time. Avoiding these health issues is the main benefit of having your indoor air quality tested and making improvements to it. For one, you’ll be able to breathe easier. When you breathe in contaminated air, you’re more likely to experience allergies and illnesses that worsen your health and increase your stress levels.
You’ll also be able to sleep better since lower stress levels make it easier to get a good night’s sleep. Once your indoor air has been tested, you can also focus on removing any remaining pollutants and allergens, which should keep you healthy all throughout the year.
Another clear benefit of improving your air quality is that your utility bills should be lower. If the poor air quality is caused by an issue with your air conditioner, fixing this problem will improve the unit’s efficiency. These changes should immediately show up in your monthly energy bill.
