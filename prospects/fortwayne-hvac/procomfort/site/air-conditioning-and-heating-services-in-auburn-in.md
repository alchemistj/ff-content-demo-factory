About Auburn, IN
Auburn, Indiana, in De Kalb County, is 17 miles north of Fort Wayne and 79 miles west of Toledo. The city is part of the Fort Wayne metro region.
Wesley Park established the city of Auburn in 1836. The town was named after the English city of Auburn. Auburn is DeKalb County’s county seat. From 1935 through 1959, the Auburn Rubber Company was one of America’s major rubber toy producers. Auburn is known as the “Home of the Classics,” in allusion to the ‘classic’ vehicles that were previously made there.
One may visit the house of author and wildlife photographer Gene Stratton-Porter from Auburn. Nearby attractions include the Mid-America Windmill Museum, the Hoosier Air Museum, and the Auburn Cord Duesenberg Museum. The city contains many parks with facilities for numerous sports and other activities. Every year, Auburn also holds the popular Auburn Cord Duesenberg Festival and HistoryFest.
