Frequently Asked Questions About HVAC Systems
When homeowners’ HVAC systems fail, they become intrigued about how these technologies, which they frequently take for granted, function. Here are some of the most frequently asked questions about heating and cooling units and services from Pro Comfort Heating & Cooling.
What is an HVAC system and how does it work?
HVAC systems are meant to circulate air while simultaneously heating or cooling an indoor environment. These systems typically consist of three basic components: a heating unit, a cooling unit, and a duct to convey the air. The ventilation that introduces fresh air into a building is the first step in an HVAC system. Natural ventilation employs pressure differences to move air through a building using open windows, doors, or gaps. This necessitates the use of fans, blowers, filters, and ducts in today’s closely sealed houses and businesses.
To improve house comfort, air purifiers, humidifiers, dehumidifiers, and other indoor air quality equipment can be added to the air system. These elements are then used to provide maximum thermal comfort via a traditional or smart thermostat. A smart thermostat with Wi-Fi connectivity allows you to make tiny adjustments throughout the day to improve energy efficiency.
How often should I change my HVAC air filter?
Most air filter manufacturers and HVAC providers recommend replacing your air filter every 90 days, or every three months. This might vary depending on where you live (for example dusty, dry regions), whether you have pets, and the age of your system and equipment. If you have pets in the house, we recommend replacing your filter every 60 days, or 2 months, and every 20-45 days for houses with many dogs or persons with allergies or respiratory disorders. Filters in vacation homes or unoccupied properties that are rarely used may usually be changed every 9-12 months. The prevailing opinion is that the more you use your house, the more frequently you should replace your air filter.
What are the benefits of regular HVAC maintenance?
You rely on your HVAC system to keep you comfortable all year. Maintaining your system on a regular basis allows it to create and distribute warm or cold air more evenly and reliably. It also lowers the chances of coming home from work one winter day to discover chilly air blasting through your vents. Investing in periodic HVAC maintenance may also save you money on future repairs or system breakdowns.

A properly sized air conditioner for your house will operate considerably more efficiently. When your system is too large or too tiny, you may have an unpleasant interior climate as well as increased energy expenditures. When determining which size HVAC system is best for your home, you need to understand the different types of HVAC units and take note of the square footage in your home. How can I improve the energy efficiency of my HVAC system?
The measurement and verification (M&V) of your HVAC’s existing energy usage should be the first step towards optimizing it. Measuring the needed energy input vs the present system’s output provides an accurate baseline of its performance.
Furthermore, innovations in technology employed throughout the system enable you to evaluate current use and compute future savings. Installing programmable control systems, ensuring the HVAC system is properly sized, and scheduling periodic preventative maintenance and tune-ups for your HVAC system can help you save the most energy.

How long does an HVAC system typically last?
The typical HVAC system lifespan ranges from 10 to 25 years, depending on your environment and the sort of heating and cooling system you have. However, if your HVAC system has been overworked owing to poor insulation and undesired airflow (due to open air leaks), it may last less time. Regular maintenance is an excellent approach to guarantee that your device lasts its entire intended lifespan.
