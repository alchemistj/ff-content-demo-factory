Furnace Installation in Anderson, IN
During a furnace installation, a Pro Comfort technician will perform several important tasks to ensure that your new furnace is installed correctly and safely. These tasks may include:
Safety checks: The technician will inspect your home to ensure that there are no potential safety hazards, such as gas leaks or insufficient ventilation.
Removing old equipment: The technician will remove your old furnace and any other related equipment, such as ductwork or chimneys.
Installing the new furnace: The technician will carefully install the new furnace in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions and local building codes.
Reconnecting & Sealing: The technician will reconnect any ductwork or other components, and seal any gaps or openings to ensure that your furnace is operating efficiently.
Performance test: The technician will run a series of tests to ensure that the furnace is operating correctly and efficiently. Once the installation is complete, the technician will show you how to operate and maintain your new furnace.
