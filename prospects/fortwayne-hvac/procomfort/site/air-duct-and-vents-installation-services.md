Signs Your Ductwork Is Bad
The proper installation and maintenance of air ducts is critical to keep the HVAC system in your home running as efficiently as possible. Your ductwork is designed to send heated or cooled air throughout your home, starting at your furnace or heat pump. Regular duct maintenance and duct cleaning is important to ensure everything continues to run smoothly. Dirty ductwork tends to produce stale air, which is why your house may seem cold despite being adequately heated.
Hot & Cold Spots
Generally, if the air ducts in your home are in good condition and installed properly, you should feel a consistent temperature in all areas of your home. However, if you notice hot or cold spots, it is probably because there are leaks in your ductwork. One of our experts in HVAC air duct and vent installation can quickly locate the leaks and repair or replace the ducts to keep your house comfortable.
Low Indoor Air Quality
If there are significant leaks in your ductwork, it will allow outside air to flow into the system, causing irregularities in air temperature and decreased efficiency. For example, you may feel warm air coming from your outlets despite your home already being warmer than what is comfortable for you. This is one of the most common signs of improper installation and ductwork leakage. The large volume of air inside the ductwork makes it difficult for your heating and cooling units to do their jobs, and the result is that your house gets too warm. Little or no airflow from supply vent registers and registers is also a common sign of improper ductwork installation and seal.
High Utility Bills
If your home is getting too cold or too hot, it may be due to poor insulation in your attic or walls. You might have a lousy duct installation system as well. If you experience high gas bills even though your thermostat is not set to extreme temperatures, this may be because there are leaks in your air ducts that allow air to escape indoors, increasing the utility bill. When you experience high bills due to a loss of hot or cool air, your ducts need repair and replacement.
