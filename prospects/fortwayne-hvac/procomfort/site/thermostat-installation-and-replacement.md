Upgrades for Convenience and Savings
Not so many years ago, homes had simple thermostats that did not give you very accurate control. You often had to keep adjusting the temperature dial to get it just right, and it usually involved a lot of trial and error. On top of that, if you forgot to adjust the thermostat before leaving home, your system utilized a lot of energy controlling the temperature in an empty house.
Today’s thermostats are far more user-friendly and energy efficient. First of all, temperature settings are made with a digital display that’s precise and easy to read. No more nudging a tiny dial to make the system kick on. We can also install a programmable thermostat. These are great for keeping the system at minimum operation while nobody’s home, then allowing it to come on just in time to get the temperature to a perfect level as everyone arrives home.
One exciting option we offer is the smart thermostat. You’ll love the option of being able to control your system remotely through your cell phone while at work or even on vacation. Wherever you are, you can adjust your thermostat and get your house to just the right temperature.
