4 Ways To Improve the Indoor Air Quality of Your Columbia City, IN Home
July 12, 2023Heating & Cooling Blogs
In the United States, the majority of people spend 90% of their time indoors.
Since we spend so much time inside, ensuring optimal indoor air quality is crucial.
Without proper air quality, people can suffer from a number of different health issues, including allergies and respiratory problems. The good news is there are many ways to improve indoor air quality in your home.
Having and maintaining a healthy living environment isn’t as difficult as it sounds. There are several effective ways to improve the air quality in your home and we are going to tell you all about them in this guide.
These four tips will give you better air quality and give you peace of mind.
1. Invest in Proper Ventilation
We know life can get busy at times and proper ventilation in your home may not be at the forefront of your mind. However, it’s important that you enhance the ventilation in your home by opening your windows and doors to let fresh air in.
You should also invest in exhaust fans. This will help keep the air clean as well.
By having a well-ventilated home, you’ll not only reduce the concentration of pollutants, but you’ll also reduce stuffiness, which will improve the comfortability of your home.
2. Keep a Clean Living Environment
One of the easiest things you can do to improve indoor air quality in your home is to keep it clean. Dusting and vacuuming regularly will ensure dust, mold, and other particles don’t build up in your home in Columbia City.
It’s also important that you don’t forget to clean or replace the filters in your HVAC system. The filter’s job is to trap harmful particles and airborne pollutants.
However, it can’t do that effectively if it’s dirty. The best thing you can do is work with a professional. They will be able to ensure a clean HVAC system and give you maintenance tips.
3. Limit Chemical Pollutants
When you’re cleaning your home, you should be using low-toxicity products. Certain cleaning products can greatly affect your health. By investing in eco-friendly and safe cleaning materials, you’ll have better air quality.
It’s recommended that you use natural alternatives such as vinegar, baking soda, or lemon juice for cleaning purposes.
4. Control Humidity Levels
Excess humidity can be very damaging to your healthy air quality. It can lead to the growth of mold, mildew, and dust mites, which is not what you want in your Columbia City, IN home.
You can improve air quality by using dehumidifiers in areas prone to dampness, such as basements, laundry rooms, and bathrooms.
