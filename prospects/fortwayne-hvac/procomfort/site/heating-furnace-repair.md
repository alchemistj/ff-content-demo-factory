Heating Systems Repair
It’s common for heating systems to experience issues and require repair from time to time. If you’re having problems with your heating system, the first important step we take is to identify the source of the issue and take the necessary steps to fix it as soon as possible. Here are some general steps we follow to repair your heating system:
1. We determine the type of heating system you have
Because there are several types of heating systems, such as furnaces, boilers, and heat pumps, each system type has its unique components and potential problems.
2. We identify the problem
We pinpoint the specific issue you’re experiencing with your heating system. Is it not turning on, or is it not heating your home effectively? Is there a strange noise from the system, or is it leaking?
3. We check the thermostat
We ensure the thermostat is set to the correct temperature and functioning correctly. A malfunctioning thermostat can cause issues with your heating system.
4. We check the air filters
Dirty or clogged air filters can restrict airflow and cause problems with your heating system.
5. We check for visible issues
We look for any visible issues with your heating system, such as loose or disconnected wires, leaking fluids, or damaged parts.
