Duct Leakage – this means that some rooms in your home aren’t receiving cooling or heating. Obtaining the ideal temperature in your home with a leaking duct is not possible.
Under-insulated ducts – if your ducts are not insulated, heat will easily escape, therefore, obtaining ideal temperatures for your home will not be possible.
Unbalanced airflow – this means your duct is not distributing heating or cooling evenly in different rooms in your home.
