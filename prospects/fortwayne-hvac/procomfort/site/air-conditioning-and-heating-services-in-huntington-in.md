About Huntington, IN
Huntington, Indiana is located in Huntington County, 25 miles southwest of Fort Wayne and 84 miles northeast of Indianapolis. The community benefits from convenient access to the adjacent cities and villages that are part of the Fort Wayne metropolitan region.
Native Americans were the first to settle in Huntington. Europeans later arrived there in the 1830s. The area’s initial settlers were Joel and Champion Helvey. Huntington was founded in 1848 and was named after Samuel Huntington, a signer of the Declaration of Independence and the Governor of Connecticut at the time.
You may arrange a day excursion to the Huntington County Historical Museum, Sunken Gardens, Waltonian Gardens, and Shakespeare Gardens while staying in Huntington. J.
