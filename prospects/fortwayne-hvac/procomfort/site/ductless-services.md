Ductless Systems Repair
If your ductless system is not functioning properly, there are a few common issues we can help you troubleshoot and fix. These include:
Clogged or dirty filters: Check the filters and clean or replace them if they are dirty.
Frozen coils: If the outdoor unit’s coils are frozen, it could be due to low refrigerant levels or a malfunctioning fan. We Check the refrigerant levels and make sure the fan is functioning properly.
Lack of power: We ensure the system is properly plugged in and the circuit breaker or fuse has not tripped.
Malfunctioning thermostat: If the thermostat is not working properly, it could prevent the system from functioning properly. Replace the batteries or calibrate the thermostat.
Repairing your ductless system on your own can be dangerous and may nullify your warranty if the problem is complex. As such, it’s always wise to contact professionals in case of any complications.
Reliable Ductless System Installation
To ensure that your ductless system is installed correctly and operates reliably, we follow these steps:
Properly locate and install outdoor and indoor units.
We locate interior and exterior units in easily accessible and maintainable locations. Install the interior units on a high wall or in the ceiling, and place the outside unit on a firm, level surface.
Determine the proper size and number of unit(s).
We select the appropriate size and quantity of units for your requirements. If your system is too small, it will not be able to offer adequate heating or cooling, and if it is too large, it will turn on and off too frequently, reducing efficiency and increasing wear and tear. In addition, we perform load calculation to ensure optimal sizing for your system.
We make sure the system is properly charged and tested.
In a ductless system, the performance and efficiency are directly proportional to the refrigerant charge. After being charged and tested by an expert at Pro Comfort, the system will function at peak efficiency.
