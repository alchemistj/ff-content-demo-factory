5 Summer HVAC Maintenance Tips for Homeowners in Auburn, IN
May 17, 2023Heating & Cooling Blogs
As the scorching summer heat creeps up on Auburn, IN, the spotlight falls on your home savior – your HVAC system. So you don’t want this crucial system to disappoint when you need it most. And that means staying on top of HVAC maintenance.
To help you, we’ve put together this short guide. Here, we’ll explain five simple tips you can try today to help ensure your air conditioning is in the best condition. Read on to learn more.
1. Schedule a Service
You’ll need to schedule an inspection and service of your HVAC to keep it well-maintained. Using professionals to service your HVAC is the best way to get optimal performance. That’s vital in those intense summer months.
These routine checks help professionals diagnose and solve minor issues. And that could help you avoid costlier problems down the road.
It’s one of the smartest ways to ensure you get longer life from your air conditioning. And save money on major repairs.
2. Clean Your Outdoor Unit
Regular HVAC maintenance of an outdoor unit is as essential as indoors. Clearing debris and dirt is your first step in keeping this clean. That will help prevent blockages and damage to the system.
Over time, the outdoor unit can collect dust. That can affect its performance and efficiency.
To clean it, first, turn off the unit for safety. Then, hose it down to remove any accumulated dirt. Avoid using a high-pressure water stream, as it could damage the unit.
3. Change the Air Filters
Air filters are a central part of your HVAC system. You must clean them as part of your summer HVAC maintenance routine. They will also need replacing when they show signs of wear to ensure optimal performance.
A clean air filter will make your HVAC run with efficiency. That, in turn, will positively impact your indoor air quality. That’s because the air conditioning won’t need to work as hard to keep your house cool.
4. Upgrade Your Insulation
One way to keep your HVAC in top condition is to address your insulation.
Proper insulation plays a big part in energy efficiency, especially during summer. It will keep cool air inside your home and hot air out. In other words, it helps reduce the strain on your HVAC system.
If you have modern insulation, check it and make it part of your annual HVAC maintenance routine. If your insulation is old, consider upgrading or replacing it. That takes investment but saves you money in the long run by reducing energy costs.
5. Upgrade to a Smart Thermostat
It may be time to consider upgrading to a smart or programmable thermostat. Doing so can revolutionize your Auburn, IN, home’s comfort.
These devices give you superior control over your HVAC settings. So you can set the temperature to one that best suits you. That will help you manage your energy costs too.
HVAC Maintenance: Keeping Your Home Comfortable
Proactive HVAC maintenance is vital to enjoying a cool and comfortable Auburn, IN, summer. Don’t wait for a breakdown to realize the importance of a well-maintained system.
