Air Purifiers
Our air purifiers use a three-step charge/capture/kill procedure to clean the air passing through the air handler of your HVAC system:
Charge: A “cloud” of electrically charged ions is produced by the purifier, and as airborne dust, pollen, viruses, germs, and other particles pass through it, they attach to the ions.
Capture: Similar to how a magnet attracts metal shavings, the ionized particles are drawn toward an oppositely charged, pleated MERV 15-rated filter and are rapidly captured.
Kill: Airborne microbes caught stay on the pleated filter rather than returning to the house and are exposed to a strong electric field. 99% of particular viruses and bacteria are rendered inactive there.
Cleaner Indoor Air
Having cleaner indoor air is important because your home can serve as a place where pathogens like mold and viruses that are carried in the air can thrive. To reduce the spread of viruses, the American Society of Heating, Refrigerating, and Air-Conditioning Engineers (ASHRAE) advises upgrading central air filtration to MERV 13 or higher. Our air purifiers offer an exceptional MERV 15 measure of efficiency for residential air filters because you have to catch it before killing it.
