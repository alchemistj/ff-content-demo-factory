Completing a Ductless Cooling Installation
The process for a ductless cooling installation typically includes the following steps:
Site assessment: A qualified Pro Comfort technician will assess the location and determine the best placement for the outdoor unit and indoor air handlers. This includes determining the power supply and making sure that the area is suitable for a ductless cooling system.
Equipment selection: A technician will help you choose the right ductless cooling unit based on the size of your space, cooling needs, and budget.
Installation: We will install the outdoor unit, indoor air handlers, and run the conduit between them. This conduit houses the refrigerant lines, power wires, and condensate drain.
Electrical and refrigerant connections: We will connect the power supply to the unit and charge the system with refrigerant.
Testing and commissioning: We will test the unit to ensure it is operating properly, and make any necessary adjustments.
Maintenance: We will provide information on how to maintain the unit and schedule regular maintenance to ensure optimal performance.
It is important to note that ductless cooling installation should be done by a qualified HVAC professional, as it involves handling refrigerant and electrical wiring.
Examining the Benefits of a Ductless Heating Installation
Ductless heating systems, also known as mini-split systems, offer a number of benefits over traditional HVAC systems. One of the main benefits is increased energy efficiency. Ductless systems do not have the energy loss associated with ductwork, which can account for as much as 30% of a home’s energy consumption. Additionally, ductless systems allow for individual temperature control in different rooms or zones, allowing homeowners to only heat the areas of the home that are being used.
Another benefit of ductless heating systems is their flexibility in installation. Ductless systems do not require extensive renovation or remodeling, as they can be easily mounted on walls or ceilings. This makes them a great option for homes without existing ductwork, or for additions and renovations where running new ductwork would be impractical. They also tend to be much quieter than traditional systems and don’t require as much space. Ductless systems are also easier to maintain and service, as the outdoor unit and indoor unit(s) can be easily accessed.
