Maintenance Tips for Ductless Heating and Cooling System
We should ensure our ductless heating and cooling maintenance is always done on schedule. There are simple maintenance practices we can do on our own while we leave professionals to do the technical bit. Here are some of the key maintenance tasks that need to be completed regularly on a ductless system.
Clean and Replace Filters Regularly
Filters are vital in ensuring quality air is circulating in our homes. They also protect the entire unit from dust accumulation. Therefore, we should thoroughly clean air filters with a dry cloth to remove dust and dirt.
With proper maintenance, the filters will be efficient. However, if the filters are spoiled, we can replace them. When your filters are properly cleaned and replaced, your ductless system will function properly.
Wipe and Clean the Outside
Cleaning the exterior of the ductless heating system hinders dust and dirt from amassing within the unit. Otherwise, the dust and dirt can easily get into the unit. We can use a damp cloth and a suitable chemical cleaner to wipe the ductless system exterior gently.
In addition, the outdoor part of the vents should be kept free of dust and dirt. Too much dust and dirt can easily clog the unit leading to malfunction.
Clean the Coil and Condenser
These are essential parts of the ductless system located on the outer unit. The coil and condenser are involved in removing heat from the refrigerant. If left unchecked, dirt, mold, and debris will accumulate, causing the whole system to fail. We can clean the coil and condenser using the manufacturer’s care instructions manual.
Clear the Pipes
The ductless air conditioner pipes connect the system’s interior and exterior portions. They are responsible for discharging cold or hot air in the room. They need proper maintenance to avoid being blocked due to the accumulation of dust and dirt. Failure to which temperature-regulated air will not circulate properly in the room.
Ductless Air Conditioning System Installation
A Pro Comfort technician will first choose a location outside. This is where the outside condenser unit with the compressor will be installed. The surface must be level and at least one foot away from the exterior walls of the house. A concrete pad is then installed where the condenser unit will sit.
The ideal interior installation location is be near the ceiling for efficient heating and cooling. We will ensure measurements are taken carefully before drilling holes in the wall so we get it done right the first time.
Once a location is chosen and the holes are drilled,, tubes and refrigerant lines from the outside condenser unit are passed through the interior wall hole into the interior unit. Then a line on the interior side for the condensation unit is installed.
