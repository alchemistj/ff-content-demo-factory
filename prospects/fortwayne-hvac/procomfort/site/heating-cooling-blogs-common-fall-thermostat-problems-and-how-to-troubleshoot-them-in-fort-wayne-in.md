Common Fall Thermostat Problems and How to Troubleshoot Them in Fort Wayne, IN
September 18, 2023Heating & Cooling Blogs
Have you been using the same thermostat in your home for more than a decade now? If so, it shouldn’t come as a huge surprise if you start to experience thermostat furnace problems with it this fall.
The average furnace thermostat is only going to last for about 10 years before it will need to be replaced. If you attempt to push it further than that, you’ll inevitably run into thermostat problems and potentially even thermostat failure.
But that isn’t to say that you won’t experience any thermostat issues before then. Even if your thermostat is under 10 years old, you might still have to do thermostat troubleshooting at some point.
Here are several of the most common fall thermostat furnace problems and how to troubleshoot them in Fort Wayne, IN.
Dead Batteries
Most thermostats, including some newer smart thermostats, will be battery-operated. For this reason, you’ll need to make sure that your thermostat always has fresh batteries in it.
If the batteries in a thermostat die, it will essentially shut down your whole HVAC system and stop it from working for the time being. It might leave you wondering if you’ll need to have furnace repairs made.
Before you get too freaked out about this, you should simply try replacing the batteries in your thermostat. It could put all your thermostat furnace problems behind you and enable you to stay warm this winter in Fort Wayne, IN.
Loose Wiring
Has a recently installed thermostat started to cause thermostat furnace problems for you? If you installed it yourself, you might have loose wiring that is preventing your thermostat from working like it’s supposed to.
In this case, you’ll most likely need to Google “heating near me” and hire a Fort Wayne, IN heating and cooling maintenance company to come figure out what’s wrong with your thermostat. They should be able to fix the loose wiring in it and get it up and running again.
Wrong Positioning
Is your home’s thermostat installed on an interior wall? Is it away from windows and sunlight? Is it positioned in a place that isn’t too close to an exterior door?
If you didn’t answer “yes” to all three of these questions, your thermostat might be in the wrong spot, and this could lead to it not working as it should. You’re going to encounter your fair share of thermostat furnace problems in Fort Wayne, IN when a thermostat isn’t installed in the proper place. You should look for a better spot for it to ensure it works correctly.
