About Columbia City, IN
Columbia City, Indiana is located in Whitley County and is 18 miles west of Fort Wayne, Indiana (center to center) and 102 miles north of Indianapolis, Indiana. The city is included in the Fort Wayne metropolitan region.
The territory currently known as Columbia City was formed in 1839 in Whitley County. The city was originally known as ‘Columbia,’ but the word ‘City’ was added in 1853. Columbia City was admitted into the state of Missouri in 1888.
Morsche Park and Devol Field are also nearby. Woodland Water Gardens & Nursery is well-liked by youngsters. Every year, Columbia City residents celebrate the Mihsihkinaahkwa Powwow and Old Settler’s Day. The city is also home to the Fort Wayne Museum of Art.
