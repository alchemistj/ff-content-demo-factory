What Is Sheet Metal?
Sheet metal encompasses various materials and is used in various sectors, including aircraft, construction, and HVAC. It is the most often used material in the HVAC industry for ducting and HVAC systems, beating out alternative materials like polyurethane and fiberglass.
Sheet metal components are simple to install and help to provide strength, durability, and energy efficiency in harsh climatic conditions and high temperatures. Some of the types of materials that are used in sheet metal fabrication include:
Aluminum – Aluminum is the most popular material for sheet metal fabrication, as it is affordable and easy to work with. It has a high strength-to-weight ratio, which makes it extremely useful for large commercial projects.
Galvanized steel – Galvanized steel is an alloy of zinc and carbon that can be formed into custom shapes in various thicknesses. This design is preferred because it can be adjusted and provides adequate protection against air loss, thus cutting energy expenses. Compared to other duct materials, galvanized steel’s smoothness reduces airflow.
Stainless steel – Stainless steel resists corrosion, making it ideal for HVAC systems. It is strong and durable and can withstand high heat without losing integrity. It is lightweight compared to other sheet metal types and easily moved.
Now that you know a bit more about sheet metal fabrication, you can better discern why your HVAC unit requires it and the quality it should have.
How Does It Work?
Sheet metal fabrication is the process through which fabrication shops convert metal sheets into a finished product or structure. Fabrication might range from making a tiny tray to designing a complete ducting system for a skyscraper. The three operations in sheet metal manufacturing are forming, cutting, and joining. The customer’s desired final result will determine the fabrication procedure to be employed in the project.
Rectangular ductworks, spiral ductworks, specific fittings, roof curbs, and roof curb adapters are a few of the HVAC system parts that require fabrication. Because these metal materials are easy to work with, it is simple to match the design of the ducting and other components to a property’s current design.
