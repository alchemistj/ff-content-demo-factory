Method: `docs/writer-guides/AVATAR-AND-USP.md` section 2. Appeal is scored against `avatars/hvac.md` (a Wichita avatar, reused for Fort Wayne; the fears and wants are the same trade). Exclusivity is scored against the five companies in `competitors/fortwayne-hvac.md`: Summers Plumbing Heating & Cooling, Rolf Griffin Service Experts, Custom Assured Mechanical, PG Heating & Cooling, Titan Heating & Air Conditioning (Titan's site was not reached, so it is not counted).

## Business in one line
JD Kuhn Heating And Air Conditioning is a locally owned Fort Wayne shop (Google: 4.9 stars, 175 reviews) founded by Justin Daniel Kuhn Sr., with one weak one-page site. Its 52-review pull is 50 five-star, one 2-star and one 1-star; both low reviews are about an unexpected charge (facts.md section 10).

## Core customer insight (Ted's three lines)
- What the buyer doesn't know: whether the system needs replacing or one part and a cleaning.
- What most companies do: quote the big job.
- What this one does instead: in two reviews, looks first. Tim D. had a competitor ask for a full AC replacement and had his old system cleaned and recharged instead. Angel S. had a second look that "got to the possible root of my problem" before buying a furnace.

## Gate result
Top claim: "looks before the big replacement quote," **15**. **Below gate** (16+ needed). The demo leads with it as the best honest claim, and the strategy page carries "The offer we'd build with you" (below).

## The offer we'd build with you (proposal, scored the same way)
A second-opinion visit for anyone holding another company's replacement quote: the visit price is texted before anyone is booked, and the repair and the replacement are written side by side. Appeal 5 (fears #2 and #5, and it answers the 1-star and 2-star reviews about an unexpected charge), Exclusivity 4 (none of the 4 fetched sites states a second-opinion price; PG states an after-hours fee only). Score **20**. Not something JD Kuhn does today. Terms and price are Justin's to set.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Fort Wayne homeowners handed a whole-system replacement quote, JD Kuhn is the shop that looks at what you have first, so you decide with the facts in front of you.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that lead with the new-system quote, JD Kuhn starts with the system you have, and when new equipment is the answer, the estimate is free and the work is quoted up front.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You stop wondering whether the big number is real.

**Pick: A.** It names fear #2 in the buyer's words and has the only named customer story. B is the outreach angle, because the "free and quoted up front" half is on their own site.

## What's thin

(2 stars) was quoted $95 and billed $336. Copy never says second opinions are free. Ask Justin what the visit charge is and when it is told.
- **No license, brand, warranty or financing.** All Unverified in facts.md, so none appears in the copy.
- **Service area.** The site names no city or towns; the demo says Fort Wayne only (Google address 4625 Speedway Dr, Fort Wayne, IN 46825).
- **The 24-hour emergency service** is a feature of the yearly service agreement per the site FAQ, not a standing promise to everyone. The demo says so.
- **The domain conflict.** The site is jdkuhnhvac.com; the email on it is justin@jdkuhnheating.com (facts.md section 11). That is also the address for the hand send.
- **Erich vs Eric.** Reviews spell the technician both ways, so the copy does not name him.
- **Service page.** HVAC replacement was chosen on 8 on-topic reviews (Adele A., Zack C., Brandon L., Angel S., Denny B., Mark Y., Nancy P., Dave R.) plus the site FAQ's free-estimate line. The site has no service pages.
