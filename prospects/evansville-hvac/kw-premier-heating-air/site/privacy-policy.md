This information is collected using cookies and similar technologies, described below.
How we use cookies and tracking tools
Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website, such as which pages are most helpful and where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information about your visit. You can learn how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
Google Ads. We advertise on Google. When you click one of our ads, Google adds a click identifier to the web address. Our website stores this identifier in a cookie and may include it with any form you submit, so we can tell which ads lead to service requests. Google Ads also measures whether ad clicks lead to calls or form submissions. You can manage how Google personalizes ads in your Google Ads Settings.
Call tracking. When you visit our website from a Google ad, you may see a tracking phone number provided by our call-tracking service, CallRail. Calls to that number are forwarded directly to our office. CallRail records details of the call, such as the phone number you called from, caller ID name, date, time and length of the call, and which ad or search led to the call. We do not record the contents of calls. To measure which ads lead to calls, CallRail may share a securely encoded (hashed) version of the calling phone number with Google.
Spam protection. Our website uses Google reCAPTCHA to keep out spam and automated abuse. reCAPTCHA collects information about your device and how you interact with the page. Use of reCAPTCHA is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
Your choices. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. If you block cookies, the website will still work, but some features, such as form attribution, may not.
