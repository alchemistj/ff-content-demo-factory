FAMILY-OWNED HVAC EXPERTS
All About K&W Premier
K&W Premier Heating & Air was founded in 2018 with one van and a mission to bring honest, reliable HVAC service to the Evansville and Wadesville communities. Built from the merger of two local businesses, we’ve grown into a family-owned team rooted in trust, craftsmanship, and care.
We offer heating, cooling, and indoor air quality solutions for homes and small businesses, with EPA-licensed technicians trained on nearly all major brands. As certified dealers for both Ruud and American Standard, we proudly install and service those systems, but we also work with all other major manufacturers to give you the flexibility and confidence to choose what’s best for your home.
From routine service to 24/7 emergency calls, we’re here to keep you comfortable year-round. At K&W, you’re not just another customer—you’re a neighbor, and we treat you like family.
About Us
Local & Family-Owned
At K&W Premier Heating & Air, we believe comfort should be simple. When your AC breaks on a hot summer day, or your furnace struggles in the middle of winter, you need a team that shows up quickly and gets the job done right.
We’re a family-owned HVAC company based in Evansville, proudly serving our neighbors across southern Indiana, western Kentucky, and parts of Illinois. From same-day residential repairs to full commercial installations and new construction projects, we bring experience, honesty, and a personal touch to every job.
Our Process
From Call To Comfort
Let's Get Started
Step 1
Book An Appointment
Call us or fill out our quick online form to book a time. We’ll confirm your appointment and gather the details we need about your heating, cooling, or generator system.
Step 2
Repairs, or New Installs
Our licensed technician will arrive on time, explain the best solution, and handle the work whether it’s a furnace repair, AC maintenance, or a full HVAC installation.
Step 3
Satisfaction Guaranteed
Once the job is done, we’ll test your system, tidy up the workspace, and make sure you’re completely satisfied. If you ever need us again, we’re just a call away for reliable HVAC service in Evansville.
