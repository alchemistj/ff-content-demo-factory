How do I know if I need a new HVAC system or just a repair?
If your system is over 10 years old, breaking down frequently, or your energy bills keep rising, it may be time to consider a replacement. We’ll inspect your system and give you honest advice on the most cost-effective option.
What brands of HVAC equipment do you install?
We’re certified to install and service most major HVAC manufacturers. Instead of pushing a single brand, we match you with the equipment that best fits your home, budget, and long-term needs.
What areas do you serve?
While we’re based in Evansville, we regularly take on projects across Indiana, Kentucky, and Illinois. Recent work has taken us as far as Indianapolis. If your job requires skilled HVAC professionals, we’re happy to discuss travel options.
Do you offer emergency HVAC services?
Yes! We understand that heating or cooling issues don’t always happen during business hours. That’s why we offer emergency repair services to restore comfort to your home quickly.
What services do you offer?
We provide a full range of heating, cooling, and generator services, including system installation, repairs, seasonal maintenance, and indoor air quality solutions. Whether you need help with your furnace, AC unit, heat pump, or thermostat, we’ve got you covered.
Why is my AC running but not cooling the house?
This can be caused by low refrigerant, a frozen coil, dirty filters, or airflow restrictions. If basic troubleshooting doesn’t fix it, our team can diagnose the issue quickly and prevent further damage to your system.
