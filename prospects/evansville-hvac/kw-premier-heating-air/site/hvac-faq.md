Can you work from construction plans?
Yes. We work with general contractors and developers on new commercial construction and tenant build-outs, including equipment specification, sizing, and coordinating installation around your build schedule.
Can you handle multiple locations?
Yes. We work with property managers and multi-location businesses across southern Indiana, western Kentucky, and southern Illinois, and we can put every property on one maintenance schedule with a single point of contact.
What does a commercial maintenance agreement cover?
Scheduled seasonal inspections of every unit, filter changes, coil cleaning, refrigerant and electrical checks, and a written report after each visit, plus priority scheduling when something does go wrong. Plans are built around your equipment and how your building is used.
Do you offer after-hours service for businesses?
Yes. Our emergency line is answered 24/7, including nights and weekends. Call 812-589-9338 and we’ll get a technician headed your way. Commercial systems don’t only fail during business hours.
How do whole-home standby generators work?
A standby generator monitors your home’s power supply. When an outage occurs, it automatically turns on within seconds and supplies electricity to your home until utility power is restored. Once power returns, it shuts off on its own.
What size generator do I need for my home?
The right generator size depends on the appliances and systems you want to power during an outage. We’ll assess your home’s electrical load and recommend a model that provides reliable backup without oversizing or unnecessary cost.
