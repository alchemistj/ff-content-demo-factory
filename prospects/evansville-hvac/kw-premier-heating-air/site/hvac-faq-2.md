Why is my AC leaking water?
Water leaks usually point to a clogged drain line, frozen evaporator coil, or damaged drain pan. These issues can lead to bigger problems if ignored, so it’s best to schedule a service call as soon as you notice moisture around your unit.
What types of air conditioning systems do you install?
We install central air conditioning systems, ductless mini-splits, heat pumps, and high-efficiency cooling units. Whether your home needs a full replacement or an add-on system for hard-to-cool spaces, we’ll recommend the best fit for your layout and budget.
How can I lower my heating bills in the winter?
Simple steps like replacing filters regularly, sealing drafts, and using a programmable thermostat can make a big difference. For bigger savings, upgrading to a high-efficiency furnace or heat pump can significantly reduce energy costs while improving comfort.
Why is my furnace blowing cold air?
Cold air can be caused by a dirty filter, thermostat issues, or an overheating furnace that’s shutting down for safety. If a filter change doesn’t help, it’s best to schedule a diagnostic so we can pinpoint the issue and get your heat working properly again.
How long does a typical HVAC installation take?
Most heating or cooling system installations can be completed in one day. Larger or more complex installs may take a bit longer, but we’ll keep you informed every step of the way.
How do I schedule a service appointment?
You can request service through our website using the form at the bottom of every page, or give us a call during business hours. We’ll work with your schedule to find a convenient time.
