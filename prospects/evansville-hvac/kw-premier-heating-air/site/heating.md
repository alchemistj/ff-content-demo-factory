How can I lower my heating bills in the winter?
Simple steps like replacing filters regularly, sealing drafts, and using a programmable thermostat can make a big difference. For bigger savings, upgrading to a high-efficiency furnace or heat pump can significantly reduce energy costs while improving comfort.
Why is my furnace blowing cold air?
Cold air can be caused by a dirty filter, thermostat issues, or an overheating furnace that’s shutting down for safety. If a filter change doesn’t help, it’s best to schedule a diagnostic so we can pinpoint the issue and get your heat working properly again.
How often should I have my furnace serviced?
We recommend scheduling a furnace tune-up at least once a year, ideally in the fall before the heating season begins. Annual maintenance helps keep your system running efficiently, reduces the chance of unexpected breakdowns, and can extend the life of your furnace.
