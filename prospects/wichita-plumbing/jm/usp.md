## Gate result
Top claim: "Explains what's wrong and why, and shows you as the work goes," 4 x 4 = 16. **Passes gate (16+).**

## Three USP statements
- **A (pain point):** For Wichita homeowners who can't tell a real repair from an upsell when the water is off, JM Plumbing is the plumber that explains what's wrong and why, and shows you as it works, so you decide knowing what you're paying for.
- **B (method):** Unlike plumbers who hand you a price and a nod, JM Plumbing finds the cause, explains why it happened and shows you the work, so the bill never arrives before the understanding.
- **C (emotional outcome):** When the water is off in your house, you stay in the room, you follow every step, and you never feel foolish for asking.

**Pick: A.** It names the avatar's fear (a repair you can't check) in the buyer's own words. B reads like a dig at competitors we haven't named.

Explain It. Fix It.

## What's thin
- **Exclusivity 4 is a partial read.** Roto-Rooter's site was not fetched and Superior has none. A competitor that states "we explain" would drop the lead to 12.
- **The lead rests on many reviews** but is still reviewers' words, not a stated policy. Copy says "we find the cause and explain it," never a guarantee. Ask the owner how a visit actually runs.
- **No owner is named.** Jaime Mayorga is "the plumber" in reviews; his role is unknown, so copy speaks as "we" and names no one.
- **Hours conflict:** 24 hours (Google, ServiceAgent) vs weekday business hours (Recommended.app). Copy states no hours, no 24/7, and says "call and ask when we can be there."
- **Years conflict:** "5 years" vs "2017." Copy states no years.
- **Review count conflict:** 127 vs 123. Copy states no counts; it is a confirm item.
- **Misses exist:** a late arrival and over-booking (Jessica D., 1 star), a voicemail run-around (3 stars), an invoice never sent (Oscar H.), a cost complaint (Victoria D.). No punctuality claim leads.
- **Price-first rests on four reviews** (Rachel M., Christopher C., Cristina M., Chad P.) and is written without a number. Confirm with the owner.
- **Not used:** shoe covers as a company practice (four reviews, kept inside the quotes), commercial work (directory only), gas line work (one 2022 review), "family-owned" and "locally owned" (unverified).
