## Gate result

## Core customer insight
- The buyer doesn't know which plumber to trust, and can't tell a small fix from a big bill.
- Most plumbers are strangers until they're in the kitchen.
- Creekmore is a family business that explains the problem and the most cost-effective fix first.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners who don't have a plumber and are scared of being sold the biggest fix, Creekmore is the third generation family plumber that explains the problem and the most cost-effective fix first, so you're calling a family business, not a stranger.
- **B, method:** Unlike plumbers who are strangers until they're standing in your kitchen, Creekmore finds what failed, explains it in plain words and gives you the most cost-effective fix before you decide.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You called a name you didn't know, and it turned out to be the family you'll keep calling.

**Pick: A.** It names the avatar's first moment (no plumber, afraid of the bill) and carries both halves of the braid. B is the method section.

## What's thin
- **Exclusivity 4 is a partial read.** Roto-Rooter's site was not fetched and Superior has none. A confirmed "third generation" elsewhere would drop it to 12.
- **Pass-down rests on four reviewers** (Jane K., Laura B., Donna R., Richard W.). Copy says "some families," never a standing practice.
- **Honest fix is not a stated policy.** It comes from five reviewers. Ask the owner how a repair-or-replace call is made.
- **Founding year conflicts** (site 1948, scout note's BBB 1962). Copy states no year. Reviews mention 1948 and "76 years"; quotes are trimmed around them.
- **No owner is named.** Steve signs a review reply and an email uses stevecoleman@, but facts.md does not say he is the owner. Copy speaks as "we".
- **Hours are Monday to Friday, 7:30 to 4:30,** while two reviews describe emergency visits. No 24/7 or after-hours claim is made.
- **Misses exist.** Four reviews describe a cancel, a no-show, no callback and a slow estimate. No punctuality claim leads.
- **Reviews show services the site omits:** a gas line (Rebecca H.), sump pumps (Susan M., dan B.), a clean-out (Rebecca A.). None is in the services list.
- **Not used:** the site's "10% off labor for new customers" (an offer to confirm) and PHCC beyond backflow (no number or date).
- **Name:** Google says "Creekmore Plumbing-Heating Inc"; no heating appears anywhere in the copy.
