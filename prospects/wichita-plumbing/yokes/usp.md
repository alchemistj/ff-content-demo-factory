## Gate result
Top claim: "a leak other people couldn't find, found and fixed," **16**. **Passes gate (16+),** just. The braid ties it at 16.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners with a leak nobody can find, Yokes is the plumber that goes after the source, fixes it and tells you what it was, so you stop guessing at the walls.
- **B, method:** Unlike plumbers who patch whatever shows, Yokes looks for the source and then explains it, so the water doesn't just turn up somewhere else.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You stop staring at the wall. Somebody found it, fixed it and told you what it was.

**Pick: A.** It names the fear and the moment, and it is the claim the reviews prove best. B is a close second for the outreach.

## What's thin
- **The lead rests on three reviews.** Dylan M.'s leak was found in minutes, so it is not a hard one. Written as "go after", not "always find".
- **Exclusivity is from an incomplete scan.** Roto-Rooter was not fetched, Superior has no site, and the scan lists claims, not every service page.
- **Speed is mixed.** Suzanne I. (1 star, water heater, no text back) and Pam M. (3 stars, price) are not used. No "same day" or "fair price" standing claim is made. Ask the owner how calls are answered and quoted.
- **Dan's role is unconfirmed.** Copy never calls anyone the owner. One review calls Dan "the owner"; the site names no one.
- **No license number, warranty, financing or years** are stated. The site's "12 years of experience" is unattributed, so it is not used.
- **Name and address:** "Yokes" vs "Yoke's" and "Remodeling"; no street address on the site, Google says 718 N Milstead St.
- **Service list:** sump pumps, sewer and water main, furnace and "Aquor Water systems" are on the site only in text or unverified, so they are left out.
