# Qualify: Yoke's Master Plumbing and Remodeling

- **Prompt data:** Google 4.9 stars, 131 reviews (from the prospect brief, not re-checked). Phone (316) 372-8210.
- **Site:** https://yokesmasterplumbing.com/ (http redirects to https). WordPress, two pages: Home and Contact.
- **Kind:** not a pure specialist. Plumbing is the headline and most of the list, but the owner says remodeling is a side of the business, so it sits between kind 1 (small specialist) and kind 2 (does many things). Decide the demo shape after the review pull.

## Fit
- Plumbing first: the hero and service list lead with plumbing. The About copy says "We are not just a plumbing company, we do it all on the remodeling side of our business."
- Remodeling share, from the site: 4 of about 35 listed services are remodel items (tub and shower conversions, toilets, vanities, fixtures). Services also cover gas lines, hose bibs and drains. The site's three testimonials are two plumbing jobs (water line, water line relocation) and one bathroom remodel. That is a weak signal, not a split. The real split needs the Google reviews, which are for the final pick.
- Owner first name: "Dan" appears in a site testimonial ("GIVE THAT DAN PLUMBER A CALL!" and "Dan was prompted to come and relocate a water line"). Role not confirmed.

## Why the site is weak (less than Creekmore)
- Two pages only. Every "MORE" button on the homepage goes to the contact page, not to a service page.
- About 390 words on the homepage, 100 on the contact page.
- No street address, no owner named, no years beyond "12 years of experience," no service area beyond "Wichita and surrounding area."
- The contact email is a Gmail address (yokesplumbingnremodeling@gmail.com).
- The site has real team and truck photos, a clear phone number and a working form, so it is a thin site, not a broken one. It is a weaker fit for the offer than Creekmore.

Qualifies: yes, as a plumbing-first demo with a remodel section. Flag for Josh: it is the kind of business the brief calls out for "what the reviews show they do". Do not pick the offer until the reviews are pulled.
