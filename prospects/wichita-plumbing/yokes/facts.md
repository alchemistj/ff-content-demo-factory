2. Who is the owner? Is Dan the owner, or staff? What is his last name?
3. Street address and the office location. Is there a shop or a yard address?
4. Regular office hours, and whether Sunday is open.
5. Trip charge for jobs outside Wichita: amount and towns.
6. License number, insurance, and any certifications (gas, backflow, remodeling).
7. Remodel share: what share of the work is remodeling vs plumbing? Which remodel jobs should be featured?
8. Which of the listed services bring in the most work? Are sump pumps, sewer and water main repair real services they want on the site?
9. The contact form's optional field: what was it meant to ask? (Its label is garbled on the site.)
10. Would they like a better contact email (a company-domain address) on the demo?
