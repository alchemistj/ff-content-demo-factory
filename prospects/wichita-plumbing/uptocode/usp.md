## Gate result
Top claim: "text a photo, hear the price before anyone drives out," **16**. **Passes gate (16+),** just. The braid ties it.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners with water on the floor on a Saturday, Up To Code is the plumber you can text a photo to and ask what it'll cost, so you aren't waiting a week to hear a number.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that must see the problem before they quote, Up To Code starts with a photo and a price, so you decide before anyone drives out.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You text a photo from the basement floor, hear what it'll cost, and know where you stand before the first visit.

**Pick: A.** It names the #1 fear and the exact moment, and the photo quote is the one thing none of the 5 states. B is the line for the outreach message.

**Text a Photo. Hear the Price.**

## What's thin
- **One review carries the lead.** George A. is the only reviewer who texted a photo. Copy states it as an invitation, not a standing practice. Ask Ken: do you quote from photos as a rule, and what can't you quote that way?
- **"Before anyone drives out" is inferred** from George A.'s "right away" and the site's text line.
- **Ownership conflicts.** The site says "owned by Ken and Amanda"; BBB lists Ken alone; Thumbtack says "staff of one"; one review names a plumber, Jen. No crew, team size or Amanda's role is stated in the copy.
- **Master plumber license is unverified,** so the copy never says "licensed" or "master plumber." Moen and Delta status is unverified and unused.
- **Hours, address and founding year conflict** (facts.md). The copy states none of them. The address may be a mailbox.
- **Sewer repair has little review evidence.** The sewer page rests on the services list and on Jim M. and Jacob G.
- **Competitors are "not found," not "absent."** Roto-Rooter was not fetched and Superior has no site.
