## Gate result
Top claim: "looks for the smaller fix before the big quote," **16**. **Passes gate (16+),** just. The braid and the seven-days row tie it. Lead with the smaller-fix claim: six customers describe it. The seven-days claim is the site's own statement with no review proving an after-hours bill, so it supports the lead and never carries it.

## Core customer insight (three lines)
- The buyer doesn't know whether the fix they were quoted is the smallest one that works.
- Some companies quote the biggest job on the table and call it the only option.
- Plumb Crazy finds what actually failed, looks at the smaller fix first, and says what both options are.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners who've just been told a plumbing job "has to be done," Plumb Crazy is the plumber that looks for the smaller fix first, so you hear both options before you spend big.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that quote the biggest job and stop there, Plumb Crazy finds what failed, checks the smaller fix, and tells you plainly which one the problem needs.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You braced for the worst number. You got the cause in plain words, a fix you could afford, and no feeling that you'd been played.

**Pick: A.** It names the #3 fear at the exact moment it hits, in the customers' own words, and the other half (seven days, no emergency rates) answers fear #1 in the problem section. B is the better line for the outreach message.

## What's thin
- **The lead is inferred from six reviews,** not stated by Plumb Crazy. Ask the owner: is "smaller fix first" how you work, every time? Written in the copy as a way of working, with the reviews beside it.
- **"No emergency rates" and "7 days a week" are site claims only.** Ask for the real hours, whether anyone answers at night, and whether weekend visits cost the same.
- **Unanswered calls:** a Dec 2023 one-star review (Cassie R.) says calls, texts and the form got no reply; other one-stars call him rude. The copy never says "we always answer" or "never had a complaint."
- **No owner, no license number, no warranty, no insurance statement.** "Fully licensed master plumbers" is on the site (facts.md section 9) but has no number, so it stays out. Nothing here is a price or guarantee.
- **Brandon's role is unstated.** Copy uses "we" and names him only inside reviews. The 2017 one-star calls him "a one man show"; other reviews mention his son and Zach. The site's "three excavators" line is a crew claim and is not used.
- **Competitors are "not found," not "absent."** Roto-Rooter's site did not load in the scan and Superior has no site.
- **The site already has a sewer replacement page** (no city in its H1) and only a one-line water heater mention; the two demo pages fill the gap in city and keyword, not in topic.
