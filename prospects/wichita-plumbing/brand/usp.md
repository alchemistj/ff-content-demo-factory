Brand Plumbing, Inc. is a Wichita plumbing company that says "Since 1981" on its site, with repairs, drains, sewers, new construction and commercial work, and three named people (Mike, Marc and Mary Brand) with direct emails on /contact/ (facts.md). Hours are Monday to Friday, 7:30 to 4:30.

## Core customer insight
- **The buyer doesn't know:** that a dead water heater can be a same-day or next-morning job if the call comes early on a weekday.
- **Most companies:** give a window next week, or lead with "24/7" and say nothing about when a heater actually goes in.
- **Brand instead:** puts new water heaters in the same day or the next morning (Kathy B., Teresa F.) and lists water heaters among its same-day repairs (site /services/repairs/).

## Gate result
Top claim: "water heater out on a weekday: a new one the same day or the next morning," **20**. **Passes gate (16+).** The braid ties it at 20.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners whose water heater quits or leaks on a weekday, Brand Plumbing is the plumber that can put a new one in the same day or the next morning, so you aren't heating pots on the stove for a week.
- **B, method:** Unlike plumbers who book your water heater call into next week, Brand tells you on the phone when it can be there, then replaces the tank the same day or the next morning.
- **C, emotional outcome:** The shower ran cold at 6:45. By tomorrow morning it's hot again, and you didn't have to beg anyone.

**Pick: A.** It names the #1 fear and the exact moment, and both reviews back it. B works as the outreach hook.

## What's thin
- **Two reviews carry the lead.** One is same day (Kathy B.), one is next morning (Teresa F.). Ask: how often is a replacement same day, and does the shop stock tanks?
- **After hours is unknown.** Hours are Monday to Friday, 7:30 to 4:30. Angi says no emergency service, and a 1-star review (Ann T.) tells of a 6:15 pm call answered by text 14 hours later. Copy says weekday only and never "24/7" or "emergency."
- **Owner and roles unknown.** Mike, Marc and Mary Brand are named; nobody is called the owner. Copy speaks as "we."
- **Warranty conflict.** Site says one year of labor on new products they supply; Angi says none. Kept out of copy.
- **No license number, free-estimate policy or prices** printed. HVAC is a Cook's referral, so left out.
- **Competitors are "not found," not "absent."** Roto-Rooter wasn't fetched and Superior has no site in the scan.
