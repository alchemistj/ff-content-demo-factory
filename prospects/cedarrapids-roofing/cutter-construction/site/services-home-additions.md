Tailored Home Additions by Cutter Construction
Discover how easy it is to expand your living space with Cutter Construction, Cedar Rapids’ trusted home addition experts. Whether you’re looking to add a new bedroom, sunroom, or a custom-designed living area, our team is skilled in delivering projects that enhance both the functionality and appeal of your home. We focus on providing seamless architectural integration and superior craftsmanship tailored to your specific needs.
Why Choose Cutter Construction for Your Home Additions?
Local Expertise: As a Cedar Rapids-based company, we have a deep understanding of local building codes and community standards, ensuring your addition is both compliant and well-integrated into your neighborhood.
Quality Craftsmanship: We utilize only top-quality materials and employ the most effective construction techniques to ensure that your new space is built to last.
Seamless Integration: Our designs are thoughtfully planned to complement your existing home’s layout and style, providing a flawless addition to your property.
Full-Service Solution: We manage all stages of your addition project—from initial design through completion—making the process straightforward and stress-free for you.
Our Home Addition Services Include:
Garage Additions: Expand your garage to have more parking, storage, a home gym, or even a larger laundry room. The possibilities are endless.
Bedroom Additions: Create the extra bedroom space you need for family, guests, or personal use, designed to match your home’s existing aesthetic.
Sunroom Additions: Enjoy the beauty of the outdoors year-round with a sunroom addition that brings natural light and warmth into your home.
Roof Line Additions: Add a simple roof line over a deck or patio to keep the sun & rain from shutting down your outdoor experience.
Living Space Expansions: Enlarge your living areas to accommodate your lifestyle, whether it’s for relaxation, entertainment, or family time.
