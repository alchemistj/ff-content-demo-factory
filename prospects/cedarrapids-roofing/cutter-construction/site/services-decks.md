Composite vs Wood decking: What’s the difference?
When thinking about having a deck built for your home one of the first questions that is going to come up is do you use composite or wood? Composite decking is a deck board made from a mixture of wood and plastic. It's designed to be durable, low maintenance, and resistant to weather as well as insects. Composite decking is also more environmentally friendly as it is made of recycled materials rather than new wood that needs to be harvested.
Composite Decking: Why Trex?
Here at Cutter Construction our choice for composite decks is Trex. Trex is our first choice as all of their decking materials comes with a 25 year warranty against fading and staining as well as a large variety of options for colors as well as material types to work for all homes on all budgets. If you would like to know more take a look at our Trex decking and Trex railing pages to learn more about some of the options available for new construction and resurfacing decks with Trex.
We are Trex pro platinum certified, the highest designation Trex offers for its installers.  Here at Cutter we take great pride in that title as it shows we know our stuff with the most trusted composite decking in the business.
