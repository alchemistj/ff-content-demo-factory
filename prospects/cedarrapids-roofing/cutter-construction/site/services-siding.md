Why James Hardie?
In the 2024 we were fortunate enough to get multiple hands on training seminars on how to install James Hardie siding products and some of their unique benefits compared to other products like  traditional vinyl siding. This was a unique opportunity for us as it makes us the only contractor in the Cedar Rapids and Iowa City area that has gotten direct training from James Hardie on their product and how to install it.
James Hardie is a what is called a fiber cement siding. It is more durable, more fire resistant, more weather resistant, and all around a superior material when it comes to holding up against the outdoor elements especially somewhere like eastern Iowa with its scorching hot summers and brutal winters.
In addition to its superior function James Hardie also has the edge in making a home look great. James Hardie has 18 standard colors as well as 3 different textures for its Hardie Plank as well as over 400 custom colors available via the Hardie Dream Collection if a homeowner has a very specific color in mind for their project.
