ROOFING BUILT TO LAST
Roofing from a company you can trust
Trust is an important thing especially for someone you hire to work on your home. After the Derecho in 2020 this was a lesson the Cedar Rapids community especially learned the hard way. Fly by night contractors swarmed into the community in to do as many sub par jobs as they could fit in a few months then get out of the state when it became apparent it was they were selling roofs that lasted for all of 2 months at a 20 year guarantee price point.
We at Cutter Construction are proud to say we are not like those companies that took advantage of this community. We don’t just work in Cedar Rapids we live here. Mike and Bryan Cutter were born and raised in Cedar Rapids and have taken great pride in doing great work as a general contracting company in our community, including working on roofs weather that be a roof repair or total replacement.
We are GAF certified installers and what that means for you as a homeowner is that when you get a roof installed by us with it comes with a GAF warranty. What that means for you is that when your new roof gets the GAF System Plus limited warranty it has 50 years of coverage against defects in the materials or installation. We are able to provide a warranty like that do to GAF recognizing our outstanding quality on getting a roof out on the right way rather than the easiest way.
Dealing with roofing contractors can sometimes feel like a daunting process especially when you have enough to worry about dealing with a who know how old roof.
From our first conversation over the phone with you to the last shingle going onto your home we will be able to walk you through every one of our steps with full communication and transparency. If you are in need to a new roof or a partial roofing tear off and replacement look no further and give us a call.
