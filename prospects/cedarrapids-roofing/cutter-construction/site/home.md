We Look Forward to Working with You in the Near Future
Cutter Construction LLC is the knowledgeable local contractor you need for your next building project. From new construction to minor repairs, we’re here for you with detailed services. We provide both residential and commercial construction services. We have been working in Cedar Rapids and Iowa City for over 20 years.
We can do it all frame to finish carpentry, concrete, decks, masonry, garages, basements, roofs, shingles, or even a custom home if you can think it we can build it.
It’s easy to schedule a consultation with us. Fill out our quick form, or call us, and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible. We look forward to working with you to improve your space. Call us at 319-533-2164 today for FREE estimates on standard jobs.
