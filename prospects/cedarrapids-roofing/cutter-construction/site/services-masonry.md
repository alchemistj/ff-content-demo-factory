Quality, Precision, and Care in Every Brick
At Cutter Construction in Cedar Rapids, we understand the art of masonry. Whether you’re seeking expert chimney repairs alongside a new roof installation or looking to enhance your brick home’s longevity, we’re the experienced contractors who prioritize quality, accuracy, and meticulous attention to detail.
Why Choose Cutter for Your Masonry Needs?
Comprehensive Masonry Services: From simple repairs to extensive maintenance, our skilled team handles all types of masonry work with unmatched precision.
Team Approach: Working closely with top local roofing contractors, we ensure seamless integration of chimney repairs with your roofing projects.
Long-Term Protection: Regular pressure washing and sealing of all masonry types to safeguard your investment for years to come.
Our Masonry Services Include:
Exterior Chimney Restoration and Repair: Crafting and restoring fireplaces to become the heart of your home.
Block, Brick, and Stone Work: Quality workmanship for a range of materials, ensuring durability and aesthetic appeal.
Complete Masonry Maintenance: Expert services to keep your exterior safe from mother nature.
Structural Integrity: Specializing in structure-saving techniques and tuck-pointing to maintain and enhance your property’s integrity.
