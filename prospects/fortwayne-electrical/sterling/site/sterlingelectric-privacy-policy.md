Sterling Electric (“Sterling Electric,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website at sterlingelectric.jobbersites.com (the “Site”) or contact us for services.
1) Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:
Information you provide to us: name, email address, phone number, service address, billing address, project details, photos you submit, and any other information you include when you request an estimate, schedule service, or contact us.
Communications: messages, emails, and notes from calls or other communications with you.
Website usage information: IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, referring/exit pages, and approximate location (e.g., city/state). This may be collected via cookies or similar technologies.
2) How We Use Your Information
We use information to:
Provide and manage services (estimates, scheduling, service calls, invoicing, and customer support)
Communicate with you about your request, appointments, project updates, and billing
Improve the Site, our services, and the customer experience
Send administrative messages (confirmations, reminders, and service notices)
Help protect against fraud, misuse, or security threats
Comply with legal obligations and enforce our policies
3) How We Share Information
We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in limited situations:
Service providers/vendors who help us operate (e.g., website hosting, scheduling software, payment processing, analytics, and email/text communications). They may access information only as needed to perform services for us.
Legal and safety reasons if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Sterling Electric, our customers, or others.
Business transfers in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our business.
4) Cookies and Analytics
The Site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how visitors use the Site. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Some features of the Site may not function properly if cookies are disabled.
5) Communications (Calls, Email, and Text)
If you provide your phone number or email address, we may contact you to respond to your inquiry, schedule service, send reminders, provide updates, and handle billing.
Text messages: Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of non-essential text messages at any time by replying STOP (where applicable) or by contacting us.
6) Third-Party Links
The Site may contain links to third-party websites (such as maps or social media). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. Please review their privacy policies.
7) Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
8) Data Retention
We keep personal information only as long as necessary for business purposes (such as providing services and maintaining records) or as required by law.
9) Children’s Privacy
The Site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
10) Your Choices and Rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To make a request, contact us using the information below.
