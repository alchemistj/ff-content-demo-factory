When Should You Call an Electrician?
​Electrical issues aren’t always obvious—but they can be serious. Here are signs it may be time to call Three Rivers Electric:
​
Frequent tripped breakers or blown fuses
Flickering or dimming lights
Warm or discolored outlets/switches
Buzzing sounds from panels or fixtures
A burning smell near electrical components
Outdated wiring or a panel that’s 20+ years old
You’re planning a remodel or installing major appliances
​
