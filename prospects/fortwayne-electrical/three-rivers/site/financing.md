Financing Solar with the Indiana Energy Independence Fund
Three Rivers Electric is proud to be one of the only approved contractors in Northeast Indiana with the Indiana Energy Independence Fund (IEIF).
​
Through this program, our customers gain access to the Smart-E residential clean energy loan, launched in Fort Wayne in partnership with local credit unions. This program makes it possible to:
Secure affordable, low-interest financing for renewable energy projects like solar panel installations.
Improve energy efficiency in your home with upgrades designed to lower utility costs and increase comfort.
Invest in long-term savings while supporting a cleaner, more resilient energy future.
​​
When you choose Three Rivers Electric, you’re not only selecting trusted expertise—you’re also gaining access to one of the most innovative clean energy financing tools available in Indiana.
