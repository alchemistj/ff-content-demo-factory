Financing Solar with the Indiana Energy Independence Fund
​
Three Rivers Electric is proud to be one of the only approved contractors in Northeast Indiana with the Indiana Energy Independence Fund (IEIF). This means our customers have access to the Smart-E residential clean energy loan program, launched in Fort Wayne in partnership with local credit unions.
​
Through this program, homeowners can take advantage of affordable, low-interest financing for renewable energy and energy-efficiency projects, including solar panel installations. It’s designed to make solar more accessible, helping families lower utility costs, increase comfort, and invest in their homes while contributing to a stronger, cleaner energy future.
​
When you choose Three Rivers Electric, you not only get the benefit of our experience—you also gain access to one of the most innovative financing tools available in Indiana for clean energy improvements.
