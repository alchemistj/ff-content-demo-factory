Your heating and cooling system works every season, so it needs attention every season. Routine maintenance is the simplest way to avoid surprise breakdowns, keep energy bills in check, and get the longest possible life from your equipment.
In this video, ACTION explains what HVAC maintenance really involves and why it matters. Start with the basics every homeowner can do: check and replace your air filter on schedule, keep vents and registers clear, and keep the area around your outdoor unit free of debris. These small habits make a real difference in airflow and efficiency.
Then there's what a professional tune-up covers. Our certified technicians inspect, clean, and test the key components of your heating and cooling systems, check for safety concerns, and catch small problems before they turn into costly repairs. Scheduling service in spring for cooling and fall for heating helps your system get ready before the busiest, most extreme weather arrives.
