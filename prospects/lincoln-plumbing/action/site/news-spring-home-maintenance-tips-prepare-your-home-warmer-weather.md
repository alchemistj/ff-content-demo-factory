Mar 8, 2024
Spring is in the air, which means warmer temperatures, blooming flowers, and more time spent enjoying the outdoors. But before you fully embrace the new season, it's important to take some time for spring home maintenance. A little preparation now will help ensure your home's systems are running smoothly and efficiently for spring and summer.
At ACTION, we stand strong in our core values of respect, service, and honesty while serving residential and commercial customers. As Lincoln's trusted service provider for Plumbing, Heating, AC, Electrical, and Indoor Air Quality products, we understand how important it is to have a safe, comfortable home environment for you and your family. We've put together these spring tips from our team of expert technicians to help get your home in top shape:
Replace HVAC Filters: Dirty air filters make your HVAC system work harder, driving up energy costs and decreasing indoor air quality. Swap out those old filters for fresh ones as you transition from heating to cooling mode.
Schedule AC Tune-Up: An annual tune-up from ACTION helps your air conditioning run at peak performance. We'll thoroughly clean the system, check all components, and make any needed adjustments to improve efficiency and prevent unexpected breakdowns.
Check Water Heater: Flush the tank and inspect the anode rod to extend your water heater's service life. Look for any signs of leaks, rust, or damage that may require a repair or replacement service call.
Inspect Plumbing: While temperatures were freezing, pipe leaks may have gone unnoticed. Examine all visible pipes, check under sinks, and keep an eye out for any moisture areas as the ground thaws.
Clean Ducts: Over time, dust and allergens accumulate in your ductwork. ACTION's duct cleaning service refreshes air quality and helps HVAC airflow for better cooling and money savings.
Test Detectors: Change the batteries in all smoke and carbon monoxide detectors when adjusting clocks for daylight saving time. These devices are vital for home safety.
