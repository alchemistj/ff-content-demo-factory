DOUG KREIFELS
Board Member
Doug Kreifels, the Board Member at ACTION, champions over 45 years of industry experience. His accomplished career is highlighted by being named President of the Lincoln Heating Association five times. Additionally, Doug has dedicated 20 years to the SCC Advisory Board, providing valuable guidance.
Away from work, Doug enjoys golfing, classic cars, and traveling to explore new places. Above all, he cherishes quality time with his grandchildren.
