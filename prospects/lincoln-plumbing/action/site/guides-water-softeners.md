Spots on your glassware, scale on your faucets, dry skin after a shower, laundry that never feels quite soft: if any of this sounds familiar, hard water could be the cause. A water softener can help.
In this video, ACTION explains what hard water is and how a water softener works. Hard water contains high levels of dissolved minerals, mainly calcium and magnesium. Over time, those minerals leave scale inside your pipes, water heater, and appliances, making them work harder and potentially shortening their lives. A water softener uses a process called ion exchange to remove those minerals, so the water that flows through your home is gentler on everything it touches.
If you suspect your home needs a water softener, you’ll also see what to expect, from testing your water to installation. The benefits can add up: less scale buildup, cleaner-looking fixtures, better lathering, and appliances that run more efficiently.
