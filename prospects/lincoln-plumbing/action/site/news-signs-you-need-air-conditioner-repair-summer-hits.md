Nebraska summers can be brutally hot—and the last thing you want is for your air conditioner to break down when you need it most. The good news? Your A/C often gives warning signs before it fails completely. The key is to catch them early and schedule air conditioning repair before the summer heat sets in.
Here are the most common signs your A/C needs professional attention—before you’re stuck sweating it out.
1. WEAK OR WARM AIRFLOW
If your vents are blowing warm air or the airflow seems weak, that’s a red flag. It could be a problem with your compressor, low refrigerant levels, or even clogged ducts. Whatever the cause, your system is working overtime—and wasting energy.
Don’t wait until temperatures climb—our certified HVAC techs at ACTION can diagnose the issue and get things blowing cool again fast.

2. STRANGE NOISES OR SMELLS
Rattling, grinding, squealing, or banging sounds are never normal. These noises might point to loose components or internal damage that could get worse if left unchecked.
Likewise, if you notice musty or burning smells coming from your vents, it could be mold in the ductwork or an electrical issue. Either way, it’s time to schedule an inspection.
3. INCONSISTENT COOLING ACROSS YOUR HOME
Do some rooms feel like a sauna while others are ice cold? Uneven cooling is a common sign that your air conditioner isn’t running efficiently. It could be due to a failing thermostat, blocked vents, or issues with the unit itself.
Professional air conditioning repair can restore consistent comfort to every room in your home.
4. SPIKE IN ENERGY BILLS
A sudden rise in your energy bills—even if your usage habits haven’t changed—is often a sign that your A/C is struggling to keep up. Worn-out components and low refrigerant levels can cause the system to work harder than it should, driving up costs.
Our team at ACTION can perform a full system check and make the necessary repairs to bring your efficiency—and your bills—back under control.
5. MOISTURE OR LEAKS AROUND THE UNIT
If you see puddles or excess moisture near your A/C unit, it could be a refrigerant leak or a blocked condensate drain. Left unaddressed, this can lead to mold growth and water damage—not to mention poor cooling performance.
Don't ignore even small leaks—call for service right away.

6. IT’S BEEN A WHILE SINCE YOUR LAST TUNE-UP
Even if your system seems to be running fine, skipping regular maintenance increases the chance of a breakdown when you need your A/C the most. Spring is the perfect time to schedule a tune-up and catch any minor issues before they become major problems.
