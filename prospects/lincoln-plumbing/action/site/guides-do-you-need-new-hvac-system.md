Repair it or replace it? It's one of the toughest questions a homeowner faces, and the right answer isn't always obvious. This video helps you recognize the signs that your heating and cooling system may be reaching the end of its useful life.
ACTION walks through the most common clues. Is your energy bill climbing even though your habits haven't changed? Does your system turn on and off throughout the day, struggle to keep your home at a steady temperature, or make more noise than it used to? Are repair calls becoming more frequent? Age matters, too. Many systems begin to decline after 10 to 15 years of service, and older equipment is often less efficient than today's models.
We also explain how to think about cost. A single repair may make sense, but a string of repairs on aging equipment can add up quickly, and a system that was sized incorrectly won't ever perform the way it should. A technician can help you weigh repair costs against the efficiency, comfort, and reliability of new equipment.
