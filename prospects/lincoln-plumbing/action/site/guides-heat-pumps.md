"What if one system could keep you warm in January and cool in July, all while using less energy? That's the promise of a heat pump, and it's a technology more Lincoln homeowners are exploring every year.
In this video, ACTION breaks down how heat pumps work in plain language. Instead of generating heat by burning fuel, a heat pump moves heat from one place to another. In the summer, it pulls heat out of your home like a traditional air conditioner. In the winter, it reverses the process, bringing heat inside even when it's cold outdoors. That efficient approach can help lower energy use compared with many traditional systems.
Is a heat pump right for your home? The answer depends on your house, existing equipment, comfort goals, and budget. ACTION can help you compare your options honestly and without pressure.
