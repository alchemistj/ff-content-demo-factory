Your plumbing system works quietly in the background every day, until the moment it doesn't. A little basic knowledge can help you prevent problems, respond quickly in an emergency, and talk with confidence when you call a plumber.
This video explains why catching small leaks early can save you a major headache later. A slow drip under the sink, a damp spot on the ceiling, an unusually high water bill, or a dip in water pressure may seem minor, but small leaks can quietly damage cabinets and flooring and lead to mold. Spotting one early often means a simple fix instead of a major repair. Check under sinks and around appliances regularly, and protect outdoor faucets before Nebraska's freezing weather.
