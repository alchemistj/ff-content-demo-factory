"Some rooms are just never quite comfortable. The sunroom that bakes in summer or the first floor that freezes in winter. A mini-split may be the answer.
In this video, ACTION explains what a mini-split system is and how it works. A mini-split is a ductless heating and cooling system. It pairs an outdoor unit with one or more indoor units mounted on a wall or ceiling, connected by a small line instead of bulky ductwork. Because most mini-splits are heat pumps, they can both cool your space in summer and warm it in winter.
We'll cover why homeowners love them. Mini-splits provide zone-by-zone comfort, so you can heat or cool only the rooms you're using. They're also known for efficient operation and quiet performance, and they're often easier to install in homes where adding ducts isn't practical, including additions, finished basements, garages, and older homes.
Is a mini-split right for you? It depends on the size of the space, your existing system, and how you use your home. ACTION's certified team can evaluate your needs and recommend the right setup, and as a factory-authorized Carrier dealer, we have quality options to choose from.
