Instant AC & Furnace Rebates Up to $1,700 in Lincoln
If your furnace or AC has been on its last leg, there's no better time to take advantage of this AC rebate program and replace it. Carrier's Cool Cash rebates are returning this fall, and ACTION is proud to bring them to homeowners right here in Lincoln, Nebraska. For over 55 years, we've been the name Lincoln trusts for heating, cooling, plumbing, and electrical, and this program is one more way we're helping our neighbors stay comfortable without breaking the bank.
What Is Cool Cash?
Cool Cash is a Carrier rebate program that puts money back in your pocket the moment you install a new qualifying system. Unlike rebates that make you wait weeks for a check or a mailed gift card, Cool Cash is an instant rebate.
ACTION applies your savings directly to the invoice at the time of purchase, which means no forms to mail, and more importantly, no waiting around.
How Much Can Lincoln Homeowners Save?
Savings depend on the system you choose, but here's a look at what's available:
Infinity® Systems (heat pump, AC, furnace/air handler, and control): up to $1,700
Performance™ Heat Pump Systems: up to $800
Performance™ AC Systems: up to $600
Ductless (mini-split) systems: up to $500
Individual unit rebates (if you're not replacing a full system) ranging from $50–$700
Specialty equipment like geothermal and VRF systems: up to $1,400
Optional add-ons like smart thermostats, air purifiers, humidifiers, and other indoor air quality upgrades stack on top of your system rebate, adding $25–$200 more
Every home and system is different, so the best way to know exactly what you qualify for is to talk with one of our comfort advisors. We'll walk you through your options and show you the numbers before you commit to anything.
Key Dates to Know
Sales Period: August 1 – November 20, 2026
Installation Deadline: December 20, 2026
Claim Deadline: December 20, 2026
Because this is a limited-time rebate and inventory is available on a first-come, first-served basis, we recommend not waiting until the last minute, especially if your system is already showing signs of wear as winter approaches.
Who Qualifies?
Cool Cash is designed for everyday homeowners:
Available to U.S. and Canada homeowners
Applies to replacement systems or add-on systems for an existing home
Covers single-family homes, whether they're your primary residence or a second home
This program isn't available for new home construction, commercial or multi-family properties, or investment/rental properties being sold. If you're not sure whether your situation qualifies, contact us.
