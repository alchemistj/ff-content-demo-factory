## Gate result
Top claim: the braid "Here fast. Straight about the fix.", 15. **Below gate** (16+ needed). Cook's 1-star reviews (repeat visits, unreturned quote, fees) also keep the "straight" half from scoring higher.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners who dread hearing "replace the whole thing" on a hot day, Cook's is the crew that comes out fast and finds what actually failed, so you decide with the facts in front of you.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that lead with the new-system quote, we find what failed, fix it if fixing makes sense, and put repair and replacement side by side when it doesn't.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You call, someone comes, and you stop wondering whether you're being sold something.

**Pick:** A. It names the #2 fear in the customer's own terms and is backed by the reviews. B is the better method line for the strategy page.

## Proposed offer (below gate)
**Proposed: strategy page only, never the hero.** "The offer we'd build with you": a second-opinion visit for anyone holding another company's replace quote, with repair and replacement written side by side. Terms and price are the owner's to set; none are stated here.
Appeal 5 (fear #2). Exclusivity 4: Midwest explains options after its own diagnosis, and none of the 5 offers a second look at another company's quote. Score 20.
