## What Happens When You Call About a New System

You tell us what you have and what it's doing. We start from a diagnosis, not a new system, because a failed ignitor or a bad capacitor is a repair, not a replacement.

If the system is finished, we lay out the repair and the replacement side by side, and the decision is yours. Already been told you need a whole new system? Call us for a second look.

## Repair or Replace

Here's a rule you can check yourself. Furnaces commonly run 15 to 20 years and air conditioners 10 to 15. If yours is near the end of that range and a single repair costs about half of what a new unit would, price the replacement.

If the unit is well inside that range and the problem is one part, repair it. We'll tell you which one you're looking at.

## When the Old System Is Done

The signs arrive over a few seasons: repairs that come more often and cost more, a unit that runs nearly all day, one room that's always too hot or too cold, an energy bill that creeps up.

At some point you're paying to keep a system alive that you'd be better off replacing, and we'll say so when we see it.

## Ducts Made to Fit Your House

A new unit only works as well as the ducts around it. A system that's too big turns on and off in short bursts and leaves the air clammy. One that's too small runs all day.

Leaky or crushed ductwork wastes what the new unit makes, and no unit can fix a room it can't reach.

That's why we have our own sheet metal shop. When a fitting or a duct run has to fit an odd space, we make it to your measurements instead of forcing a stock part close enough.

## Questions About a New System

**How do I know if I should replace instead of repair?**

Look at the pattern. If repairs are frequent, the unit runs constantly or one room never evens out, a replacement is worth pricing. A diagnosis tells you what has actually failed, and we'll tell you whether repairing it is worth it.

**How long does a new system last?**

Furnaces commonly run 15 to 20 years and air conditioners 10 to 15, depending on how hard they work and how often someone services them.

**Can you replace heating and cooling together?**

Yes. We install both. If the budget says one now and one later, we install whichever you need.

**What will it cost?**

It depends on the size of the house, the type of system and the ductwork. Call and describe what you have, and we'll quote the job.

**Do you work on the brand I already have?**

Yes. We service all brands.

**Do you install for businesses?**

Yes. We design and install for homes and commercial buildings.
