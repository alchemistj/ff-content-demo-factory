## When the Old System Is Done

Nobody wants to replace a furnace or air conditioner. The signs usually arrive over a few seasons.

The repairs get more frequent, and each one costs more than you hoped.

The unit runs nearly all day and never quite catches up. One room is always the hot one, or the cold one. The energy bill creeps up with nothing else changed.

Parts wear, and old equipment loses efficiency. At some point you are paying to keep a system alive that you would be better off replacing, and we'll tell you so when we see it.

## Repair First, Replace When It Makes Sense

We don't start from a new system. We start from a diagnosis, because a failed ignitor or a bad capacitor is a repair, not a replacement.

When the system is old and the failures are piling up, or the fix costs more than the unit is worth, we lay out both numbers side by side, and the decision is yours.

If you've been told you need a whole new system and aren't sure, call us for a second look.

## Sized to the House, Connected to the Ducts

A new unit only works as well as the house around it. A system that is too big turns on and off in short bursts and leaves the air clammy. One that is too small runs all day.

The ducts matter just as much. Leaky or crushed ductwork wastes what the new unit makes, and no unit can fix a room it can't reach.

That's one reason we keep a sheet metal fabrication shop of our own. When a fitting or a duct run has to fit an odd space, we make it to the measurements instead of forcing a stock part close enough.

## Questions About a New System

**How do I know if I should replace instead of repair?**
Look at the pattern. If the repairs are frequent, the unit runs constantly or one room never evens out, a replacement is worth pricing. A diagnosis tells you what has actually failed, and we'll tell you whether repairing it is worth it.

**How long does a new system last?**
A well-kept furnace or air conditioner commonly runs 15 years or more. Regular servicing is a big part of how.

**Can you replace heating and cooling together?**
Yes. We install both heating and cooling. If the budget says one now and one later, we install whichever you need.

**How long does the install take?**
It depends on the system and the ducts. Swapping a unit in the same spot is shorter than a job that changes ductwork. We give you the schedule when we price the job.

**What will it cost?**
It depends on the size of the house, the type of system and the ductwork. Call and describe what you have, and we'll quote the job.

**Do you work on the brand I already have?**
Yes. We service all brands.

**Do you install for businesses?**
Yes. We design and install for homes and commercial buildings.
