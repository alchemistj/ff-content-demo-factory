## What a Servicing Looks For

Most furnace and air conditioner failures start as something small. A servicing is when someone checks for it, while the weather is mild and the part is still cheap.

**A filter that's clogged or the wrong size** It makes the blower strain and chokes airflow. We check it and swap it for the right one.

**Dirty coils on the air conditioner** They make the unit work harder for less cooling. We check them before the first hot week.

**A loose wire or a tired connection** It holds on until the first hard freeze and then doesn't. We check the wiring and the connections.

**A worn ignitor on the furnace** It's behind many a furnace that clicks and never lights. Caught early, it's a small repair.

**A slow refrigerant leak** The unit still runs but cools less each summer. We run leak detection on air conditioners.

**Carbon monoxide from a furnace that burns poorly** You can't smell it. If you burn gas for heat, we run carbon monoxide detection.
