# Qualify: Rods Cooling & Heating LLC (Wichita, KS)

**Verdict: qualifies as a small HVAC specialist with a weak site.**

Brief correction: the site is not one page. It is a WordPress site with about 120 live URLs, most of them template or spam pages. The weakness is real but is mostly the site's contents, not its size.

Sources: site https://rodshvac.com/, sitemap http://www.rodshvac.com/sitemap.xml (fetched 2026-11-09), Google figures from `reviews.json` (place metadata, pulled 2026-11-09).

## HVAC share of services
- **All HVAC.** Homepage "Our Services": Maintenance ("air conditioners in the spring, and furnaces in the fall"), Diagnostic & Repair, Installation & Replacement, Cleanings (indoor blower, indoor coil, outdoor unit). https://rodshvac.com/
- Inner pages add Residential HVAC, Commercial HVAC, Furnace Installations & Maintenance, Cooling Systems, AC Preventative Maintenance, Air Filters/Scrubber, Indoor Air Quality (duct cleaning). https://rodshvac.com/ac-repair/ and https://rodshvac.com/air-duct-cleaning/
- No non-HVAC trade appears on the site.

## Site size
- Sitemap: 120 site URLs at http://www.rodshvac.com/sitemap.xml. A curl GET of each returned 200 (the browser was not used for this check):
  - 42 core pages and posts (services, commercial, contact, about-style blog posts)
  - 37 town pages under /locations/ (e.g. https://rodshvac.com/locations/cooling-and-heating-installation-and-services-in-haysville-kansas/)
  - 8 dated archive pages (2022 to 2024)
  - **31 gambling-spam URLs** (e.g. https://rodshvac.com/slot-gacor-online-spaceman/, https://rodshvac.com/category/judi-online/)
  - 2 interstitial pages ("One moment, please...", https://rodshvac.com/privacy-police/)

## H1 and title
- Homepage H1: "Rods Cooling and Heating". That is the business name only, no trade and no town. H2: "Make a Wise Decision, Invest in Rods for All Your Indoor Air Quality Needs". https://rodshvac.com/
- Homepage title: "Rod Heating & Cooling | HVAC Repair, Installation & Maintenance". Wrong name ("Rod" not "Rods") and no town.
- Inner page H1 is better: "Professional AC Repairs for Your Home in and around the Wichita, KS area" (https://rodshvac.com/ac-repair/), so the work and town are on the inner pages, not on the homepage.

## Age signals
- Site: "We have been serving Wichita and the greater surrounding area in Kansas for over 20 years!" https://rodshvac.com/
- Third-party, unverified: MapQuest says "over 20 years" (search snippet only). An Expertise.com snippet says "over 40 years" (search snippet only, not opened).
- Earliest review in the file: 2018-07-07 (Jerry T.). Blog archive dates run 2022 to 2024. The homepage still shows a default "Hello world!" post dated August 1, 2023.

## Why it is a weak site
- The /contact/ page carries no Rods content (Indonesian news-portal copy and gambling menu links).
- Gambling spam is live across the domain (see above).
- Most nav links go to "#" or to the homepage.
- Homepage shows "Coming soon" under its blog section and "Coming Soon!" for duct cleaning.
- Phone: the site dials (316) 776-4088. Google metadata shows (316) 516-0406. The site shows no visible number on the homepage.

## Trust signals (strong for a demo)
- Google: 4.8 stars, 190 reviews (`reviews.json` place metadata). The homepage widget says "Based on 180 reviews" (https://rodshvac.com/).

## Flags before pitching
- A 1-star review says the business is "closed up and shut down" and "the phone number is out of order" (Wanda M., 2023-10-30). Unverified. Check it is still operating before any outreach.
- No text reviews after 2024 in the file (see `facts.md`, Reviews).
