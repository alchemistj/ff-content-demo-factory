## Request Service

Prefer to write? Send us a message with your name, phone number, address and what the system is doing. The more you tell us about the system, the better we can plan the visit.

## What to Have Ready

- Whether it's the furnace, the AC or both
- What it's doing: no heat, no cooling, noises, a smell, a shutoff
- About how old the system is
- The model number on the unit, if you can read it
- The address of the house
