## AC repair page
Idea: a running AC that does not cool is usually a worn part, and the fix is a repair, not a new system. We find the part, tell you the cost, and say honestly when replacement makes sense (older R-22 systems).
Sections: the moment (outside unit hums, house warm) + what fails (capacitor, relay, fan motor, airflow, drain) with Clarence D. and Ron D.; how we diagnose; repair vs replace with Julia K.; what you can check first (filter, thermostat, breaker); Paul F. for diagnosis, options and a levelled unit; questions; call.

## Furnace repair page
Idea: a furnace that will not heat is usually a small failure (ignition, sensor, filter, blower) and you should know what it is. When it is old and failing, you get the options and sizing explained.
Sections: the moment (cold house, clicking, no flame) + what fails; how we come prepared (Jake A.); simple fixes and what you can check (Megan B.); safety (heat exchanger, gas smell: call the gas company); repair vs replace (Mandi A., Courtney B.); questions; call.
