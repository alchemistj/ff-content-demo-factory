## Signs Your AC Needs Repair

The house won't get below 80 even though the unit is running. The vents blow warm air.

The outdoor fan sits still, or the unit starts, stops and starts again. Ice shows up on the lines, or water pools around the indoor unit.

Each of those points to a specific failure, and some of them get worse every hour the unit keeps running. If you see ice, turn the system off and call us.

## What Usually Goes Wrong

Air conditioners work hard in a Wichita summer, and the same few parts wear out first.

A capacitor helps the compressor and fan motor start, and when it weakens the outdoor unit hums and does nothing. A dirty condenser coil can't shed heat, so the system runs long and cools poorly.

Low refrigerant means a leak somewhere, because an air conditioner doesn't use it up. And a restricted filter or a failed thermostat can make a healthy system look broken.

Telling these apart takes testing, not guessing. Our technicians check the system, find the failure and then tell you what it is.
