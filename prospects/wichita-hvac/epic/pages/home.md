## AC Repair

Warm air from the vents, a unit that runs all afternoon and never catches up, an outdoor fan that won't start. Those are the usual signs of a system that has lost a part or lost its charge.

Most of the time it's something small: a capacitor that has worn out, a coil caked with dirt, a refrigerant leak, or a thermostat that has stopped talking to the unit. Finding which one takes a technician who has seen it before.

We work on all makes and models. We diagnose the problem, show you what we found and fix it. If the unit is beyond saving, we'll tell you that too, and why.

## Furnace Repair

A house that won't warm up, a furnace that clicks and never lights, a blower that rattles or a smell you can't place. In a Kansas winter, none of that can wait.

Furnaces fail for a short list of reasons: an ignition part that has worn out, a clogged filter, a blower motor, a thermostat problem. Most are fixable, and the repair is worth making.

Some furnaces are at the end of their life, and we'll say so plainly. When that's the case, we'll show you what's worn out, so the decision is about the furnace in front of you, not a sales pitch.
