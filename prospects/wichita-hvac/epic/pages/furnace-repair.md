## When a Furnace Needs a Technician

The house won't warm up. The furnace clicks and never lights, or it lights and shuts off a minute later.

The blower rattles, squeals or never starts. You smell something burning that isn't a candle.

Don't keep restarting a furnace that shuts itself off. It's shutting off for a reason, and the reason is easier to fix before you've run it ten more times.

## What Usually Goes Wrong

Furnaces start the same way every cycle, so the parts that do the starting wear out first.

An igniter or flame sensor that has gone bad is a common reason a furnace clicks and quits. A clogged filter chokes airflow, and the furnace overheats and shuts down to protect itself. A blower motor can wear out, and a thermostat problem can leave a healthy furnace never getting the call to run.

Each of these is a different repair with a different cost. We find the real one before we start.

## A Word on Safety

If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside before you call anyone else. If a carbon monoxide alarm goes off, get everyone out and call 911.

A furnace that makes a burning smell or soot around the vents also needs to be shut off until a technician has looked at it.

## Questions Before You Call

**What should I check before I call?**
Make sure the thermostat is set to heat and turned above room temperature, and that the filter isn't clogged. Check that the furnace switch and breaker are on. If it still won't run, call us.

**Does my furnace's age matter?**
It matters, but it isn't the whole story. A well-kept furnace can run a long time. We'll look at the condition, not just the number.
