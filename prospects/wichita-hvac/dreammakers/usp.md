## Gate result

Top claim: "Text Nate, and he's there in hours," **15** (was 20). **Below gate** (16+ needed): the Nate claims are owner-access claims capped at E 3, and no other row reaches 16. The best honest claim still leads the demo's hero, but the strategy page carries the proposed offer below.

## Three USP statements

**A, pain point.** For Wichita homeowners and renters whose AC or furnace quits and who can't get anyone to call back, Dream Makers Heating and Cooling is the heating and cooling company where you text Nate and he answers, so you aren't waiting on a call center.

**B, method.** Unlike companies that put you through a dispatcher, Dream Makers Heating and Cooling has you text or call Nate directly, so the person who hears the problem is the one working on it.

**C, emotional outcome.** When the house is getting hot and the kids are home, you have someone to text who knows your name.

**Pick: B.** It is the best honest claim and true to how reviews describe Nate, without promising a time. Any owner-run shop can say it, so it scores 15 and does not pass the gate on its own.

## What's thin

Ask Nate on the call:
- **Does he come out himself?** Reviews also name Aaron and Cody. "The person who answers is the person who comes out" in words.json is stronger than the reviews prove; soften or confirm.
- **Hours.** Site says "24/7"; BBB says Monday to Friday, 8 to 5; reviews show evening and early-morning visits. Don't say 24/7 until he confirms.
- **How long they've been around.** BBB start date is 2021, but a reviewer (Gary V.) had a furnace installed "12 yrs ago." No years claim until resolved. Also confirm the second BBB profile (John Salmans) is separate.
- **Speed.** "Within hours" comes from reviews; ask what he can honestly promise.
- **Renters.** Terms for tenants and landlords (who pays, who he calls).
- **Credentials.** Licenses, insurance and brand dealer status are unverified, so none are claimed.
- **Competitor gaps.** Crockett and Air Source sites weren't read, so exclusivity scores rest on 3 sites plus directory listings.
