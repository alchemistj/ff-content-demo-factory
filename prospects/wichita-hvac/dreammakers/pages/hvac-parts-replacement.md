## The Parts That Fail Most

Moving parts wear out first, and the ones doing the hardest work wear fastest.

- **Motors and fans.** The condenser fan outside and the blower inside run every time the system does. Bearings wear and they stall.
- **Capacitors and contactors.** These give motors their start-up jolt. A weak one makes a unit hum but not start.
- **Igniters and sensors.** A furnace that clicks and quits is often failing here.
- **Refrigerant lines and coils.** A leak lets refrigerant out, and cooling slowly fades. Adding more without finding the leak just starts the clock again.

Repairing the right part is cheaper than replacing a system. Repairing the wrong part is how you pay twice.
