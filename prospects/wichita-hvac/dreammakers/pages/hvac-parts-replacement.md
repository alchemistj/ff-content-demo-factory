## The Parts That Fail Most

Moving parts wear out first, and the ones that work hardest wear fastest.

- **Motors and fans.** The condenser fan outside and the blower inside run every time the system does. Bearings wear, and they stall.
- **Capacitors and contactors.** These give motors their start-up jolt. A weak one makes a unit hum but not start.
- **Igniters and sensors.** A furnace that clicks and quits is often failing here.
- **Refrigerant lines and coils.** A leak lets refrigerant out, and cooling slowly fades. Topping it off without finding the leak just starts the clock again.

Repairing the right part costs far less than replacing a system. Repairing the wrong part is how you pay twice.

Refrigerant doesn't burn off like gasoline. If the level is low, there is a leak somewhere, and a slow leak can take more than one visit to chase down.
