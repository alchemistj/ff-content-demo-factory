# Homepage ideas: four Wichita HVAC demos

Written 2026-11-10. Method: `docs/writer-guides/HOW_TO_WRITE_A_PAGE.md`, section 1, "They argue one idea". Sources: each company's `facts.md`, every review in `reviews.json`, `audit.md` and (where one exists) `plan.md`. Review lines are quoted word for word from `reviews.json`; reviewer names are as Google shows them.

The four ideas, in one line each:

| Company | The idea in a word | What it is NOT about |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cook's | Staying power: a Wichita household's HVAC company for decades | speed, price, owner |
| Integrity | Accountability: a company that lives up to its name, vouched for by people who own property | speed, "family" |
| Air Capital | New company, proven in one summer, with experienced owners | years in business, owner |
| Dream Makers | Nate's number: customers text the owner, he answers and knows them by name | being new, being old, commercial |

"Fast, honest, fair price" is true of all four sets of reviews, so it is not an idea for any of them. It appears in every section below as a supporting detail at most, never as the argument.

## 1. Cook's Heating and Cooling

**The idea.** Wichita households stay with Cook's for decades. Customers describe the same company installing the system, fixing it, and getting called again by the next generation, so the homepage argues that this is the company you call once and keep.

**Why it is Cook's and not the others.**
- Only Cook's reviews measure the relationship in decades and generations: "We have used Cook's for the last 35 years couldn't be happier" (Sandra S., 2019-06-15); "I called Cook because that's who my grandparents always called" (Richard R., 2009-09-10); "I have known Gerry for 30+ years. I have always used him for all my HVAC needs" (Jeff W., 2020-05-29); "The first system in Belle Plaine, a Carrier, still runs good after almost 40 years" (John E., 2024-07-02). No Air Capital, Integrity or Dream Makers review reaches past about 12 years (Gary V. for Dream Makers: "installed our furnace and A/C 12 yrs ago"; Chris W. for Integrity: "last 7+ years").
- Cook's is the only one of the four with a full installation trade behind the service: `facts.md` section 4 lists furnaces, unit heaters, electric air handlers, humidification and filtration installed, commercial and "advanced mechanical system design", and an "in-house sheet metal fabrication shop" (https://www.cookshvac.com/about/). Reviews back it: "new HVAC equipment built right here in Wichita" (Patrick, 2020-02-25); a third-party duct test on a new home came in under 5% leakage (The G., 2014-06-03).
- Cook's is the only one with a written maintenance plan on its own page: the Premium plan, two servicings a year, 20% off parts and labor, on 1-, 5- or 10-year terms (`facts.md` section 7, https://www.cookshvac.com/maintenance/). Integrity has a $150 plan only in an FAQ; Air Capital and Dream Makers have no plan terms.
- Cook's is the only one whose people are a crew, not one name: Ricky, Charlie, Shannon and Jose, Derek, Tyler, Glenn, Don, Rosa in the office (`facts.md` section 1). There is no owner to lean on. The owner is unknown, so this idea needs none.

**Limits to hold (from `facts.md`).**
- Do not state a founding year, "35 years" or "second generation" as the company's own claim. The About page says "second generation family business" but a 2009 review says it was bought out in 2008, so it is unresolved. The decades live only inside customer quotes.
- No dealer status, brand "top brands" line, licence, warranty or free-estimate claim.
- The rating is 4.0 with about 14 one- and two-star reviews, several about repeat visits and a warranty complaint (Blakeracer R., 2023-07-11). Quote five-star reviews only and do not promise a warranty. A "stays for decades" idea is only safe if the page does not promise "fixed the first time".

**H1 company type.** `HVAC Company in Wichita, KS — Cook's Heating and Cooling` ("HVAC Company": the site and Google both say "Heating and Cooling" and the service pages cover both).

**Two service pages.**
1. HVAC Installation: `HVAC Installation in Wichita, KS — Cook's Heating and Cooling`, keyword "HVAC installation Wichita KS". Facts: new heating units, new air conditioners, furnaces, unit heaters, electric air handlers (https://www.cookshvac.com/services/heating and /air-conditioning).
2. Maintenance Plans: `HVAC Maintenance Plans in Wichita, KS — Cook's Heating and Cooling`, keyword "HVAC maintenance plan Wichita KS". Facts: Premium plan terms. Price is not confirmed, so none is stated.

**Reviews that prove it.**
- Sandra S. (2019-06-15): "We have used Cook's for the last 35 years couldn't be happier. Thank you Sheldon you are amazing"
- John E. (2024-07-02): "I have had Cooks do my HVAC work and installations for many, many years and the work has always been good, in each of my two homes. The first system in Belle Plaine, a Carrier, still runs good after almost 40 years... I always use Cook's for service also."
- Richard R. (2009-09-10): "When my ac went out 2 years ago I called Cook because that's who my grandparents always called. They had a new system in place within 48 hours." (Use only the first two sentences. The buy-out line later in the review is the unresolved one.)
- Jeff W. (2020-05-29): "I have known Gerry for 30+ years. I have always used him for all my HVAC needs, always great pricing, and the workmanship is second to none."
- Joshua M. (2020-09-15): "I used to wire new houses in Wichita and the surrounding area... Cooks has always been one of my top three companies that I suggest or would use to do any work for me or anyone I know. The installation guys work their buts off and they do their best to make everything right."

## 2. Integrity Heating and Cooling

**The idea.** The company is named Integrity and the people who judge vendors for a living, landlords, short-term-rental hosts and property owners, say it lives up to the name: the owner takes the inconvenient call himself, the price stays fair when it is 8pm on a weekend, and the work is left clean. The homepage argues accountability, not speed.

**Why it is Integrity's and not the others'.**
- Only Integrity's reviews use the company name as the proof, over and over: "I especially appreciated they live by their name, Integrity" (Rita P., 2024-09-27); "He and his team are the epitome of integrity-based service" (Jennifer J., 2024-08-02); "Sam with Integrity is just that. A company with integrity" (Ryan S., 2023-04-15); "dependable and accountable" (Brett J., 2024-03-16).
- Only Integrity has reviewers who speak as property owners and vendor-pickers: an investor's short-term rental fixed after 8pm (Hunter M., 2024-05-17), a landlord with "a lot of commercial buildings" (Lynne L., 2024-04-29), Brett J. who "worked with many HVAC companies over the years in Wichita" and replaced the furnace and A/C at his mother's house, and Phil D., who sends customers Sam's way (2024-04-11). `facts.md` section 11 says Lynne L. is the only commercial job described in any review.
- Integrity is the only one with an after-hours price point reviewers praise: "fair in his pricing after hours" (Hunter M.). The site itself says weekend after-hours is "at additional cost. Please call for quote" (https://integrityheatingandcooling.net/contact/), so the idea is the fairness, not a promise of 24/7.
- All 45 reviews are five stars, and Sam is named in about 16 (`facts.md` section 11).

**Honest overlap with Dream Makers, and where the line is.** Both have an owner who personally shows up (Hunter M. for Integrity; Ady G. and Sinman for Dream Makers). Integrity's page does not argue "you can reach the owner". It argues "this company does the right thing when it costs something", and its proof comes from property owners comparing vendors, which Dream Makers' reviews do not contain (see the swap check, section 5).

**Limits to hold (from `facts.md`).**
- Owner: "Sam" is named by reviewers; Hunter M. calls him "the owner" and Phil D. gives "Sam Chaney". The site never names him, so keep the surname off until confirmed.
- No "24/7" (site contradicts itself), no years in business (15, "two decades" and 30 all appear), no licence, no brand or dealer claim, no Kechi office (site says Kechi, Google says Wichita), no free on-site estimate (only a free phone consultation).
- No owner reply on any review.

**H1 company type.** `HVAC Company in Wichita, KS — Integrity Heating and Cooling`.

**Two service pages.**
1. Furnace Repair: `Furnace Repair in Wichita, KS — Integrity Heating and Cooling`, keyword "furnace repair Wichita KS". Reviews: Joyce A. (2024-10-16), TheDaendric (2024-12-03), Karen E. (2026-04-03, new furnace), Brett J. (furnace and A/C replaced).
2. Commercial HVAC: `Commercial HVAC in Wichita, KS — Integrity Heating and Cooling`, keyword "commercial HVAC Wichita KS". Facts: the site's services page lists "Commercial HVAC" ("install professional quality HVAC systems into your new business or need to renovate an aging system") and the AC page says "homes and businesses". Thin: one review (Lynne L.) plus Hunter M.'s rental. Flag for Josh: if that is too thin, swap in AC Repair, which has the strongest evidence (Jessica H., Colton K., Dayna B., Tyler J., Madison S., Justus K.), at the cost of overlapping Air Capital.

**Reviews that prove it.**

He was very efficient, great with communication, and fair in his pricing after hours. Amazing leadership example and world class service."

## 3. Air Capital Heating and Air

**The idea.** We are a new Wichita company, run by people with years of hands-on HVAC experience, and we earned our record in the open: a summer of 104 and 106 degree calls answered. The homepage speaks to the visitor who has never heard of us and shows them what the first customers said.

**Why it is Air Capital's and not the others'.**
- Only Air Capital says it is new, in the site's words and the customers': "While we're new to the market, the experience behind our team runs deep" and "new company, proven expertise" (https://aircapitalheatingandair.com/about-us-1); "They're new to market but years of experience between the owners" (Mai D., 2026-08-08). The other three have years of history or leave age unstated.
- Its whole review record is one short stretch: 26 reviews, 22 of them in August 2026, 2 in September, 2 in October (`reviews.json`). The heat-wave setting is in the lines: "it's 104°F" (Carlos F., 2026-08-10), "106 degrees today" (Tammy S., 2026-08-11), "brutal heatwave" (J D., 2026-09-08).
- Only Air Capital has a customer who tried the established player first: "I called Reddi Industries originally and it took them 4 hours to have someone reach out to me just to get on their schedule. When I reached out to Air Capital Heating and Air they showed up within an hour" (Jayci G., 2026-08-19). Only Air Capital has a customer who had "just moved in" (J D.).
- Only Air Capital has confirmed financing on a named merchant page: Enhancify, page exists under the business name (`facts.md` section 7). That backs a newcomer asking for trust on a full system replacement.
- No owner name and no founding year are available, so the idea leans on neither. This is the fact that stops it from swapping with Cook's: Cook's argues time served, Air Capital argues the opposite and then proves it.

**Limits to hold (from `facts.md` and `audit.md`).**
- No founding year, no owner name, no "authorized Ruud dealer" (unverified and on three pages of the current site), no licence number, no warranty.
- "Se Habla Español" and "Bilingual Team" are on the site but unverified. Leave out.
- Hours: the site says Mon-Sun 8am-5pm; Sunday is an open question. Do not lead on "seven days".
- `facts.md` still says reviews are "pending" and the plan was written before `reviews.json` landed. Both are stale against the 26 reviews now in the folder.
- Do not call it "one summer" in copy unless Josh is fine with it: the reviews span August to October 2026. "This summer's customers" is safer.

**H1 company type.** `HVAC Company in Wichita, KS — Air Capital Heating and Air`. The current site's H1 says "Expert Heating & Air Services" and has no town. The audit flags it.

**Two service pages.**
1. AC Repair: `AC Repair in Wichita, KS — Air Capital Heating and Air`, keyword "AC repair Wichita KS". Strongest evidence by far: Carlos F., Tammy S., Hannah M. (motor, within an hour), Oscar M., Maria F.
2. AC Replacement: `AC Replacement in Wichita, KS — Air Capital Heating and Air`, keyword "AC replacement Wichita KS". Facts: residential AC page lists AC replacement; financing through Enhancify. Reviews: Sabrina F. (2026-08-29, "replaced a full hvac system"), Victor V. (2026-08-10, installation). Two reviews, so teach more than quote.

**Reviews that prove it.**

- Jayci G. (2026-08-19): "I called Reddi Industries originally and it took them 4 hours to have someone reach out to me just to get on their schedule. ## 4. Dream Makers LLC Heating and Cooling

Customers text and call the owner directly, he answers, and he knows them by first name through the long jobs, not just the emergencies: a Freon leak chased over a year and a half, two summers with toddlers, a landlord's rental, a wedding reception at 84 degrees. The homepage argues a relationship with one local person who picks up.

**Why it is Dream Makers' and not the others'.**
- Only Dream Makers' reviews describe reaching the owner by text and getting an answer: "Nathan is amazing always answers when I text or call him" (Mashaela W., 2025-05-21); "you are on top of every reply, every question and every texts and you just don't get that kind of customer service anymore" (Allen B., 2024-07-29); "I was able to text my issue from their website (very convenient)" (Carolyn S., 2022-12-13).

Love the turbo VW's!!!" to Jimmy M.; "Enjoy the cooler temps this summer!!" to Bailey L.). Cook's, Integrity and Air Capital have zero owner replies on their pulls (`facts.md` for Cook's and Integrity; Air Capital `reviews.json` shows none).

## 5. Swap checks (6 pairs)

Each check puts one company's idea on the other company and asks whether it still reads true.

1. **Cook's vs Integrity.** Cook's idea on Integrity fails: no Integrity review spans more than 7+ years (Chris W.), nobody says "my grandparents", and Integrity has no maintenance-plan page or fabrication shop. Integrity's idea on Cook's fails: no Cook's review says the company lives up to its name, no Cook's customer is a landlord comparing vendors for the owner, and Cook's has no owner who takes the call.
2. **Cook's vs Air Capital.** Direct opposites. "Staying power" on Air Capital is false (new company, reviews from August 2026), and "new, proven in one summer" on Cook's is false (no review is about a new company, and the earliest is from 2009).
3. **Cook's vs Dream Makers.** "Staying power" on Dream Makers is thin (its longest customer history is 12 years, Gary V.), and it has no named crew, no maintenance plan and no fabrication shop. "Nate's number" on Cook's fails: no Cook's review mentions texting, no owner is known, and no review gets an owner reply.
4. **Integrity vs Air Capital.** "Accountability, vouched for by property owners" on Air Capital fails: no Air Capital review is from a landlord or investor and none mentions the owner or accountability. "New company, proven in one summer" on Integrity fails: its reviews run back to 2022 and one customer says "7+ years".
5. **Integrity vs Dream Makers.** The closest pair. "Nate's number, texts answered, known by first name" on Integrity fails: Integrity's reviews have almost no texting language and no owner replies, and Hunter M. and Brett J. judge it against other vendors, which no Dream Makers review does. "Lives up to its name, vouched for by property owners" on Dream Makers fails: no Dream Makers review says "accountable", "integrity" or compares it with other vendors, and its renters are tenants of a landlord who hired it, not owners of the property.
6. **Air Capital vs Dream Makers.** "New company, years of experience between owners" on Dream Makers fails: a customer there says "12 yrs ago" and its reviews begin in 2021. "Nate's number" on Air Capital fails: no owner is named, no owner replies, no review mentions texting the owner, and its reviews praise "the crew" and "Juan" without a relationship history.
