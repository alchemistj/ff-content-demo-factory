## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners whose AC or furnace quit and who dread a week of waiting or a pitch for a new unit, Air Capital is the heating and air company that shows up the same day and explains the cause and every option first, so you decide with the facts.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that book you out and then hand you a number, we come the same day, find the failed part, and walk you through your options before any work starts, so nothing is a surprise.
- **C, emotional outcome:** Cold air back, and the quiet relief of knowing exactly what happened and that nobody played you.

**Pick: B.** It names the two behaviors reviewers describe (fast arrival, explained first), and "unlike" sets us against the F2 and F3 experiences in the avatar. A leans on pain but is longer; C is better as the subline.

## What's thin
- No owner or founder named; the "we" voice has no face. Ask who the owners are and who Juan is (a tech in reviews).
- "Shows the failed part" is not in any review; reviews say "explained". Confirm it is the real practice before the hero says "show".
- Same-day speed rests on August 2026 reviews; ask about winter and weekend capacity, and the emergency process (hours are 8 to 5).
- No warranty, price, maintenance plan contents, license numbers or verified Ruud dealer status, so none are claimed.
- Bilingual team is site-only; confirm it.
- Competitor "same-day" and "options" findings come from site copy only and may understate them.
