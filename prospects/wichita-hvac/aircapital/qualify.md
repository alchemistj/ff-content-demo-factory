## HVAC share of services
- Every service on the site is HVAC: AC installation, heating repair, ductless mini split installation and repair, preventative maintenance plans (https://aircapitalheatingandair.com/). The services page sections are the same four, plus separate pages for residential AC, residential heating and light commercial (https://aircapitalheatingandair.com/residential-ac, https://aircapitalheatingandair.com/residential-heating, https://aircapitalheatingandair.com/light-commercial).

- Light commercial is a real page with its own H1 "Commercial HVAC Services in Wichita, KS" (https://aircapitalheatingandair.com/light-commercial). It is HVAC work, so it does not change the fit.

## Site size and structure
- Ten pages crawled, all returning 200: home, Financing, About Us, Residential AC, Contact Us, Residential Heating, Light Commercial, FAQs, Privacy, Terms (https://aircapitalheatingandair.com/sitemap.xml lists the main pages).

- Homepage title: "Air Capital Heating and Air | HVAC Services in Wichita, KS". The title is the strong part.
- The About H1 is "About Air Capital Heating and Air"; the Contact H1 is "Contact Air Capital Heating and Air" (https://aircapitalheatingandair.com/about-us-1, https://aircapitalheatingandair.com/contact-us). Neither names the trade.

- No age date found in the site copy. Searches did not find a business record under this name (see `facts.md`). The age is unknown; "new" is the company's own word.

## Builder and template

## Other site problems that make it a weak fit today
- The Residential AC and Residential Heating pages say "Ruud Authorized Dealer" and "Ruud certified" (https://aircapitalheatingandair.com/residential-ac). Ruud's locator is a JavaScript tool that could not be queried here, so this claim is unverified.
- The homepage form and the contact form have no form action in the HTML, and the contact form's submit path is not tested (form not submitted, by rule).
- The contact email is `aircapitalheatingnair@gmail.com`; the site domain is `aircapitalheatingandair.com`. The spelling does not match the domain.
- Full crawl and click results are in `audit.md`.

## Fit call
Small specialist, HVAC only, multi-page but small, with a home H1 that does not say the trade and town together. It is the kind of site a 5-page demo can improve. The owner is not identified yet, and there is no review pull yet, so this is a qualification from the site and the web only.
