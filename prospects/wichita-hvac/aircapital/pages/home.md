## What Wichita Homeowners Call Us About

Most breakdowns come down to one part, and the symptom usually points to it. Here is what we look for, and what we do about it.

**The house won't get below 80** The vents blow warm, and the cause is often a failed part such as a weak capacitor, a worn fan motor or low refrigerant from a leak. We find which one, show you, and fix that part instead of guessing at the whole system.

**The outdoor unit hums and the fan never spins** A weak capacitor can't give the motor its starting jolt. It's one of the cheapest and most common AC repairs, and we replace it once we've confirmed it's the cause.

**The AC shuts off and water sits by the unit** Algae has usually clogged the condensate drain, and a safety switch has tripped to protect the house. We clear the line and check why it clogged.

**The furnace clicks but never lights** A dirty flame sensor or a worn igniter is the usual reason. We test both, show you which one failed, and replace only that.

**It runs all afternoon and the house still sits at 82** On an older system that has needed repeated repairs, each fix buys a few months. We lay the repair next to replacement, with reasons for each, and you decide.

## Repair or Replace

A new system is the biggest bill in this trade, so it should never be the first thing you hear. If a capacitor, a motor or an igniter has failed on a system that otherwise runs well, repair it.

Replacement starts to make sense when the system is old, the repairs keep coming, or it's on the older R-22 refrigerant. You can check the age and refrigerant on the label of the outdoor unit.

When it is time, we install new air conditioners and furnaces. Here is [what a replacement involves](/ac-replacement-wichita-ks/).
