## AC Repair

Most calls start the same way. The thermostat says 74, the house says 85, and the vents are blowing warm air. Or the outdoor unit hums and the fan never turns.

The cause is usually one small part: a capacitor that can't start the motor, a tired fan motor, a drain line clogged with algae. Heat wears all of them out, which is why they quit on the worst days. A repair that swaps one part costs far less than a new system.

We find what failed, show you what it is and get the cold air back.
