## When the System Is Telling You It's Done

You've paid for the same system three summers running. It runs all afternoon and the house still sits at 82. The outdoor unit rattles, the vents are weak, or the electric bill climbs while the comfort drops.

None of those alone means replace. Together they usually do. Each repair buys a few months, not years.

Most central air conditioners are built to last around 10 to 15 years, and Kansas summers sit at the hard end of that range. As a system ages, repair calls come closer together and it uses more electricity to deliver less cooling.

A system on the older R-22 refrigerant is also getting costly to service, because that refrigerant is no longer produced. A new system fixes all three at once.

## What We Install

We install energy-efficient air conditioning systems for homes, matched to your house and your needs. The goal is a system that is the right size for your home, not the biggest one on the truck.

Size matters more than most people expect. A system that is too large cools the air quickly, shuts off and never pulls much moisture out, so the house feels clammy. One that is too small runs without stopping and still can't keep up on a 100-degree day.

If your furnace is also near the end of its life, we install and replace furnaces too. Replacing both together means the two pieces of equipment are matched.

## What a Good Replacement Quote Shows You

A replacement price should be something you can read and compare. Ask any company, including us, for these:

- What equipment is being installed, and its efficiency rating
- What's included in the install, and what the old system's removal covers
- Whether your existing ductwork and thermostat are staying or changing

If a quote leaves those out, you're comparing a number, not a system.

## Common Questions

**How do I know whether to repair or replace?**

Look at the age and the repair history. An older system that has needed repeated repairs, or can't keep up even with every part working, is a replacement candidate. A newer system with one failed part usually isn't. If one part has failed, [AC repair](/ac-repair-wichita-ks/) is usually the better answer.

**What does a new AC cost?**

The price depends on the size of your home, the efficiency of the system and whether your ductwork needs work. We don't quote a number without seeing the house, so call for a free estimate.
