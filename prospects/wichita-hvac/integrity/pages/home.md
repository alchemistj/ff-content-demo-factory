## Furnace Repair

A furnace that quits rarely says why. It clicks and never lights, runs two minutes and shuts down, or blows cold air while the house gets colder.

The cause is often small: an igniter, a flame sensor, a clogged filter or a failing blower motor. We show you what failed and fix it. A cracked heat exchanger is a safety matter, and we'll tell you plainly if that's what we find.
