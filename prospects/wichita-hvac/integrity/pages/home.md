## AC Repair

The house is crawling past 80, the air coming out of the vents is lukewarm, or the unit outside runs and runs without cooling anything. Sometimes it just won't start at all.

Air conditioners usually fail in a few predictable places. A weak capacitor keeps the compressor or fan from starting.

A dirty coil or a clogged filter chokes the airflow until the coil ices over. Low refrigerant means there is a leak somewhere, and topping it off without finding the leak only buys a few weeks.

We find the actual cause first, explain it in plain words and fix it. If the repair isn't worth it on an old unit, we say so.

## Furnace Repair

A furnace that quits doesn't always say why. It clicks and never lights, it runs for two minutes and shuts down, or it blows cold air while the house gets colder.

The cause is often small. A worn igniter, a dirty flame sensor, a clogged filter or a failing blower motor can each stop the heat, and each is a much smaller job than a new furnace.

We diagnose it, show you what failed and fix it. A cracked heat exchanger is the one problem that is a safety matter, and we'll tell you plainly if that is what we find.

## Keeping It From Breaking Again

Most breakdowns start long before the day the system quits. A dirty filter, a coil caked with dust and a motor running dry all wear a system out a little more each season.

A yearly check before the heavy season catches those things while they're still cheap to fix. We offer HVAC maintenance plans for homeowners who would rather pay for a tune-up than for an emergency, so ask about one when you call.

It also helps to change your filter on a schedule. A clean filter is the cheapest thing you can do for either your furnace or your air conditioner.
