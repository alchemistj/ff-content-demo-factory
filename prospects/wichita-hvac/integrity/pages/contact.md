## Send a Request

Not a good time to talk? Use Request Service and tell us what's going on. Include your name, a phone number and the address of the house or building.

## What to Have Ready

- What the system is doing: no heat, no cooling, noise, a smell, ice, a tripped breaker.
- Whether it's a furnace, an air conditioner or a heat pump, and the brand if you can see it on the unit.
- Roughly how old the system is, and any repairs you remember.
- Whether it's a home or a commercial building.
