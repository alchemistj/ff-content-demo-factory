## When to Call

The house won't cool below 80 no matter how long the unit runs. The air from the vents is lukewarm. The outdoor unit hums but the fan isn't spinning, or the breaker trips every time it starts.

Ice on the copper line outside, water around the indoor unit and a system that cycles on and off every few minutes are all signs to turn it off and call. Running a struggling air conditioner makes a small repair bigger.

## What Usually Goes Wrong

Most AC failures come from a short list of parts, and knowing the list helps you follow what a technician tells you.

**Capacitor.** This small part gives the compressor and fan motor the jolt they need to start. When it weakens, the unit hums and won't run. It's one of the most common and cheapest repairs.

**Dirty coil or clogged filter.** Dust on the coil and a choked filter cut the airflow until the coil freezes into a block of ice. The fix is cleaning and finding out why it got that way.

**Low refrigerant.** An air conditioner doesn't use up refrigerant. If it's low, there's a leak, and adding more without fixing the leak is money that drains away again.

**Fan or compressor motor.** Motors wear, and a noisy or slow-spinning motor is a warning. Replacing it early is a far smaller job than waiting for it to fail.

## Repair or Replace?

Most of the time a repair is the right call. A capacitor, a motor or a cleaned coil gets you back to cool air for a fraction of the cost of a new system.

Replacement starts to make sense when the unit is old, repairs are piling up, or the problem is something like a failed compressor on a system that's already near the end of its life. A big repair bill on an old unit is often money you'd rather put toward a new one.

We tell you which situation you're in, and we'll tell you if the answer is to repair. We also install new air conditioning equipment when it's time.

## Keeping Your AC Running

A few habits stretch a system's life. Change the filter on a schedule, keep the outdoor unit clear of grass, leaves and anything stacked against it, and don't ignore a new noise.

A spring check before the first hot week catches weak parts before they fail on you. We offer maintenance plans, so ask about one when you call.
