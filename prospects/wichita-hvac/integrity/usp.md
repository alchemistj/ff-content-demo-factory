## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners whose AC or furnace quits on a Saturday, a holiday or after 8pm, Integrity is the company that tells you the after-hours price on the phone before anyone drives out, so you decide first.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that say "24/7" and leave the price for the doorstep, Integrity quotes the after-hours price when you call, then sends the tech, so you know what it costs before anyone is on the road.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You call on a Saturday afternoon, hear the price, and decide before anyone drives out. No surprise on the doorstep.

**Pick: A.** It names the #1 fear and the exact moment, and it leads with the price-first half that no competitor states. B is a close second for the outreach message. The weekend-visit speed moves to the subline, not the lead.

## What's thin
- **Inferred from the contact page's "Please call for quote"; confirm with Sam.** The lead (price quoted by phone before anyone drives out) is inferred, not stated by any reviewer.
- **The phone quote is inferred.** The site says "call for quote" and Hunter M. says "fair in his pricing after hours." No review says the price was given before the tech left. Ask Sam: do you quote the after-hours price on the call, and what is it?
- **Night calls:** only one review (Hunter M., after 8pm). The site claims "24/7" on two pages and weekdays 8 to 5 on another. Do not use "24/7" until Sam says which is true.
- **Sam's role:** "the owner" comes from one reviewer; the site names no one. Copy says "Sam" only.
- **Competitors are "not found," not "absent."** Crockett's site did not load and Air Source has no site in the scan.
