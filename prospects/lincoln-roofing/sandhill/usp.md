## Business in one line
A family-owned Lincoln roofer, owned by Dusty Rieck, serving Eastern Nebraska with roofing, siding, gutters, windows and doors, and insurance claims (facts.md). Its reviews (72 written, 101 on Google) are about Dusty personally: honest, no pressure, and there years later.

## Core customer insight
- **What the buyer doesn't know:** whether the roof needs replacing or a repair, or if the leak is even the roof.
- **What most roofers do:** quote the bigger job, and claim honesty in general terms ("no unnecessary surprises").
- **What Sandhill does instead:** says what the roof needs and what it doesn't, and answers the phone about it later.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point.** For Lincoln homeowners who can't tell whether a roofer is telling the truth, Sandhill Roofing says what your roof needs and what it doesn't, so you never pay for work you didn't need.
- **B, method.** Unlike roofers who quote the bigger job, Sandhill Roofing looks at the roof, explains it in plain words, lays out your choices, and says so when it's a repair or not the roof at all.
- **C, emotional outcome.** You finally get someone who knows roofs to tell you straight, and you stop wondering whether you're being sold something.

**Pick: B.** It states the contrast with the roofer customers fear (fear #4), and each part comes from named reviews. A is the same claim aimed at the pain, and reads like most roofers.

## What's thin
- **The lead is Dusty's behavior, from reviews only.** The site says nothing about honesty, a second opinion or no-pressure. Ask Dusty how often an inspection ends in "you don't need it", and whether he'd say it on the page.
- **Owner access.** Almost every review names Dusty. Any "he picks up" line is capped at E 3 and stays supporting copy.
- **Years in business and "ten years."** The site says Dusty has been a "leading roofing expert in Lincoln... for ten years". The founding year is unknown and Nicole S. and Jena A. describe a roof from 2016, so no year count is in the copy.
- **Warranty.** The site headlines a "Lifetime Workmanship Warranty on Roofing, Siding, Gutters" with no terms. Not in the copy; in `confirm`.
- **Certifications and brands.** "Trusted Brands and Partners" has no names. Tim L. and Ben C. mention Malarkey shingles, which is not a certification claim. Not in the copy.
- **Insurance.** Reviews describe Dusty working with adjusters; some name outcomes (savings, a full replacement). The copy says only that claims are part of the work and that the insurer decides.
- **Address.** The site has none. The Google listing (reviews.json) has 8120 Trendwood Dr, Lincoln, NE 68506, which may be a home. Left out of the copy; in `confirm`.
- **Discounts.** The site lists a senior discount and a military/government employee discount with no terms. The copy says to ask about current terms.
- **Service area.** "Serving Eastern Nebraska" with no towns. Reviews mention Henderson and a home closer to Omaha. Towns are left out of the copy.
- **Negative review.** Toxic L. (1 star, 2026) says the work was okay but the crew left nails and trash. No cleanup claim is stated as "always".
- **Google rating.** Only the review count (101) is on file. No star rating is used.
- **Competitors.** A Better Way and Turbo did not load, so exclusivity rests on three sites.
