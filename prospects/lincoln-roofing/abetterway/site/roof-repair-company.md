Have a leak?  We can find it. We can fix it.
Your roof requires proper care and maintenance.  That holds espcially true in Nebraska’s intense weather extremes. So it’s vital to inspect, detect, and repair roofing issues at the beginning before the damage becomes too severe!  When a roofing issue goes undetected, not only does the damage get bigger, but so can the cost of repairing the roof, or in a worst case-scenario, replacing the roof!
We also specialize in venting issues. You’d be surprised that some leaks can be from the venting (or lack of) on your roof.
Consulting, Inspections & Diagnosis
We are experienced roof detectives. We have been able to diagnose many problems that are a mystery to other contractors.
For a competitive fee, we will inspect your roof and you will receive a written detailed report on the condition of your roof.
