Hire a Local Roofing Contractor or DO-IT-YOURSELF?
AUGUST 4, 2024
Roofing projects can be a lot of hard work, difficult, and downright dangerous. Before you jump on Google or YouTube and rush out to your nearest home improvement store, take a moment to consider the advantages of hiring a professional roofing company. Yes, we know there’s a certain satisfaction when tackling a do-it-yourself home improvement project, but when it comes to roof repair or replacement, it’s best to hire a professional roofing contractor.
If you’re needing to repair or replace your roof in the Lincoln/Omaha area, consider the following straight forward reasons of why you might want to stick with the pros when it comes to your roofing project:
4 REASONS TO HIRE A PROFESSIONAL ROOFING CONTRACTOR
Save Money On Roofing Costs
We know how it sounds to suggest that you would save money. But when it comes to something as technical and as important as roofing, you need to make sure that you get the highest quality work done right the first time around. No amount of research, studying, or how-to videos will ever come close to the same level of expertise and knowledge that is gained through experience. Do you really want to experiment with your home? A Better Way Construction & Roofing has been working on roofs for more than 40 years with nothing but positive reviews each and every time. Our team of Nebraska roofing contractors understand the challenges with weather, city ordinances, and more. And even though your roofing project may seem pretty straight forward, our professional roofers will have the skills to get the job done in the most efficient and reliable way possible. We can also identify roofing problems which an untrained eye may overlook. Hiring a professional can definitely save you some money!
Also consider the many components that make up your roof, from the nails to the shingles, the underlayment and gutters, all of which have to come together as one unit to protect your home. A single misplaced shingle can allow your roof to leak, which in turn can cause $1,000’s of dollars worth of damage. A small mistake can lead to bigger costlier damage months or even years down the road, resulting in unnecessary expenses!
Save Time on your Roof Repair or Replacement Project.
A majority of DIY roofers find that completing a roofing project takes a lot longer than they originally anticipated. Worst part is that if you don’t have the full day to work on installing your new roof, you may end up leaving your home exposed overnight, putting your home and family at risk to the elements. A roofing contractor is going to have the time, the experience, and a qualified team to get the job done quickly!
Save More Money on Top Quality Roofing Materials
Sourcing the roofing materials your need on your own takes time. Like a lot of time. When you work with us, you can be sure that we’ll procure the highest-quality materials for you, and because we’ve been in business for so long, we’ve built relationships with suppliers which means we get better pricing on materials than you’d be able to get on your own!
Save Your Life. Roofing Work can be fatal!
Did you know that Roofing is consistently ranked as one of the top 5 most dangerous jobs in America?!? Roof repair is risky work. If you don’t have the experience and are learning a new skill, chances are you’ll be more focused on the task and resources at hand, than your surroundings. Some roofs have steep slopes, some roofs have areas which may not be safe to walk on because of rotting or other issues. Several thousand people go to the ER every year as a result of falls. Some falls are fatal! Professional roofers have the right training, the right safety equipment, and the experience to complete your roofing project safely.
