GAF Has Great Warranties!
JUNE 9, 2022
A Better Way Construction and Roofing offers GAF Roofing System Limited Warranty and GAF System Plus Limited Warranty.
The Basic GAF Roofing System Limited* Warranty covers manufacture defects on materials for a lifetime however, replacement materials and installation labor are pro-rated after ten years.
When you install a qualifying GAF shingle, the basic GAF shingle and Accessories Limited Warranty provides a Lifetime Limited Warranty term for the shingles. When you install 3 qualifying accessory products you are automatically covered by the GAF Roofing System Limited* Warranty, which also provides Lifetime* coverage for your qualifying accessory products! “Lifetime” means as long as the original owner(s) or the second owner in certain circumstances owns the single-family detached residence where the shingles and qualifying accessories are installed.
The Lifetime* Warranty term also comes with 10 years of 100% coverage, during which GAF will provide any replacement GAF products necessary to repair your roof due to a manufacturing defect. GAF will also pay for reasonable cost to install them. *This Warranty is also transferable once to the next homeowner for FREE.
In addition to the GAF system, protect your home with the System Plus limited Warranty. The System Plus Limited Warranty increases the 100% Coverage period against the manufacturing defects from 10 years to 50 years! This Warranty is also transferable once to the next homeowner for FREE.
