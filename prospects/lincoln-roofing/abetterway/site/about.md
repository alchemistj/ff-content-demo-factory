Our Story
When the previous owners decided it was time to retire and enjoy the next chapter of their lives, they wanted to ensure the company remained in good hands. They offered the business to Alex, who had worked alongside them for more than 10 years and had become a familiar face to many customers. His experience, knowledge, and dedication made him the natural choice to continue the company’s legacy.
For the past six years, Alex and Maria have proudly owned and operated A Better Way Construction & Roofing. Together, they have continued building relationships with both new and returning customers while maintaining the personalized service and quality workmanship the company is known for. Their goal is simple: make every customer feel informed, comfortable, and confident throughout the entire project. From answering questions and addressing concerns to assisting with insurance claims, they are committed to providing support every step of the way.
