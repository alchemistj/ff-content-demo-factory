## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners with a warm outlet, a tripping breaker or a panel they don't trust, ElectriCraft is the electrician that fixes the problem and then tells you in plain words what else it sees, so you never wonder what's behind the wall.
- **B, method:** Unlike electricians who fix the one thing and leave, or quote a big job without showing why, ElectriCraft finds the cause, looks past it, and lays out your options before anything else is done.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You know what's in your walls, someone told you straight, and you chose what happens next.

**Pick: A.** It names the #1 fear and the moment she is in. B is a close second for the outreach message.

## What's thin
- **The lead is built from a handful of reviews.** Four reviews show a technician pointing out an extra issue; no review calls it a policy. An onboarding call should confirm they do this on every call.
- **Storm and utility work** rests on two reviews. Ask how storm calls are handled and whether the phone is answered after hours.
- **Hours.** The site says 24/7 emergency and also Monday to Friday, 7 to 5. Copy uses neither "24/7" nor a night promise.
- **Licence.** "Kansas Contractor Lic# 1399" is printed in the site footer (https://www.electricraftks.com/contact) and is used once, on the contact page, as the site's own claim. It is not checked against Kansas or MABCD.
- **Competitors.** Central States and Graf were not checked on their own sites, so "none of the 5" means none we could read.
- **Not in the copy:** years in business (three conflicting numbers), brands, financing, prices.
