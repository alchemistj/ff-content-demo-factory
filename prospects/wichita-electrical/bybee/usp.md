## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners who've been quoted a new meter, a new panel or a rewire and can't tell if it's real, Bybee Electric is the electrician that looks at the problem first and tells you straight when it's smaller than you feared or not ours to fix, so you decide with the facts.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that quote the fix before they've found the cause, Bybee looks first, explains what it found in plain words, and lays out options, so the quote matches the problem.
- **C, emotional outcome:** Someone told you it's the whole panel. Bybee came, looked, and told you what it was, and you stopped wondering whether you were being sold.

**Pick: A.** It names the avatar's quote-in-the-driveway moment and the claim no competitor makes. B is a close second for the outreach message.

## What's thin
- **The lead rests on three reviews** (Jennifer B., Bruce P., Jackie W.), all five-star. No page or owner statement says "we'll tell you if you don't need it." Ask: is that how you work, and how often does a call end that way?
- **Evergy-first is one story.** Ask whether they routinely send utility-side faults to Evergy.
- **No named person.** "DJ Bybee" is one unverified source; the copy speaks as "we". Ask who answers the phone and who gives the estimate.
- **No license number.** The site says "licensed and insured" with no number and MABCD has no public lookup. Copy repeats it as their claim only.
- **Years conflict** (2011 on About, "25+" elsewhere), so no year is used.
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8 to 5. No after-hours or emergency claim is made; storms and night calls are the avatar's moment 1 and are unanswered here.
- **Complaints exist** (rudeness, a billing dispute, repeat trips). The lead is "we look first", not "never a complaint".
- **Competitors:** Central States and Graf were not checked on their own sites ("not found" means not checked).
