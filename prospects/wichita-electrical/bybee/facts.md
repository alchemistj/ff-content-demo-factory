## Open questions for the onboarding call
1. Legal name, entity type and formation year: check the Kansas Secretary of State Business Entity Search (https://www.sos.ks.gov/eforms/BusinessEntity/Search.aspx, or kansas.gov/bess). My automated request was blocked (CloudFront 403); not retried or bypassed. Ask the owner for the exact entity name.
2. Years in business: 2011 (About) or "25+ years" (homepage, Our Team, FAQ)? Which is true?
3. Owner names: "DJ Bybee" came from one unverified search summary. Confirm the owner's full name and whether anyone else runs the business.
4. Electrical license: ask for the MABCD electrical contractor license number and holder name. The MABCD portal has no public lookup. Call the MABCD licensing line, 316-660-1840, or ask the owner for the license and insurance certificates. Record only what the owner shows or MABCD confirms.
5. Insurance: carrier and whether the certificate is current. Site says "fully insured" with no detail.
6. Street address and whether the Wichita shop address is the public one. Google shows 232 N Pennsylvania Ave; BBB snippet shows 1424 N Mosley St.
7. Service area: which towns beyond Wichita? Site says "Kansas and surrounding areas."
8. Commercial work: About lists commercial new construction, remodels, gas stations and pools. Is that real current work, or copied template text? Which of these should a demo mention?
9. Warranty: what does the one-year warranty cover, and is it in writing?
10. Free 10-point inspection: what does it include?
11. Hours: confirm Mon-Fri 8-5, and whether they take after-hours or emergency calls.
12. Reviews: confirm Google review count and rating (scout: 101, 4.7). Who owns the Hibu review widget? Can the "Write a Review" button be pointed at the Google review link?
13. Facebook page: owner to confirm the page is theirs and active (login wall blocked the check).
14. BBB: accredited status and rating, and whether BBB lists the Pennsylvania or Mosley address.
15. Brand and dealer status: "Siemens Breaker Panels" claim; ask for a dealer or certification proof before the demo says so.
16. Associations: IEC of Kansas, WABA. Ask for membership proof or drop them.
17. Google reviews text: owner first name appears in reviews or owner replies? (Not pulled; reviews are pulled only for final picks.)
18. Hibu contract: whether they pay Hibu, and whether the site and reviews can move.
