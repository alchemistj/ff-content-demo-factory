## Call Us

The quickest way is to call. Tell us the room, what you see, what you heard or smelled, and whether a breaker is involved.

Call (316) 409-4186, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We're closed Saturday and Sunday.

## Send a Message

Prefer to write it down? Send your name, phone number and a few lines about the problem, and we'll call you back. You can also email admin@bybeeelectric.com.

## What to Tell Us

- Where it's happening: which room, which outlet or breaker
- When it started, and whether anything changed first, like a new appliance
- Anything you've noticed: warmth, buzzing, a burning smell, flickering

If there's smoke, sparks or any sign of fire, get everyone out and call 911 first.
