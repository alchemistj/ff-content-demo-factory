## Gate result
Top claim: **Finds the cause when other electricians couldn't**, 16. **Passes gate (16+).** It is the minimum, and it rests on one strong review (Dod I.) plus one supporting one (Rachael R.).

## Core customer insight
- The buyer doesn't know why the problem keeps happening, only that it does.
- Most electricians fix the nearest symptom and send a bill.
- Linnebur works back to the cause and fixes that.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners who've paid an electrician and still have the same dead outlet or tripping breaker, Linnebur Electric is the crew that finds the cause, so you stop paying for guesses.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that swap the nearest part and move on, Linnebur traces the problem back to its source and fixes that, so you aren't calling a third electrician.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You stop living with a problem you can't explain, because someone found it and told you straight.

**Pick: A.** It names the parking-lot moment (the repair that didn't hold) in the buyer's words, and it states the claim without promising an outcome.

## What's thin
- **The lead rests on one review.** Dod I. (2019) is the only reviewer who says others failed. Ask: what kinds of problems do you get called in on after someone else tried?
- **Who is speaking.** Kent Linnebur is president on BBB and his email is on the site; "Rod" appears in Danny D.'s review. Copy speaks as "we" until the family confirms.
- **Years.** The site says both "Since 1982" and "over 30 years." No year is used.
- **License #1353.** Printed on the site only; type, holder and status unconfirmed with MABCD.
- **Hours, after-hours and storm calls, warranty, financing:** nothing found. None is claimed.
- **Recent reviews are light on trouble-finding.** Most of the 16 with text are short praise. The one 3-star (Monica R., 2019) is about a slow install and communication; not used.
- **Competitors:** Central States (blocked) and Graf (not checked) are "not found," not "absent."
