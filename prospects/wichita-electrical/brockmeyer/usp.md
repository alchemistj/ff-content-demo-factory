## Gate result
Top claim: "the electrician you can send to your mom's house," **16**. **Passes gate (16+),** just. It rests on two reviews, so the copy shows it through those two reviews and does not state it as a standing practice.

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita adult children who have to call an electrician for a parent's house they can't be at, Brockmeyer is the company that looks at the job in person, explains it in plain words and treats whoever is home with respect, so you stop wondering who is in her kitchen.
- **B, method:** Unlike companies that sell a guarantee and a fast truck, Brockmeyer sends someone to see the actual job and explain it, so the bill matches what was found.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You call from work, and by the afternoon your mom's outlet is fixed and she's telling you how nice the electrician was.

**Pick: A.** It names buyer C and the exact moment, and none of the five competitors says it. B supports the sentiment section.

## Brand short form
**Look First. Treat Them Right.**

## What's thin
- **Two reviews carry the lead.** Katherine A. (2023) and Olivia V. (2025). Neither says the electrician is a standing practice. Ask the owner how parents, tenants and people home alone are handled.
- **No owner or named staff.** The site names nobody, so the copy speaks as "we" and never says "owner". Will, Jeremy, Adam and Shawn come from reviews; their roles are unknown.
- **Response time.** One review has a same-day reply and one a next-day visit. No response time is stated.
- **24/7 emergency.** The site says "24/7 Emergency Services Available" next to weekday hours and closed weekends. We left it out until someone confirms who answers at night.
- **Union labor.** Olivia V. says "we love union labor." Nothing on the site says so, so it is not used.
- **License.** The logo reads "LIC # 11704" from a low-resolution crop. We did not print the number until the original file is checked against MABCD.
- **Competitors.** Central States (blocked) and Graf (site not checked) are "not found," not "absent."
- **Exclusivity of the lead** is "none found of 5", not a guarantee that no Wichita electrician says it.
