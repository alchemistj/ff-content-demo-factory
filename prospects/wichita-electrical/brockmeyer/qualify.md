# Qualify: Brockmeyer Inc.

- **Scout:** Google: 24 reviews (from the research prompt). Google star rating not given in the prompt and not verified. Google place ID not provided.
- **Site:** http://www.brockmeyerinc.com/ (redirects to https://www.brockmeyerinc.com/). Pages: /, /residential-electrician, /commercial-electrician, /blog (2 posts), /contact-us.
- **Phone:** 316-262-1440 (tappable tel: links in header and footer). **Email:** office@brockmeyerinc.com.
- **Kind:** electrical contractor, residential and commercial. Not a single-niche specialist. Site is weaker than it looks at first glance (see below), but it is not a bare template.

## Fit: borderline, not a clear demo case
- **Residential-focused brand, commercial page exists.** The brand voice leads residential: slogan "Powering Homes, One Wire at a Time!" on the residential page, the homepage lead is "Your home's electrical system...", the blog is two homeowner posts (backyard outdoor wiring; summer safety "for Wichita Homeowners"), and the contact form's Service list puts Residential first. The commercial page is real, though: its own H1 "High Voltage Expertise for High-Performing Businesses!", a 305-word page, and a 6-item service list (lighting, emergency lighting, breakers/panels, high and low voltage, DC power plants, data/telephone/cable wiring). Commercial is the second story, not an afterthought.
- **Site is weaker in copy and structure than in pages.** Two real service pages with titles that name the service and Wichita ("Residential Electrician in Wichita", "Commercial Electrician in Wichita"). But the visible H1s are slogans, not service-plus-city. Copy is generic. No owner, no About page, no review text rendered. 58 of 61 images have no alt text. See audit.md.
- **Brief rule check:** "Only weak sites become demos." The rule is about services that already have their own page with a service+city H1. Here the service pages exist but their H1s do not name the service and city, so the rule does not clearly block a demo. Still a judgment call.
- **Review base is small.** 24 Google reviews against 20 years in business (2005 on the site) is thin for a demo's social proof. Ballinger had 158.
- **Contact form:** present on every page, not required fields, Service select, textarea. Markup says CAPTCHA is required (`data-require-captcha="true"`), but no CAPTCHA widget or script rendered in the page. Not submitted (per the brief). The form may be blocked at send time.

## Recommendation for Josh
Lean fit as a 5-page demo only if Josh wants a residential-first demo with commercial as a second page. If the goal is a commercial-heavy demo, the site does not show enough commercial proof yet. Decide before the outreach step. Not picked here.
