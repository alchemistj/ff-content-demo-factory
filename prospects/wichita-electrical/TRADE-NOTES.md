## Trade knowledge readers value
Use these where they fit the page (general trade knowledge, not claims about the business):
- Why breakers trip: an overloaded circuit, a short, or a ground fault; a breaker that trips again right after reset is telling you something; a breaker that's warm or won't reset needs an electrician.
- What warm outlets and flickering lights mean: loose connections heat up; a warm or discolored outlet or switch plate, a burning smell or buzzing is a loose or failing connection, a fire risk; flicker on one fixture is often the bulb or fixture, flicker across rooms or when a big appliance starts points to a loose connection or an overloaded service.
- When an old panel is a safety problem: fuse boxes, panels with breakers known to fail to trip (Federal Pacific Stab-Lok, Zinsco), rust or scorch marks, double-tapped breakers, a panel with no room for new circuits, 60- or 100-amp service in a house now running AC, an EV charger or a hot tub.
- What a panel upgrade involves: a permit and city inspection, the power company disconnecting and reconnecting service, usually a day of work with the power off for part of it, a new panel (often 200 amps), sometimes a new meter base or service mast, every circuit labeled.

Keep it honest and Wichita-plain. Don't scare people; explain what the sign means and what we do about it.
