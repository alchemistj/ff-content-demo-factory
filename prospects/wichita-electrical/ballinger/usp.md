## Core customer insight
- **The buyer doesn't know:** whether the mess in her walls is a small fix or a big one, or why the electricians she called won't come look.
- **Most companies:** quote the clean jobs and quietly pass on the old-house puzzles, or lead with a hotline, a guarantee or a coupon.
- **Ballinger instead:** comes to look at the mess, traces the cause, and gives options and a price before any work.

## Gate result

## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners with an older house, a dead circuit or wiring a previous owner left behind, Ballinger is the electrician that comes to look when others won't, so you aren't left holding the problem.
- **B, method:** Unlike electricians who quote the easy jobs and pass on the messy ones, Ballinger comes to look, traces the cause and gives you options and a price before any work starts.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You stop being the person nobody will call back. Somebody comes, finds out what's wrong and tells you straight.

**Pick: A.** It names the fear of being turned away and the exact kind of house, and no competitor states it. B supplies the method section.

## What's thin
- **"Others turn it down" rests on two reviews** (Elena W., Wayne R.). The older-house and DIY jobs Ballinger accepted are in seven or more. Ask Ben: how often do people call after being turned away?
- **The owner is unconfirmed.** Copy says "we" and names Ben, Kenny and Jayse only as the people reviews name.
- **License, insurance and years are unconfirmed.** The MABCD lookup tied nothing to Ballinger; none appears in copy.
- **Competitor scan is partial.** Central States was blocked and Graf's site was not checked, so "none of the 5" means none we could read.
- **Hours conflict.** The site says emergency service "any time" and closed weekends. Copy makes no after-hours promise.
- **"Looks over the rest" is a pattern in six reviews, not a stated policy.** Confirm it is free.
