- Service page 1: Electrical Repair in Wichita, KS — Ballinger Electric. It is the biggest group in the reviews (outlets dead across a house, a bad breaker, a garage without power, a well pump behind a high bill, a doorbell, "figuring out the problem in my 50 year old house").

Not panel upgrade: the reviews have far more lights, ceiling fans and under-cabinet and outdoor lighting (Ron P., Nancy C., Alex K., Steve A., Kenetta M., Ariana B., Jeff D., C T., Dusten M.) than panels (Aaron W., Wayne R., Bill H.). ## The idea
Ballinger is the electrician a household keeps. People call for a dead outlet and call back for the next project: Robert R. (4 jobs), Blake (5+ times), Stephanie S. (since 2019), Terri G. ("several jobs ... suggestions have improved our projects"), Rudy P. (multiple jobs over the years). It earns the call-back three ways the reviews repeat: they will take the messy problem others won't (Elena W., Wayne R., Rajeeb H., Che J.), they get you on the schedule in a day or two (Todd C., Taygen A., C T., Nancy C., Joshua D.), and you hear your options and the price before the work (Mary K., Chris B., Lan N., Alan B.).
- Different from Enegren (a named person at the door, walked through it) and Bybee (look first, tell you straight, smaller fix): this is the repeat-customer, whole-house, call-first electrician who takes the jobs others turn down and fits you in fast.

## Questions a visitor asks
- Do you charge for an estimate? Site: free estimates.
- How do you price? By the completed job; quote before the work (owner reply; Lan N., Alan B., Tony H.).
- How fast can you come? Reviews: next day to a few days, one within the hour. Stated as "often within a day or two", not promised.
- Will you take a strange old-house problem? Reviews yes.
- Homes or businesses? Site: both; reviews show homes mostly.
- When is the office open? Site: Mon-Fri 8-5.
