## Why Your Breaker Keeps Tripping

A breaker is a safety switch. It trips when a circuit is carrying more than it should, and there are three usual reasons.

An overloaded circuit has too many things plugged into it. A short is a hot wire touching something it shouldn't. A ground fault is current leaking where it doesn't belong, often from a damaged wire or moisture.

If it trips once when you run the microwave and the toaster together, that may just be load. If it trips again right after you reset it, something is wrong in the circuit, and resetting it over and over doesn't fix it.

A breaker that's warm, or one that won't reset at all, needs an electrician.

## Warm Outlets, Buzzing and Flickering Lights

Loose connections heat up. A wire that isn't tight in an outlet, a switch or the panel creates resistance, and resistance makes heat.

A warm or discolored outlet or switch plate, a burning smell or a buzzing sound is a loose or failing connection. Stop using it and call. It's a fire risk, and it only gets worse.

Flicker is a different question. One fixture flickering is often the bulb or the fixture. Flicker across several rooms, or lights that dim when the air conditioner kicks on, points to a loose connection or a service that's carrying too much.
