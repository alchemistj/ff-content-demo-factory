## Three USP statements
- **A, pain point:** For Wichita homeowners who can't see behind the wall and can't tell a small fix from a sales pitch, Enegren is the electrician who finds the cause, explains it in plain words and gives you options, so you decide what gets done.
- **B, method:** Unlike electricians who hand you one number, Enegren finds the cause first and lays out your options, so the choice stays yours.
- **C, emotional outcome:** You know who is coming, you understand what they found, and you chose how far to go.

**Pick: A.** It names the fear and the moment, and the options half is the part no competitor states. The name-and-photo half rides in the hero and the sentiment section.
