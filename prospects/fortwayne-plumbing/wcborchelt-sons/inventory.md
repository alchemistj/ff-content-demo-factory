## Lead candidates (Appeal × Exclusivity, 1–5 each; method in docs/writer-guides/AVATAR-AND-USP.md)

Gate: a claim must score 16 or more to lead. Nothing reaches 16, so any demo built from this is "below gate."

1. **"Same-day, on time, and the tech tells you what he's doing."** Appeal 5 × Exclusivity 2 = **10**. Speed is the top want (28 reviews), but nearly every HVAC company claims same-day. Only the reviews back it up.
2. **"They fix what's broken, not sell you a new one."** Appeal 5 × Exclusivity 3 = **15** (top lead, one point below the gate). The fear of upselling is strong, and the reviews show it (Kerstin R., Kari G., Danielle L.). One 1-star review says the opposite (Josh C.), so this claim is "what customers saw," not a standing practice.
3. **"The same technician comes back: Cameron, Hayden, Mike, Seth, RJ."** Appeal 4 × Exclusivity 3 = **12**. Named staff is a repeat theme (about 20 reviews). Only reviews name them; the site names no one.
4. **"Family owned since 1962, third generation."** Appeal 3 × Exclusivity 3 = **9**. Stated on the site only, not independently checked. Nice to have, not a fear.
5. **"Two visits a year, diagnostic waived up to four times."** Appeal 4 × Exclusivity 2 = **8**. Plan terms are on the site. Many local companies offer plans too. Price is "call for pricing."

Scores are my judgement from the material above, not a competitor survey (no `competitors/` file was checked for this prospect).
