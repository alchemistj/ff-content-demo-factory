ABOUT US
Family Owned and Operated since 1962.
We opened our doors in May of 1962, but our founder, Wally, had been working the business long before that. Wally was known for helping everyone at any time, even if it meant showing up to work in his Sunday best. Then, in 1993, his sons took over. Leading the company into the next generation and growing the business. In 2020, the brothers retired, and three new owners from within the
company took over with one of the three being from the Borchelt family making it a third-generation family owned & operated company. All our owners, past and present, have and continue to
strive to serve our community with the best quality and service
there is.
