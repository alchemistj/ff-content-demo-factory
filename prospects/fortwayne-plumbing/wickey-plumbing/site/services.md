FULL-SERVICE PLUMBING IN FORT WAYNE & NORTHEAST INDIANA
From drain cleaning and water heaters to gas lines, repiping, and water quality systems, Wickey Plumbing handles it all — veteran-owned, licensed, and built on honest, upfront service. No surprises, ever.
Drain Cleaning & Sewer Lines
A clogged drain or backed-up sewer can bring your whole day to a halt. We handle everything from a slow kitchen sink and clogged toilet to a fully backed-up sewer line, clearing the blockage and getting your plumbing flowing again. If you're dealing with recurring clogs, gurgling drains, or a sewage smell, those are signs of a deeper problem we can diagnose and fix. We also repair and replace damaged sewer lines that have failed due to age, shifting, or tree root intrusion.
Our licensed and insured team gets to the root of the problem instead of just treating the symptom, so the clog stays gone. We explain what's happening in plain language and give you upfront pricing before we start — no surprises, ever. Serving homes and businesses across Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana, Wickey Plumbing brings honest, dependable drain and sewer service every time. Call us when you need a clog cleared right the first time.
Water Heater Repair & Installation
Few problems disrupt a household like no hot water. We repair, replace, and install water heaters of all types, including energy-efficient tankless water heaters that deliver endless hot water and save space. If your water heater is leaking, making strange noises, running out of hot water quickly, or simply getting old, we can help you decide whether to repair or replace it. We walk you through the pros and cons of tank versus tankless so you choose the right system for your home and budget.
Every installation is done by a licensed and insured Indiana plumber and backed by our satisfaction guarantee and warranty. We give you honest, upfront pricing and never push you toward more than you need. Serving Fort Wayne and the surrounding Northeast Indiana communities, Wickey Plumbing makes getting reliable hot water simple and stress-free. Whether it's a quick repair or a full replacement, we get it done right.
Gas Line Services
Gas line work is no place for guesswork — it has to be done safely and to code. We provide professional gas line repair and installation for water heaters, ranges, dryers, fireplaces, outdoor grills, and more. If you smell gas, suspect a leak, or need a new line run for an appliance or remodel, our licensed team handles it with the care and precision the job demands. A gas leak is a serious safety issue, and we treat it that way.
As a licensed and insured Indiana plumbing contractor, we install and repair gas lines correctly the first time, with your family's safety as the top priority. We keep you informed throughout and provide clear, upfront pricing before any work begins. Serving Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana, Wickey Plumbing brings veteran-owned integrity to every gas line project. Trust us to get it done safely and right.
Fixture Repair & Installation
The everyday fixtures in your home see constant use, and a leaky faucet, running toilet, or worn-out sink can waste water and money. We repair and install faucets, toilets, sinks, and other plumbing fixtures, whether you're fixing a frustrating drip or upgrading to something new. A running toilet or dripping faucet may seem minor, but it adds up on your water bill — and we'll fix it fast. We also handle full fixture installations for remodels and upgrades.
Our licensed and insured plumbers do clean, careful work and stand behind it with our satisfaction guarantee. You always get honest, upfront pricing with no hidden fees — no surprises, ever. Whole-House Repiping
Older homes often have aging, corroded, or leak-prone pipes that lead to recurring leaks, discolored water, and low water pressure. When repairs are no longer enough, a whole-house repipe replaces your failing plumbing with new, reliable piping throughout. If you notice frequent pipe leaks, rusty water, or pressure problems in an older home, those are common signs you may need repiping. It's a major upgrade that protects your home and restores your water system for decades.
We approach every repipe with careful planning to minimize disruption to your home, and our licensed and insured team keeps you informed at every stage. As always, you get honest, transparent pricing up front — no surprises, ever. Serving Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana, Wickey Plumbing brings veteran-owned craftsmanship to one of the most important investments you can make in your home. Sump & Sewage Pumps
A working sump pump is your basement's first line of defense against flooding and water damage, especially during heavy Indiana rains and spring thaws. We install, repair, and replace sump pumps and sewage pumps to keep water where it belongs and out of your home. If your pump is running constantly, failing to turn on, or making unusual noises, it may be time for service or replacement before the next big storm. A failed pump at the wrong moment can lead to costly flooding.
Our licensed and insured team helps you choose the right pump for your home and installs it for dependable performance year-round. We provide honest, upfront pricing and stand behind our work with a satisfaction guarantee. Serving Fort Wayne and the surrounding Northeast Indiana area, Wickey Plumbing helps protect your basement and your peace of mind. Don't wait for the next heavy rain to find out your pump has failed.
Water Quality Systems

If you're dealing with hard water, scale buildup, or water that just doesn't taste right, we can recommend the right solution for your home. Better water protects your fixtures and appliances while making everyday water cleaner and healthier.
Our licensed and insured team takes the time to test, explain your options, and install the system that fits your needs and budget — with honest, upfront pricing and no surprises. Serving Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana, Wickey Plumbing offers a fuller range of water quality solutions than many local plumbers.
