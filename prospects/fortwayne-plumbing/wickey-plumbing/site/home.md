Wickey Plumbing LLC is led by Josh Wickey, a Christian, U.S. Army veteran, and licensed Indiana plumbing contractor who has served Northeast Indiana as a plumber since 2016 — over 10 years of experience across residential and industrial plumbing. We are a Christian-owned, veteran-owned, and family-run business built on a simple promise: do honest work, treat people right, and be completely upfront and transparent every step of the way. No surprises, ever. We are proud to serve our neighbors throughout Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana, and to give back to the veteran and local causes that matter to our community.
As a full-service plumber, we handle everything your home or business needs — drain cleaning, sewer line repair, water heater repair and installation, tankless water heaters, gas line work, whole-house repiping, sump pumps, and complete water quality systems. Whether you're dealing with a clogged drain, no hot water, a leaking pipe, low water pressure, or hard water, our licensed and insured team diagnoses the problem and fixes it the right way. We serve both residential and commercial customers across Fort Wayne and the surrounding Allen County area.
