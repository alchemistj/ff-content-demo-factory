Regional Public Safety Academy
The Regional Public Safety Academy is 132,000 square feet of joint-use training and education space. An emphasis on technology and on-line learning will help the Academy export its training services throughout the region and the state using a new fiber optics network. Conference areas, a Planning Center and public access space is available for regional training and meetings. The firearms training simulator, wet and dry fire training rooms, large indoor shooting range, health and fitness center, emergency vehicle driving simulator and customized exercise scenario software makes it the public safety training destination of choice for Northeast Indiana.
