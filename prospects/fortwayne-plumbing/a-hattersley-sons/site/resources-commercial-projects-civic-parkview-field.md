Parkview Field
Parkview Field includes approximately 6,500 fixed seats, specialty seating, 16 suites, a suite-level lounge, party deck, rentable event space, kid’s zone and amphitheatre. The ballfield includes a state of the art drainage system, dugouts, bullpen areas, groundskeeping and maintenance areas, picnic area and multiple concourse level food and beverage options, the TinCaps administrative offices, press facilities, box office and a 5,000 square foot kitchen facility.
Project Highlights:
Location: Fort Wayne, Indiana
Architect: HOK Sport
Engineer: Henderson Engineers, Inc.
Total Project Cost: $30.6 Million
