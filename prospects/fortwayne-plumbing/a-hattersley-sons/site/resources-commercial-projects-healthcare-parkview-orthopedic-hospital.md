Parkview Orthopedic Hospital
Major additions and renovations to existing Parkview Orthopedic Hospital. This project has added two operating rooms, six surgical admission bays, six post-anastheia recovery rooms, pre-admission testing services, and enhanced ergonomics for patient and employee safety. The existing operating rooms were kept operational during their construction and renovation.
Project Highlights:
Location: Fort Wayne, Indiana
Architect: Vintage Archonics
Engineer: Vintage Archonics
Total Project Cost: $20 million
