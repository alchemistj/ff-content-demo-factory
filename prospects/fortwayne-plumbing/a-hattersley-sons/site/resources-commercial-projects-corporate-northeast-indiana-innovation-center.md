Northeast Indiana Innovation Center
The Northeast Indiana Innovation Center houses and operates a 40,403 sq. ft. business incubation and accel­eration facility on a 55-acre Innovation Park campus adjacent to the IPFW campus and the Ivy Tech north campus in Fort Wayne, Indiana. The biomedical and advanced security wing offers 13 wet labs and 11 executive office suites. A common equipment room houses an autoclave, a -80 degree freezer, a refrigerator, bottle washer, reverse osmosis water system, and a fully operational replica of a hospital sterilization area, complete with RFID tracking.
