Salin Bank
The new 10,000 sq.ft. Salin Bank building is located in a historic area of downtown Fort Wayne. The building includes personal and business banking areas along with a 5,000 sq.ft. leasable tenant area. This building features a 56 foot clock tower, making it an instant landmark in the downtown area.
Project Highlights:
Location: Fort Wayne, Indiana
Architect: MSKTD and Associates, Inc.
