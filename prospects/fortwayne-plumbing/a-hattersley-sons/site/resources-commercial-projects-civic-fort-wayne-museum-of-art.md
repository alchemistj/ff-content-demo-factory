Fort Wayne Museum of Art
The $5 million art gallery and auditorium expansion of the Fort Wayne Museum of Art is underway. This Fort Wayne museum project includes a new outdoor sculpture court and arts terrace. The interior renovations include: the subdivision of the open gallery floor plate to distinct galleries, a new education center, a new library, a new bistro, gift shop, various improvements to the art collection storage, and administrative offices.
Project Highlights:
Location: Fort Wayne, Indiana
Architect: Morrison Kattman Menze
