# Contact New Windows For America

Call us and tell us what is going on in your house. We serve Wichita and nearby areas.

**Buttons:** Call (316) 263-0711 | What to Have Ready

## Call Us

Call (316) 263-0711.

Tell us whether it's the attic, the floors, the windows or the furnace, and we'll take it from there.

## What to Have Ready

You don't need to know the answer to any of these. It just helps to have a few things in mind when you call.

- Which rooms are cold, hot, drafty or loud
- Whether you want insulation, windows or both
- Whether your furnace or air conditioner needs a look
- Whether you are already part of the SCKEDD weatherization program

## Where We Work

We serve Wichita and nearby areas, and our crews have worked in Newton and Parsons. If you're in a town not on that list, call and ask.

## Before You Call

**Do you work on weatherization program homes?**
Yes. We work under the SCKEDD Weatherization program. If you're not in the program, call and tell us what you're after.

**Do you do windows as well as insulation?**
Yes. We replace windows and insulate, and we install furnaces and air conditioners.
