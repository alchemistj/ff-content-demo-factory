## When a Window Stops Doing Its Job

Windows wear out in ways you can feel before you can see them. The curtains move with the window shut. The glass is cold to the touch in January and hot in July.

Fog between the panes means the seal around the glass has failed and the air space has filled with moisture. A sash that sticks or won't stay up means the parts that carry it have worn or the frame has shifted.

Street noise coming straight through is the same story. Every one of these is a window that has stopped sealing, and you are heating and cooling the outdoors through it.

## Rule Out the Rest of the House First

A drafty room is not always a drafty window. Air leaking in through an empty attic, a bare floor or a gap in a wall can feel just like a window problem.

New windows will not fix a room that is cold because there's nothing in the wall behind it. That's a job for [insulation](/insulation-wichita-ks/), and insulation is most of what we do.

So when we come out, we can tell you whether the windows are what's costing you, whether the house is, or whether it's both. If your windows are the problem, replace them. If they're not, we'll say so.

## Comparing Window Quotes

If you're getting more than one estimate, compare the same things on each. Ask how many windows are in the quote, how long until they're installed, and what the price covers.

Ask whether the install is part of the price, and who does it: the company's own crew or someone hired for the job. A low number means little if the install date is five months out.

And if anyone tells you the price goes up tomorrow unless you sign today, you can take that as a reason to slow down. A window is something you'll look at every day for years.

## Why Vinyl

We replace windows with vinyl windows. Vinyl frames don't rot, don't need painting and don't swell shut in the humid months the way an old wood frame can.

A vinyl window has a sealed glass unit and weatherstripping that presses against the sash, so it closes tight and stays closed. In a house that's lost its seals, you can feel that on the first cold day.
