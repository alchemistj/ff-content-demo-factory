# New Windows For America, LLC - facts (Wichita KS)

Researched 2026-11-09. Read-only; nothing submitted. Sources: site rendered with Playwright, 54-review file (wichita/reviews/new-windows-for-america.md), qualify/12.md, BBB profile (fetched), Hitachi dealer page (fetched). BuildZoom, Manta, Angi and Yelp returned 403 to me, so facts from them are "search-summary only" and marked as such.

## 1. Owner
- **Heath Marx - probable, not confirmed as sole owner.**
  - BBB profile (https://www.bbb.org/us/ks/wichita/profile/window-installation/new-windows-for-america-0714-4659), which I fetched: Management "Heath Marx, Owner".
  - Review by Deric C. (2022-05-10): "Heath with New Windows For America was the last company that came out for my estimate." So Heath is the person who does window estimates. No surname in any review.
  - qualify/12.md says BuildZoom lists Heath Marx on the KS roofing license. I could not load BuildZoom.
- **Kirby Stucky: NOT confirmed.** Manta returned 403, and web searches for the name together with the company found nothing. I could not settle it from Manta. Evidence favors Heath Marx (BBB plus a review). Kirby may be a former owner or an older record, but I have no source for that.
- Owner replies on Google (to Deric C., Rina B., Shannon P., Mary H., Dell J., Steven L., Sandy M.) are all unsigned. They name no one except "Danny's crew". Do not use a name that is not Heath. Use "Heath" only if the pitch is softened (for example "Heath Marx per BBB").
- Other staff named in reviews, none of them owners: Zach P. (crew lead, mentioned in about 30 reviews), Danny Rich (crew lead 2021-2023), Alex, Albert, Donna (scheduler/receptionist, "Donna arranging for the appointments", reviews by Cassy C. and Linda S.). A 2019 review (Lee W.) says the receptionist is the mother of a crew supervisor.

## 2. Independent / age
- Independent and locally owned: no franchise or chain branding on the site, and BBB shows a single Wichita LLC. The Hitachi page calls them an "authorized manufacturer's rep" for Hitachi HVAC, which is not a franchise.
- **Founding date conflicts.** The site says "Since 2014". The BBB page I fetched says "Founded May 1, 1998" and "BBB Accredited since July 1, 2000". qualify/12.md says BBB lists a new-ownership date of 1/1/2014, which I did not see on the page. Safest wording: "Serving Wichita since 2014" is the site's own claim. Do not say 1998.
- First Google review is dated 2019-05-01, and the earliest dated reviews are 2019.
- Staff: Angi says 8 employees (search summary only). qualify/12 says Manta estimates 5-9.

## 4. Claims
- Site claims: "Since 2014"; "dependable solutions backed by skilled workmanship and a commitment to doing the job right". Nothing else. No license, insurance, warranty, financing, free estimate, service-area or brand claim on the site.
- BBB (fetched): Accredited Business, A+, no complaints in last 3 years, 1 BBB review. Only one phone listed there. The 1-star Google reviewer Debbie H. (2025) said she would contact the BBB.
- Licensed: roofing license on BuildZoom per qualify/12 (not seen by me). A review says they use "lead safe practices" (Lee W., 2019, disputed).
- Warranty: Richard G. says a "lifetime warranty" was sold but not honored; no warranty text anywhere on the site.
- Service area from reviews: Wichita, Newton (Sandie T.), Parsons (Jeff W.). The Hitachi page says "Wichita and nearby areas".
- Weatherization: Cary W. says "This company performs work under the SCKEDD Weatherization program"; Connie H. mentions a grant project.
- **Brand: Hitachi (HVAC) - VERIFIED.** The manufacturer's own locator page (https://www.hitachiaircon.com/us/residential-contractors/new-windows-for-america) lists them: "authorized manufacturer's rep of Hitachi products ... residential and commercial Hitachi Mini-Split systems, Mini-VRF systems, VRF systems as well as controlling systems", 3949 Bridgeport Cir, 67219 Wichita, (316) 263-0711, https://nwfawichita.com/. No tier/certification level is given. The page also says Hitachi Cooling & Heating is now made and sold by Bosch Home Comfort Group. Their own site does not mention Hitachi.
- No window brand (Pella, Andersen, etc.) named in the site or reviews, so nothing to check in a locator. Site says only "Vinyl Windows".
- Financing, free estimates: not claimed anywhere I could see.

## 5. Website problems (http://nwfawichita.com/, redirects to https)
- Built on the Gator template builder (assets on storage.googleapis.com/gator-users); JS-rendered; title is just "Home"; meta viewport fixed `width=1024`, so not mobile-responsive.
- Top text: "NWFA" logo, nav Home / Services / About us / Contact, "Since 2014", "New Windows For America", "Windows • Siding • Roofing • Insulation • HVAC • Remodeling", headline "Known for windows. Trusted for the rest of your home." Sub-copy: "From energy-efficient windows and durable roofing to siding, HVAC, and insulation services, we provide dependable solutions backed by skilled workmanship and a commitment to doing the job right." Then "Our Services": Vinyl Windows, Siding, Roofing. Footer "© 2026 All Rights Reserved."
- The word "Wichita" appears nowhere in the page text. No phone number and no tel: link. No address, no hours, no reviews, no photos of work in text, no per-service pages.
- **Inner pages are dead.** /services, /about%20us and /contact all show "Page Not Found / Oh, man. Looks like you're totally lost." (The /services and /contact files were 986 bytes of 200 status, so the 404 text sits in a 200 response. /about-us gives a real 404.) The Vinyl Windows and Siding tiles both link to /services, which is the 404; Roofing has no link.
- Debbie H. (2025-10-29) says she used the "contact us" page on their website to email about damage and got no reply, so the contact page may have worked once or she means an older version.

## 7. What customers praise
- Crew professionalism and cleanup: polite, explain everything before they start, "cleaned up completely", on time, finished in a day. Most of this is credited to named crew leaders (Zach, Danny). Several reviewers say "ask for Zach and his crew".
- For windows: honest estimate, faster than the 14-18 weeks others quoted (7 weeks), one-day install, product looks good.
- Weak spots to avoid promising: unanswered phone calls and voicemails (Meagan B. 2019, Debbie H. 2025), a refused warranty (Richard G. 2019), and a messy crew (Debbie H. 2025, Lee W. 2019). Google rating is 4.7 over 54 reviews.
