## The idea
A house gets uncomfortable through the parts you can't see (the attic, the floor, the gaps), and the window gets the blame because it's the part you can see. So we're an insulation company and a window company: we fix what is actually leaking, and our crews tell you what they're doing while they do it.

Source: 36 of 48 written reviews are insulation and weatherization; the same reviews praise crews who "explained everything" and "cleaned up completely." Windows are 4 reviews, all 2019 to 2022.

## Questions a visitor asks, and the answers we can give
- **Windows or insulation first?** If rooms are cold or hot and the windows aren't fogged or broken, the attic and floor are the likelier cause. We do both, so we can say which. (Trade knowledge plus 36 insulation reviews.)
- **How long?** Many insulation jobs are done in a day (Steven L., Rina B., Mary H.); a whole-house job can bring two crews (Cary W., Diana P.). Window install is a day (Deric C.); the wait is for the windows to be built, 7 weeks for Deric against 14 to 18 quoted elsewhere.
- **Do you work with the SCKEDD weatherization program?** Yes (Cary W., Cassy C., Connie H.). The program decides who qualifies and when your home comes up; the wait can be long (Cary W.).
- **Will it be a mess?** Cleanup is in about a third of the reviews.
- **Furnace and AC?** Yes, and Hitachi's own contractor page lists us as an authorized manufacturer's rep for its mini-splits.
- **Where?** Wichita and nearby areas; crews have worked in Newton and Parsons.
