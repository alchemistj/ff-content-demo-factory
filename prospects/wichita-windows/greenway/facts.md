## 6. What is actually weak on the site
The site is not basic. It has 81 pages and 91 posts, a page per service, 14 suburb pages, and a Trustindex reviews widget. Loads are fast: home about 2.3 s, window pages about 0.8-0.9 s in headless Chrome with no throttling, 49-78 requests each. All 81 pages in the sitemap returned 200. No horizontal scroll at 390 px. So the weakness is design, trust, and conversion, not SEO or speed.

1. **Trust claims that BBB contradicts.** /windows/ says "BBB accreditation"; BBB says not accredited. The "over 15 years" line is 14 per BBB, and the site's own "since 2012" pages disagree with it. These sit in the exact text a homeowner reads when deciding whether to trust a window installer.
2. **Promo banner.** A black bar reading "Offering Flexible Financing: No Payments Until 2027 + 0% for 18 Months" is on every page and sticks to the top on scroll. On a phone it takes about 85 px at the top, and with the header about 170 of 844 px. It will expire in about 2 months (2027 starts 7 weeks from today) and is hard-coded in the home hero too ("No Payments Until 2027 / Get 0% Financing For 18 months!!!").
3. **Phone, mobile.** The home page first screen (390x844) shows the banner, logo, hamburger and a before/after photo only. The H1 starts about 1,000 px down, so the H1 and a phone number are not on the first screen. The tap-to-call link is in the hamburger menu (7 tel: links in the markup). There is no sticky call button. On the window page the hero has only a green "Get Free Quote" button.
4. **Window pages on a phone.**
   - /windows/ (final URL after /window-replacement/ redirects to it) is 9,346 px tall on a phone and /window-installation/ is 16,433 px. The page opens with an H1 and a quote button, then a stack of large photos with card text.
   - The headline "GET YOUR FREE WINDOW CONSULTATION | CALL (316-641-8365)" is plain text, not a tap-to-call link.
   - The sticky header is slightly see-through: scrolling text shows behind the logo and menu.
   - The page never names a window brand. ProVia, the one brand customers mention, appears only as a gray logo on the home page.
5. **Stock look.** Window photos on /windows/ are clean generic exterior shots (brick houses, black-framed windows) with no people, no installers and no Greenway branding. The home page does use real before/after project photos with the logo. The window-page images do not look like Wichita jobs. I cannot prove they are stock.
6. **Siding and windows share the home page.** The home H1 is "Window Replacement & Siding Installation In Wichita, KS", but siding cards come first and there are 4 siding cards, 1 window card, 1 gutter card. Windows are 44% of what reviewers talk about.
7. **Copy.** Long, repeated SEO paragraphs. About 25 of the 81 sitemap pages are Wichita attraction pages (museums, breweries, zoo, drive-in) that have nothing to do with their work. Reviewer names are shown in full in the widget.
8. **Small errors.** The top nav item "Siding" links to "/" (the home page). "Window Replacement" in the nav 301-redirects to /windows/, so there are two names for one page. "/eastbrough-..." is a typo slug that redirects to /eastborough-.... Footer says "© 2026". Brand-logo alt text is auto-generated nonsense ("logo for proview professional", "logo of the company timesstar").
9. **Reviews on the site.** 83-review Trustindex widget on home and /windows/ shows 9 reviews on each of those pages. It also has a /testimonials/ page and /video-testimonials/. No BBB badge appears on the testimonials page, so the BBB claim has no visual backing either.
