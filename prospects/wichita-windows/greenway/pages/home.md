## Siding Replacement and Repair

Siding takes the Kansas wind, hail and summer sun before anything else on the house does. When it wears out you can see it: cracks, warped or loose pieces, faded panels, a stain on the ceiling inside.

What you can't see is the wall behind it, which is where the water goes when siding stops shedding it.

You don't always need the whole house done. If three walls are fine and one is shot, we'll tell you so and price the one. When the whole house does need it, the old siding leaves with us.

We also repair siding: rotted boards, storm damage and chimney siding.

## One Company for the Whole Outside

Water doesn't care which part of the house it gets past. A gap at a window, a loose piece of siding and a sagging gutter can all send it to the same wall.

That's why we do all of it, and why I look at the whole house even when you call about one window. A leak at a window sill is often a siding or flashing problem, and you should know that before you pay to replace the window.

Do the whole exterior at once or one project at a time. Either way, you get one company to call.

## Questions Before You Call

**Is the estimate free?**
Yes. I come to the house, look at the job and give you the price at no charge.

**Do you do small jobs?**
Yes. One bathroom window, a garage wall, a chimney or a single section of siding are all jobs we take.

The price is for the work I saw. If we open a wall and find rot or damage nobody could see from outside, that is extra work, and it gets its own price.

Get other bids and compare them line by line. We've come in under other quotes again and again, because we price the work your house needs and not a package.

No. Doing them together means the trim gets finished once, but it's your call and your budget.
