## The idea (homepage)
Replacing windows should be the easy part of your year. You deal with the owner, Rick, who tells you straight what you need and what it costs; then a crew shows up on the date, finishes fast and leaves the house cleaner than it found it. Windows are what we do most, and the same owner and crew handle the doors, siding, gutters and storm claims around them.

Proof pattern in the reviews: "Rick was straightforward", "zero upselling or pressure", "very down to earth", "kept me informed with their schedule", "professional, efficient, cleaned up", "left the job site clean", fair price after shopping around.

### Windows (links to the window page)
- **Moment:** a room that never gets comfortable, fog between the panes, a sash that sticks or will not stay up, the road noise you stopped noticing.
- **Reason:** double-pane windows fail when the seal fails; once it does, the window leaks air and noise whatever the glass looks like.
- **What we do:** replace double-hung, sliding, casement, picture, awning, shaped and basement windows. Custom-built, double-pane, Low-E glass with argon, from the site's own spec. One window or the whole house (Stacy C. did part; Brooke B. did all; Daniel H. did the rest after two).
- **Proof:** Greg S.: "Since the installation our house is much quieter, I should have done this sooner."
- Not claimed: brand (ProVia unverified), American-made, "lifetime warranty".

### Storm damage and insurance claims
- **Moment:** hail or a summer storm has cracked glass or damaged a roof, and an adjuster's estimate has just landed on the table.
- **Reason:** an insurance claim has paperwork and an adjuster to deal with; insurers pay for the approved work, so the person doing the work should be the one working the claim.
- **What we do:** work with your adjuster through the claim and do the approved work with our own crews (site, roofing page; Lakesha B. for windows). Roofing line (833) 972-7663 (site; the demo shows it as the roofing line).
- **Proof:** Lakesha B.: "They worked diligently with me throughout the insurance claim process to secure a full window replacement, making everything smooth and stress-free." plus the pay-nothing-out-of-pocket sentence. Used as her result, not a promise.
- Not claimed: that any claim will be paid in full, license numbers, Owens Corning.

1. **Is the estimate free?** Yes (site: free estimates).
2. **Will you push me to buy more?** Pattern in reviews: "zero upselling or pressure" (Callie W.), "very down to earth" (Brooke B.), "Honest, honest, honest" (ghuskerdude1). Answer: no; Rick lays out the options and the price.
3. **How long until the crew can come?** Plan on a couple of weeks (Chicken W.); the crew keeps you posted (Brooke B.); dates have come early (Callie W., Dwight S.). Exact date depends on the order.
4. **Do I have to replace everything?** No. One window, a few, or the whole house (Stacy C., Daniel H., Brooke B.).
5. **Do you handle insurance claims?** Yes, for windows (Lakesha B.) and hail roofing (site).
6. **What does the warranty cover?** Our installation (Rick's own statement). Show the window's own paperwork for the product. Nothing on lifetime or glass breakage is stated (unconfirmed; Bret O. dispute).
7. **How do I pay, and is there financing?** Synchrony Bank, 18 months no monthly interest if paid in full; checks and cards.
8. **Do you speak Spanish?** Se Habla Español (site).
