## Window page: sections
1. Hero: Owner-led, one paragraph ending in "Call for a free estimate." Review right under it: Jennifer M., "I can sit in my office and the curtains do not move anymore!!" (old house, 14 windows and a slider).
2. A quote with no clock running. Moment: dreading the evening-long pitch. Reason: price shouldn't depend on signing tonight. We do: measure every opening, show the actual window, price with no pressure, whole house or a few rooms, beat a lower comparable price (facts: price match).
3. Signs your windows are costing you. Moment: curtains move, fog, one hot room, street noise, sticking sash. Reason for each. Do: replace only what needs it. Review: Aaron W., "I can already tell the difference in the house temperature wise as well as diminished noise from the outside."
4. Window styles: casement, double hung, sliding, awning, hopper, picture, bay, basement (facts), with the reason each is picked. Colors, hardware, grids at the consultation. Review: Kathleen M., oil rubbed bronze hardware, custom grids, quote under three others.
5. Fewer, bigger windows. Moment: rows of small windows in an older house. Reason: fewer units to buy and install. Do: show both versions. Review: Rob R., 34 windows down to 17.
6. Old openings. Moment: crooked 1940s-50s openings. Reason: stock sizes don't sit right. Do: measure each opening, order to fit, finish trim, cover and protect first. Review: Dustin M., 80 year old openings.
7. Basement windows. Moment: low windows in the dirt and cold. Do: same fitting and finishing as upstairs (facts: basement windows are a listed service; Debbie G. not quoted, review cap).
8. Warranty: manufacturer's lifetime warranty, glass breakage to how the window works (facts).
9. After you call: consultation about 1.5 hours, built to measure, a few weeks, install usually a day, walkthrough and follow-up.
10. Windows and siding together, with a link to the siding page.
11. Area, questions, final call to action.

Questions a visitor asks, with answers from the facts: how long the install takes (most homes a day, second morning for trim on bigger jobs); what affects price; what the warranty covers; a few windows now and the rest later (yes); financing (yes, low-to-no-interest options); what if I get a lower quote (bring it, we beat a comparable one); free estimate (yes).
