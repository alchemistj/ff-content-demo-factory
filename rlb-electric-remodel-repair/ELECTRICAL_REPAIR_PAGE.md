### FAQ

**What should I do if an outlet stops working?**  
First, check if a breaker has tripped by looking at your electrical panel. If a breaker is in the "off" position, flip it back on. If the outlet still doesn't work or if the breaker immediately trips again, call us. Don't keep resetting a tripping breaker—that's usually a sign something's wrong that needs professional attention.

**Why does a breaker keep tripping?**  
A breaker that trips repeatedly usually means something on that circuit is drawing too much power, there's a short, or there's a ground fault. It's a safety feature, not something to work around. We can find the problem and fix it.

**Is this an emergency?**  
If power is completely out or if something sparked or burned, call us. If one outlet isn't working and the rest of your home is fine, it can usually wait for a regular appointment, but we can often fit you in quickly.

**What if I think there might be a fire risk?**  
If you smell burning wires, see scorching around an outlet or breaker, or suspect a fire hazard, turn off that circuit immediately and call. Those are safety problems that need urgent attention.

**Why should I call RLB instead of trying to fix this myself?**  
Electrical repairs can be dangerous if you're not trained. We know how to diagnose the problem safely, and we understand the code requirements that keep your home safe. Most electrical problems look simple until something goes wrong.

**How quickly can you come out?**  
We do our best to fit repair calls in quickly, especially if there's a real safety issue. Call us and we'll let you know what we can arrange.
