### FAQ

**What should I do if an outlet stops working?**  
Check your electrical panel for a tripped breaker. Flip it back on. If the outlet still doesn't work or the breaker trips again, call us. Don't keep resetting—that's usually a sign something needs professional attention.

**Why does a breaker keep tripping?**  
Usually means something on that circuit is drawing too much power, there's a short, or there's a ground fault. It's a safety feature. We can find and fix the problem.

**Is this an emergency?**  
If power is completely out or something sparked or burned, yes. If one outlet isn't working and the rest of your home is fine, it can wait—but call and we'll fit you in quickly.

**What if I smell burning wires?**  
Turn off that circuit immediately and call. Fire risk needs urgent attention.

**Why not fix this myself?**  
Electrical work is dangerous without training. We diagnose safely and know code requirements that keep your home safe. Most problems look simple until something goes wrong.

**How quickly can you come?**  
We fit repair calls in quickly, especially for safety issues. Call and we'll let you know what we can arrange.
